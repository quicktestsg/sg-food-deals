Budget Date Night in Singapore (7–15 Nov 2026): 1-for-1 Dinner & Dessert Deals for Two Under $30

A cheap date doesn't have to feel cheap. This week lines up 1-for-1 Korean fried chicken, a one-day TamJai mixian double, Starbucks vouchers from $7.40 and $5.90 yakiniku tea-time sets — every deal verified with dates, prices and the fine print.

Dating in Singapore gets expensive fast, but the 7–15 November window this year is unusually generous. Deepavali promotions are still running, 11.11 sales have started early, and several chains are running 1-for-1 deals that happen to be perfect for two. Here is a date-night battle plan organised by how soon each deal disappears.

1. This Weekend Only (7–9 Nov): Move Fast

Jinjja Chicken — 12 drumettes for $8.90

From 7 to 9 November 2026, Jinjja Chicken's 1-for-1 promotion turns a 6-piece Drumettes order ($8.90) into 12 pieces of crispy Korean fried chicken — effectively $0.74 a piece. Three days only, at participating outlets. Split a box between two people, add rice or fries, and the savoury half of your date is done for under $10.

AMACHA VivoCity — 1-for-1 herbal drinks, opening day only

Today, 7 November 2026, AMACHA celebrates its VivoCity grand opening with a one-day 1-for-1 on five signature herbal drinks (Grandma's Remedy, Breeze Cloud, Ethereal Beauty, Rosy Radiance, Respira Bloom) from 12pm to 8pm, plus a lion dance at 11.30am. Two drinks for the price of one, at the mall with the best harbourfront sunset walk in Singapore — the date practically plans itself.

Whole roast duck for $13 at Singapore EXPO

For an unconventional date, the Asia Pacific Food Expo 2026 runs at Singapore EXPO Hall 4 from 6 to 9 November, 11am–9pm. Tiong Lian Food (Booth SPA04) is selling whole Roasted Beijing Duck, Soy Braised Duck or Salted Braised Duck at $13 each, or 2 for $20. A his-and-hers duck for a tenner each is hard to beat — go before the fair ends on Monday.

2. 11.11 Week (9–15 Nov): The Main Event

TamJai SamGor — 1-for-1 mixian on 11.11 only

On 11 November 2026 only, build your own Mixian bowl with at least two toppings and get a second bowl free at The Clementi Mall and Junction 8. Note the split: Clementi is dine-in, while the Junction 8 deal is takeaway-only. Build the second bowl differently and share — two custom noodles for the price of one.

Starbucks — 1-for-1 Venti vouchers from $7.40 on Shopee

Starbucks' 11.11 Shopee sale offers 10 discounted drink deals, with 1-for-1 Venti bundles starting from $7.40 — a 50% saving on two large drinks. Vouchers must be redeemed at eligible outlets and stay valid for 30 days after purchase, so even if you buy during the 11.11 rush you have until mid-December to use them. The catch: buy on Shopee, not in store.

Secret Recipe — 1-for-1 cake or starter, but only at Toa Payoh

From 7 to 13 November 2026, the 11.11 Double the Treats promo gives you one free selected sliced cake or starter for every main course purchased at the Secret Recipe Toa Payoh outlet. Dine-in only, one redemption per main. Main-course prices aren't part of the promo — budget for those separately.

Ah Mah Castella — original cake at $6 with any purchase

From 9 to 13 November 2026, buy any flavoured Castella cake in-store at Ah Mah (Iluvahmah) and add the Original Castella Cake for just $6 — a saving of over 39% on the second cake. Available at Paya Lebar Square and VivoCity, while stocks last. One flavoured + one original = dessert course sorted for the walk home.

3. Running All Month: No Rush, But Don't Forget

Yakiniku LIKE — tea-time sets from $5.90

Ongoing since 3 November 2026: weekday tea-time sets from just $5.90, available 3pm–5pm at 12 selected outlets, with savings of up to 40% on chicken, karubi, pork belly, tiger prawn and sausage sets. A 3.30pm grilled-meat date for two could cost less than one dinner main elsewhere. Weekdays only — this is a skip-work-early special.

Springleaf Prata Place — Deepavali Trio at $6.90

Until 5 December 2026, the Deepavali Trio serves three crispy prata tacos — Butter Chicken, Chilli Fish and Gulab Jamun — plus a cup of hot masala tea for $6.90, at almost all Singapore outlets. Sweet, savoury and spicy in one platter: arguably the most date-efficient $6.90 on the island.

MIXUE — Orh Nee bundles from $5

Until 19 November 2026, MIXUE's Deepavali bundles offer 1 drink + 1 Orh Nee Pearl Sundae for $5, or 2 drinks + 1 sundae for $8. Two drinks and a shared sundae for $8 is a legitimate dessert date. Note these are bundle prices at participating outlets — check the MIXUE app before heading down.

Sushi Tei Bukit Panjang Plaza — opening month treats

The new Bukit Panjang Plaza outlet is celebrating from 6 to 30 November 2026 in stages: 6–15 November brings a free glass of Jabara Juice with a minimum $40 subtotal spend; 16–22 November adds a free dessert with a qualifying spend; and the promotion series includes $1 lobster maki offers. If you're in the north-west, the $40 threshold is achievable for two and the freebies rotate weekly — worth two visits.

CHAGEE 9th anniversary — bundles and 20% off Wednesdays

From 6 to 25 November 2026, CHAGEE's anniversary promo includes an app-exclusive $17.90 milk tea + plush hand towel bundle, 20% off milk tea on selected Wednesdays, sure-win prizes and voucher packs. The bundle leans more "gift for someone" than "cheap drink" — save it for when the relationship merits a towel-level gesture.

Mr Bean — pancake BOGO and Crazy Thursdays

Two overlapping promos: until 13 November, buy two Orh Nee Mochi Pancakes and get a third (worth $4) free by flashing your anniversary coupon; and on Crazy Thursdays, the second eligible soy drink is 50% off (discount applied to the lower-priced drink) — last confirmed for 5 November 2026, so check the Mr Bean app for whether 12 November repeats it. Orh Nee anything is peak Singapore comfort-dessert energy.

4. Two Verified Sub-$20 Date Plans

💡 Date Plan A — Korean Fried Chicken Night ($16.90 for two)

This weekend (7–9 Nov): Jinjja Chicken 12 drumettes ($8.90) + MIXUE 2-drinks-and-a-sundae bundle ($8) = $16.90 total for two people, verified prices. Do it before Sunday night — the drumette deal ends 9 November.

💡 Date Plan B — 11.11 Coffee & Noodles Day

Wednesday 11 November: Starbucks 1-for-1 Venti voucher from $7.40 on Shopee (buy in advance, redeem later — 30-day validity) + TamJai SamGor 1-for-1 mixian at Clementi (dine-in) or Junction 8 (takeaway). Mixian bowl prices vary by toppings, so check the in-store menu for the exact build you want doubled.

5. What to Skip

  • Starbucks vouchers if you rarely go: 30-day validity sounds long until life happens. Only buy what you'll realistically redeem.
  • Secret Recipe if you're not near Toa Payoh: travelling across the island for a free cake slice eats the savings. The promo is single-outlet.
  • TamJai on any day but 11.11: the 1-for-1 is strictly one day. A regular-price visit on 12 November is just a regular Tuesday.
