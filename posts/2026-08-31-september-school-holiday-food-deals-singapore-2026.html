September School Holiday Food Deals Singapore 2026: Student 1-for-1 KBBQ, Free Crab Bee Hoon & Free Family Events

The September school break starts tomorrow — and the deals line up almost perfectly with it. Student 1-for-1 Korean BBQ, free crab bee hoon with buffet, a free Premier League screening party, Mid-Autumn lights and $3 every Saturday at the hawker centre. Every date and catch checked.

August's promotions wrapped up over the weekend, and September's wave begins on the 1st — almost in lockstep with the school holiday. That is not a coincidence: restaurants know families and students are out in force this fortnight, and several of the month's best offers are explicitly built around them. This guide organises everything by week, so the answer to "what should we do today?" is always one scroll away.

Week 1 (Sep 1–7): Free Crab Bee Hoon, Student KBBQ & a Free Football Party

Penang Culture (Compass One) — free Crab Bee Hoon with buffet, from Sep 1 (end date not announced). From tomorrow, the buffet at Penang Culture's Compass One outlet is upgraded with a new Crab Bee Hoon — sweet crab with savoury bee hoon — and it comes FREE with the buffet. The buffet price itself is not stated in the source, so check the restaurant's official channels before you go. If the price point matches other Penang Culture buffet promos, a crab dish folded into an existing buffet is one of the better "free upgrade" structures around: you are not buying anything extra to qualify.

I'm Kim Korean BBQ (Dhoby Ghaut) — student 1-for-1, Sep 1–13. Students get a 1-for-1 Korean BBQ promotion running the first thirteen days of the month. The source lists booking requirements, specific lunch and dinner timings, and student eligibility rules — meaning: bring your student ID, book ahead, and check which session the promo applies to before turning up. This window is deliberately matched to the school break, so treat Sep 13 as a hard deadline.

StarHub WatchParty — free Premier League screening, Sep 6 at SAFRA Punggol. One date only: Everton vs Manchester United and Arsenal vs Chelsea, screened live, with games, prizes, photo opportunities and free snacks and goodie bags for registered attendees, while stocks last. Registration is required — walk-ins are not guaranteed anything. For a football-mad family with kids old enough to enjoy a stadium atmosphere, this is a $0 evening out.

Week 2 (Sep 8–13): Last Call for Student Deals & the Mid-Autumn Warm-Up

I'm Kim student 1-for-1 ends Sep 13. The second week is your buffer — but do not leave it to the final weekend, when every other student in Singapore has the same idea. Book a weekday lunch slot if the promo's timings allow it.

Gardens by the Bay Mid-Autumn Festival — from Sep 12, free admission. This year's Mid-Autumn light-up at Gardens by the Bay opens Sep 12, headlined by local character 芬宝 (Fenbao), with dreamlike light installations, a giant sky whale, LED lion dance displays and a food fair. Entry is free. The full run dates are not stated in the source — the festival typically runs for about two weeks around Mid-Autumn itself (Sep 25 this year) — so check the official Gardens by the Bay site for the closing date before planning a late-September visit.

Aperia Mall school holiday programme — September, free activities. For parents who want an air-conditioned, low-planning option, Aperia Mall's September school holiday family guide promises free play areas, interactive activities and food discoveries — a mall day that does not cost admission. The mall has not published a day-by-day schedule in the source, so check their site or socials for specific event timings during the break.

Mid-September Onwards: HEYTEA's Free Bag, Half-Price A5 Wagyu

HEYTEA ION Orchard reopening — free oil wax bag with any drink, Sep 14–20. To celebrate its reopened ION Orchard store, HEYTEA is giving away a free oil wax bag with any in-store drink purchase — but only 100 bags per day, while stocks last. That daily cap means the bag is gone by mid-afternoon on busy days; if you want it, be there when the doors open. The drink itself is regular price — the freebie is the bag, not the tea.

Yakiniku Like — 50% off A5 Wagyu Set (150g), Aug 29–Sep 28, conditions apply. Foodie San members get half price off the A5 Wagyu Set — A5 Miyazaki wagyu brisket with rice, soup and salad or kimchi — with a minimum $40 nett spend per table. Two catches to note: you must be a Foodie San member (free to sign up, but do it before ordering), and the $40 minimum is on the whole bill, not on the wagyu set itself. The set's original price is not stated in the source, so work the maths backwards from your bill once you see the menu.

All Month: Group KBBQ Where the Cheapest Eater Pays Nothing

I'm Kim Korean BBQ 4PLUS1 — Sep 1–30, Dhoby Ghaut. The headline September offer at I'm Kim: dine in a group of five (90-minute KBBQ session) and the lowest-paying diner eats FREE for the whole month. Compare the two I'm Kim promos: the student 1-for-1 (one student + one friend) is better for pairs, while 4PLUS1 is strictly a group play — but note it requires four paying diners, so a family of four cannot "split" into a free fifth meal without a fifth person at the table. Session length is capped at 90 minutes, and the source mentions terms you need to meet before dining — read them when booking.

DBS PayLah! $3 Saturday cashback — Aug 29 to Dec 19. Every Saturday, eligible DBS/POSB customers get up to $3 cashback when paying via PayLah! at participating hawker stalls, coffee shops, wet markets and heartland shops. The Chinese-language source adds detail the English one omits: the weekly cap is the first 160,000 customers each Saturday, the campaign runs 17 Saturdays with a $2.7 million total budget, and the qualifying spend per transaction is $5 or more. A school holiday Saturday hawker lunch for two, paid via PayLah!, effectively drops $3 — small, but it repeats weekly until 19 December.

Strategy: What to Do Each Week of the Break

  • This week (Sep 1–5): Students book I'm Kim 1-for-1 for a weekday lunch; families hit Penang Culture at Compass One for the crab bee hoon buffet while the month is young and tables are easier to get.
  • Saturday, Sep 5: PayLah! hawker breakfast or lunch for the $3 cashback (min $5 spend, first-come basis).
  • Sunday, Sep 6: Register (in advance!) for the StarHub WatchParty at SAFRA Punggol — free EPL double-header, snacks and goodie bags.
  • Week 2 (Sep 8–13): Last window for the student 1-for-1 (ends Sep 13 — avoid the final weekend rush). Rainy day? Aperia Mall's free holiday activities.
  • Saturday, Sep 12: Mid-Autumn lights open at Gardens by the Bay — free entry, go early in the run before the Mid-Autumn week crowds.
  • After the break (Sep 14–20): HEYTEA ION reopening freebie for the students who survived exams week — 100 bags a day, morning visit recommended.
💡 The Family Math: Why Groups Win in September

The two I'm Kim promos stack the same tables differently: a pair of students uses the 1-for-1 (one pays), while a family of five uses 4PLUS1 (four pay, cheapest eats free). Either way, the discount lands on the person who ordered least — so the smart move is to order evenly rather than letting one person "sacrifice" a cheap meal. Combine that with $3 PayLah! Saturdays and the free Mid-Autumn lights, and a full holiday fortnight's entertainment bill can stay remarkably close to zero.

What to Skip or Double-Check

Two grocery-adjacent September promos look tempting but deserve scrutiny. The Nissin Cup Noodles Plushie Bundle (from Aug 29, end date not announced) — five cups plus a collectible plushie and tote — is a collectible play, not a savings play; the bundle price is not stated in the source, so unless the plushie matters to you, plain cup noodles on regular promo are usually cheaper per cup. Likewise the F&N MAGNOLIA Winnie the Pooh pouch promo (from Aug 29 onwards, buy two 946ml packs to redeem) is a purchase-with-redemption, not a discount — you are paying full price for milk to get a pouch. Fun for kids who love the characters; not a deal if you just need milk. And FairPrice Xtra's Senior Week (Buy-1-Get-1-Free supplements and adult diapers, ends Sep 2) is genuinely good value — but strictly for households with seniors, and it closes the day after tomorrow.

Bottom line: September's school holiday is unusually rich in genuinely free family outings — a live football party, a major light festival, free mall activities — while the paid-discount side concentrates on Korean BBQ in groups. Plan the free events for weekends, the KBBQ for weekday lunches, and let the $3 Saturday cashback quietly subsidise the hawker meals in between.
