Payday Food Deals Singapore (Aug 27–31, 2026): Free Burgers, 1-for-1 Coffee, Supermarket Sales

Payday lands this week and the deal calendar is unusually loaded: 101 free burgers at Meatsmith today only, 1-for-1 coffee at Kenangan all payday weekend, $2.90 smoked duck at Yakiniku Like, and all four major supermarkets resetting their weekly specials on 27 August.

Salaries typically land in the last week of August, and Singapore cafes and supermarkets have timed their promotions to match. This guide covers every deal worth your attention from Thursday 27 through Monday 31 August 2026 — with exact dates, prices and conditions from the source announcements, plus a day-by-day plan so nothing slips past before it expires.

1. Today Only: Meatsmith Gives Away 101 Free Burgers (27 Aug)

Smoked-meat specialists Meatsmith just ranked #50 on the World’s 101 Best Burger Places list — making it Singapore’s top-ranked burger destination in that ranking — and the team is celebrating with a one-day giveaway. On Thursday 27 August 2026, from 11:30am onwards, Meatsmith is giving away 101 MSX Single Cheeseburgers for free at its Telok Ayer outlet.

The maths is simple: 101 burgers, one location, while they last. If you work in the CBD, this is a lunch you walk to. Once the 101st burger is claimed, the giveaway ends — arriving at 11:30am sharp beats hoping for a late-afternoon slot.

🍔 Deal Facts

What: Free MSX Single Cheeseburger (101 available). When: 27 August 2026 only, from 11:30am onwards. Where: Meatsmith Telok Ayer. Conditions: One-day giveaway while stocks last — no purchase requirement mentioned in the announcement.

2. Payday Weekend Coffee: Kenangan 1-for-1 (27–29 Aug) and Dunkin’ Payday Promo

Two coffee chains are running overlapping payday promotions this week, so caffeine is effectively half-price if you play it right.

Kenangan Coffee — the Indonesian specialty-coffee chain — is running its Buy 1 Get 1 FREE coffee promo from Thursday 27 to Saturday 29 August 2026. Buy any qualifying coffee and the second one is free, which makes it the natural grab-and-go option for the payday Friday morning rush. The promotion carries exclusions on certain drinks, so check the counter before ordering your usual.

Dunkin’ started its payday promotion on 26 August 2026 (end date not announced): 1-for-1 on Cold Brew and on Hot or Iced Teh Tarik, plus 2 free doughnuts when you buy 5. If your payday ritual is coffee plus a box of doughnuts for the office, Dunkin’s version covers both in one stop.

Mr Bean rounds out the breakfast run: from 26 August 2026 onwards (end date not announced), buy a 3-in-1 Riceball and the second eligible soy drink is 50% off. Notable exclusion: only eligible soy drinks qualify, so flavoured or seasonal blends may sit outside the promo — the source notes specific drinks are excluded.

3. Cheapest Full Plate This Week: Yakiniku Like Smoked Duck from $2.90 (till 7 Oct)

Yakiniku Like has brought back its popular Smoked Duck with Original BBQ Sauce, and the pricing is aggressive: $2.90 when ordered with a set, or $3.90 à la carte. The promotion runs from 26 August to 7 October 2026 at participating outlets island-wide — a six-week window, so there is no rush, but the price is hard to beat for an actual hot meal.

One condition to note: participating outlets only. Yakiniku Like runs a large network, so if you are making a special trip, confirm your nearest branch is on the list first.

4. New Launch: Pizza Hut Cheesy 7 Soy Garlic Chicken Pizza from $13.95 (27 Aug – 11 Nov)

Pizza Hut launches its new Cheesy 7 Soy Garlic Chicken Pizza today, 27 August 2026, alongside new Garlic Cream Cheese Melts, available island-wide through 11 November 2026. Prices start from $13.95, with the usual spread of pizza deals, delivery bundles and dine-in offers layered on top.

The quirky extra: a #Cheesy7Idol Dance Challenge runs with the launch, carrying a S$1,000 cash grand prize. Participation details are on Pizza Hut’s channels; the pizza itself requires no choreography.

5. Mid-Autumn Watch: Starbucks 8 Mooncakes at $69.90

Starbucks has opened its Mid-Autumn season from 26 August 2026 onwards (end date not announced): 8 assorted mooncakes for $69.90, mixing traditional and modern flavours. Starbucks Rewards members additionally earn Bonus Stars when purchasing multiple regular-priced items — useful if a mooncake box was already on your gifting list. For a wider early-bird mooncake comparison across hotels and brands, see the earlier guide dated 24 August.

6. All Four Supermarkets Reset Their Specials Today (27 Aug)

Thursday 27 August is the biggest supermarket reset day of the month: FairPrice, Giant, Sheng Siong and Cold Storage all switch to new weekly specials simultaneously. Here is the full comparison.

FairPrice’s cut spans fresh produce, frozen and chilled food, pantry staples, beverages and household products.

FairPrice is also running freebies-with-purchase from 26 August: SadShrimps Blind Box Charms, Hello Kitty Eco Bags and more, spotted at VivoCity. Availability varies by store and is while stocks last — worth a detour only if you were heading there anyway.

💡 One-Trip Stacking Strategy

Do the big payday grocery run today, Thursday 27 Aug: FairPrice’s new specials just went live, Blue CHAS cardholders get the extra 3% Thursday discount on top, and the freebies-with-purchase table (SadShrimps charms, Hello Kitty eco bags) is at its fullest stock. Cross to Cold Storage in the same trip for the yuu-member 1-for-1 Kinder ice cream — one afternoon, three layers of value.

7. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Thursday 27 Aug (today): Meatsmith Telok Ayer from 11:30am for the free burger — this expires today. Do the payday grocery run: new specials at all four supermarkets, plus Blue CHAS 3% off at FairPrice on Thursdays. Pizza Hut Cheesy 7 launches today if dinner is sorted late.
  • Friday 28 Aug: Kenangan 1-for-1 coffee for the payday-morning treat. Dunkin’ 1-for-1 Cold Brew or Teh Tarik if the queue is shorter. Yakiniku Like $2.90 smoked duck set for a cheap dinner.
  • Saturday 29 Aug: Last day of Kenangan 1-for-1 — grab it before it ends. Mr Bean riceball + 50% off second soy drink for the family breakfast run.
  • Sunday 30 Aug: Final day of the Sheng Siong 4 Days Special (remember the 3-unit limit) and last day of Cold Storage’s yuu-member 1-for-1 Kinder ice cream.
  • Monday 31 Aug: Wind-down day. FairPrice, Giant and Cold Storage weekly specials continue to 2 Sep; anything you missed can wait. The Yakiniku Like duck and Pizza Hut Cheesy 7 run well into October and November, so no urgency.
⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal above has a catch worth knowing: Meatsmith — 101 burgers only, one outlet, while stocks last. Kenangan — certain drinks excluded. Dunkin’ — end date not announced. Mr Bean — only eligible soy drinks get the 50% off. Yakiniku Like — participating outlets only. Giant — availability varies by store. Sheng Siong — 3 units per product per customer. Cold Storage Kinder 1-for-1 — yuu membership required. FairPrice CHAS 3% — Blue CHAS cardholders, Thursdays only.

8. The Verdict

This is one of the better payday weeks of the quarter. The two genuinely time-critical plays are Meatsmith’s free burger (today only) and the supermarket reset (biggest selection today, CHAS discount stacks on Thursdays). The coffee promos are nice-to-haves that happen to overlap, and the Yakiniku Like duck at $2.90 is the week’s best value for an actual meal with a long runway. Everything else can wait for the weekend — which is exactly what payday weekends are for.
