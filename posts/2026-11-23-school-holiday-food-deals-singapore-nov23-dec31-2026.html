School Holiday Food Deals Singapore (23 Nov – 31 Dec 2026): Kids Eat Free Buffet, $19.90 Mookata & Student KTV

The school holidays start today — and restaurants across Singapore have rolled out their festive artillery. Here's the full playbook: kids-eat-free buffets, $19.90 free-flow Mookata, $24++ student KTV sessions, $1 dim sum and free family outings that cost nothing at all.

School holidays in Singapore run from 23 November to 31 December 2026, and that five-week stretch is exactly when dining promos get aggressive. Restaurants know families are eating out more, students are free all day, and everyone is hunting for airconditioned activities. This guide rounds up the best verified deals running these holidays, organised by what kind of eater you're feeding — with dates, prices and the fine print for each.

1. Kids Eat Free — The Family Buffet Plays

Yum Cha Changi — Kids Eat Free Dinner Buffet (20 Nov – 31 Dec 2026). The headline family deal of the season: children aged 5 to 12 get a complimentary dinner buffet with every paying adult at the Changi outlet. For a family of two adults and two kids, that's effectively half the bill gone. The trade-off is location — Changi is a trek if you live in the west — but pair it with a Jewel trip and it becomes a full day out.

131 Mookata — S$19.90 Free-Flow Buffet (from 18 Nov 2026, end date not announced). Not strictly a kids deal, but the best-value buffet of the holidays: S$19.90 per person for a free-flow Thai barbecue-hotpot spread with Australian crayfish, garlic butter prawns, scallops, squid, marinated meats and over 50 buffet selections. Available at Paya Lebar and Kelantan Road. Two outlets means you can pick whichever side of town you're on.

Monster Planet — $9.90 Kids Meal Set + Free Christmas Colouring Page (from 17 Nov 2026, end date not announced). For younger kids (7 and below), the themed restaurant is giving a free Christmas colouring page with every $9.90 kids meal set, plus a festive Toasty Winterland dessert. Cheap seat-and-entertain option when you need 90 minutes of peace.

💡 Family Math: Yum Cha vs 131 Mookata

Two adults + two kids (aged 5–12): at Yum Cha Changi you pay for 2 adult dinner buffets and the kids eat free. At 131 Mookata everyone pays S$19.90 each (4 × S$19.90). If your kids can out-eat their age group in crayfish, Mookata wins; if they're picky small eaters, Yum Cha's free-tier is unbeatable. Prices shown exclude ++ where applicable — confirm the adult buffet price directly with the outlet before booking.

2. Student Hangouts Under $25

ChaoK Party Bugis — Student Holiday KTV from $24++ (23 Nov – 31 Dec 2026). Timed exactly to the school holidays: 2-hour karaoke packages from $24++ per group — not per person — including snacks and free-flow drinks with unlimited refills. A group of four splits that into pocket-money territory. Bugis location makes it an easy MRT meetup.

Play Nation — Unlimited Board Games from $13.90++ (till 31 Dec 2026). The Prinsep Street board game café runs its student deal daily from 12pm to 6pm: unlimited board gaming with free-flow drinks and snacks from $13.90++ per person. Rainy afternoon solved.

Lotteria — Any 2 Burgers for $10 (19 – 30 Nov 2026). The Korean burger chain's student promotion runs at all outlets: any two burgers for $10, with an optional $3.50 meal upgrade. Two students, one tenner, done.

Genki Sushi Waterway Point — 2+1 FREE Sushi (20 Nov – 10 Dec 2026). Buy two promotional sushi plates, get one free, with flavours (Salmon, Unagi, Ebi) rotating weekly. Bonus: spend $50 nett and get a free pair of Genki socks worth $3.90. Punggol residents, this is your week-one plan.

3. Restaurant Deals Worth a Family Trip

Peach Garden — $1 Six-Flavour Siew Mai Platter (from 19 Nov 2026, end date not announced). The most ridiculous discount of the season: the new six-flavour siew mai platter, usually $18, is going for $1 — with unlimited promotional platters per eligible table. Stack it with 50% off weekday dim sum specials (30% on weekends) at selected outlets, and a dim sum lunch for four lands in budget-hawker territory. Registration/NRIC may be required at participating outlets — bring it.

TungLok Group 90-Minute Madness — 30% Off (23 Nov – 30 Dec 2026). Starts today. Thirty percent off regular-priced à la carte food and set menus across selected TungLok restaurants, with two windows daily: Early Bird (11am – 12:30pm) or Late Dinner (8:30 – 10pm). No blackout dates — weekends, Christmas Eve and Christmas included. If a festive feast at a mid-upscale Chinese restaurant is on your list, this is the discount that makes it reasonable.

Jack's Place — $15++ Ribeye Steak, 3 Days Only (23 – 25 Nov 2026). The 60th anniversary deal: a 180g New Zealand ribeye for $15++ instead of the usual $29++, dine-in from 5pm at all Jack's Place and Eatzi Gourmet outlets. Catch: it's for JP Pepperdine members only (free to sign up if you aren't yet). Three-day window — this is the most time-sensitive deal in this guide.

4. Free & Nearly-Free Family Outings

School holidays don't have to be spend-holidays. Three zero-cost anchors to build meals around:

  • Doulos Hope Floating Book Fair (24 Nov – 13 Dec 2026): the floating bookstore docks at VivoCity with over 2,000 titles — science, arts, languages and children's books. Free to board. Combine with a HarbourFront lunch and you've got a full day under $20 per person.
  • Free Cable Car Sentosa Line Rides (21 Nov – 20 Dec 2026): daily from 8.45am to 11am at Sensoryscape, Imbiah Lookout or Siloso Point — no booking, no voucher. Ride over, lunch on Sentosa, bus back.
  • McDonald's Worldwide Menu Launch (from 26 Nov 2026, 11am): not free, but the cheapest family hype event of the season — the Maple BBQ burgers, Sichuan mala sauce, Pistachio McPops and Hamburglar merchandise land on Thursday. Expect queues; go off-peak if you value your sanity.

5. Strategy: Your First Week, Day by Day

  • Monday 23 Nov (today): TungLok 90-Minute Madness begins — book an Early Bird lunch this week before word spreads. Jack's Place $15++ ribeye also starts today (members, from 5pm).
  • Tuesday 24 Nov: Doulos Hope book fair opens at VivoCity — morning cable car is too far, so pair the floating bookstore with lunch in the HarbourFront area instead.
  • Wednesday 25 Nov: Last day for Jack's Place $15++ ribeye. Also a good weekday for Peach Garden's $1 siew mai + 50% off weekday dim sum specials.
  • Thursday 26 Nov, 11am: McDonald's Worldwide Menu launch. Off-peak only.
  • Saturday/Sunday: Free Sentosa cable car before 11am, Sentosa lunch, then let the kids crash. Students can slot ChaoK Party KTV ($24++/group) or Play Nation ($13.90++) any afternoon.
  • Before 30 Nov: Lotteria's 2 burgers for $10 and the Giant/FairPrice weekly supermarket deals close — stock the pantry this weekend.
⚠️ Condition Check — Read Before You Go

Every deal above has a catch: Yum Cha kids-free is for ages 5–12 at the Changi outlet only, with every paying adult. Jack's Place $15++ ribeye requires JP Pepperdine membership and dine-in from 5pm, 23–25 Nov only. TungLok 30% applies to regular-priced à la carte and regular set menus during the two promotion windows only. Peach Garden $1 siew mai is at selected outlets and may require NRIC registration. ChaoK Party student pricing is per group for the 2-hour package, ++ applies. Free cable car is mornings only (8.45am–11am) on the Sentosa Line's three stations. When in doubt, call the outlet before travelling.

The Bottom Line

The school holiday promo season runs hot from now till Christmas, and the best value clusters in three places: kid-friendly buffets (Yum Cha Changi's free-tier for the little ones, 131 Mookata's S$19.90 for everyone else), student hangouts under $25 (ChaoK, Play Nation, Lotteria), and restaurant discounts with strict timing windows (TungLok's 30% starts today, Jack's Place ribeye ends Wednesday). Deals marked 'end date not announced' can finish without warning once stocks or capacity run out — the earlier in the holidays you go, the safer the deal. Prices and terms verified as of 23 November 2026; always reconfirm with the outlet before making a special trip.
