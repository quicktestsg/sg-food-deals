SIA Time To Fly Travel Fair 2026: 380,000 Discounted Flights & This Week's Travel Deals in Singapore

The biggest travel event of the season opens Thursday - 380,000 discounted SIA and Scoot tickets to 125 destinations. Here is the full week's travel playbook, plus the cheap eats to fuel your planning.

If you have been holding off on booking a trip, this is the week to move. Three travel windows overlap within days of each other: the Singapore Airlines Time To Fly Travel Fair opens on 23 October, AirAsia's up-to-30% sale closes for bookings on 25 October, and Star Voyager's cruise promotion from S$180 per person is already live. This guide lays out the dates, the catches and a day-by-day plan so nothing slips past you.

1. The Headline: SIA Time To Fly Travel Fair 2026 (23-30 Oct)

Singapore Airlines' Time To Fly Travel Fair returns from 23 to 30 October 2026, with more than 380,000 discounted flight tickets across Singapore Airlines and Scoot to 125 destinations. Whether you want a full-service SIA long-haul trip or a low-fare Scoot hop to a nearby city, both sit under the same fair this time.

⚠️ Price Unconfirmed

The deal announcements so far confirm the ticket volume (380,000+), destination count (125) and the fair dates (23-30 Oct 2026), but specific promotional fares per route have not been published yet. Do not trust screenshots quoting exact prices until the fair opens - check SIA's official channels on 23 October for the real fare tables.

2. AirAsia: Up to 30% Off, But Book by 25 October

AirAsia is running up to 30% off flights for travel all the way until September 2027 - an unusually long travel window. The catch is the booking deadline: 25 October 2026. The sale also covers travel extras and Fly-Thru connections (AirAsia's same-ticket transit routing), plus extra deals for cardholders.

Strategy note: because AirAsia's deadline falls inside the SIA fair window, you get a genuine comparison period. From 23 October you can check the newly released SIA/Scoot fares side by side with AirAsia's sale fares for two days before the AirAsia window shuts on the 25th.

3. Star Voyager Cruise: 4D3N From S$180 Per Person (From 20 Oct)

Prefer a getaway without the airport chaos? Star Voyager's 4-day-3-night cruise promotion is live from 20 October 2026, with advertised fares from S$180 per person. Two itinerary options are on the table: Phuket, or Melaka and Penang. At that entry price, a short cruise competes directly with a budget flight-plus-hotel package for a long weekend.

One caveat: S$180 is the advertised starting fare. As with all cruise promos, actual pricing depends on cabin type, sailing date and occupancy, so treat it as a floor, not a quote. (End date not announced - verify current pricing when booking.)

  • Thursday, 23 Oct: SIA Time To Fly Travel Fair opens. Fares drop across SIA and Scoot - start with your dream destination list, then shortlist realistic dates.

  • Sunday, 26 Oct onwards: SIA fair continues to 30 Oct. Meanwhile the food deals below run into November - no rush.

7. What To Skip

Not everything tagged October is worth your attention this week. Electronics roadshows (HONOR, 19-25 Oct) and bedding promos (Dunlopillo, 19-29 Oct) are running concurrently but have no connection to travel and no food angle - only worth a detour if those purchases were already on your list. And a reminder from the selection desk: bank time-deposit promos also circulating this week are finance products, not lifestyle deals, so they do not factor into an eating-and-travelling budget.

The Bottom Line

Mark two dates on your calendar: 23 October (SIA fair opens) and 25 October (AirAsia booking closes). Everything else this week - the cruise fares, the $6 whisky, the $10 chicken combos - is flexible. Those two dates are not. Plan the week around them, and let the cheap eats fill the gaps.
