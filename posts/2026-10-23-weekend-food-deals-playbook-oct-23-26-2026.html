Weekend Playbook: Best Food Deals in Singapore (23–26 Oct)

A family buffet where the fourth diner eats free, $5 Japanese food at Donki, salmon sushi week, and an ice cream price war — this weekend's playbook.

Weekends are when the deal calendar gets interesting — weekday flash promos expire tonight, supermarket specials hit their stride, and family-style dining deals come out to play. This guide covers 23–26 October only.

Buy single-serve this weekend; the tub promos run to 28 Oct, so do your freezer run on Monday — except Andersen's, which will sell out first.
