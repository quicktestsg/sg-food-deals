October 2026 Singapore: $1 Coffee, 50% Off Sushi & the Best Cheap Eats (Oct 1–14)

October opens with a rare pile-up: International Coffee Month makes coffee nearly free, a new Sushiro outlet brings half-price sushi to Hougang, and DON DON DONKI turns Sushi Day into a one-day $8.90 fiesta.

The first two weeks of October 2026 are unusually generous — coffee giveaways, overlapping sushi promos, and fresh supermarket specials, all organised below by craving.

1. The Coffee Play: $1 and Free

  • McDonald's — $1 coffee, October 1–31, members only. Selected Americano, Latte or Cappuccino for $1 daily from 10am via the app.
  • Cedele — free regular hot coffee, October 1 only. Any purchase + say \u201cIt's time for coffee!\u201d — first 100 cups per store, so go early.
  • KFC — 25% off selected Healthier Choice breakfast items, from October 1 (end date not announced). Check the counter for the participating-item list.

2. Sushi Week: Four Deals, One Fortnight

  • SUSHIRO Hougang Mall — grand opening, October 1–14. Selected sushi up to 50% off, $1.50++ free-flow drinks; Oct 1 only, spend $50 for a Sure Win Lucky Dip.
  • DON DON DONKI — Sushi Day, October 1 only. One-day deals from $8.90, while stocks last — a lunch-hour mission, not a dinner plan.
  • The Sushi Bar — 14th anniversary, October 1–25, in two phases. $14 Aburi Roll with selected Don (Oct 1–11), then a no-minimum-spend $14 return voucher for dine-ins (Oct 12–25).
  • Genki Sushi — 50% off Salmon Mentai every Thursday, Oct 1 – Dec 31, DBS Chromo card only. Three months of Thursday discounts, strictly for cardmembers.

3. Cheap Japanese & Taiwanese Comfort Food

  • Typhoon Cafe — 80-minute Taiwanese buffet from $19.90++, from September 30 onwards (end date not announced). 24 dishes with free-flow Xiao Long Bao, Oyster Mee Sua and Lu Rou Fan; +$12.90++ adds premium dish and DIY tau huay.
  • Ramen KIOU — 31st anniversary dice roll, October 1–31. Roll three dice for a chance at a 50% OFF return voucher — the prize applies to your next visit, not the current bowl.
  • Yakiniku Like — chawanmushi at $2.90, from September 30 onwards, participating outlets. While stocks last, so it may not be available all day.
  • Suki-Suki Hot Pot — weekday set lunch from $10.90++, from September 30 onwards. Mon–Thu 11.30am–3pm only; soup base, staple and free-flow drinks included.
  • YAYOI — meat-free bento selection, from September 30 onwards (end date not announced). Price not confirmed — check the menu in store.
⚠️ Read the Fine Print

McDonald's needs membership + 10am start; Genki needs a DBS Chromo Card; Krispy Kreme's free 6 doughnuts (Oct 1–7) need a dozen bought in a single transaction.

4. The Grocery Reset: October's First Wave

  • FairPrice weekly specials — up to 58% off, October 1–7. Also: CHAS discounts increase to 6% until January 1, 2027.
  • FairPrice Korea Fair 2026 — October 1–21. Three weeks of Korean food, snacks and special events.
  • Sheng Siong seafood specials — September 30 – October 6, while stocks last. Crab and squid among the highlighted picks.
  • Giant — storewide up to 63% off from October 1 (varies by store), plus Tampines Hypermarket opening specials up to 56% off with B1G1F, October 1–7.

The Game Plan

October 1 is the convergence day: Cedele's free coffee, DONKI's Sushi Day, and the Sushiro opening. Thursdays repeat via Genki (with the right card), Typhoon Cafe and Suki-Suki cover dinners, Krispy Kreme runs to October 7, and CHAS Blue holders quietly win the month at FairPrice.
