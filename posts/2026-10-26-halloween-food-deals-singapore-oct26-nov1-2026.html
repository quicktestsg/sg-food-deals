Halloween Food Deals Singapore 2026: $2 Sundae, 1-for-1 Cheesecake & Sweet Treats (26 Oct – 1 Nov)

Halloween week is secretly one of the best weeks of the year for food deals in Singapore — cheesecake anniversaries, $2 sundaes, half-price ice cream and a $7.90 yakiniku set all land before 1 November. Here is the full trick-or-treat game plan.

Halloween in Singapore has never really been about the costumes — it is about the excuse. This week, bakeries roll out spooky cakes, fast food chains discount desserts to spooky-season levels, and one cheesecake chain celebrates its anniversary by literally giving product away. Everything below is organised by how soon it disappears, because the biggest trap in deal-hunting is finding out about a promotion three days after it ended.

1. Ending This Week — Grab Before They're Gone

Cat & the Fiddle — 1-for-1 whole cheesecakes (27–29 Oct)

The headline deal of the week. Cat & the Fiddle is marking its 12th anniversary with buy-one-get-one-free on whole cheesecakes from 27 to 29 October 2026, with early access opening a day earlier on 26 October. This is the kind of deal that sells out popular flavours by midday — if you have a birthday or office gathering coming up, this is the week to lock it in. Check the brand's official channels for exactly how the early access on 26 Oct works and which flavours qualify before heading down.

McDonald's — $2 Hershey's Sundae with any purchase (till 30 Oct)

Add a chocolatey Hershey's Sundae to any order for just $2, now until 30 October 2026. The catch is mild but real: you need to buy something else first — a $2 sundae stacked onto a full-price meal is a nice finish, not a standalone dessert run. The play here is combining it with a cheaper main (or a Happy Meal, see below) rather than treating it as the whole trip.

Pizza Hut — Cheesy 7 Trick or Treat Feast (16–30 Oct)

Pizza Hut's Halloween bundle runs until 30 October 2026: a large Cheesy 7 pizza, a side dish of your choice, two pieces of garlic bread and a mystery gift, while stocks last, for takeaway and delivery. The source listing does not publish a fixed price — treat the mystery gift as the bonus and check the current price on the ordering app before committing, since bundle pricing can shift mid-promotion.

Supermarket ice cream — half-price and 3-for-$25 windows close 28 Oct

Two overlapping freezer deals end Wednesday 28 October: FairPrice has half-price Andersen's ice cream plus discounts on Häagen-Dazs and Cornetto, while Cold Storage is running Häagen-Dazs tubs at any 3 for $25 (a saving of $19.25 off the listed regular price, per the source). If your freezer has space, this is the cheapest premium ice cream will get before the Christmas hampers crowd the aisles. Supermarket promos are while-stocks-last by nature — go early in the window, not on the final evening.

Hoshino Coffee — 50% off soufflé pancakes (21 Oct – 1 Nov)

To celebrate the reopening of its Plaza Singapura outlet, Hoshino Coffee is taking 50% off featured Signature Soufflé Pancakes when you order any main course or drink from the Grand Menu, from 21 October to 1 November 2026. Condition to note: the discount applies only at the reopened Plaza Singapura branch, and only with a main or drink in the same order — a pancake-only visit pays full price.

Boost Juice — Buy 1, Get 1 for $1 at Bugis Junction (ends 26 Oct)

Boost Juice's Bugis Junction reopening deal is a buy-one-get-one-for-$1 on selected Berries & Tropical range drinks. One detail worth flagging: the listing headline says 22–26 October, but its own details mention the promo days as 22 and 26 October. Sources disagree, so treat today (26 Oct) as the confirmed last day and call the outlet if you planned to go mid-week. Single-outlet reopening deals like this do not islandwide-apply — Bugis Junction only.

2. Halloween Weekend — 30 Oct to 1 Nov

Yakiniku-GO — $7.90++ Meat It Yourself set, one day only (30 Oct)

For its 5th anniversary, Yakiniku-GO is selling its Meat It Yourself set at $7.90++ (usual price $17.90, per the source) on 30 October 2026 only. There is also a members-only extra perk — if you are not already on their membership list, sign up before you order. A one-day, sub-$8 yakiniku set will draw queues; go at opening or well after the lunch rush.

GYUPOP Wagyu Beef Fair — Jurong, 30 Oct – 8 Nov

If Halloween for you is less about candy and more about meat, the GYUPOP Wagyu Beef Fair at Perennial Business City in Jurong (30 October – 8 November, 11am–9pm daily) has Japanese A5 Wagyu from $30 per 200g, $5 wagyu skewers, $9.90 shabu beef and A5 wagyu patties at $15. The $5 skewer is the sensible entry point — you can sample real A5 without committing to a $30 block. Prices are as listed by the source; bring cash/card flexibility for fair-style stalls.

Starbucks Christmas menu lands 28 Oct

The seasonal handover happens mid-week: Starbucks Singapore brings back its Christmas favourites alongside two new cold foam creations from 28 October 2026. No Halloween costumes required — just the psychological acceptance that the year-end festive season now starts before Halloween is even over. Watch for launch-week offers, which Starbucks typically runs (nothing confirmed in the source yet).

3. Ongoing Through November — No Rush, But Don't Forget

Châteraisé Halloween collection (from 21 Oct, end date not announced)

The Japanese patisserie's spooky-season lineup arrived 21 October and includes the Halloween Magical Cat Cake, Black Cat Cake, Cute Monster Cake, Halloween Party Assorted Whole Cake and seasonal Langue de Chat sandwiches. The source does not state an end date — seasonal bakery collections typically run until stock clears around 31 Oct, so do not assume these survive into November.

Baskin-Robbins Japan Summer Series (limited time)

Three Japan-exclusive flavours — Popping Shower, Matcha and Musk Melon — are available at participating outlets in a Japan Triple Fun Scoop at S$8.50 or a Japan Trio Waffle at S$13.90. Halloween-appropriate in spirit (the Popping Shower literally pops), though note the mismatch of a 'summer' series arriving in late October. Limited-time run; end date not stated in the source.

7-Eleven icy treats + $1 deals (till 3 Nov)

Until 3 November, 7-Eleven is running icy treats at up to 42% off — including a 4-for-$9 Cornetto Royale deal and Häagen-Dazs ice cream sandwiches at $4 — alongside its $1 deals range covering snacks like M&M's and Nutella B-Ready. Participating stores, while stocks last. This pairs neatly with the McDonald's $2 sundae as evidence that Halloween week is the cheapest week of the year for sugar.

Genki Sushi Shinchan promo (till 8 Nov)

Spend $50 nett at Genki Sushi before 8 November and you get a free Gashapon spin for a chance at Shinchan collectibles — phone straps, cup holders, cable tidies. The $50 threshold is per the source's stated spend requirement; whether it is per receipt or per table is not specified, so ask before ordering if you are dining in a group and want the spin.

Two quieter long-runners

  • Délifrance Better Breakfast Challenge (till 15 Nov): add $1 to an eligible All-Day Breakfast for a milk-based drink made with CP-Meiji milk, collect stamps toward freebies, and enter for a chance to win return economy flights for two.
  • McDonald's Happy Meal toys (15 Oct – 11 Nov): eight transforming robots and dinosaurs released weekly — the Happy Meal Box Tyrannosaurus and McNuggets Robot among them. Combine with the $2 sundae for the ultimate budget kid (or kid-at-heart) Halloween.

6. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Monday, 26 Oct (today): Cat & the Fiddle early access opens — check if you qualify. Boost Juice's $1 second drink is (probably) live at Bugis Junction today; call first.
  • Tuesday–Wednesday, 27–28 Oct: Cat & the Fiddle 1-for-1 officially starts — go early for flavour choice. Supermarket ice cream windows (FairPrice half-price, Cold Storage 3-for-$25) close Wednesday night. Starbucks Christmas menu lands Wednesday 28 Oct if you want first-pour bragging rights.
  • Thursday–Friday, 29–30 Oct: Last call for Cat & the Fiddle (29 Oct), McDonald's $2 sundae and Pizza Hut's Trick or Treat Feast (both 30 Oct). Friday 30 Oct is also Yakiniku-GO day — $7.90++ set at opening or after 1.30pm. GYUPOP Wagyu Fair opens Friday in Jurong.
  • Halloween weekend, 31 Oct – 1 Nov: Wagyu skewers at the GYUPOP fair, Châteraisé spooky cakes (while stock lasts), and Hoshino's half-price pancakes close out the week on Sunday. Stock up on 7-Eleven $1 snacks for trick-or-treaters before 3 Nov.

7. On the Radar: Tiong Bahru Bakery's 28cm XXL Croissant (6 Nov – 31 Dec)

Not this week, but worth planning for: from 6 November to 31 December 2026, Tiong Bahru Bakery is selling a 28cm XXL Grand Croissant with three sweet and savoury dips at $29.90 before GST. Pre-order is required (collection timings and outlet details on their channels). A sharing-size $29.90 pastry is a commitment — go halves with friends and turn it into a post-Halloween brunch event.

Bottom line: the next five days are dense with expiring deals — cheesecake first, ice cream second, sundae and pizza by Friday, yakiniku as the weekend finale. Everything after that runs calm through mid-November. Happy hunting, and may your Halloween be more treat than trick.
