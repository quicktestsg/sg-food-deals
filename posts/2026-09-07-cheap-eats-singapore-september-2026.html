Cheap Eats Singapore September 2026: Breakfast & Supper Deals From $0.99 (Sep 7–13)

Monday, September 7 is the single densest cheap-food day of the month: Subway is giving away a free breakfast wrap before 11am, KFC's Curry Twister meal drops to $5.50 for the week, IKEA starts kids-eat-free, Popeyes launches supper sets from $8.90 after 10pm, and the 9.9 flash window opens. Add free McDonald's mini cones for the school holidays and a 1-for-1 at Luckin's newest outlet, and this is the week to eat cheap from 7am to midnight.

Most deal roundups list promotions. This one is organised the way you actually eat: breakfast first, then the daytime anchors, then supper — because the deals landing this week conveniently split that way too. Everything below is checked against the source announcements: dates, prices, and the catches (member cards, app requirements, time windows, participating outlets). Where a price was not published, it says so — no guessing. The week's structure is simple: one big one-day breakfast freebie today, four deals that start today and run all week, a three-day 9.9 flash, and a school-holiday freebie that stretches to Sunday.

1. Today Only: Subway Buy-1-Get-1 Breakfast Wraps (Mon, Sep 7, Before 11am)

The single best breakfast deal of the week is a one-day-only flash: on Monday, September 7, 2026, buy any Breakfast Wrap with a coffee at Subway and get a second wrap free — but only before 11am, at participating outlets, while stocks last. The timing works out neatly with Subway's new Breakfast Wraps menu, which launched September 4: five varieties from $3.90 with chicken, egg, cheese, mushrooms and avocado, served daily from opening until 11am, with optional add-ons of coffee or Waffle Cut Fries. Two caveats from the announcement: 'participating outlets' means exactly that (check your nearest branch is in), and if you pick two wraps at different prices there is a stated rule for which one you pay for — confirm the mechanics at the counter before ordering two premium wraps. One day, one morning: miss the 11am cutoff and it is gone.

Also new for breakfast: Coffee Bean's Mini Croissants set (from Sep 7)

Also landing today, September 7, 2026: The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf's new Mini Croissants & Scrambled Eggs Breakfast Set — two mini croissants, scrambled eggs, salad, juice and an Americano in one set. It is a proper full-breakfast lineup rather than a pastry-and-coffee pair. The set price was not published in the announcement, so treat it as 'check the menu board' rather than assuming a deal price — new-set launches sometimes come with opening-week pricing, sometimes don't. Worth a look if you're already at a mall with a Coffee Bean this week; not worth a dedicated trip until the price confirms.

2. The Week's Anchor: KFC Curry Twister Meal at $5.50 (Sep 7–13)

KFC's Curry Twister Value Meal runs September 7–13, 2026 at $5.50, down from the usual $6.80 — a stated, source-confirmed discount of $1.30, which is the kind of honest number deal writers like because it needs no estimating. A full value meal under $6 at a major fried-chicken chain is the dependable anchor of a cheap-eats week: no app, no member card, no time window mentioned in the announcement — walk in any day this week and the price applies. Pair it with the timing of the school holidays and expect lunch crowds; the value-meal queue at 12:30pm will move slower than 10:30am. This one ends Sunday, September 13.

3. For Families: IKEA Kids Eat Free (Sep 7–11, Members Only)

From September 7 to 11, 2026, IKEA Singapore is running kids-eat-free: a complimentary kids' meal with every eligible adult main course purchased. The catch is membership — this is for IKEA Family members (free to sign up, but you need to do it before you order, and the meal is tied to an eligible adult main, not any purchase). School-holiday weekdays are exactly when IKEA's meatball queues get long, so the deal and the crowd arrive together; go early or go late-lunch. For a family with two kids, one adult main unlocking kids' meals is one of the better school-holiday economics available this week — and it runs only Monday to Friday, ending September 11.

The other school-holiday freebie: McDonald's Mini Cones (till Sep 13)

McDonald's is giving away free Mini Cones for kids from now until September 13, 2026 — no purchase required, available after breakfast hours. Of everything in this post, this is the one with zero catch beyond the age framing and the timing window: no app, no coupon, no minimum spend. After-breaknoon hours means roughly from late morning onwards; walk up, ask, done. If you are doing a cheap-eats circuit with children this week, this is the dessert course that costs nothing. Ends Sunday, September 13.

4. The 9.9 Flash: Popcorn Chicken + $0.10 Matcha (Sep 7–9)

Shake Shake In A Tub's 9.9 promotion runs just three days, September 7–9, 2026: a $9.80 Popcorn Chicken Combo with the option to top up $0.10 for a Ceremonial Matcha Latte. That ten-cent matcha is the hook — at any normal price a ceremonial-grade matcha latte in this city costs comfortably more than the entire combo. Two locations only: Tampines OTH and 111 Somerset, so this is an East-or-Central play. The combo itself at $9.80 is a full snack-meal; adding the matcha makes it a sub-$10 lunch with a drink that should cost multiples of a dime. Wednesday, September 9 is the last day — the 9.9 window closes and the price of that matcha goes back to reality.

5. Supper Shift: Popeyes Late Night Crunch (From Sep 7, After 10pm)

Popeyes Singapore launches its Late Night Crunch deals on Monday, September 7, 2026, available daily from 10pm. Three supper sets start from $8.90, with the headline set at that price: one Hainanese Fragrant Rice Bowl plus a regular drink — a nod to the Hainanese chicken rice heritage underneath all that Cajun frying. The end date was not announced, so treat it as ongoing until Popeyes says otherwise (these late-night menus usually run for a season, but 'not announced' means exactly that). The strategic note: 10pm-late suppers at fried-chicken prices under $9 are how night owls survive; this fills the post-10pm gap that most breakfast-and-lunch promotions ignore. Pair with the last MRT home, not a Grab — the point of an $8.90 supper evaporates in an $18 ride.

7. Strategy: The Week, Day by Day

  • Monday, Sep 7 (today): Subway BOGO breakfast before 11am — the day's non-negotiable. KFC $5.50 meal and Popeyes supper (from 10pm) both start today; Shake Shake's 9.9 combo opens; IKEA kids-eat-free begins (Family members); Coffee Bean's new set lands.
  • Tuesday, Sep 8: Beard Papa's Day final day (3 puffs $8.80 + vouchers). Last full weekday without mid-week waffles.
  • Wednesday, Sep 9: Double play — Shake Shake's last day ($0.10 matcha!) and A&W Waffle Wednesday at Jurong Point ($8 waffle ice cream / $9.20 with root beer). East-siders take the first, West-siders the second.
  • Thursday, Sep 11: IKEA kids-eat-free final day — the last weekday of the family play.
  • Friday, Sep 11–12: Popeyes after 10pm is the end-of-week supper; KFC $5.50 still running for a cheap lunch.
  • Sunday, Sep 13: Everything-week closes: KFC meal ends, McDonald's free Mini Cones end. Grab a cone on the way home — it's free.

8. What to Skip

Skip the Coffee Bean set if the menu-board price disappoints — an unpublished price is not a deal until proven otherwise, and there are enough confirmed prices this week that you don't need to gamble on breakfast. Skip crossing the island for the $0.10 matcha if you're neither East (Tampines) nor Central (Somerset): two MRT transfers to save a few dollars on a latte is a net loss once you count fare and time. And skip Beard Papa's if you won't realistically use the vouchers — the $8.80 price only beats the usual range meaningfully when the follow-up 1-for-1 gets redeemed; three puffs eaten once is just dessert, not a strategy.

Bottom line: this is a from-dawn-to-midnight cheap-eating week. Subway's wrap giveaway is today and today only; KFC holds the $5.50 anchor to Sunday; families get IKEA weekdays and free cones all week; the 9.9 flash burns Wednesday-out; and Popeyes owns everything after 10pm. Set the alarm for breakfast.
