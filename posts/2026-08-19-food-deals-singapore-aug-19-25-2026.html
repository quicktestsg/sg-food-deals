Singapore Food Deals This Week (Aug 19–25, 2026): Best Cheap Eats From 10¢ to $9.90

Wednesday is doing the most this week: 7-Eleven's 10¢ vouchers drop at 8am, McDonald's 1-for-1 runs its final day, Rive Gauche launches a $33.30 anniversary cake, and KFC adds a $5.50 weekday set. Here's everything, sorted by how fast you need to move.

Not every week has a clear 'act now' day — this one does. August 19 stacks four time-sensitive promos on top of each other, and a few more expire by Sunday. This guide is organised by deadline, not by cuisine, because that's the question that actually matters: what needs to happen today, what can wait until the weekend, and what's worth diary-ing for next week. Every price and date below comes from the merchants' own promo announcements, and every catch is spelled out.

1. Today Only: Four Deals That Expire on Aug 19

7-Eleven 10¢ vouchers — claim from 8am

The headline deal of the week. From 8am on August 19 until August 25, the 7-Eleven app releases a set of 10¢ vouchers — each item costs literally ten cents. Open the app at 8am sharp, claim first, redeem later — the vouchers stay valid through August 25.

McDonald's Happy Festival has been running 1-for-1 deals on GrabFood since August 13, and today is the last day. Dinner tonight practically plans itself: order one, get one, pay nothing for delivery. GrabFood orders only — counter orders aren't part of this one.

Cold Storage's sweet promo (August 15–19) wraps up today: two The Ice Cream & Cookie Co. ice cream cookie sandwiches for $22.45. The supermarket is also stocking The Fine Cheese Co. SUPERPARK 8.8 flash deal — last day for $88 party credits

Not food, but if a birthday is looming: SUPERPARK's 8.8 flash deal (August 5–19) ends today. Code SPS88FLASH gets $88 Party Credit with the Super Party package, or $188 credit with Super Unlimited. Burning energy works up an appetite — pair it with any of the dinners below.

2. Starting Today: Rive Gauche $33.30 Cake and KFC's $5.50 Set

KFC's $5.50 Real Deal — Curry Macaroni Bowl + Coke Zero (from Aug 19)

Starting today, KFC adds the Curry Macaroni Bowl Set to its $5.50 Real Deal line-up: the bowl plus a regular Coke Zero, available on weekdays after 11am.
