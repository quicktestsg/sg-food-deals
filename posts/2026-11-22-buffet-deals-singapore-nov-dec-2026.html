Best Buffet Deals Singapore (Nov–Dec 2026): $19.90 Mookata, 1-for-1 Lunch Buffets & Kids Eat Free

Buffet season is here. Seven verified deals under S$30 per person — free-flow mookata with crayfish, a 1-for-1 lunch buffet at Stevens Road, kids-eat-free dim sum, and senior rates at Hillion Mall. Every price, date and catch checked against the source.

December in Singapore means two things: school holidays and buffet season. Restaurants know it too — this week's deal feeds are stacked with all-you-can-eat promotions, from a S$19.90 Thai mookata spread with Australian crayfish to a hotel lunch buffet that works out to S$24++ per person when you bring a friend. This guide rounds up every buffet deal currently running that we could verify, with the exact prices, dates and fine print from the original sources.

1. 131 Mookata — S$19.90 Free-Flow Buffet With Crayfish (Best Value Overall)

The headline deal of the season: from 18 November 2026 onwards, 131 Mookata is running a S$19.90 free-flow mookata buffet at its Paya Lebar and Kelantan Road outlets. The spread covers more than 50 buffet selections — Australian crayfish, garlic butter prawns, scallops, squid, marinated meats and hotpot ingredients. At under S$20 for seafood-on-grill, this is the cheapest verified buffet on this list.

Dates: from 18 November 2026 onwards (end date not announced — treat as ongoing but reconfirm before making a trip). Locations: Paya Lebar and Kelantan Road only.

2. Commune @ Metropolitan YMCA — 1-for-1 Lunch Buffet at S$24++ Per Person

Running now until 31 December 2026, this 1-for-1 lunch buffet at Commune @ Metropolitan YMCA (Metro Y Hotel, Stevens Road) works out to S$48++ for two adults — S$24++ per person. The menu leans local and Asian: claypot chicken rice, curry fish head, Teochew porridge, Nyonya kueh pie tee and fruit rojak. The catch is the tight window: lunch runs 12pm to 2.30pm only, so it is a weekday-treat or early-weekend-lunch play, not a dinner option.

3. Yum Cha Changi — Kids Eat Free Dinner Buffet (School Holiday Special)

The school-holiday winner: from 20 November to 31 December 2026, children aged 5 to 12 eat free at Yum Cha Changi's dinner buffet, with every paying adult. Dim sum dinners with the kids during the holidays can easily spiral past S$100 for a family of four; one free child per adult meaningfully cuts that. Note the age band — under-5s and teens are not covered by this promotion as published.

4. Big Fish Small Fish — S$24.90++ Senior Buffet, All Day at Hillion Mall

From 17 November 2026 onwards, seniors aged 60 and above pay S$24.90++ for the buffet at Big Fish Small Fish (Hillion Mall), down from the usual adult price of S$29.90++ — a saving of S$5 per head, and it applies all day rather than being locked to off-peak hours. End date not announced.

5. IKEA Christmas Buffet — S$29.90, Returning in December

IKEA Singapore's Christmas buffet is back for S$29.90 this December. The source confirms the price and the December return but does not publish exact session dates at the time of writing — check IKEA Singapore's site or app for booking slots before planning around it. Based on the price point alone it slots in as the mid-tier option between the mookata and the hotel lunch.

⚠️ Price Unconfirmed — Session Dates

The S$29.90 IKEA Christmas buffet price is confirmed by the source, but session dates and booking details were not yet published when this guide was written. Verify on IKEA Singapore's official channels before travelling.

6. Haidilao 14th Anniversary — S$14++ Hotpot Deals (Ends 30 Nov, Not a Buffet)

Not strictly a buffet, but hotpot's biggest name is running anniversary pricing through 1–30 November 2026: S$14++ buy-one-get-one-free selected meat platters, S$14++ seafood specials, and a S$29.90 recharge set at Sun Plaza. Spend S$140 nett in a visit and you enter a lucky draw for an Apple iMac, iPhones, AirPods Max 2 and Dyson prizes. With eight days left on the calendar as of this writing, this is the most time-sensitive deal on the list.

7. G.Spa — 24-Hour Spa Access With Buffet Included (Till 30 Dec)

For a different kind of feast: from now until 30 December 2026, G.Spa's bundle pairs 24-hour spa access with buffet dining, plus 12 months of UPlay Premium membership with the first 3 months free (thereafter membership rates from S$2/month for NTUC members or S$3/month for the public). The exact bundle price varies by package — treat the buffet here as a bonus on a spa day rather than the reason to go.

⚠️ Condition Check — Read Before You Book
  • ++ pricing: Commune (S$24++), Big Fish Small Fish (S$24.90++) and Haidilao (S$14++) prices exclude service charge and GST — the final bill lands roughly 20% higher.
  • Age bands: Yum Cha's free buffet covers kids 5–12 only; Big Fish Small Fish's senior rate covers 60+. Bring IC — age-verified promos will check.
  • Time windows: Commune's 1-for-1 is lunch-only (12pm–2.30pm); Yum Cha's free kids buffet is dinner-only. Haidilao's anniversary deals end 30 Nov 2026.
  • Ongoing deals: 131 Mookata and Big Fish Small Fish have no announced end dates — ongoing as published, but call ahead for holiday-period availability.

Strategy: How to Stack These Deals Across the Season

  • This week (by 30 Nov): Hit Haidilao's anniversary pricing before it lapses — the S$14++ meat BOGO plus seafood specials is the strongest hard-deadline deal. Pair a S$140 nett spend with the lucky draw if you were going anyway.
  • Weekday lunches through December: Commune's 1-for-1 (S$24++/pax, 12pm–2.30pm) is the play for work-from-office days near Stevens Road.
  • School holiday dinners with kids: Yum Cha Changi's kids-eat-free dinner buffet (one child 5–12 per adult, till 31 Dec) turns a family dim sum dinner into a two-pay-three-eat.
  • Feast days with friends: 131 Mookata's S$19.90 free-flow with crayfish is the value pick for a group grill session — two outlets, Paya Lebar and Kelantan Road.
  • Grandparents' day out: Big Fish Small Fish's all-day S$24.90++ senior rate at Hillion Mall, no off-peak restriction.

What to Skip or Double-Check

Two honest caveats. First, IKEA's S$29.90 buffet still has no published session dates — do not plan a trip around it until slots appear. Second, both 'ongoing' deals (131 Mookata, Big Fish Small Fish) could be withdrawn without notice since no end date was announced; a two-minute phone call beats a wasted trip. And as always with ++ pricing, the advertised number is not the number on your bill.

💡 Bottom Line

Best pure value: 131 Mookata at S$19.90 with crayfish. Best family play: Yum Cha kids-eat-free (till 31 Dec). Best deadline pressure: Haidilao anniversary (ends 30 Nov). Best workday lunch: Commune 1-for-1 at S$24++. Whatever you pick, book early — December buffet slots disappear fast once school holidays start.
