Best 1-for-1 & BOGO Food Deals in Singapore This Week

Buy-one-get-one deals are the gold standard of food savings — effectively halving your bill if you're dining with someone. This week, Singapore's deal scene is stacked with BOGO offers.

There's a simple reason 1-for-1 deals beat percentage discounts: the savings are transparent and immediate. When a burger goes buy-one-get-one-free, you know exactly what you're getting — two items for the price of one, no math required. This week happens to be one of the densest BOGO windows in recent memory.

💡 Pro Tip: The BOGO Split

The smartest way to use any Buy 2 Get 1 Free is to go with a friend and split the cost evenly. Two people each pay for one drink, the third is free. This is why BOGO deals have the highest real-world savings rate of any promotion type.
