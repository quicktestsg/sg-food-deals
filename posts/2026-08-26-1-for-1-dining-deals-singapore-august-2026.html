Best 1-for-1 Dining Deals Singapore: $18.90 Korean Chicken, Thai Buffet, Steak & Waffles (Aug 26–Sep 30, 2026)

Payday week is peak 1-for-1 season in Singapore, and this stretch has some genuinely big ones: a Korean fried chicken buy-1-get-1 that works out to $9.45 a head, a 1-for-1 unlimited Thai buffet on weekday dinners, a 1-for-1 Angus ribeye that ends Sunday, and rotating waffle deals that run till mid-September. Here is every one worth splitting, sorted by how soon it dies.

A 1-for-1 deal is only as good as its fine print. Half of them are 1-for-1 on the cheapest item, dine-in only at one outlet, or quietly limited to a drink nobody orders. This guide filters all of that out: every deal below has a verified price, a real end date (or an explicit note that none was announced), and its catches stated up front. Three of them close within five days — so the order you visit them in matters.

1. BHC Chicken: Buy 1 Get 1 FREE Korean Fried Chicken, $18.90 (Till Sep 24)

The headline deal of the month. From 24 August to 24 September 2026, order 1 Retro Boneless and get 1 Hot Retro Boneless FREE, plus a Coca-Cola or Coca-Cola Zero, for $18.90 — against a stated usual price of $41.80 for the combination. Split between two people, that is $9.45 each for two chicken flavours and a drink, which is food-court money for proper Korean fried chicken. The catches are firm: dine-in only (no takeaway), and only at BHC Chicken Marina Square and BHC Chicken at Raffles Hospital in Bugis. A separate follow-up promo runs 5–13 September: Japchae with Macho King Riceball for $21, also dine-in.

2. Ending Sunday: Morganfield’s 1-for-1 Angus Ribeye, $42.90++ for Two

Morganfield’s Suntec City is running 2 Angus Ribeye Steaks with French fries and garden salad for $42.90++, from 21 to 30 August 2026 — about $21.45++ per person before the ++ adds service charge and GST. Multiple sets can be ordered if the table is hungrier than two steaks. Both SINGPromos and EverydayOnSales carry the same price and window, so the numbers are consistent across sources. The urgency is real: Sunday 31 August is a hard stop, and it is Suntec City only — no other outlet is running it.

3. Also Ending Soon: Luckin 1-for-1 Coffee & ChaPanda BOGO

Two drink deals with short fuses. Luckin Coffee’s Payday Best Day 1-for-1 runs 24–30 August islandwide on selected coffees, including the Pandan Coconut Latte and Coconut Latte. Separately, its new KK Women’s and Children’s Hospital outlet is celebrating its grand opening with 1-for-1 drinks from 20 August to 2 September, plus a $0.99 first-drink offer — if you are anywhere near Bukit Timah, the outlet-specific promo outlasts the islandwide one. And ChaPanda’s Buy 1 Get 1 FREE on L-size Oolong Milk Tea with Soybean Cheese Foam ends tomorrow, 27 August — only at SCAPE, Century Square and Clementi.

⏰ The 48-Hour Priority List

If you only read one box: ChaPanda BOGO dies tomorrow (27 Aug), Morganfield’s steak and the islandwide Luckin 1-for-1 both die Sunday (30 Aug). Book Morganfield’s for Friday or Saturday night now — the last weekend of a steak promo is exactly when Suntec runs out of tables. Everything else in this guide has two or more weeks left.

4. Central Thai: 1-for-1 Unlimited Buffet, $44.90++ Weekday Dinners (Till Sep 30)

For pure volume, nothing this month beats Central Thai at Changi Airport Terminal 3: from 17 August to 30 September 2026, its Unlimited Thai Buffet is 1-for-1 at $44.90++ for weekday dinners — two diners, one buffet bill, roughly $22.45++ per person for unlimited Thai favourites. The gates: weekday dinners only, and it is the Changi Airport T3 outlet specifically. Families should note the companion deal running 13 July to 31 August: kids aged 7–12 eat the unlimited Thai buffet for $9.90 each with every paying adult — that one has less than a week left.

5. Geláre Westgate: Rotating 1-for-1 Deals, Waffles at $9.90 (Till Sep 13)

Geláre’s Westgate outlet is running a series of rotating 1-for-1 promotions from 17 August to 13 September 2026, starting with Classic Waffles at $9.90 and cycling through ice cream, coffee, mocktails and croffles in the weeks that follow. Rather than one static deal, think of it as a new 1-for-1 every week or so at a single outlet — worth a look if you are already at Jurong East, not worth a cross-island trip for the waffle alone. Westgate only; check Geláre’s official channels for which item is on 1-for-1 during the week you visit.

6. TANYU: 1-for-1 Beer & 20% Off Skewers After 8PM, All Outlets

TANYU’s grilled fish deals run all day in two layers. Weekday lunches (Monday to Friday, Tampines 1 and Westgate only): a solo Pax Lunch Set at $15.90 for one grilled fish fillet with rice, with a $2.90 top-up adding a drink and cold dish; or a two-person Pax Lunch Set at $49.90 (usual price stated as $61.80). Then every evening, across all four outlets — 313@somerset, Bugis Junction, Tampines 1 and Westgate — Happy Hour kicks in after 8pm: 20% off skewers and 1-for-1 beer, Monday to Sunday. The source does not state an end date for any of these, so treat them as ongoing until announced otherwise.

7. Swensen’s Geneo: 20% Off Unlimited Sundae Club for Two (Till Sep 30)

Not technically 1-for-1, but it is built on the same bring-a-friend mechanic. At Swensen’s Unlimited @ Geneo (Furama RiverFront side of the brand’s new concept), from 19 August to 30 September 2026 you get 20% off the Unlimited Sundae Club when you bring a friend, order a minimum of two bowls, and show the promotional post from Swensen’s Singapore Instagram. The showing-the-post requirement is real — save it before you leave home, because the staff do check.

8. The Steady Eddies: Dunkin’ Payday & Tsui Wah Egg Tarts

Two more with no announced end dates, both running since mid-August. Dunkin’s Payday Deals: buy 5 donuts and the 2 lowest-priced ones in your batch are free, plus 1-for-1 on Cold Brew and hot or iced Teh Tarik; selected outlets only, so confirm your nearest branch participates. Tsui Wah is running Buy 3 Get 1 Free on its Hong Kong-style egg tarts at six participating outlets, from 19 August onwards — simple maths, four tarts for the price of three, and egg tarts are exactly the thing worth a 25% subsidy for. Both are “while the campaign lasts” deals: no end date announced as at publication.

How to Stack This Week

  • Tonight (Wed, Aug 26): TANYU Happy Hour after 8pm — 1-for-1 beer and 20% off skewers at any of its four outlets, no booking gymnastics required. ChaPanda BOGO on the way home if you pass SCAPE, Century Square or Clementi — tomorrow is the last day.
  • Thursday or Friday (Aug 27–28): Central Thai’s 1-for-1 unlimited buffet is a weekday-dinner deal — $44.90++ for two at Changi Airport T3. Go this week if you have kids aged 7–12: their $9.90 buffet ends 31 August.
  • Friday or Saturday (Aug 28–29): Morganfield’s 1-for-1 Angus ribeye at Suntec before it dies Sunday. Book ahead — final weekend of a steak promo is peak queue.
  • Sunday (Aug 30): Last day of Luckin’s islandwide 1-for-1 — grab the free second Pandan Coconut Latte while the payday banner is still live. KK Hospital’s outlet version keeps running to 2 September.
  • Next week and beyond: BHC Chicken BOGO ($18.90, till 24 Sep), Geláre Westgate rotations (till 13 Sep), Swensen’s Geneo sundae 20% off (till 30 Sep) — no rush, but note BHC’s $21 Japchae window is only 5–13 September if you want both.

The Verdict

The single best value here is BHC Chicken: $18.90 against a stated usual price of $41.80, two flavours plus a drink, and a full month to use it — subject to the two-outlet, dine-in catch. The most urgent is Morganfield’s ribeye, which folds Sunday. And the most underrated is Central Thai’s weekday-dinner buffet 1-for-1, because all-you-can-eat multiplied by half price is the deepest discount structure on this list. Do those three and the drinks and desserts are bonus rounds. As always: prices are as stated by the sources at publication, ++ means plus-plus, and deals with no announced end date can disappear without notice — move on those first.
