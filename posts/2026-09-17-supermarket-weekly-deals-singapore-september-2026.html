Supermarket Weekly Deals Singapore (Sep 17–23, 2026): FairPrice 55% Off vs Giant 63% Off vs Cold Storage

All three major supermarket chains launched new weekly promotions on the same day — 17 September 2026. Giant claims the biggest headline discount (up to 63% off), FairPrice counters with 55% off plus a boosted CHAS discount, and Cold Storage fights on freshness. Here is the honest breakdown of which chain wins for what, and the one stacking move most shoppers miss.

Every Thursday-ish, Singapore's supermarkets flip their promo calendars in near-unison, and this week is a textbook example: from 17 to 23 September 2026, FairPrice, Giant and Cold Storage all have fresh weekly specials running simultaneously. The trap is treating them as interchangeable. The headline percentages measure different things (a '63% off' sticker on one clearance item is not the same as 55% off across a wider range), availability varies store to store, and the CHAS discount — the quiet workhorse of grocery savings — stacks differently at each chain. This guide breaks down what each promo actually covers, what the catches are, and how to plan one efficient run instead of three.

1. Giant — Up to 63% Off, 7-Day & 14-Day Sale (17–23 Sep)

Giant's new weekly cycle starts 17 September 2026, with its 7-day and 14-day sale offering discounts of up to 63% off. That is the biggest headline number of the three chains this week, and Giant's promo pages are typically where you find the deepest single-item cuts — clearance-style pricing on specific SKUs rather than a broad thin discount. Two important conditions from the official announcement: availability differs from store to store, and everything is while stocks last. The 63% figure is a ceiling, not an average — most items will sit well below it, so treat the flyer as a treasure hunt list rather than a blanket sale.

The standout named deal in Giant's own headline: Häagen-Dazs ice cream tubs at any 2 for $19.80, running till 23 September 2026. If premium ice cream is on your list at all this week, this is the deal to anchor a Giant trip around — the same trip can absorb your household staples from the 63%-off racks.

💡 Giant Stacking Strategy

Giant has extended its 6% CHAS discount until 25 September 2026 — that is two days beyond the weekly sale's end on 23 Sep, but no reason to wait. The play: build one basket from the 7-day/14-day sale items, add the Häagen-Dazs 2-for-$19.80, and pay with a registered CHAS card for the 6% off. One trip, three layers of savings. Check whether the 6% applies on top of promotional prices or only regular prices at the counter — when in doubt, watch the screen as each item scans.

2. FairPrice — Up to 55% Off Weekly Specials + Boosted CHAS Discount (17–23 Sep)

FairPrice's latest weekly promotion runs 17 to 23 September 2026, with savings of up to 55% off spanning fresh produce, frozen and chilled food, pantry staples, beverages, and household products. That is the widest category spread of the three chains this week — where Giant goes deep on specific items, FairPrice goes broad, which matters if you are doing a full weekly shop rather than cherry-picking. Beyond the sticker discounts, FairPrice is boosting its CHAS discount to 6% for a limited period from now (the announcement's exact end date was not fully stated in the source — verify the current rate in-store or in the FairPrice Group app before relying on it).

The strategic difference: FairPrice's promotion pages give you the best chance of covering an entire grocery list — proteins, vegetables, drinks and detergent — inside one discounted trip. For CHAS Blue holders in particular, a 6% discount layered across a broad 55%-off catalogue is arguably the strongest total-basket play this week, even though Giant's headline number is bigger.

3. Cold Storage & CS Fresh — Freshness Play (17–23 Sep)

Cold Storage and CS Fresh launch their latest Fresh and Grocery Selections from 17 to 23 September 2026, with savings across fresh produce, meat, seafood, deli items, groceries and household essentials. No single headline percentage this time — and that is consistent with how Cold Storage positions itself: fewer dramatic stickers, better baseline quality on the fresh counters. If this week's menu is built around proteins and produce (a dinner-party protein cut, specific seafood, deli cheeses), CS Fresh is where the discount lands on items you actually want at full quality, rather than on whatever was overstocked.

⚠️ Condition Check

Every promo this week is while-stocks-last, and Giant explicitly warns availability differs store to store — the item you saw in the flyer may not be at your nearest branch. Both the FairPrice CHAS boost (exact end date unstated) and store-level stock are moving targets: verify in the FairPrice Group app / Giant app before building your list. Weekly specials generally require a membership or app account to see the full discounted price at some chains — when a price looks wrong at the scanner, ask whether the promo price needs the app before paying.

Strategy: One Run, Not Three

Running all three supermarkets in one week is a false economy — the extra travel eats the savings. The smarter play is to pick your chain by what your household actually consumes this week:

  • Stock-up households: Go Giant. The 7-day/14-day sale plus the Häagen-Dazs 2-for-$19.80 (till 23 Sep) plus 6% CHAS (till 25 Sep) is the deepest stack this week. Shop early in the window — while-stocks-last means the best cuts are gone by Sunday.
  • Full weekly-shop households: Go FairPrice. The 55%-off range spans every aisle category, and the 6% CHAS boost (limited period, end date not stated) applies across the basket — widest coverage for one trip.
  • Cook-to-impress weeks: Go Cold Storage / CS Fresh. When the menu is a specific protein or seafood, the Fresh Selections discount on exactly what you need beats a bigger number on things you don't.
  • Two-store maximum: If you genuinely need both a stock-up and a fresh run, pair Giant + Cold Storage only if they share a mall (several do) — otherwise the second trip is usually not worth the fare.

What to Skip This Week

Two things the deal aggregators are pushing alongside these grocery promos that are not grocery plays at all: the Maybank SGD Time Deposit at up to 1.75% p.a. (from 17 September 2026, requires S$20,000 across 6-, 9- or 12-month tenors) is a banking product, not a deal for your shopping basket. And the NLB Big Book Giveaway on 19–20 September 2026 is a genuinely free and lovely event — pre-loved library books at no cost — but it is about books, not groceries. Both are worth knowing about; neither belongs in a supermarket run.

Deal facts above are sourced from the chains' official weekly promotion announcements (17 September 2026 cycle) and are accurate as of publication. Discounts are while-stocks-last and vary by store — verify prices and the current CHAS rate in-app or in-store before checkout.
