Free Food & Freebies in Singapore (Aug 23–31, 2026): Free Chicken Rice, Ya Kun Pies, Bakkwa & More

The cheapest meal in Singapore this week costs exactly $0. Ten verifiable giveaways are running between August 23 and 31 — plus free Din Tai Fung wings from September 1 — and this guide puts them in date order so nothing expires before you get to it. Every limit, address and catch comes from the merchants' own announcements.

Free food promotions in Singapore tend to come in waves — a grand opening here, a national celebration there — and this particular week is unusually rich. A 40-year-old chicken rice institution is giving away its signature dish at its new airport flagship, a kopi chain is handing out 200 chicken pies to celebrate a reopening, a bakkwa giant is feeding CHAS cardholders, and Din Tai Fung is quietly comping chicken wings for most of September. This guide verifies each one, states the exact redemption windows, and flags every condition — because a freebie you queue two hours for and can't redeem is worse than no freebie at all.

2. Ending Today (Sun, Aug 23): 88 Free Chicken Rice Packets at Jewel

Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice is marking two things at once — its 40th anniversary and the opening of its largest-ever flagship at Jewel Changi Airport — and it is celebrating the only way a chicken rice legend should: by giving the food away. On August 22 and 23, the first 88 guests each day get a free takeaway packet of its signature Hainanese chicken rice from 10am, while stocks last. Both the English and Chinese-language announcements agree on the mechanics: 88 packets per day, takeaway only, one outlet. This is the last day, so treat a 9.45am arrival as the price of admission.

Two smaller freebies also expire tonight. The POKKA Greener On Our Side pop-up at Plaza Singapura (August 20–23) hands every participant a sure-win prize — cooler bags, jasmine tea cushions, portable blenders — plus free tea sampling as part of a golden tea leaf hunt. And Kenangan Coffee's Moncha sticker pack (August 21–23) comes free with any Matcha Series drink, but only when ordered through the Kenangan app. If matcha is already on today's agenda, the sticker costs nothing extra; if not, skip it — it is a bonus, not a destination.

3. The Week's Best Pure Freebie: Ya Kun's $6.80 Chicken Pie (Wed, Aug 26)

Strip away everything else and this is the strongest giveaway of the fortnight. To celebrate the grand reopening of its Far East Square outlet, Ya Kun Kaya Toast is giving away 200 free Classic Comfy Chicken Pies on Wednesday, August 26, from 3pm — no purchase required. The pie usually sells for $6.80, which makes this the highest-value no-strings freebie on the board: 200 pies is roughly $1,360 of food walking out the door. The outlet is at 18 China Street, #01-01, Singapore 049560, in the heart of the CBD — a five-minute walk from Raffles Place MRT, which also means office crowds will know about it. A 3pm start conveniently misses lunch, but not the tea break; anyone genuinely wants one should be in the queue by 2.30pm.

💡 Ya Kun Math — Why 200 Pies Will Go Fast

200 pies ÷ a CBD tea-break crowd = well under 30 minutes of supply if the queue moves briskly. The giveaway is strictly while stocks last, one pie per early customer, from 3pm at the Far East Square outlet only — no other Ya Kun branch is participating. If Wednesday 3pm is a meeting slot that cannot move, this deal is not for you; there is no app fallback and no rain check.

4. Weekend Play (Aug 29–30): Free Bakkwa for CHAS Cardholders

Bee Cheng Hiang is giving away free 100g packs of Bakkwa Bites at its Clementi Central outlet on Saturday August 29 and Sunday August 30 — but only to holders of Blue and Orange CHAS cards, the tiers issued to lower- and middle-income households. Redemption runs in two windows each day, 12pm–2pm and 5.30pm–7.30pm, at Blk 449 Clementi Ave 3, #01-255, Singapore 120449, while stocks last. Green CHAS cardholders are not listed as eligible, and no purchase is mentioned in the announcement — this appears to be a straight flash-the-card giveaway, one of the very few in Singapore that requires nothing but the card itself.

5. Marking September: Free Din Tai Fung Wings (Sep 1–24)

For most of September, Din Tai Fung is comping a plate of its Crispy Chicken Wings Marinated in Shrimp Paste — three pieces, worth $7 — at its IMM and Jurong Point outlets, with any bill of $38 or more. The reason is practical: the JEM outlet is undergoing renovation, and this is how the chain keeps West-side regulars close while it sleeps. The smart read: a $38 spend at Din Tai Fung is easy for a table of two and trivially cleared by three, so the wings effectively rebate 18% back on a meal that was happening anyway. Date nights and family dinners in the west just found their September appetiser.

6. Free-With-Receipt: The Four 'Buy Something, Get Something' Plays

Not every freebie is truly free — four of this week's giveaways bolt onto a purchase. They are still worth it when the purchase was already on the list. MARIGOLD's Moo Crew promo (until August 31) gives a free plush keychain for spending $7 on MARIGOLD UHT milk at major supermarkets, selected minimarts and RedMart — $7 is roughly a two-litre plus a one-litre carton, so this is a normal grocery basket, not a stretch. Giant's MILO Sports Day socks (from August 20, ongoing, no end date announced) come free with the $8.95 MILO Van sachets pack, with six random designs to collect — nostalgia bait for parents, but the sachets themselves make actual drinks. Eu Yan Sang's buy-2-get-2-free essence of chicken (from August 20, ongoing) is members-only — EuRewards — but that is a free sign-up at the counter. And PlayMade at One Holland Village (from August 21, ongoing) trades a free $10 Whole Animal Butchery pet treat pack for two drinks plus a pet photo posted on Instagram, capped at the first 100 redemptions — dog owners, this one is aimed squarely at you.

💡 One-Trip Stacking: Giant

A single Giant run can carry two freebies: grab the $8.95 MILO Van sachets pack (free socks) and $7 of MARIGOLD UHT milk (free plush keychain, until Aug 31) in the same basket. Total spend $15.95, two collectibles out the door — and both items are pantry staples that would have been bought eventually anyway. Check the MARIGOLD keychain redemption mechanics at the customer service counter before checkout, as supermarket gift-redemption counters vary by store.

7. Nearly Free: The Under-$5 Support Cast

If the queues defeat you, three near-free deals soften the week. Chateraise's Mix & Match ice cream deal (August 21–31) sells any 8 ice creams for $12 — or $9 if you bring a Chateraise cooler bag, which works out to $1.13 a piece and is about as close to free dessert as retail allows. llaollao's student promo (from August 18, ongoing) prices the Small Tub at $4.90, down from its usual $6.70, every Monday and Tuesday from 2.30pm to 5.30pm — valid student ID or school uniform required, and the Raspberry Cookie Crumble is in the lineup. And Luckin Coffee's new KK Women's and Children's Hospital outlet is running 1-for-1 drinks from August 20 to September 2 — hospital visitors and staff get the most out of this one, and the outlet's app reportedly has a $0.99 first-drink offer for new users.

9. Bottom Line

A disciplined freebie hunter can clear four genuine giveaways in nine days — chicken rice today, a $6.80 pie on Wednesday, bakkwa on the weekend, and wings through September — plus two collectibles folded into grocery trips, all for under $16 of spending that was happening anyway.
