Credit Card & App Food Deals Singapore (31 Oct – Nov 2026): $3 Hawker Cashback, 1-for-1 Starbucks & More

The best restaurant deals this season aren't flyers or coupons — they live in your wallet. Here's how DBS PayLah!, Trust Bank, CapitaStar and FairPrice are paying you to eat, with every date and catch spelled out.

Most people hunt for deals at the restaurant door. Savvier eaters know the biggest discounts in Singapore right now are unlocked at the moment of payment: scan a QR code, tap the right card, or clip a voucher in an app, and the same meal gets quietly cheaper. This guide rounds up every card- and app-linked food deal running from 31 October into November 2026 — organized by how fast you need to move.

1. Today Only (Saturday, 31 October): Two Deals Expire at Midnight

DBS PayLah! $3 Saturday Cashback at Hawkers and Coffeeshops

DBS's recurring Saturday cashback is back on 31 October 2026, and it's the most broadly useful deal on this list. Scan and pay with PayLah! at participating heartland shops, wet markets, coffeeshops and hawker centres islandwide and enjoy up to $3 cashback. The offer starts at 6am and is limited to the first 160,000 redemptions — that sounds like a lot, but popular hawker lunches burn through it fast, so an early breakfast or 11am coffee run is safer than a 1pm peak-hour meal.

Luckin Coffee 1-for-1 Payday Deal — Last Day

Luckin Coffee's payday promotion — 1-for-1 on all coffee ordered through its app — runs until 31 October 2026. If you've been meaning to try it, today is the day: order two, pay for one, and pair it with the PayLah! cashback above if your Luckin outlet accepts scan-and-pay. The deal is app-only, so you can't get it walking up to the counter.

2. Ending Tomorrow (Sunday, 1 November): Starbucks 1-for-1 with Trust Bank

Trust Bank cardholders get a free second Tall-sized handcrafted drink with every purchase at Starbucks in Singapore, until 1 November 2026. It's the classic coffee promo structure: buy one Tall drink, get one free — ideal for a colleague run or a two-person study session. The catch is size (Tall only, the smallest handcrafted size) and timing: the offer ends Sunday. Trust Bank is Singtel's digital bank, so if you have a Singtel line the card application is famously quick — but realistically, apply another day and use this promo next time it cycles around rather than rushing a bank application for a coffee.

3. Running Through 9 November: Grocery Vouchers and Deepavali Rewards

FairPrice $5 Return Voucher for Every $50 Spend

From 29 October to 9 November 2026, every $50 nett spend at FairPrice, FairPrice Finest, FairPrice Xtra and Unity earns a $5 Return Voucher — effectively 10% back if you calibrate your basket to the threshold. Collected vouchers can be redeemed with no minimum spend until 30 November 2026. A family that spends $150 across two weekend shops collects $15 in vouchers, which then covers a free tray of eggs plus change. The trick is not to overspend to chase the threshold: $49 baskets earn nothing, so consolidate receipts into one trip.

CapitaStar Deepavali Festival: Free $5 eCapitaVoucher with Code RANGOLI26

CapitaStar's Happy Deepavali Mini Shopping Festival runs from 30 October to 9 November 2026. Enter promo code RANGOLI26 in the app, link an eligible Mastercard, and receive a free S$5 eCapitaVoucher plus unlock up to S$15 in shopping rewards at participating CapitaLand malls. eCapitaVouchers spend like cash at many mall food courts and eateries, so this is genuinely free food money — the whole process takes about two minutes in the app. Mastercard linking is required, so have your card ready.

4. Longer Runway: November and Beyond

Bank of China UnionPay at Gardens by the Bay — Till 30 November

Planning the tourist loop with visiting relatives? BOC UnionPay cardholders get S$4 off a minimum S$40 spend, or S$8 off S$80 and above, at participating Gardens by the Bay ticketing counters and gift shops, until 30 November 2026. Redemptions are limited, so this pairs best with a weekday visit. Note the deal covers tickets and retail — the food outlets inside the attractions are a separate matter.

Trip.com OCBC Wander Wednesday — Every Wednesday Till 30 December

For the travel-planners: OCBC Visa cardholders get S$60 off flight or hotel bookings of S$600 and above, or S$120 off S$1,200 and above, on Trip.com every Wednesday at 12pm, from now until 30 December 2026. It's not a food deal in the strict sense, but a S$120 discount on the year-end trip funds quite a few destination meals. Set a Wednesday noon reminder if a booking is on your list.

5. The Pattern Worth Knowing: CapitaStar's Weekly Free Food Codes

Both of CapitaStar's recent free-food redemptions — a free 6-piece KFC Chicken Bites box (code CRUNCH, 26 October, Junction 8 and Tampines Mall) and a free McDonald's McChicken (code TREAT, 28 October, Aperia Mall and Funan) — are now fully past. They're worth studying anyway, because they reveal a reliable pattern: CapitaStar drops a no-purchase-needed freebie code almost weekly, usually capped at the first 250 redemptions per outlet and valid for one day only at specific mall locations. The takeaway: open the CapitaStar app on Monday mornings, enter the code early, and redeem early in the window. The next free meal is a when, not an if.

⚠️ Condition Check

Before you head out: the DBS PayLah! cashback needs a DBS/POSB account with PayLah! activated, and only participating stalls count — look for the QR standees. Starbucks needs the physical or mobile Trust Bank card at payment. The CapitaStar voucher needs a Mastercard linked in-app before the code works. The FairPrice voucher triggers on nett spend — after discounts, before it counts. None of these stack with each other automatically; stacking happens by sequencing trips, not by tapping twice at the same terminal.

Strategy: A Wallet-Optimized Week

  • Saturday, 31 Oct (today): Breakfast or early lunch at a hawker centre with PayLah! ($3 back), grab Luckin 1-for-1 via the app for the afternoon.
  • Sunday, 1 Nov: Last day for Starbucks 1-for-1 with Trust Bank — split a Tall with a friend before the promo closes.
  • Early in the week: Two minutes in CapitaStar — enter RANGOLI26, link a Mastercard, collect the free S$5 eCapitaVoucher (festival runs till 9 Nov).
  • Midweek grocery run: One consolidated FairPrice trip just over $50 nett (or $100 for two vouchers) — vouchers must be earned by 9 Nov and can be spent till 30 Nov.
  • Any Wednesday, 12pm: If a year-end trip is booked, check Trip.com's OCBC Wander Wednesday before paying.
  • Weekend of 7–8 Nov: Visiting relatives? Gardens by the Bay with a BOC UnionPay card before 30 Nov shaves S$8 off the S$80+ day.

What to Skip

Not every card tie-up is worth the effort. Skip sign-up-driven promos that require opening a new bank account or card just to claim a one-off coffee discount — the Starbucks–Trust Bank deal is great for existing Trust cardholders, but a bank application for a Tall latte is upside-down economics. Likewise, treat 'up to' cashback caps with clear eyes: the PayLah! $3 is per qualifying transaction at participating merchants, not a guaranteed $3 on everything you buy that day. And the Gardens by the Bay discount is real but only meaningful if you were already going — a S$8 saving doesn't justify S$80 of unplanned gift-shop spending.

The pattern across all of these: the discount happens at payment, not at the menu. Decide where you're eating for the week, then match the payment method to the meal — that's how a normal hawker Saturday quietly becomes a subsidized one.
