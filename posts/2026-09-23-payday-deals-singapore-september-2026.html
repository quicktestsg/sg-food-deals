Payday Deals Singapore (Sep 23–30, 2026): Last-Chance 1-for-1s, Meat Day Steals & New Opening Promos

The September payday wave is here. This week splits into two halves: 1-for-1s that die on Sep 24–25, and longer-running promos you can plan around — Meat Day, a $14 anniversary menu and two new-store opening deals. Every price, date and catch below comes from the source promos.

Payday week is when Singapore's food promos peak, and the last week of September 2026 is no exception. The trick this week is sequencing: two of the best 1-for-1s close within 48 hours, while the biggest-value promos run for another week or more. Here's the full breakdown, organised by how fast you need to move.

1. Ending in 48 Hours — Move Fast

Shake Shack: 1-for-1 Summer Greek Menu (ends Sep 25)

Shake Shack Singapore is running five days of 1-for-1 Summer Greek deals from 21 to 25 September 2026 at participating outlets, rotating across burgers, chicken bites and fries. The catch: redemption goes through a "Double the Greek flavor" voucher on the Happy Point app — so install it and claim the voucher before you queue. September 25 is a Friday, expect after-work crowds.

Kenangan Coffee: Buy 1 Get 1 Free Payday Promo (ends Sep 24)

The Indonesian coffee chain's payday deal runs 22–24 September 2026: buy one drink, get one free — but only through the Kenangan Coffee app, not at the counter. Today is the last day. If you're near an outlet, this is the cheapest caffeine two-for-one on the island this week.

⚠️ Condition Check

Both deals above are app-gated: Shake Shack needs the Happy Point app voucher, Kenangan needs the Kenangan Coffee app. Download and register before you're standing at the counter — both apps require an account at redemption time.

2. The Meat Day Play — Don Don Donki, Sep 21–30

DON DON DONKI's Meat Day promotion runs 21–30 September 2026 across all Singapore stores, with special prices on A5 Wagyu Kata Rosu steak, Mini Beef Hamburg and chicken or Hokkaido pork meatball bundles. Exact prices vary by store and cut (the source doesn't publish one flat price list) — treat this as a "go browse the red-tag shelf" mission rather than a single-target buy. The window includes two weekends, but A5 cuts sell out early in the day; evenings are slimmer pickings.

4. New Openings, New Deals

New-store promos are the most reliable discounts in Singapore — operators stack their marketing budget into the first fortnight. Three this week:

  • Luckin Coffee, Alexandra Retail Centre (opens Sep 22): 1-for-1 drinks from 22 September to 5 October 2026 to launch the outlet at 460 Alexandra Road. Two weeks is a long window for Luckin — go on a weekday morning to avoid stock-outs.
  • Maison Yoshoku, Raffles City (opens Sep 23): Surrey Hills Group's Japanese-Western yoshoku concept — with Singapore's first floating dessert bar — opens today and runs a 1-for-1 promo from 26 to 28 September only. Note the gap: opening day is not promo day, the deal starts Saturday.
  • Chocolate Origin, City Square Mall (reopened Sep 21): 1-for-1 Chocolate Lava Cake with ice cream at unit B2-K9, 11am–10pm daily, while stocks last (end date not announced). Reopening deals tend to be short — assume single-week and go early.

5. One-Day Hit: KOI Thé × ARKNIGHTS: ENDFIELD (Sep 26)

Gamers, mark Saturday. Buy two participating collaboration drinks at selected KOI Thé outlets (NEX, PLQ Mall, Bugis+ and Great World City), show the receipt, and redeem one of four exclusive ARKNIGHTS: ENDFIELD reward sets — sure-win, 26 September 2026 only. Collab merch in Singapore disappears within hours; if you want the full set of four, plan a two-outlet route.

  • Sheng Siong housebrand + PWP (Sep 22–28): up to 68% off selected housebrand items, including buy-one-get-one deals on Tasty Bites and Golden Swiss frozen foods. Weekly cycle — new specials every Tuesday.

7. Strategy: Your Day-by-Day Payday Plan

  • Wednesday, Sep 23 (today): last day for Kenangan Coffee app-only 1-for-1. Don Don Donki Meat Day continues — evening A5 pickings get thin, go after work but early.
  • Thursday, Sep 24: Morganfield's $14 anniversary menu at Suntec makes a solid pre-weekend dinner. Sheng Siong BOGO frozen deals run till Saturday — restock the freezer.
  • Friday, Sep 25: final day for Shake Shack's Greek 1-for-1 (Happy Point voucher) — go before the after-work rush. It's also Mid-Autumn Festival: grab discounted Westin mooncakes if any stock remains.
  • Saturday, Sep 26: KOI Thé × ARKNIGHTS: ENDFIELD giveaway day (buy 2 collab drinks, sure-win merch). Maison Yoshoku's 1-for-1 opening promo also starts today at Raffles City — expect queues at both.
  • Sunday–Tuesday, Sep 27–29: cooling-off period. Luckin ARC 1-for-1 (till Oct 5), BlackTree 313@somerset drinks deals (till Oct 5) and Kenny Rogers' Quarter Meal (ongoing) all have runway. MUJI Tampines 1 reopening carnival runs Sep 25–30 if you're east-side.
  • Wednesday, Sep 30: hard deadline day — Morganfield's $14 menu, Don Don Donki Meat Day and the Sheng Siong weekly cycle all end. Anything you've been postponing, do it today.

This payday week rewards the fast and the organised. Move within 48 hours for the app-gated 1-for-1s, plan Saturday around the KOI collab and the Maison Yoshoku opening, and use the Sep 30 cluster (Morganfield's, Meat Day, Sheng Siong) as your backstop deadline.
