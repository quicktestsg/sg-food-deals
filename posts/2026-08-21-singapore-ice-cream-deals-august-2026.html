Singapore Ice Cream Deals (Aug 21–31, 2026): Häagen-Dazs, Magnum, Chateraise 8-for-$9 & More

Chateraise's 8-for-$9 freezer-filler starts today, FairPrice's Häagen-Dazs and NZ Natural markdowns close on Aug 26, and Swensen's Sundae Club runs all the way to Sep 30. Every deal below has a start date, an end date, and its catch spelled out — sorted by how soon each one disappears.

This is the densest week for ice cream deals Singapore has seen all month. Convenience stores, supermarkets and dessert chains are all running frozen promotions at once — but the windows vary wildly, from a deal that ends in four days to one that stretches into October. Rather than a store-by-store list, this guide is ordered by urgency: what closes first, what starts today, and what can wait for the weekend. Every price and date below comes from the brands' own promo announcements, and the fine print sits next to each deal rather than buried under it.

1. Closing First: 7-Eleven's Icy Treats Ends Tuesday, Aug 25

7-Eleven's 'Icy Treats' promotion runs from August 12 to 25, with up to 45% off across the big ice cream brands. The named prices: Cornetto Royale at 4 for $9 ($2.25 a cone), Magnum at 3 for $10 (about $3.33 a stick), plus bundle pricing on Häagen-Dazs Mini Cups and Stickbars, with Ben & Jerry's and Wall's items in the mix too. Participating stores only, while stocks last — and with four days left, the popular flavours are the first to thin out.

Separately, 7-Eleven has brought in an exclusive durian range from August 12 onwards — Mao Shan Wang durian ice cream in cups and sticks, plus a D24 Durian Crepe Ice Cream — available while stocks last, with no end date announced. Durian fans should treat that as 'get it before it's gone' rather than a dated promo.

⚠️ Price Unconfirmed — 7-Eleven Durian Range

The announcement for the Mao Shan Wang and D24 range didn't publish prices, so none are quoted here. Durian ice cream at convenience stores typically lands in the S$3–6 range per piece, but check the freezer tag before assuming — limited-run durian products are sometimes priced above the standard range.

2. Closing Second: The Häagen-Dazs Double-Header (Both End Aug 26)

Two separate Häagen-Dazs plays close on the same day — Wednesday, August 26 — at two different chains. At FairPrice, the ice cream markdown runs until August 26 with 50% off NZ Natural and 41% off Häagen-Dazs (the specific discounted items are marked in-store). At Giant, Häagen-Dazs goes at 3 for $25 — about $8.33 a tub — as part of its Super Savings promotion running to August 26 alongside broader grocery discounts of up to 56% off.

Which one wins? No verdict here, because the answer depends on which Häagen-Dazs items each chain has discounted and in what size — the announcements don't publish matching item lists, so a like-for-like comparison isn't possible from source data. The practical read: if a FairPrice and a Giant are both nearby, check the freezer aisle of each before committing to three tubs.

One more FairPrice thread that outlives the others: the Ice Cream & Dairy Delights price drop — covering ice cream, cooking cream and cheese — saves up to 35% and runs until September 2, so the FairPrice ice cream window doesn't fully close on the 26th.

3. Starts Today: Chateraise 8 for $9 — If You Bring the Bag

From today, August 21, through August 31, Chateraise is running an Ice Cream Mix & Match at all its Singapore stores: 8 selected ice cream bars for $9 — $1.13 a bar — if you bring your own Chateraise cooler bag, or 8 for $12 (with an S-size cooler bag plus dry ice) if you don't. That bag clause is a $3 swing, which is a third of the cheaper price — worth checking the cupboard before heading out.

The mix-and-match list, from the announcement: Juicy White Peach, Juicy Mango and Juicy Strawberry ice bars; Chocolate Mint Bar; the Chocolate Bucky range in Vanilla, Chocolate and Dry Mint; the Yatsugatake Contracted Farmer Freshly Milked Bar; and the Yatsugatake Strawberry Milk Bar. That's nine picks across fruit bars and milk-based bars — enough variety that eight doesn't mean duplicates.

4. The Sit-Down Plays: Swensen's, Geláre, A&W, After Hours

Swensen's Unlimited Sundae Club: 20% Off, Bring a Friend (Aug 19 – Sep 30)

At Swensen's Unlimited @ Geneo, the Sundae Club — build-your-own from 48 ice cream and gelato flavours and 50+ toppings — is 20% off when at least two bowls are ordered, from August 19 to September 30. The catch is structural: the discount needs a friend and a two-bowl minimum, so it's really a 'per-bowl price drops when you pair up' deal. Location catch too — Geneo only.

Geláre Westgate: Rolling 1-for-1 Series (Aug 17 – Sep 13)

Geláre's Westgate outlet is running a series of 1-for-1 deals from August 17 to September 13, and the keyword is 'series': it started with Classic Waffles at $9.90 (Aug 17–23), with ice cream, coffee, mocktail and croffle promos rolling through the following weeks. Each wave is announced weekly, so the current deal depends on the week of the visit. Westgate only — not islandwide.

A&W Godzilla Waffle: Milo Everything (Aug 11–31)

At all A&W outlets from August 11 to 31: the Godzilla Waffle — a warm Milo-flavoured waffle topped with vanilla ice cream and a Milo powder dusting, available dine-in and takeaway. The announcement also mentions a percentage discount attached to the promo, but doesn't complete the figure in the published excerpt — confirm at the counter what this week's discount actually is before ordering around it.

After Hours Gelato: The Nostalgia Menu, Open Till 2AM

The late-night angle: After Hours Gelato (Bishan and Haw Par Villa, both open till 2am daily) has a limited-time nostalgic series at $7 a cup or $25 for a set of four — buying the set saves $3 against four single cups. The four flavours turn childhood snacks into gelato: Paddle Pop Rainbow with iced gem biscuits; Wang Zai Niu Nai paired with Wang Zai Xiao Man Tou buns; Hawflakes with a hawflake disc; and Ribena Sorbet with lychee jelly. No end date has been announced — treat it as available until it isn't.

5. The Long-Runners: Udders × Ribena and the $2 BK Sundae

Two deals with comfortable runways. First: the Udders × Ribena limited-edition Blackcurrant Sorbet is $9.95 at FairPrice Finest stores (down from a published $11.87 — about $1.92 off) from August 19 to October 31, with a free Ribena eco bag per purchase while stocks last, and sampling sessions at FairPrice Finest locations. Second: Burger King's app-deal series runs until September 6, including a $2 Choco Malt Sundae made with Milo with any purchase — the sundae isn't standalone; it needs something else on the order. Selected outlets, via the BK app.

6. Strategy: The Week's Ice Cream Calendar

  • Friday, Aug 21 (today): Chateraise's 8-for-$9 opens — first-day stock is the fullest, and the cooler bag determines whether it's $9 or $12. Evening option: After Hours Gelato's nostalgia series runs till 2am at Bishan or Haw Par Villa.
  • Saturday–Sunday, Aug 22–23: The sit-down window: Swensen's Sundae Club with a friend at Geneo (20% off, two bowls minimum), or A&W's Godzilla Waffle at any outlet. Geláre Westgate's waffle 1-for-1 wave is in its final weekend (ends Aug 23) before the next wave takes over.
  • Monday, Aug 24: Last full day before the double closing. If the Häagen-Dazs math from either FairPrice or Giant is on the list, tomorrow is the deadline.
  • Tuesday, Aug 25: Final day of 7-Eleven's Icy Treats — Cornetto Royale 4-for-$9 and Magnum 3-for-$10 end tonight. The durian range (no end date) presumably continues while stocks last.
  • Wednesday, Aug 26: Häagen-Dazs closes at both FairPrice (50% NZ Natural / 41% Häagen-Dazs) and Giant (3-for-$25). The FairPrice dairy price drop (up to 35% off) survives until Sep 2 for anyone who misses the cut.

💡 The One Freezer-Stocking Move

If only one trip happens this week, make it Chateraise today or this weekend with the cooler bag in hand: eight bars at $1.13 each is the lowest per-piece price on this entire page, the mix-and-match list spans fruit and milk bars so nothing repeats, and the Aug 31 window means the deal survives well past this week's chaos — but first-day stock doesn't. Pair it with a FairPrice or Giant Häagen-Dazs run before Wednesday's double close, and the freezer is set for the whole of September.
