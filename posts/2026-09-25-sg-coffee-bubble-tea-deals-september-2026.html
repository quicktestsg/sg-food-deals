Coffee & Bubble Tea Deals Singapore (Sep 25 – Oct 2, 2026): Luckin $2.99, TOMORO 1-for-1

Payday week is here and every major coffee and tea chain in Singapore is running something. Luckin is celebrating 100 stores with $2.99 packs, TOMORO has app-only 1-for-1, CHAGEE members get a $5 voucher, and CHICHA San Chen's $3.50 tea ends today. Here is the full caffeine playbook, dates and catches included.

If the weekly budget has a line item for caffeine, this is the week to spend it wisely. Between September 25 and October 2, 2026, at least six chains are running simultaneous promotions — a rare overlap that means almost any drink craving can be satisfied at a discount. This guide sorts them by urgency: what ends today, what to claim this weekend, and what runs into October.

1. Ending Today — CHICHA San Chen $3.50 Pure Tea (Sep 21–25)

CHICHA San Chen members can get any Pure Tea for $3.50 with a member voucher, choosing from six tea bases. The promotion runs September 21–25, 2026 — meaning today is the last day. The voucher is valid for walk-in purchases at participating stores, and the Jewel outlet is excluded. If a membership account is not set up yet, registering in the app before heading down is the fastest route.

⏰ Last-Day Checklist

CHICHA San Chen's $3.50 Pure Tea ends tonight (Sep 25). Two things to remember: the voucher must be claimed/used via membership, and CHICHA San Chen @ Jewel is excluded. Also expired as of Sep 24: Dunkin's payday deals (buy 5 donuts get 2 free, 1-for-1 Cold Brew and Teh Tarik) — don't walk in expecting them.

2. Claim This Weekend — Luckin Coffee 100-Store $2.99 Packs (Claim Sep 25–28, Drink Sep 29 – Oct 2)

Luckin Coffee has hit 100 stores across Singapore and is marking the milestone with a two-phase promotion. Phase one: claim a $2.99 Coupon Pack on the Luckin app from September 25–28, 2026. Phase two: use the pack to get signature drinks for $2.99 from September 29 to October 2. The catch is scarcity — only 5,000 packs are released daily, so claiming early in the day matters. Nothing needs to be spent today; the app claim is the action item.

3. All Week — TOMORO Coffee 1-for-1 via App (Sep 24–30)

TOMORO Coffee's payday promotion runs September 24–30, 2026: 1-for-1 drinks, but only when ordered through the TOMORO COFFEE SG app at participating outlets. This is the strongest sharing deal of the week — two drinks for the price of one makes the per-cup cost the cheapest among this week's coffee promos for anyone drinking in pairs. The trade-off is the app requirement: walk-in counter orders do not qualify.

4. Ongoing, No Announced End Date — CHAGEE $5 Members' Voucher & Hanis 60¢ Upsize

CHAGEE Singapore is issuing a $5 Members' Day Drink Voucher to eligible CHA Explorer, CHA Pioneer and CHA Master members, valid on selected Fresh Milk Tea, Brewed Tea and Fresh Fruit Tea drinks. The promotion started September 23, 2026, with no announced end date — treat it as available until CHAGEE says otherwise. A $5 voucher on milk tea is effectively a free or near-free drink, so checking the app for eligibility costs nothing.

For kopi-style traditional drinks, Hanis Cafe & Bakery is running a drink upsize promotion from September 24, 2026 onwards (end date not announced): pay 60¢ more for a bigger cup, including favourites like Iced Honey Lemon and Kopi-Si. This one is less about flash and more about quiet value — 60¢ is the smallest spend on this list.

5. The Merch Play — ZUS Coffee Free Ube Bae Soft Sleeve ($15 Spend)

From September 21, 2026 onwards, ZUS Coffee gives a free Ube Bae Soft Sleeve with $15 spent on handcrafted drinks, while stocks last. Full terms are in the ZUS app. Fair warning on the math: $15 is multiple drinks for most people, so this is a collectible-chasing deal rather than a savings deal. Only worth it if that spend was happening anyway.

  • CHICHA San Chen $3.50: member voucher required; walk-in only at participating stores; Jewel outlet excluded; ends Sep 25, 2026.
  • Luckin $2.99 pack: must claim in app Sep 25–28; drinks redeemable Sep 29 – Oct 2 only; 5,000 packs daily, while stocks last.
  • TOMORO 1-for-1: app orders only (TOMORO COFFEE SG App); participating outlets; ends Sep 30, 2026.
  • CHAGEE $5 voucher: CHA Explorer / Pioneer / Master members only; selected drinks; no announced end date.
  • Hanis 60¢ upsize: from Sep 24, 2026 onwards; end date not announced; selected drinks.
  • ZUS free sleeve: $15 minimum spend on handcrafted drinks; while stocks last; full T&Cs in ZUS app.

6. Strategy: What to Do Each Day

  • Fri, Sep 25 (today): Use the CHICHA San Chen $3.50 member voucher before it expires tonight. Open the Luckin app and claim the $2.99 coupon pack (5,000 daily — earlier is safer).
  • Sat–Sun, Sep 26–28: TOMORO 1-for-1 for any café catch-up (order in the app). Re-claim the Luckin pack each day if multiple packs are wanted for next week.
  • Mon, Sep 28: Last day to claim the Luckin $2.99 pack — the claim window closes tonight even though redemption runs to Oct 2.
  • Tue, Sep 30: Last day of TOMORO's 1-for-1. Also the day to check the CHAGEE app for the $5 voucher if it hasn't been used yet.
  • Sep 29 – Oct 2: Redemption window for the Luckin $2.99 signature drinks.

7. Worth Knowing: Grocery Coffee Is Also on Sale

For home-brewers, FairPrice is running 2-week promotions from September 24 to October 7, 2026 that include Price Drop deals on coffee, alongside savings across local favourites, beverages and meal options. Giant's weekly specials (September 24–30) also cover groceries and household items. Specific shelf prices vary by store, so check the FairPrice app or in-app weekly specials before shopping — the discount categories are confirmed, exact prices are not published in the deal sources.

💡 The Play in One Sentence

Claim the Luckin pack today, use CHICHA's voucher tonight, ride TOMORO's 1-for-1 through the weekend, and drink $2.99 signature cups when the redemption window opens Sep 29 — that is a full week of discounted caffeine for the price of roughly one specialty coffee.

One final note: McDonald's is also in the drink-adjacent game with a 1-for-1 McSpicy deal running 2pm–5pm until September 25, 2026 (today) at participating outlets, and 20pc McNuggets at $9.90 until September 30 after breakfast hours — not coffee, but the same payday-week logic applies. As always, confirm details in each brand's official app before travelling, as stock and participation vary by outlet.
