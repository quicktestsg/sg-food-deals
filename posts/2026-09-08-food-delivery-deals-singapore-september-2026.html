Food Delivery Deals Singapore September 2026: Popeyes 1-for-1 on Grab, 20% Off DONKI GrabMart (Sep 8–28)

Food delivery is usually where food budgets go to die — platform markups plus fees can quietly add 30–40% to a meal. But September 2026 is running an unusually good delivery window: Popeyes has a buy-one-get-one sale on Grab and 50% off on foodpanda until September 28, DON DON DONKI is taking 20% off GrabMart orders for Donpen's birthday, and foodpanda itself is discounting selected best sellers by 25% all month. This guide covers all three, then flips the frame: the pickup and off-peak dine-in deals (Domino's $9.99 Solo Craving, Ajumma's $9.90++ Korean set) that beat every delivery fee in town.

Everything below is checked against the source announcements — dates, prices, minimum spends, caps and platform conditions. Where the source did not publish a detail (which exact items, which outlets), it says so explicitly. One framing note before the deals: a delivery deal is only a deal if the discount outruns the fees. A 1-for-1 mains promo on a platform can still lose to walking to the counter once small-order fees and service charges stack. The math section at the bottom shows where each deal clears that bar — and where it doesn't.

1. The Headliner: Popeyes Delivery Mega Sale — 1-for-1 on Grab, 50% Off on foodpanda (Now Till Sep 28)

Popeyes Singapore is running a Delivery Mega Sale on both Grab and foodpanda from now until September 28, 2026 — three full weeks, which is long for a platform promo. Six menu items are discounted across the two platforms. On Grab it works as 1-for-1: buy one, get one free, on items including the 2pc Chicken Hainanese Fragrant Rice Kick Bowl, the Zesty Tomato Kick Bowl, and the Chicken Shepherd's Pie (the source lists these three by name; the remaining items in the six were not itemised in the announcement, so check the Popeyes storefront in the Grab app for the full list). On foodpanda the same sale runs as 50% off the eligible items. Which platform is better depends on your order shape — the math section below breaks it down. The dates are clean and long: anything ordered off the eligible list until September 28 gets the discount. Note the promo lives on the platforms, not in-store: this is a delivery-exclusive, so don't expect the 1-for-1 at the counter.

2. The Grocery Play: DON DON DONKI GrabMart Mega Sale for Donpen's Birthday (Sep 7–16)

DON DON DONKI is celebrating mascot Donpen's birthday with a GrabMart Mega Sale from September 7 to 16, 2026, and the mechanics are unusually generous for grocery delivery: 20% off orders with a minimum spend of $100, capped at $20, plus $5 off the delivery fee on orders of $55 and up. Read those thresholds carefully — they stack. A $100 order hits exactly the 20% cap ($20 off) and clears the $55 bar for the $5 delivery-fee discount simultaneously: $25 of total relief on a hundred-dollar basket. A $55–$99 order gets the fee discount but only a partial percentage; under $55, nothing. So the strategy writes itself: consolidate. This is the week to do the big DONKI stock-up (sashimi-grade fish, Japanese snacks, the frozen gyoza mountain) in one order rather than three small ones. Ten days only — it ends September 16.

Also at DONKI this month: the Aburi Sushi Fair (till Sep 30)

Separate from the GrabMart sale, DONKI's physical stores are running an Aburi Sushi Fair from now until September 30, 2026: selected flame-seared sushi at up to $4 off, with the headline MEGA Aburi Salmon Mix at 24 pieces for $27.90, down from a stated $31.90 — a source-confirmed $4 saving. While stocks last, all stores. Pair the two DONKI promos in one week: GrabMart order for the pantry, in-store visit for the aburi, and the birthday sale effectively subsidises the trip.

3. Platform-Level: foodpanda 25% Off Selected Best Sellers (Sep 1–27)

Foodpanda itself is in on the September discounting: from September 1 to 27, 2026, 25% off selected best sellers — the platform's own promotion, meaning it applies across participating restaurants, not one chain. The source did not publish the list of participating dishes, so treat the app's 'best sellers' tag as the live menu: browse, filter, and see what's flagged. The interesting play is stacking potential: if a Popeyes eligible item is also flagged as a best seller, foodpanda's 50% platform-chicken discount already beats 25%, so use the 25% for everything that ISN'T Popeyes. The promotion runs nearly the whole month, ending September 27 — one day before the Popeyes sale closes, which makes the last week of September a double-dead zone worth clearing your calendar for.

4. The Counter-Argument: When Pickup and Dine-In Beat Delivery

Two deals that started September 7 make the anti-delivery case better than any lecture. First, Domino's Pizza Singapore's new Pizza Puff: from $6.99 in Loaded Cheese, Loaded Chicken and Loaded Beef flavours, with the $9.99 Solo Craving Offer — 1 Pizza Puff plus 2 sides — available when ordering over the counter or for pickup. Note what that condition implies: the bundle is explicitly structured away from delivery. Pickup means no delivery fee, no small-order surcharge, no platform markup. Second, Ajumma's Korean Restaurant's off-peak deal: $9.90++ for a main, free-flow side dishes and a K-Treat, on weekdays from 2pm to 5pm. That is a full Korean spread at near-hawker pricing, and it exists only in the dead zone between lunch and dinner — no delivery platform will ever sell you free-flow banchan. Both deals are 'from September 7 onwards' with no announced end date, so treat them as ongoing until stated otherwise (the price-unconfirmed rule cuts both ways: ongoing also means it can end quietly — a one-minute call before a dedicated trip is cheap insurance). The point is not that delivery is bad; it is that September's best flat prices live at the counter, and the best platform discounts live on the apps. The optimal September is both.

💡 The Math: Which Popeyes Platform Wins
  • Solo order: foodpanda's 50% off one item beats Grab's 1-for-1 (two of an item you didn't want is not a discount, it's groceries). If you only eat one bowl, order foodpanda.
  • Two people, same item: Grab's 1-for-1 wins outright — pay for one, receive two, effective 50% with no cap logic. On foodpanda you'd pay 2 × 50%-off, which equals the same discount but double the eligible-item pool usage.
  • Different items: foodpanda — 50% applies per eligible item, so two different discounted items both drop. Grab's BOGO pairs one paid with one free, typically the cheaper one free (confirm pairing rule in-app before mixing prices).
  • Fee reality check: platform delivery + service fees typically run a few dollars per order. A 50% discount on a ~$10–15 item absorbs that comfortably; on a single $6 item it may not. Scale the order to the discount.

6. Strategy: The Delivery Calendar

  • This week (Sep 8–13): Do the consolidated DONKI GrabMart order before you forget — the $100-threshold play is the single biggest dollar saving in this post ($25 off), and the window closes Sep 16. Pair with an in-store aburi run if you're near a DONKI.
  • Weekday afternoons (2–5pm): Ajumma's $9.90++ is the off-peak dine-in play for flexible schedules — WFH lunch-at-3pm people, this is your deal.
  • Any rainy evening: Popeyes 1-for-1 on Grab (two people) or 50% on foodpanda (solo). The sale runs to Sep 28, so there is no rush — but the foodpanda 25% ends Sep 27, one day earlier. Don't confuse the two end dates.
  • Sep 27–28: The double-closing window — both platform promos end within 24 hours of each other. Last-call orders on the 27th catch both.

7. What to Skip

Skip ordering DONKI on GrabMart below the $55 threshold — the sale mechanics mean a $40 basket gets zero benefit, so either consolidate up or buy it in person. Skip solo Popeyes orders on Grab: one bowl through the 1-for-1 wastes the free item's value if you won't eat it (foodpanda's 50% is the solo instrument). Skip Domino's delivery entirely this month — the Solo Craving bundle is deliberately counter/pickup, and paying delivery fees to buy a deal engineered around not paying delivery fees is a philosophical defeat. And skip the foodpanda 25% on anything Popeyes-eligible: 50% is sitting right next to it on the same app.

Bottom line: September 2026 is a genuinely good delivery month — the first in a while where the platform discounts (Popeyes' 1-for-1/50%, foodpanda's 25%, DONKI's stacked thresholds) are big enough to survive the fees. Use Grab for pairs, foodpanda for solos, consolidate the DONKI basket to $100 before September 16, and let Domino's and Ajumma's remind you that the counter still holds some of the best flat prices in town. Both worlds, one month.
