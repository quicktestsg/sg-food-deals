New Food Deals This Week Singapore (2–8 Nov 2026): McCrispy Chicken $7.95, $9.90 Crab Yakiniku & Free Coffee Week

The first full week of November fires on all cylinders: a new-recipe fried chicken at McDonald's, king crab at a yakiniku chain for less than $10, weekday breakfast sets at Coffee Bean, a $1.80 bao-and-kopi combo islandwide, and a free-entry coffee festival with a Johor bakery pop-up. Here is what launches on which day — and what each one really costs.

Deal hunting is usually a game of catching things before they end. This week flips it: almost everything worth eating is just starting. Between Monday 2 and Sunday 8 November 2026, McDonald's relaunches its fried chicken with a new recipe, Yakiniku-GO bolts a $9.90 crab onto every set meal, Coffee Bean rolls out a new weekday breakfast set, Kopitiam keeps a $1.80 tea-break duo running all month, and Singapore Coffee Week opens free at Sands Expo with JB-famous 365 Bakery in tow. This guide walks through them in launch order, with prices, dates and the fine print from the promo announcements.

1. Monday, 2 Nov: KFC $6.50 App-Only Chicken Meal (Today Only)

Start the week with the tightest deadline on this list: KFC's Monday app deal on 2 November 2026 offers a 2-piece chicken meal — two pieces of fried chicken, classic sides and a drink — for $6.50, ordered through the KFC app. It is a one-day-only offer tied to this specific Monday, so if you are reading this on Tuesday, it is gone. The deal is app-exclusive: walk-in counter orders at the same price are not part of it, so claim it in the app before you queue.

2. From Monday, 2 Nov: Coffee Bean's New Weekday Breakfast Set — $6.25 Each When You Buy Two

Also launching today (2 November 2026): The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf's new Turkey & Chicken Cranberry Mayo Wrap Breakfast Set, available Monday to Friday until 12pm. Each set pairs the festive wrap with a side salad, a mini red velvet cupcake and an Americano. The headline number is $6.25 per set — but that price applies when you buy two sets; the single-set price is not stated in the promo, so budget accordingly if you are flying solo. No end date has been announced, which usually means it runs until further notice — but weekday-mornings-only is the hard rule.

3. From Tuesday, 3 Nov: Yakiniku-GO $9.90 Red King Crab or Snow Crab With Every Set Meal

From 3 November 2026, Yakiniku-GO is adding big, meaty Red King Crab and Snow Crab to any yakiniku set meal for $9.90 (end date not announced). Read that structure carefully: the crab is an add-on price on top of your set meal, not the total bill — so the real cost is your set meal plus $9.90. Even so, crab legs at a ten-dollar markup during a normal weeknight barbecue is an unusually good play, and it is the kind of add-on that turns a $20-something dinner into something that feels twice as expensive. Bring a friend who 'doesn't share food' and let them order their own.

4. Thursday, 5 Nov: McDonald's New-Recipe McCrispy Fried Chicken, 2pc Meal at $7.95 (Until 25 Nov)

The biggest launch of the week: from 5 to 25 November 2026, McDonald's introduces its retooled McCrispy Fried Chicken with a spicier kick and a new coleslaw, and the 2-piece meal is priced at $7.95 — down from a usual price stated as 'from $9.25' in the promo. Three weeks is a long window by fast-food standards, but launch-week stock and queue times are their own problem: if you want to taste it before the internet forms an opinion, go on day one or wait for the second week. At roughly $4 a piece with sides and a drink included, it undercuts most chicken specials this month — including KFC's own one-day $6.50 deal tomorrow-hindsight.

5. 5–8 Nov: Singapore Coffee Week at Sands Expo — Free Entry, Free Tastings, JB's 365 Bakery Pop-Up

From 5 to 8 November 2026, Singapore Coffee Week takes over Sands Expo with free entry, a Sip & Stamp digital stamp trail with rewards, coffee tastings, competitions and a S$10,000 24K gold bean treasure hunt. The sleeper hit for bakers: Johor Bahru's cult-favourite 365 Bakery — famous for its Swiss rolls (flavours reported include taro-seaweed-chicken floss, 70% dark chocolate and pistachio), salted croissants and chewy bagels — is crossing the Causeway for the event, per Chinese-language food media. That is the queue worth joining: JB weekends mean two-hour customs lines for these bakes, and here they land at a free-entry venue four MRT stops from Marina Bay. Prices at the pop-up are not confirmed — treat it as tasting-priced exploration, not a bargain hunt.

6. Friday, 6 Nov: Tiong Bahru Bakery's 28cm Grand Croissant ($29.90++) — Worth Knowing, Not a Deal

For completeness: from 6 November to 31 December 2026, Tiong Bahru Bakery launches The Grand Croissant — a 28cm showpiece at $29.90++. This is a splurge, not a saving, and it belongs on this list only because it launches the same week and will dominate your feed. If you go, treat it as a shareable table centrepiece for four people (call it $7.50 a head after ++) rather than a personal pastry — that is the only math that makes sense of it.

7. Running All Month: $1.80 Kopitiam Bao + Kopi, 1-for-1 Unagi at $6.45 a Serving

Two November-long deals started on 1 November and are easy to forget because they lack a deadline: first, Kopitiam's tea-break duo with Lim Kee — a Sweet Yam or Fragrant Pandan Bao with a hot kopi or teh for $1.80 per set, from 1 to 30 November 2026. At 2026 prices, $1.80 for any assembled snack-plus-coffee is nearly unheard of; this is the single cheapest item on this page. Second, Takagi Ramen's Unagi Fiesta Secret Menu: 1-for-1 on Unagi Tonkotsu Ramen, Unagi Shoyu Ramen, Unagi Don and Unagi Ochazuke — two servings for $12.90, working out to $6.45 each (from 1 November, end date not announced). And Shabu Sai's November premium soup — Thai Green Curry at $3.99, down from its stated usual of $4.99 — comes with a complimentary platter at all outlets. Note the fine print on all three: the Kopitiam deal runs while participation lasts at outlets, and the Shabu Sai free platter applies to the soup purchase, not the buffet.

Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Monday, 2 Nov (today): Claim the KFC $6.50 2pc meal in the app — tonight's deadline, not this week's.
  • Tuesday, 3 Nov: First day of Yakiniku-GO crab — go early in the promo's life; add-ons like this sell out on weekends once word spreads.
  • Wednesday, 4 Nov: Weekday breakfast at Coffee Bean before 12pm — pair up with a colleague to lock the $6.25-per-set price, then grab a $1.80 Kopitiam tea-break duo for the 3pm slump.
  • Thursday, 5 Nov: McCrispy launch day + Coffee Week opening day. If queues scare you, do the coffee first and the chicken next week — the chicken runs to 25 Nov.
  • Friday–Sunday, 6–8 Nov: Coffee Week's closing stretch: 365 Bakery pop-up, Sip & Stamp trail, and the gold bean hunt — all free to enter. Friday is also Grand Croissant day if a $29.90++ table centrepiece is your kind of Friday.
  • Any day, before 30 Nov: Takagi Ramen unagi 1-for-1 at $6.45 a serving — the week's best sit-down value with no day-of-week restriction.

What to Skip

Two traps this week. First, 'new' does not mean 'cheap': the Grand Croissant is a $29.90++ event pastry — go for the spectacle or skip it, but do not file it under savings. Second, be honest about add-on economics: Yakiniku-GO's $9.90 crab is excellent, but if you were not already planning a yakiniku dinner, the deal has made you spend $30+ to 'save' on crab. The same logic applies to Coffee Week — free entry is free, but a stamp trail designed to move you past paid booths is marketing, not a discount. The genuinely unconditional picks this week are the $1.80 Kopitiam duo and the Takagi unagi 1-for-1: both are complete, self-contained meals at fixed prices with no purchase-on-top. Deal facts verified against promo announcements current as of 2 November 2026; terms can change, so double-check with the outlet before travelling for a specific deal.
