The $5 Meal Club: Cheapest Full Meals in Singapore Right Now

Post-exam season and payday week landed at the same time — and the chains responded. Here is every budget full-meal deal running right now, with dates and the fine print.

A strange thing happens in Singapore in late October: exam season ends, payday lands, and every fast-food chain launches a value promotion at once. This week, three separate chains are selling complete meals at $5 or less. Here is the breakdown, sorted by price.

1. Marrybrown Value Bowls — $3.50 (from 26 Oct 2026)

The cheapest full meal on the island this week. Marrybrown's Value Bowls are $3.50 each from 26 October 2026 onwards at participating outlets (end date not announced). A bowl is an actual rice-based meal — the standout budget pick of the week.

2. Marrybrown Weekday Combos — $4.90 (Mon–Fri, from 26 Oct 2026)

If you want a combo instead of a bowl, the same chain runs a $4.90 weekday promotion from Monday to Friday, starting 26 October 2026 (end date not announced). Options include the Spicy Wrap Combo, Fish Fillet Burger Combo, Chicken Burger Combo, or a one-piece MB Crispy Chicken meal with rice and curry gravy. Weekdays only.

3. Burger King King's Value — $5 Full Meal (from 26 Oct 2026)

Burger King's King's Value promotion launched on 26 October 2026 and offers a full meal for exactly $5 (end date not announced). A flat $5 complete meal at a major burger chain is the most aggressive mainstream pricing logged this month.

🎯 Best Pick Strategy

Marrybrown $3.50 bowls win on price, but Burger King's $5 meal is the better protein-per-dollar play if you are genuinely hungry. Students should read the next section first.

At Novena, the Meat Feast Set is S$19.90++ for four meats from six options plus a free plush toy per table (while stocks last), until 30 November 2026 during selected off-peak hours — confirm timing with the outlet.
