Starbucks 2 Tall Drinks $9.90 & Breakfast Deals Singapore (Oct 5–12, 2026)

The week of October 5–12, 2026 in Singapore is unusually good for cheap mornings and cheaper afternoons: Starbucks is pairing two Tall drinks for $9.90, a Michelin-recognised prata chain is giving away free prata, jumbo salmon sushi costs 80 cents a piece, and a honey French toast breakfast set lands at Coffee Bean. Every date, price and catch, checked.

Some weeks the deal calendar is all noise; this isn't one of them. Between 5 and 12 October 2026, Singapore's coffee chains are openly fighting for the 3pm crowd, a prata institution is handing out free food to celebrate its fifth straight Michelin Guide mention, and the sushi counters at three downtown malls are dropping jumbo salmon to 80 cents a piece. The through-line of this week: nearly every headline deal is time-boxed — afternoon windows, one-day giveaways, first-50-customer caps. Here's the week, sorted by deadline, with the fine print intact.

1. Starbucks 2 Tall Drinks for $9.90 (Oct 5–8, 2–8pm Daily)

The headliner. From 5 to 8 October 2026, any two Tall drinks go for $9.90 — that's $4.95 a cup, in-store, daily between 2pm and 8pm. The catches are real but manageable: you must be a Starbucks Singapore Rewards member (free to join via the app), the deal excludes Reserve beverages and Weave exclusive drinks, and it's in-store only. The sources note each member gets a limited number of redemptions during the promo window, so pair your usual order with something adventurous rather than burning all redemptions on doubles of the same latte. Best play: the 3pm slump, a study-date partner, two drinks that would normally cost more separately (the source doesn't quote regular Tall pricing, so no fake savings math here — check the board in-store).

2. Free Prata at Springleaf, Fifth Michelin Year (Oct 6, One Day Only)

Springleaf Prata Place is celebrating its fifth consecutive Michelin Guide recognition by giving away 2 free plain pratas per diner on 6 October 2026 — one day only, dine-in only, at participating outlets (Tang Plaza is excluded). The gate: you need to join their Telegram channel and show your joined status when ordering. It's a data-for-prata trade, plain and simple, but two free plain pratas at a Michelin-listed institution is one of the better free-food offers this month. Insider note: plain pratas are the cheapest item on any prata menu — the giveaway is a teaser, and the smart move is to add one order of something with egg or cheese and treat the free pair as the starter. Go early; one-day Telegram-gated freebies at recognisable chains reliably draw queues by mid-morning.

3. Sushi Express & Sushi Plus: Jumbo Salmon at $0.80 a Piece (Oct 5–11)

From 5 to 11 October 2026, Jumbo Salmon Sushi goes for $0.80 per piece at Sushi Express and Sushi Plus — but only at the Funan, PLQ and 313@somerset outlets, and strictly while stocks last. That geography matters: this is squarely a city-lunch deal for the CBD and town crowds, not an islandwide drop. At 80 cents for a jumbo-cut piece of salmon, a modest six-piece salmon run costs $4.80 — genuinely cheap for salmon in these neighbourhoods. The catch is the stock clause: salmon specials at conveyor chains routinely sell out by the lunch peak, so treat this as an 11:30am-or-bust mission, not an after-work plan. The source doesn't state a per-person cap; assume the kitchen closes the promo when the fish runs out.

4. Coffee Bean's New Honey French Toast Breakfast Set (Weekdays from Oct 5)

The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf Singapore launches its Honey French Toast Weekday Breakfast Set from 5 October 2026: sweet French toast with chicken sausages, scrambled eggs, salad, and a Tea Latte, all in one set. The promo material confirms the set is weekday-only and new from 5 October, but does not publish the set price or an end date — price not confirmed, so check the counter or the app before committing, and treat availability as ongoing until stated otherwise. The strategic angle: if the Starbucks afternoon deal (2pm–8pm) and this morning set are both on your radar, they're not competitors — one covers breakfast, the other the post-lunch slump. A sweet-and-savoury set with a drink included is the structure most café breakfasts charge separately for.

5. Shihlin Reopens at The Seletar Mall: 1-for-1 Rice Bowls (Oct 8–9, First 50 Customers)

Shihlin Taiwan Street Food is back at The Seletar Mall, and it's reopening with a 1-for-1 Rice Bowl promotion on 8 and 9 October 2026: two rice bowls for $7.50, available to the first 50 customers only. The bowls on offer are the crowd-favourite Braised Chicken Rice Bowl and its companion (the source names two choices) — tender slow-braised chicken in a rich sauce over rice, i.e. actual lunch, not a snack. The maths: $3.75 a bowl for the first fifty people, two days only, Northeast-side. That's the cheapest proper lunch of the week if you're anywhere near Sengkang or Fernvale — but the first-50 cap makes this an opening-time mission (mall doors, not 1pm). Note the source text contains an internal inconsistency ("8 to 8 October") against the headline's 8 & 9 October; the two-day headline from the more detailed source is used here — confirm at the counter if you're going on day two.

6. Burger King: Two Coupon Stacks, Up to 60% Off (Till 3 Jan 2027 / Till 31 Dec)

Two separate Burger King campaigns are live at once. First, the refreshed BK e-Coupon sheet: 20 deals with savings of up to 60%, covering breakfast, meals, snacks, desserts and drinks, valid until 3 January 2027. Second, the BK App deals run: items from $1.50, valid until 31 December 2026, including value meals, multi-buy deals and cheaper snacks and drinks — plus a birthday-month free Hershey's dessert offer for members celebrating in the right month. Conditions: e-coupons are claimed through official channels and typically one per transaction; app deals require the BK app. Neither campaign publishes a full price list in the cached sources, so treat the "from $1.50" and "up to 60%" as the honest bounds and check the app for the current sheet. The stacking opportunity is real: this week's cheapest full fast-food day is plausibly a $1.50-level app snack plus a 60%-off e-coupon meal, both under one roof, both valid past New Year.

  • <strong>Zero effort (just show up with an app):</strong> Starbucks $9.90 pairs and Burger King's two coupon stacks — both long-dated, both chain-wide, no queues if you avoid peak hours.
  • <strong>Some effort (one Telegram join, one early lunch):</strong> free Springleaf prata on Oct 6 and $0.80 salmon sushi before the lunch rush — both expire fast, both worth the timing.

The Bottom Line

October 5–12, 2026 rewards the time-aware. The Starbucks $9.90 pair is the best simple value (four days, afternoon window, members only); the Springleaf giveaway is the best free food (one day, one Telegram join); the 80-cent salmon is the best cheap lunch for anyone within reach of Funan, PLQ or 313@somerset (one week, while stocks last); and the Burger King coupon stack is the best long game, running past New Year. The Coffee Bean breakfast set and the Shihlin reopening round it out — one needs a price check, the other needs an alarm clock. Plan Tuesday and Wednesday now; everything else can wait for the weekend.
