Mooncake Deals Singapore 2026: Early Bird Savings Up to 40% Off Before Mid-Autumn (Sep 25)

Mid-Autumn Festival falls on September 25, 2026 — and the maths of mooncake season is always the same: the same box costs less in August than it will in late September. Seven verified promotions are live right now, from Imperial Treasure's up-to-30% early bird (closing August 31) to a 40% SAFRA-member rate at a Marriott restaurant. This guide puts them in deadline order, with every minimum purchase, promo code and membership catch stated plainly.

Mooncakes are the one category in Singapore where waiting is genuinely expensive. Bakeries and hotels discount hard in the weeks before Mid-Autumn to lock in production volume, then quietly let the discounts lapse as the festival approaches and desperate last-minute gift-buyers take over. This year the festival lands on Friday, September 25 — which means the best early-bird windows are open right now and the first one slams shut in seven days. Below is every mooncake promotion we could verify from merchant announcements, sorted by how soon you have to act, not by how famous the brand is.

2. Most Urgent: Imperial Treasure Up to 30% Off (Ends Aug 31)

Imperial Treasure — the group behind some of Singapore's most consistently good Cantonese kitchens — runs its early-bird mooncake promotion from August 15 to 31, with up to 30% off its handcrafted mooncake collection. The condition that matters: a minimum purchase of two boxes. That makes this a family-or-office deal rather than a solo indulgence, but it is also the cleanest headline discount from a premium Chinese restaurant group this season, and the window closes one week from today. Anyone planning to gift mooncakes to parents or in-laws this year should treat August 31 as the real deadline of the season — after that, the same boxes revert to full price right through the festival.

3. Biggest Discount: Wan Hao 40% Off for SAFRA Members (Till Sep 22)

The largest percentage on the board belongs to Wan Hao Chinese Restaurant at the Singapore Marriott Tang Plaza Hotel: 40% off selected mooncakes from now until September 22, with promo code SAFRA26 at ordering. The catch is in the code — this is a SAFRA member-exclusive price, so NSmen and their families get first bite. The selection includes flavours you will not find at neighbourhood bakeries, such as Matcha with Balsamic Strawberry Praline and Da Hong Pao Tea with Cranberries and Golden Raisins — hotel mooncakes aimed at the gift market, where the packaging does half the work. If you hold a SAFRA membership (or can borrow a family member's), this is the strongest discount-to-quality ratio of the season; if not, the honest answer is that Imperial Treasure's open-to-all 30% is your ceiling.

4. Budget Route: Genki Sushi From $30.80 (Early Bird Till Sep 9)

Not every mooncake budget stretches to hotel tiers. Genki Sushi's early-bird special starts from $30.80, running August 20 to September 25, with the early-bird savings themselves valid only until September 9. The range includes Japanese-leaning flavours such as Tokachi Red Bean Lava Matcha and Red Bean with Egg Yolk — a Japanese chain's take on a Chinese classic, which is exactly the niche it occupies. Two practical notes: $30.80 is the starting price of the range, not the price of every box (the source does not list per-flavour pricing, so treat anything above the entry point as unconfirmed); and after September 9 the same boxes cost more, so the real deadline here is more than two weeks earlier than the festival.

5. The Chocolate Route: GODIVA Up to 20% Off (Aug 20–Sep 25)

GODIVA's Mid-Autumn Collection 2026 runs August 20 to September 25, with up to 20% off combining member pricing and early-bird savings. This is the pick for recipients who do not actually like mooncakes — chocolate gift boxes wearing Mid-Autumn clothes, which in an office-gifting context is often the safer bet. The fine print: the full 20% is tied to GODIVA membership (free to join, but a step not everyone will take at the counter), and as with every 'up to' discount, entry-level items will sit below the headline rate. What the source does not specify is exact per-box pricing — so walk in expecting a discount structure, not a guaranteed number, or check the member price online first.

6. The Wellness Angle: Eu Yan Sang's $68 Gift Set (From Aug 28)

Eu Yan Sang's contribution to the season is the Beneath One Moon gift set, priced at $68 and available in stores and online from August 28, 2026. This is not a percentage discount — it is a fixed-price curated set built around the theme of gathering, from a brand whose gift credibility with older relatives is close to unmatched. The timing is worth noting: it launches four days from now, so if the grandparents on your list are TCM-inclined, this is the one to diary. The announcement does not itemise the set's contents beyond describing it as bringing together items around the Mid-Autumn theme — check the full contents in-store or online on launch day before committing all your gifting budget to it.

7. Gift-With-Purchase: Yue Hwa Lantern & Wellness Set (Ongoing)

Yue Hwa Chinese Products at Jurong Point is running a Mid-Autumn gift-with-purchase from August 11 onwards (no end date announced yet, but these promos traditionally run to the festival): spend $58 and receive an exclusive Yue Hwa lantern, or spend $128 and take home a Mid-Autumn Wellness Gift Set. The play here is different from the others — you are not buying mooncakes at a discount, you are buying whatever you were already buying (tea, snacks, homeware) and letting the festival pay you back in kind. The lantern is also the only item on this list that children will actually care about, which for a family visit to Jurong Point is worth more than any percentage.

💡 The Early-Bird Math, In One Place

Same market, three deadlines: Imperial Treasure's 30% dies Aug 31, Genki's early-bird pricing dies Sep 9, Chou Niang Jiu Niang's 15% already died Aug 21 and dropped to 8%. The pattern is consistent across every merchant — discounts shed roughly a tier every two weeks as the festival approaches. Buying this week instead of mid-September is the single highest-return decision in this entire guide, and it costs nothing but a calendar reminder.

8. Strategy: Your Mid-Autumn Buying Calendar

  • This week (by Aug 31): Imperial Treasure early bird — up to 30% off, minimum 2 boxes. The hardest deadline of the season.
  • Thursday, Aug 28: Eu Yan Sang's $68 Beneath One Moon set launches in stores and online — check contents on day one if elders are on your list.
  • Any weekend before Sep 9: Genki Sushi early bird from $30.80 — the budget tier's deadline. SAFRA members: order Wan Hao with code SAFRA26 before Sep 22.
  • When near Jurong Point anyway: fold a Yue Hwa stop into the trip — $58 for a lantern, $128 for the wellness set, on purchases you were making regardless.
  • Friday, Sep 25: Mid-Autumn Festival. By now every early-bird rate above is gone and you are paying full price with the crowd. This is the date you are beating.

9. Bottom Line

Mooncake season rewards the organised and taxes everyone else. The organised buyer orders two boxes from Imperial Treasure before August 31 at up to 30% off, adds a $30.80 Genki box for the colleague list before September 9, and — if SAFRA is in the family — picks up hotel-tier boxes at 40% off along the way. Everyone else shops in the third week of September at full price, in queues. The festival is a month away; the savings window is a week wide. Set one reminder for August 31 and the season takes care of itself.
