Coffee & Bubble Tea Deals Singapore (November 2026): ZUS 50% Off, CHAGEE Anniversary Vouchers, $1.99 Milk Tea & 80¢ Kopi

Caffeine is the quiet line item in every Singapore budget — so this fortnight's brew board is stacked: a coffee chain turning its birthday into 50% off vouchers, a tea brand selling anniversary packs at effectively buy-5-get-2 pricing, a $1.99 milk tea that keeps running, and a new Punggol food court pouring kopi at 80¢. Here is every dated, fact-checked deal for 14–30 November 2026.

Singapore runs on two fuel systems: specialty coffee in the morning and bubble tea after lunch. Both are easy to overspend on — a daily $6 latte is $180 a month before you even touch brown sugar milk. This guide rounds up every live coffee and tea promotion we could verify from source announcements this week, organised by urgency: what ends in the next few days, what runs through late November, and what is quietly ongoing with no announced end date. Prices and conditions are exactly as published — where a source stayed silent on price or end date, we say so instead of guessing.

It is a one-day, single-Saturday promo — miss the window today and it is gone. Pair it with any coffee and you have a complete weekend breakfast for the price of one bagel.

  • Deal: Buy 1 Get 1 Free bagels with cream cheese, 4 varieties
  • Dates: 14 November 2026 only (Bagel Saturday)
  • Catch: Bagel price not stated in the source — check in store; single-day promo

2. ZUS Coffee Anniversary: Up to Nine 50% Off Vouchers

From 12 November 2026 onwards, ZUS Coffee is celebrating its anniversary with a top-up bonus: load your ZUS Balance with $30 or more and receive up to nine 50% OFF vouchers, spread across three top-up tiers.   • Deal: Up to 9 × 50% OFF vouchers with a S$30+ ZUS Balance top-up (3 tiers)

3. CHAGEE 9th Anniversary: Buy 5 Get 2 Free Voucher Packs

The headline tea deal of the fortnight: from 15 to 17 November 2026 (Sunday to Tuesday), CHAGEE members can buy anniversary drink voucher packs exclusively on the CHAGEE App — Buy 5 Get 2 Free or Buy 10 Get 5 Free. And there is a second layer: every Wednesday from 11 to 25 November, CHAGEE's Members' Day flash redemption opens at 11.17am (a nod to 9/9), where just 9 Tea Leaves can be swapped for exclusive merchandise and vouchers, plus an extra 20% off for CHA Explorer, Pioneer and Master tier members.

  • Deal: Buy 5 Get 2 Free (7 drinks) or Buy 10 Get 5 Free (15 drinks) voucher packs; vouchers valid 60 days
  • Dates: Voucher packs: 15–17 November 2026 only. Members' Day: every Wednesday, 11–25 November, 11.17am flash redemption
  • Catch: CHAGEE App + membership required; pack price per drink not stated in the source — confirm in-app before buying; flash merch redemption will sell out fast

4. The $1.99 Milk Tea That Refuses to Die: I Love Taimei

Running since 1 March 2026 and still live: at I Love Taimei, any purchase lets you claim a Medium Milk Tea for $1.99 instead of the usual $3.50 — a saving of $1.51 per cup — at 14 outlets islandwide. This is the cheapest verified milk tea in our current cache, and the fact that it has run since March suggests it is a durable customer-acquisition play rather than a flash promo.

  • Deal: Medium Milk Tea at $1.99 (U.P. $3.50) with any purchase
  • Dates: Ongoing since 1 March 2026 (no announced end date)
  • Catch: Requires a purchase; voucher valid 31 days from claim; 14 participating outlets

💡 The Caffeine Budget Math

Run the numbers on a daily habit: at full price, a $3.50 medium milk tea five days a week is $17.50 a week, roughly $75 a month. Switch that same habit to I Love Taimei's $1.99 voucher price and it drops to about $43 a month — saving roughly $32 monthly or nearly $400 a year, with zero change in consumption. Add the ZUS 50% vouchers on top for coffee days, and a two-drink-a-day habit gets dramatically cheaper. The lesson: for repeat purchases, voucher mechanics beat one-off discounts every time.
