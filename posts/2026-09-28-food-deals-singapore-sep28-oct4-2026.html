Food Deals Singapore (Sep 28 – Oct 4, 2026): McDonald's × G-Dragon, KFC $6.50 Zinger & Halloween Donuts

A crossover collab, a farewell snowfall, and enough fried chicken to carry you to Friday — here's what's actually worth your money this week.

The last week of September arrives with a stacked lineup: McDonald's officially brings its G-Dragon collaboration to Singapore on October 1, KFC is running both a one-day $6.50 Zinger special and a weekday breakfast discount that stretches all the way to New Year's Eve, and Halloween-season treats are already hitting bakery shelves. Sorted by urgency, here's the full week at a glance.

1. Ending This Week — Grab Before Friday

  • Luckin Coffee 1-for-1 (ends Sep 28): The payday buy-one-get-one deal on all coffees runs through today only, ordered via the Luckin app. Tomorrow it's gone.
  • KFC $6.50 Zinger Meal (Sep 28 only): A one-day app-exclusive — the full Zinger Meal for $6.50, today only, through the KFC app.
  • Starbucks 20% off merchandise (Sep 28–29): Members get 20% off merchandise across all Singapore stores for two days only. Not a coffee discount — this one is for tumblers and merch.
  • Sheng Siong seafood promo (till Sep 29): Fresh seabass, grouper, threadfin, salmon, squid and crab from $0.88/100g, while stocks last. The weekly promo window closes Tuesday night — steamboat at home just got cheaper.
  • Snow City farewell promo (final day Sep 30): After 26 years, Snow City closes for good. The One Last Snowfall farewell runs to Sep 30 with child tickets from $16 and adult tickets from $19, including 1-hour snow play and an ice bumper car ride. Wednesday is literally the last chance.
  • DON DON DONKI mooncake buy-1-free-1 ($9.90): Still running in-store for a limited time (end date not announced). Four flavours — Matcha Red Bean, Matcha Strawberry Milk, Sweet Potato with Yolk, Black Sesame with Almond Slices — two mooncakes for $9.90. With Mid-Autumn just past, expect this to vanish without notice.

2. New This Week — Running Through October

McDonald's × G-Dragon (from Oct 1)

The collab lands in Singapore on October 1 from 11am, headlined by the Spicy Bulgogi Chicken Burger and the Bulgogi Beef Burger, plus returning limited-edition snacks and ice cream, and a fresh drop of merchandise. The sweetener: an exclusive G-Dragon photocard comes free with qualifying meals, which is exactly the kind of freebie that sells out within hours. No prices published yet — expect the usual premium-collab pricing, and expect queues at flagship stores on day one.

KFC Breakfast, Up to 40% Off (Sep 28 – Dec 31)

A quiet long-runner worth bookmarking: every Monday to Wednesday until December 31, selected KFC breakfast items are up to 40% off — but only on GrabFood and foodpanda, not in-store. Selected Buddy Value Meals start from $9. If your weekday routine already includes a delivery breakfast, shifting it to Mon–Wed is free money.

Popeyes Delivery Month (Sep 28 – Oct 27)

Popeyes is running a month-long delivery promotion from Sep 28, 10am to Oct 27, 10am. The fine print matters: 50% off selected items comes via Grab, while the 1-for-1 deal is on foodpanda (that pair runs Sep 28 to Oct 17 per the source). Same chicken, two apps, two different mechanics — check both before ordering.

Mister Donut Halloween: Buy 6 Get 1 Free (Sep 28 – Nov 1)

Pon De Lion's spooky-themed donuts arrive September 28, priced at $3.20 per piece, with the bundle deal — buy 6, get 1 free — running to November 1. For office sharing or a Halloween party table, the bundle is the only way to buy these.

3. Weekend Playbook (Oct 3–4)

  • KidZania 50% off tickets (Oct 3 only): Half-price tickets on October 3, and here's the flexible part — they're valid for visits until December 31. Buy Saturday, use during a quiet school-holiday weekday later.
  • Royal Caribbean roadshow at NEX (Sep 28 – Oct 4): Cruise deals, extra passenger savings and a free blind box for attendees. Cruise pricing varies by cabin and sail date — treat the roadshow as a negotiation window, not a fixed-price list.
  • Branded toys warehouse clearance, The Chevrons (Sep 30 – Oct 4): Up to 50% off Marvel, Pokémon, Stitch and Sanrio toys at the Jurong venue, with Pokémon toys at $20, Stitch 3D puzzles at $5 and selected Sanrio keychains from $2 — hard-to-beat stocking-stuffer pricing.
  • LovethatBag sale at AMK Hub (till Oct 4): 1,000+ brand-new authentic designer bags from S$50. Not food, but the deal hunters of AMK can pair it with a hawker lunch next door.
⚠️ Condition Check
  • Luckin 1-for-1 and the KFC $6.50 Zinger: app orders only.
  • Starbucks 20% off merch: membership required; applies to merchandise, not drinks.
  • KFC breakfast discount: GrabFood and foodpanda only, Monday to Wednesday; in-store orders don't qualify.
  • Sheng Siong seafood: while stocks last — go early on promo days.
  • Mister Donut free donut: requires buying the 6-piece bundle, not individual pieces.
  • KidZania half-price: purchase on Oct 3 specifically; visit can be any date till Dec 31.

4. Strategy: The Week, Day by Day

  • Monday, Sep 28 (today): Triple play — Luckin 1-for-1 (last day), KFC $6.50 Zinger (app), and the first day of the KFC Mon–Wed breakfast discount if you order breakfast instead.
  • Tuesday, Sep 29: Last day of Sheng Siong's seafood promo — swing by for $0.88/100g fish. Also day two of the Starbucks merch discount.
  • Wednesday, Sep 30: Final day of Snow City, ever. Toys clearance opens at The Chevrons. Order KFC breakfast one last time this week (Mon–Wed window closes).
  • Thursday, Oct 1: McDonald's × G-Dragon launches at 11am. Go early if you want the photocard; popular outlets will run out.
  • Friday, Oct 2: Low-key day. Mister Donut Halloween range is now fully stocked — grab the 6+1 bundle for the weekend.
  • Saturday, Oct 3: KidZania half-price ticket day (buy now, visit later). Royal Caribbean roadshow and toy clearance continue.
  • Sunday, Oct 4: Last day for the NEX roadshow, the AMK Hub bag sale and the toy clearance. Sunday night, the slate resets.

5. What to Skip

Not every promo deserves a detour. The Bank of China 2% time-deposit offer (from Sep 28) is a banking product, not a lifestyle deal — rate shoppers should compare against other banks' promo boards before locking funds. And the adidas x Pokémon 30th Anniversary collection (from Sep 27) is a full-price launch, not a discount — only worth queueing for if you specifically want Pikachu sneakers, not if you're hunting savings.

That's the week: two fried-chicken plays, one K-pop burger drop, a goodbye to snow, and a toy hall in Jurong. Deals come and go — the archive on the homepage updates daily with every fresh promo as it's announced.
