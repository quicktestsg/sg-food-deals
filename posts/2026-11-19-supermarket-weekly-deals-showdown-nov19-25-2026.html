Supermarket Weekly Deals Singapore (19–25 Nov 2026): FairPrice vs Giant vs Sheng Siong vs Cold Storage

All four major Singapore supermarkets switched over to a fresh week of specials on the same morning. Here is what each chain is actually offering, the catches nobody prints on the front page, and how to sequence your shopping trips before Sunday.

Thursday, 19 November 2026 is one of those rare alignment days in the Singapore grocery calendar: FairPrice, Giant, Sheng Siong and Cold Storage have all rolled over to a brand-new week of discounts simultaneously. Giant headlines the biggest number on paper (up to 64% off), FairPrice counters with up to 60% off plus a boosted CHAS discount, Sheng Siong runs a short, sharp 4-day sale, and Cold Storage quietly starts its own weekly specials. Add 7-Eleven's ongoing $1 promotion into the mix and this is arguably the best grocery week of November so far.

The Four-Way Showdown at a Glance

One caveat before the breakdown: the percentages above are each chain's headline "up to" figures, drawn from their published promo listings this week. The deepest cuts always apply to a subset of items, so treat these as the ceiling, not the average.

1. FairPrice Weekly Specials: 19 – 25 Nov, Up to 60% Off

FairPrice's new weekly cycle covers fresh produce, frozen and chilled food, pantry staples, beverages and household products, with savings of up to 60% across the board. That breadth is the point: this is the promo to build a full week's grocery list around rather than cherry-pick.

💡 The CHAS Multiplier Most People Forget

Separately from the weekly specials, FairPrice has increased its CHAS discount to 6% from now until 1 January 2027. If you hold a CHAS card, that discount applies on top of the promo cycle — a quiet stack that compounds every single trip until New Year's Day. Specific item-level prices for this week's specials are listed in FairPrice's own promo pages, so verify individual deals there before heading out.

2. Giant: 19 – 25 Nov, Up to 64% Off — the Biggest Number This Week

Giant takes the headline crown with up to 64% off across its Super Savings weekly deals, exclusive hypermarket specials and a Year-End Savings promotion running through November. The discount pool spans fresh groceries, everyday essentials, snacks and household items. Giant's hypermarket format rewards big single trips — bulk-size packs and household staples are where the deep cuts usually concentrate, so if your plan is a month-end restock rather than a quick top-up, this is the run to make.

3. Sheng Siong 4-Day Sale: 19 – 22 Nov Only — Ends Sunday

Sheng Siong's 4 Days Special Promotion runs Thursday 19 to Sunday 22 November, with savings of up to 50% on groceries, fresh produce, frozen food and household essentials. The defining feature is the window itself: unlike the other three chains running until 25 Nov, Sheng Siong pulls its sale three days early. Anything on your Sheng Siong list has to happen this weekend.

⏰ Urgency Check

Sheng Siong's sale closes at end of business on Sunday, 22 November. Popular fresh items in short 4-day sales tend to sell out on Saturday evenings — if the list includes fresh or frozen picks, shop Friday or Saturday morning rather than Sunday night.

4. Cold Storage Weekly Specials: From 19 Nov, While Stocks Last

Cold Storage and CS Fresh started their latest weekly storewide specials on 19 November. The chain did not publish a fixed end date in this week's listing — offers run while stocks last, so the practical deadline is whenever shelves empty. This is the store for premium and imported items, and it also carries the most interesting standing condition of the four.

🧓 Senior Wednesday: Extra 3% Off

Senior citizens aged 60 and above get an additional 3% off at Cold Storage every Wednesday. This week that lands perfectly: Wednesday, 25 November is also the last day of the FairPrice and Giant cycles — a neat split where seniors do their Cold Storage run on Wednesday morning with the extra 3%, after comparing prices against the other chains' final-day deals.

5. Bonus: 7-Eleven $1 Deals Until 1 Dec

Running alongside the supermarket war, 7-Eleven is in week two of a two-week $1 promotion (18 November – 1 December) covering snacks, chips and drinks. Confirmed items from the listing include Pocari Sweat Isotonic Water 500ml, IF 100% Coconut Water 350ml and selected Pokka products — all at $1 each, while stocks last. For context on how good that is: isotonic drinks at convenience stores normally sit far above $1, though exact regular prices vary by outlet, so judge the saving at the shelf.

⚠️ Condition Check: The Fine Print in One Place

  • Everything is "while stocks last": all four supermarket promos this week carry stock-availability conditions — shop early in the cycle for the best selection.
  • Headline discounts are ceilings: "up to 60/64/50% off" applies to selected items only; plenty of the store runs at smaller discounts.
  • FairPrice CHAS 6%: requires a CHAS card; runs till 1 January 2027 alongside (not instead of) the weekly specials.
  • Cold Storage senior 3%: Wednesdays only, ages 60 and above.
  • 7-Eleven $1 range: applies to selected snacks and drinks only, while stocks last, until 1 December.
  • Item-level prices vary by outlet: the source listings do not publish a single nationwide price sheet — always check the shelf label at your nearest branch.

Strategy: How to Sequence This Week

🗺️ The One-Trip-Per-Store Play

Sheng Siong forces the first move with its Sunday cut-off, so the sequence writes itself: Sheng Siong first (by Saturday), FairPrice and Giant any day through the week as their cycles run to 25 November, and Cold Storage saved for Wednesday 25 November if a senior shopper in the household can claim the extra 3%. Cap each trip with a 7-Eleven $1 drink on the way home — the promotion runs to 1 December, so there is no rush on that one.

  • Thu 19 Nov (today): all four supermarket cycles go live — early birds get full shelves; 7-Eleven $1 deals continue.
  • Fri 21 – Sat 22 Nov: last window for Sheng Siong's 4-day sale (closes end of Sunday 22 Nov); weekend crowds build at all chains.
  • Sun 22 Nov: Sheng Siong sale ends; FairPrice, Giant and Cold Storage specials continue.
  • Wed 25 Nov: final day of FairPrice and Giant weekly cycles — and Cold Storage senior Wednesday (extra 3% for ages 60+). The single most valuable shopping day of the week for senior households.
  • Thu 26 Nov – Mon 1 Dec: FairPrice and Giant roll into their next weekly cycle; 7-Eleven $1 promotion wraps up on 1 December.

The Bottom Line

This week rewards planning more than loyalty. Giant posts the biggest headline number (up to 64% off, 19–25 Nov), FairPrice pairs up to 60% off with a 6% CHAS stack that runs all the way to New Year's, Sheng Siong demands urgency with a 50%-off sale that dies on Sunday, and Cold Storage throws in a senior Wednesday bonus with no published end date. Pick the store that matches the shopping list, respect the Sunday and Wednesday deadlines, and let the $1 isotonic waters at 7-Eleven handle the small stuff.
