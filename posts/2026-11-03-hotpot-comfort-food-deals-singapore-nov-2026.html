Hotpot & Comfort Food Deals Singapore November 2026: Haidilao 50% Off Wagyu, $1 Soups & More

The northeast monsoon is rolling in and every promo calendar suddenly reads hotpot. Here is the full November lineup — three stacked Haidilao promos, a new malatang opening with 50% off, $1 Cantonese soups and the DIY home-hotpot route.

November in Singapore means two things: afternoon thunderstorms and a sudden craving for bubbling soup. The deals have followed the weather. This month you can stack up to three separate Haidilao promotions, try a brand-new malatang concept at half price for the second bowl, drink $1 Cantonese soups at Suntec, or assemble a home hotpot spread starting from $8.80 premium cuts. Every deal below comes with its dates and fine print, because the catches are where these promos usually get you.

2. New Opening: Hi Bowl Malatang, 50% Off Your Second Bowl

Hi Bowl, a modern Chinese malatang concept backed by one of Singapore's leading hotpot brands with 30 years of culinary history, opens at Wilkie Edge on 3 November 2026. Pick your own ingredients, pay by weight or item, and customise the spice level. Opening promotion: 50% off the second malatang bowl. The source announcement does not state an end date or the per-bowl price, so treat this as an opening-week window and verify in store before ordering.

3. The Buffet Lane: Kids Eat Free + a $3.99 Premium Soup Base

For family outings, GoroGoro Steamboat & Korean Buffet at The Centrepoint runs a kids-eat-free special from 1–30 November 2026: one complimentary child buffet for every two paying adults, covering both steamboat and Korean-style barbecue stations. The adult buffet price is not stated in the deal announcement, so check the current rate when booking.

At Shabu Sai, the November premium soup promo (from 1 November 2026, all outlets, end date not announced) puts the Thai Green Curry soup base at $3.99 instead of the usual $4.99, and every purchase of the promo soup comes with a complimentary platter. If your hotpot order lives or dies by the broth, a dollar off plus a free platter is a quietly decent add-on.

4. Soup & Claypot Comfort: The $1 Soup Trick at Suntec

The Soup Expert 尚一汤 at Suntec City turns one year old and is celebrating with $1 soups from 1 November to 31 December 2026. The mechanics: spend $29.90 in a single bill and one selected Cantonese soup is $1; spend $59.80 and two soups drop to $1 each. This is a spend-threshold promo, not a walk-in giveaway — it rewards a proper meal, not a soup-only visit.

Dian Xiao Er's claypot deal is more direct: from 1 November 2026 onwards, Emerald members can get the Claypot Pork Tripe & Pork Rib Pepper Soup at $9.90 nett instead of $15.90 — a $6 saving, no minimum spend, valid for dine-in at all Singapore outlets. The coupon must be purchased as an eCoupon through the Dian Xiao Er app, so download it before you head down.

For a group Chinese dinner, Famous Kitchen on Sembawang Road has a November member promotion (1–30 November 2026): 25% off the à la carte menu from Monday to Thursday, whole Peking Duck at $49, selected dishes at special prices, and free corkage for up to two bottles of hard liquor or three bottles of wine. Membership requirement applies — register first.

  • Sushi Express Black Plate Happy Hour (2–6 Nov 2026): qualifying black plate sushi at $1.80++ each, down from $2.00++, daily 3pm–5pm at Century Square only. Limited to 10 promotional plates per bill.
  • Genki Sushi Thankful Days (3–4 Nov 2026): 20% off for Premium members on 3 Nov and Club members on 4 Nov. App membership required — check your tier in the Genki app before going.
  • SEORAE JIB Christmas Grill BBQ Set (29 Oct–11 Dec 2026): a 650g beef, pork and chicken grill set for 2–3 pax at $128, with 10% off if you reserve during the early-bird window — bringing the set to an estimated $115.20.

6. The DIY Home-Hotpot Route: From $8.80 Premium Cuts

Restaurant deals aside, November is also a strong month to hotpot at home. DON DON DONKI's Black Friday Sale (1–30 November 2026, all stores, while stocks last) includes special deals on A4 Wagyu, Shirobuta pork, Angus beef, wagyu skewers and menchi katsu, with prices starting from $8.80 — the easiest one-stop grab for hotpot-grade meat. Pair it with FairPrice's 2-week promotions (up to 57% off instant noodles, local favourites and household essentials, valid until 11 November 2026) for the carb and drinks side of the table.

For bulk buying, Sigma Food's warehouse at 2 Bukit Batok Street 23 (#01-15) stocks frozen food, seafood and hotpot ingredients, open Monday to Saturday, 9am–6pm (from 28 October 2026, ongoing). Item prices are not published in the deal announcement, so go with a list and compare against your usual supermarket prices before committing.

8. What To Skip

Not everything warm is worth the trip. If you are a solo diner, the $1 soup promo at The Soup Expert only makes sense attached to a $29.90 bill — as a standalone it is not one. The Haidilao anniversary B1G1 is off-peak only, so peak-hour dinner plans should aim at the members' 50% off dishes instead. And the Sigma Food warehouse is a Mon–Sat, Bukit Batok trip — worth it for a big family haul, skippable for a two-person hotpot.
