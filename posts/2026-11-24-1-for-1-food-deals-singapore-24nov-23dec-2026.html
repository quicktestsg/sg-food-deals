1-for-1 Food Deals Singapore (24 Nov – 1 Jan 2027): Korean Sotbap, Festive Buffets, Coffee & Donuts

A brand-new Korean sotbap 1-for-1 at VivoCity, a snow-crab hotel buffet that runs all the way to New Year's Day, and payday coffee deals — this week's buy-one-get-one lineup, sorted by how soon each one expires.

Payday week meets Black Friday week, and Singapore restaurants are responding with one of the denser 1-for-1 lineups we have seen this quarter. A newly opened Korean eatery at VivoCity is running an opening buy-one-get-one, Holiday Inn Atrium has brought back its marathon Christmas buffet 1-for-1 that stretches to 1 January 2027, and the coffee chains are stacked with payday promos. Here is everything worth claiming, organised by how urgently you need to act — with every price, date and catch taken from the source announcements.

1. Act by Wednesday: Kenangan Coffee Payday Buy 1 Free 1 (ends 25 Nov)

The tightest deadline of the week. Kenangan Coffee's app-exclusive Buy 1 Free 1 payday promotion runs 23–25 November 2026 only, with two cups going for S$7.50 through the Kenangan app. Because the deal is claimed in-app, download it and register before you queue at the counter — walk-in orders without the app will not get the second cup.

2. Opening Week Only: Sagye Lite 1-for-1 Sotbap at VivoCity (25–30 Nov)

Sagye Lite, the casual Korean restaurant concept, has opened its second Singapore outlet at VivoCity (near the Shake Shack, a short walk from HarbourFront MRT) and is celebrating with a 1-for-1 Sotbap promotion from 25 to 30 November 2026. Sotbap — Korean stone-pot rice bowls — start from S$12.90, so two diners can realistically eat a proper Korean meal for under S$13 per person during the six-day window. Opening promos like this rarely get extended; treat 30 November as the real deadline.

3. Through Early December: Luckin Coffee Anchorpoint Opening Coupons (until 5 Dec)

Luckin Coffee's new Anchorpoint Shopping Centre outlet opened 22 November, and its welcome coupons run to 5 December 2026: S$3.99 drinks plus 1-for-1 drink deals, claimed through the Luckin app, valid daily 8am–10pm at the new store. If you work or live near Alexandra Road, this is the cheapest caffeine of your fortnight.

4. Through December: KFC 5-Piece Chicken at $10.80 (Mon–Wed, until 23 Dec)

Not technically a 1-for-1, but the best-structured fast-food deal running this month: 5 pieces of fried chicken (Original Recipe or Hot & Crispy) for S$10.80 via Grab self pickup at participating KFC outlets, every Monday to Wednesday until 23 December 2026. The catch is membership tiering — without GrabUnlimited the same order is S$13.50, so the S$2.70 difference is worth checking before you order. That works out to S$2.16 per piece with the subscription.

5. All the Way to New Year: Holiday Inn Atrium 1-for-1 Festive Buffet (23 Nov – 1 Jan 2027)

The longest-running headline deal of the season. Holiday Inn Singapore Atrium's Atrium Restaurant (Level 4) is running a 1-for-1 Christmas and New Year buffet from 23 November 2026 to 1 January 2027, daily for lunch (12pm–2.30pm) and dinner (6pm–9.30pm). From S$120++ for two adults, the spread includes chilled seafood, snow crab legs, grilled lobster, roast turkey and festive specials. Two adults at a hotel festive buffet for S$120++ works out to roughly S$60++ per head — strong value for a snow-crab-and-lobster lineup, though ++ service and tax will add to the final bill. Book ahead for Christmas week itself; the 1-for-1 makes peak slots disappear fast.

6. Student Exclusive: Awfully Chocolate 1-for-1 Gelato at Tampines 1

From 23 November 2026, students showing a valid student ID get two gelatos for the price of one at Awfully Chocolate's Tampines 1 outlet, available daily (Monday to Sunday) while stocks last. The source announcement does not state an end date — treat it as ongoing but potentially withdrawn at any time, and go sooner rather than later if you are in the east.

7. Weekday Staples: Golden Pillow 933 Buns & DUMPLING JIJI Sides

Two weekday-only freebies that pair well with a workday routine. Golden Pillow 933's 1-for-1 bun promotion is back from 23 November: buy one get one free on weekdays with no minimum spend — but you must sign up on Cloft and claim the e-voucher first, and it is self-pickup only at the Enterprise One outlet. Over in Sengkang, DUMPLING JIJI at Sengkang Grand Mall runs weekday Happy Hours from 3pm to 5pm where any dumpling or stir-fry order comes with one free selected side dish — useful for a late-lunch or early-dinner slot. Neither source lists an end date yet (end dates not announced as at 24 November 2026).

8. Donuts and Drinks: Dunkin' Payday Deals

Dunkin' Singapore's payday lineup from 23 November 2026: buy 5 donuts and get 2 free (the two lowest-priced donuts in your selection are the complimentary ones), plus 1-for-1 on Cold Brew and on hot or iced Teh Tarik at participating outlets. No end date has been announced; as with all 'onwards' promos, assume it can end without notice.

9. Bonus Crab Watch: Penang Culture Crab Feast at Compass One

Not a 1-for-1, but the best crab value launching this week. From 23 November 2026, Penang Culture at Compass One runs its Crab Feast Edition: every buffet diner gets a complimentary choice of Chilli Crab or Salted Egg Crab, alongside unlimited mantou, Penang-style dishes and Häagen-Dazs ice cream, with the buffet starting from S$31.90++. At that price with a whole crab thrown in, it undercuts most dedicated crab promotions in the north-east — end date not announced in the source.

Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Tuesday, 24 Nov (today): Download the Kenangan app and register — the Buy 1 Free 1 ends Wednesday. Claim your Luckin Anchorpoint coupons while you are installing apps.
  • Wednesday, 25 Nov: Last day for Kenangan 1-for-1; also day one of Sagye Lite's sotbap deal at VivoCity if you are near HarbourFront.
  • Thursday–Friday, 28–29 Nov: Golden Pillow 933 bun 1-for-1 and DUMPLING JIJI's 3pm–5pm free side dish — both weekday-only, so use them before the weekend.
  • Monday, 30 Nov: Final day of Sagye Lite 1-for-1 — the hardest deadline of the week. First Monday of KFC's $10.80 run (continues weekly to 23 Dec).
  • Anytime before Christmas: Book Holiday Inn Atrium's 1-for-1 buffet for a date that suits you — it runs daily to 1 Jan 2027, but Christmas-week slots will be the first to go.
⚠️ Condition Check — Read Before You Queue

Almost every deal above has a gate: Kenangan and Luckin require their respective apps; Golden Pillow 933 requires a Cloft account and e-voucher, self-pickup only at Enterprise One; KFC's best price requires GrabUnlimited and self pickup; Awfully Chocolate requires a valid student ID and is at Tampines 1 only; Dunkin' and KFC apply at participating outlets only. The Holiday Inn and Penang Culture prices are '++', so budget for service charge and GST on top. Deals marked 'no end date' can be withdrawn without notice.

The Verdict

If you only do two things this week: hit Sagye Lite's opening 1-for-1 at VivoCity before 30 November (sub-S$13-per-head Korean food is rare at a mall like that), and lock in a Holiday Inn Atrium buffet booking — the 23 November to 1 January window is unusually generous for a hotel festive promo. Everything else is opportunistic: grab the coffee apps today, save the weekday bun and dumpling freebies for a workday, and let the KFC Monday-Wednesday run cover your cheap-dinner nights through to 23 December.
