Black Friday Weekend Final 48 Hours: Singapore Food & Drink Deals (28–29 November 2026)

The sales clock is ticking. One deal lives for today only, a dozen die on Sunday night, and the smartest plays for December are already open. Here is the triage.

If you slept through Black Friday itself, good news: the weekend is where the actual value sits. Retailers extended their windows through Sunday 29 November, several food chains launched new promotions today (Saturday 28 November), and a handful of the best December deals quietly opened their doors this week. This guide sorts all of it into three buckets — what must happen today, what must happen before Monday, and what you can calmly schedule into December.

1. Today Only — Saturday 28 November

  • Dunkin' — Buy 1 Get 1 Free bagels (today only, 28 November): Dunkin's Bagel Saturday special gives you a second bagel free with every one purchased, with four varieties served with cream cheese. It is a single-day promotion — miss today and it is gone. Bagel price is not stated in the announcement, so check the counter or app before ordering.
  • LiHO — Royal Black Friday promo starts today (from 28 November, end date not announced): Buy the Plush Card Holder Blind Box for $11.90 and receive a free medium Da Hong Pao Milk Tea plus a free princess-themed thermal bag. Works in-store, through the LiHO TEA App and on delivery platforms. The catch is the $11.90 spend — the free items come attached to the blind box, not to any drink.
  • Giordano Bazaar Sale — AMK Hub B1 (28–29 November only): A two-day pop-up: men's T-shirts from S$9, Disney T-shirts at S$11 (minimum 2 pieces), ladies' T-shirts from S$13.90 and polos from S$14.90. Strictly while the bazaar runs this weekend.
💡 Saturday Morning Route

Dunkin's 1-for-1 bagel is a breakfast move, LiHO's milk tea is the afternoon pick-me-up, and both are cheap add-ons to any mall trip. If you are near Ang Mo Kio, the Giordano bazaar is in the same building as your tea run — one MRT stop strategy, three deals.

2. Last Call — Everything That Dies on Sunday 29 November

This is the bucket that matters most this weekend. Roughly a dozen promotions close their windows tomorrow night. Triage by what you would actually use.

  • Isetan THERMOS Black Friday Sale (26–29 November): Selected THERMOS bottles from just $10 at Isetan Scotts, Level 4. Practical, giftable, and the cheapest confirmed drinkware price of the weekend — while stocks last.
  • BHG SPayLater Sale (26–29 November): Up to 70% off storewide, plus SPayLater-linked vouchers worth up to $100 ($10 off for new users; $20/$35/$60/$100 tiers). BHG members get 2X points and up to 9% cash rebate on 27–29 November. This is the deepest storewide discount of the weekend.
  • OG Prestige Beauty Black Friday (26–29 November): An extra 20% off selected Shiseido, Estée Lauder, Lancôme and Clinique skincare and fragrance sets at OG Albert, OG People's Park and online — a strong Christmas-gift window if you have names left on your list.
  • Crocodile Black Friday (until 29 November): 30% off one regular-priced menswear piece, 40% off two or more, at IMM, Changi City Point, VivoCity and Lot One only.
  • Metro Causeway Point Atrium Sale (until 29 November): Up to 60% off Adidas, Under Armour and Waveline sportswear and everyday essentials, while stocks last.
  • Takashimaya Household & Interior Bargains (27–29 November): Up to 60% off home essentials from Charles Millen, Tefal, Tupperware and more at Talking Hall, B1. Three days only.
  • BEYOND 9 TO 5 (27–29 November): 50% off regular-priced fashion when you buy two or more pieces, at Changi City Point #02-39 and Pasir Ris Mall #02-69.

3. Running Through Monday 30 November and Into Early December

Not everything expires tomorrow. These run through Monday or into the first days of December — no weekend stampede required.

  • Bacha Coffee Black Friday (27–30 November): 20% off sitewide plus a complimentary lion charm with your order. Ideal for Christmas gifts for the coffee people in your life — the charm is a genuinely nice touch for gifting.
  • PlayMade (from 27 November, end date not announced): $9.90 for a medium drink plus an Everyday Tote via an exclusive app voucher, or the tote alone for $6.90. The app is the gate — no voucher, no bundle.
  • Cold Storage Christmas Treats (26 November – 2 December): Festive biscuits, Lindt chocolates and seasonal goodies at participating Cold Storage and CS Fresh outlets. The sensible play: buy Christmas pantry staples now, skip the December price surge.
  • Eu Yan Sang online (26–30 November): $18 off a $180 spend, $30 off $300, or $60 off $500 — applied automatically at checkout and stackable with eligible promo codes. Good timing if the household restocks cordyceps or bird's nest before the festive season.
  • adidas KINEX outlet grand opening (until 30 November): 50% off storewide — sneakers, sportswear, accessories — at the new outlet, #01-37/38/39, KINEX, 11 Tanjong Katong Road.
  • Levi's at OG (until 30 November): 30% off brandwide at OG Albert and OG People's Park.
  • Unity Pharmacy (until 2 December): Buy 1 Get 1 deals and up to 25% off health, beauty and personal care — Kinohimitsu, Senka, Simple, Aveeno, Caltrate and more, from $9.90.
💡 The 48-Hour Shopping Logic

Do the food runs today (Dunkin', LiHO, PlayMade), do the physical retail tomorrow before 6PM (BHG, Isetan, OG — crowds thin out by Sunday evening), and leave the online orders for Sunday night from the sofa: Bacha, Eu Yan Sang, and anything left on your list. The Monday 30 November deals like Bacha's 20% and adidas' half-price outlet are your buffer if the weekend slips away.

4. The December Plays Already Open

  • Springleaf Prata Place — Buy 1 Free 1, all December (1–28 December, Woodlands CC): Four straight weeks of weekly rotating 1-for-1: Murtaburger, Maggi Goreng, selected cheese prata and lamb shank briyani. Dine-in and takeaway both qualify. If you live in the north, this is the best value commitment of the month.
  • Beef & Beyond Tai Seng Beef Fair (5–20 December): Japanese A5 Miyazaki Wagyu from $20, halal-certified beef from $9.90 and lobster at $15, at Tai Seng Sakae Building. Note 'from' pricing — the best cuts will sit above the floor. Still, halal wagyu-adjacent shopping under one roof before Christmas is rare.
  • Kim San Leng — free food photography workshops (1 December, 7 December and 5 January, Ang Mo Kio & Pek Kio): Free sessions teaching food photography angles and techniques, with complimentary food tasting. Only 25 spots per session — sign up early if interested.
  • Chan Brothers Travel — Dream Cruises Black Friday/Cyber Monday (24 November – 8 December): Genting Dream cruise getaways from $150, with complimentary food flask or luggage for UOB cardmembers meeting the minimum spend. A quiet window for a post-Christmas sea escape.

6. What to Skip or Downgrade

Three efficiency notes. First, don't make a dedicated trip for Cat & the Fiddle's pre-opening 1-for-1 baked cheese tart box at Tiong Bahru Plaza — it is a pre-opening special, the source lists no end date, and 'upcoming outlet' promotions have a habit of shifting. Confirm the outlet is actually trading before you go. Second, the UNIQLO Seamless Down Parka deal (from 27 November) carries no published price or end date in the announcement — treat it as 'check the app', not a planned purchase. Third, several of the weekend's biggest percentage numbers (BHG 70%, Metro 60%, Takashimaya 60%) are 'up to' figures on selected items; the floor prices that are actually confirmed — THERMOS at $10, Giordano tees at S$9, wagyu at $20 — are the anchors worth planning around. Percentage theatre is marketing; floor prices are facts.
