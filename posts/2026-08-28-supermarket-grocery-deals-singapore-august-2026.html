Supermarket Deals Singapore This Week (Aug 28–31): FairPrice vs Sheng Siong vs Giant — The Payday Grocery Strategy

Salary is in, and all three major supermarket chains launched promotions on the same days. Here's how to split one shopping trip across Sheng Siong's 4-day housebrand sale, FairPrice's 2-week price drops, and Giant's yuu member specials — without buying stuff you don't need.

Payday week is when Singapore supermarkets go all-in, and the last weekend of August 2026 is a textbook example: Sheng Siong, FairPrice, Giant and even 7-Eleven all have overlapping promotions between August 28 and 31. The catch is that each deal has a different window, a different condition, and a different shelf life. This guide breaks down what's actually running, when it ends, and how to plan a single efficient restock.

The Four Promotions At A Glance

Store Deal Dates Catch

FairPrice Price Drops up to 61% + Buy-1-Get-1 deals Aug 27 – Sep 9, 2026 Longest window — no rush

Notice the pattern: the tightest windows (Sheng Siong, Giant, 7-Eleven) all close by August 30–31, while FairPrice runs until September 9. That alone tells you the priority order for the weekend.

1. Sheng Siong: The 4-Day Housebrand Blitz (Aug 28–31)

Sheng Siong's Housebrand 4 Days Offer runs August 28 to 31, 2026, with discounts of up to 50% on selected housebrand products. The promotion spans everyday groceries, snacks, household and personal care essentials — basically the boring stuff you buy every month anyway. When housebrand staples hit half price, that's the moment to bulk-buy tissue paper, detergent, rice, noodles and pantry fillers, because shelf-stable items don't punish you for stocking up.

Two things to keep in mind: 'up to 50%' means the deepest discount applies to selected items only — plenty of products will sit at 20–30% off. And the window genuinely closes on August 31; this is a 4-day flash format, not a monthly fixture. Specific item prices vary by store, so check the in-store flyer or app for your nearest outlet.

2. FairPrice: The Two-Week Marathon (Aug 27 – Sep 9)

FairPrice's two-week promotion started August 27 and runs to September 9, 2026, covering groceries, snacks, household essentials, personal care, honey and wines. The mechanics are a mix of Price Drop deals (up to 61% off), multi-buy savings and Buy-1-Get-1-Free offers. Because the window is long, this is your flexible baseline: anything you forget during the weekend blitz can still be picked up at FairPrice the following week.

FairPrice is also running a separate, targeted promo at the same time: Xtra Senior Week at FairPrice Xtra hypermarkets, from August 27 to September 2, 2026, with Buy-1-Get-1 and other savings on senior essentials like milk powder and oral care. It's a monthly event, so if you're shopping with or for parents or grandparents, this week is the one to do it.

3. Giant: The Membership Gate (Aug 28–30)

Giant's yuu Members Specials run just three days, August 28 to 30, 2026, with savings of up to 50% across selected fresh fruits, meat and other foods. The headline difference from the other two: every price is gated behind yuu membership. If you already carry a yuu card or have the app, fresh produce and meat at up to half price is the strongest fresh-food play of the weekend — Sheng Siong's blitz is housebrand-focused and FairPrice's strength is packaged goods, so Giant covers the fresh category.

If you're not a yuu member yet, registering is free via the yuu app — but do it before you hit the checkout, not at the register with a full trolley behind you.

4. 7-Eleven: The Snack Gap Filler (Ends Aug 30)

7-Eleven's 4-day specials run until August 30, 2026, headlined by 47% off the Magnum Stickbar, plus discounts on Ferrero Rocher and instant noodles. Convenience stores are never where you do a weekly shop, but a Magnum at close to half price is a legitimate dessert-hack if a 7-Eleven is already on your commute. Treat it as a bonus stop, not a destination.

Strategy: What To Do Each Day This Weekend

  • Friday, Aug 28 (today): All three supermarket promos are live. Best day for Giant fresh buys — weekend crowds haven't arrived and the 3-day yuu window just opened.
  • Saturday, Aug 29: Sheng Siong housebrand run for shelf-stable bulk items (tissue, detergent, pantry staples). Go early to beat the crowd.
  • Sunday, Aug 30: Last day for BOTH the Giant yuu specials AND 7-Eleven's 4-day specials. If you skipped anything fresh, today is the final call.
  • Monday, Aug 31: Final hours of the Sheng Siong housebrand sale. Weekday evening = empty aisles if you can swing it.
  • Tuesday, Sep 1 onwards: Only FairPrice remains (till Sep 9) plus FairPrice Xtra Senior Week (till Sep 2). Catch-up window for anything you missed.
⚠️ Condition Check

Before you head out: Giant's deals require a yuu membership (free via app). FairPrice Xtra Senior Week savings apply to selected senior-essentials products at Xtra hypermarkets, not all FairPrice stores. Sheng Siong's 'up to 50%' covers selected housebrand items only. 7-Eleven specials are item-specific. None of the sources publish a minimum spend, but multi-buy pricing at FairPrice may require buying 2+ units to hit the deal price.

💡 One-Trip Stacking Strategy

If you only have one slot this weekend: do Sheng Siong first for half-price housebrand shelf-stables (the discount you can't get elsewhere), then Giant with your yuu app for fresh produce and meat (the category Sheng Siong's promo doesn't emphasise), and leave packaged-goods gaps to FairPrice any time before Sep 9 — its 61% Price Drops run the longest, so it's the safety net, not the sprint. Grab the discounted Magnum at 7-Eleven on the way home if you pass one before Aug 30.

What To Skip

Payday sales are engineered to make you overspend on the salary high. Three rules: don't bulk-buy fresh items you can't finish this week no matter how good the discount — half price on spoiled meat is 100% waste. Don't buy housebrand versions of things you've never tried just because they're 50% off; test one unit first. And don't treat the 61% FairPrice ceiling as typical — that's the best single price drop, not the average, so still compare unit prices on the shelf.

The Bottom Line

This weekend is a rare alignment: the three major chains' promos overlap almost perfectly from August 28 to 31. Split your list by category — shelf-stables at Sheng Siong (by Aug 31), fresh at Giant with yuu (by Aug 30), everything else at FairPrice (by Sep 9) — and you'll capture the best of each without a single wasted trip.
