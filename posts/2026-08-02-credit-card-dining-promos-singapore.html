Singapore Credit Card Dining Promos Guide 2026

Your DBS, UOB, OCBC or Citi card is secretly a dining discount card. This guide breaks down the best credit card dining promos in Singapore — from 1-for-1 restaurant deals to 8% cashback rates — and shows you how to stack them with app promos for maximum savings.

Most Singaporeans carry at least two credit cards but only use them for dining 30% of the time they could. Every major bank runs rotating dining promotions that can cut your restaurant bill by 15-50%.

DBS / POSB Dining Promos

DBS is Singapore's largest bank, and their dining promo ecosystem is the most extensive.

  • DBS Cardmembers' Privileges: Quarterly 1-for-1 dining deals at featured restaurants. Check the DBS Card Offers page or the PayLah! app under 'Explore' → 'Dining'.
  • DBS Live Fresh Card: 5% cashback on online and contactless spending — includes food delivery and payWave at restaurants.
  • Scoot + DBS Partnership: DBS/POSB cardholders regularly get 20% off Scoot flights and SIA promo codes.
💳 DBS Pro Move

Download the DBS PayLah! app even if you bank elsewhere. Many dining promos are app-exclusive and don't require a DBS credit card — just link any Visa/Mastercard.

UOB Dining Promos

UOB runs some of the most generous dining promotions, especially for mid-range and premium restaurants:

  • UOB Miles / Rewards: Quarterly 'Treats' campaigns featuring 1-for-1 dining at hotel restaurants and premium establishments.
  • UOB One Card: Up to 5% cashback on all spending if you hit S$2,000/month. Dining is included.
  • UOB YOLO Card: 8% cashback on weekend dining and bars — the highest dining cashback rate among Singapore cards.
🔥 UOB YOLO Sweet Spot

The YOLO card's 8% weekend rate is unmatched. A typical S$100 weekend dinner returns S$8 in cashback. Combined with a 1-for-1 Burpple Beyond deal, you're getting a 58% total discount.

OCBC Dining Promos

OCBC has quietly built one of the best dining promo programs, especially for everyday spending:

  • OCBC 365 Card: 6% cashback on weekend dining, 3% on weekdays. No minimum spend — the best 'set and forget' dining card.
  • OCBC Plus: The OCBC app's 'Lifestyle' tab shows current dining promos, rotating monthly with 15-25% off at specific restaurant groups.
  • OCBC FRANK Card: 6% cashback on weekend dining for younger cardholders (under 30).

Citi & Other Bank Promos

Don't overlook these players:

  • Citi Prestige / PremierMiles: Quarterly dining campaigns with 1-for-1 at upscale restaurants.
  • HSBC Revolution: 4 points per dollar on dining (online and contactless).
  • Standard Chartered Unlimited Cashback: Flat 1.5% cashback on everything with no caps.

The Art of Stacking

The real magic happens when you combine credit card promos with other deal platforms.

🧊 The Stack Recipe
  • Layer 1: Use Burpple Beyond or Entertainer for the base 1-for-1 deal.
  • Layer 2: Pay with your highest cashback dining card (YOLO 8%, OCBC 365 6%, or Live Fresh 5%).
  • Layer 3: Check bank apps for restaurant-specific vouchers before paying.
  • Layer 4: During GSS, National Day, or festive seasons, banks layer additional dining cashback.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using the wrong card: Don't pay for dining with a card optimized for petrol or online shopping.
  • Missing minimum spend: UOB One Card requires S$2,000/month for 5% cashback.
  • Forgetting to register promos: Some bank promos require advance registration via the app before you dine.
  • Ignoring cashback caps: OCBC 365 caps at S$80/month per category. Use a second card beyond that point.

Seasonal Dining Promo Calendar

Bank dining promos follow a predictable rhythm. Plan accordingly:

  • January (CNY): Biggest 1-for-1 dining campaigns of the year.
  • May (Mother's Day): Hotel buffet and high tea promos peak.
  • June-August (GSS + National Day): GSS brings dining cashback multipliers and National Day 1-for-1 deals.
  • September-October (F1 + Festive): Premium dining promos tied to Singapore Grand Prix.
  • November-December (Year-end): Christmas set menu promos. Banks clear annual promo budgets.

Quick Reference: Best Card by Scenario

📊 Cheat Sheet
  • Weekend fine dining: UOB YOLO (8% cashback)
  • Weekday lunch: OCBC 365 (3% cashback, no min spend)
  • Food delivery: DBS Live Fresh (5% on contactless/online)
  • 1-for-1 restaurant deals: DBS PayLah! or UOB app quarterly promos
  • High overall spend: UOB One Card (5% on everything above S$2K/mo)
  • Miles chaser: HSBC Revolution (4 pts/dollar dining)

Final Word

The combination of a good dining credit card, a deal app like Burpple Beyond, and awareness of seasonal bank promos can realistically save a regular diner S$1,500-2,000 per year. The key isn't having every card — it's using the right card at the right time.
