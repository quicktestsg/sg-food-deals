The Optimal Shopping Route

For maximum savings, don't try to do everything at one store. Here's a strategy leveraging each supermarket's strengths:

🗺️ The Multi-Store Strategy
  • Stop 1 — Sheng Siong: 2 packs $1 eggs → housebrand 52-69% off → seafood specials
  • Stop 2 — Giant: Hypermarket 50% off (Tampines/IMM) or National Day 61% specials
  • Stop 3 — FairPrice: Consolidate remaining items, aim for $61+ → $6 voucher. CHAS: shop Thursday.
  • Optional — Cold Storage: Free Shiopan (go early). Scarlett for fruit.

Pro Tips for National Day Grocery Shopping

  • Go early on 9 or 10 Aug: Limited-stock deals sell out fast.
  • Register yuu for Giant: Free signup, unlocks extra 37% off.
  • Bring CHAS card: FairPrice 3-4% off (Thursdays) + free BCH bakkwa.
  • Seniors: ask for discounts. Sheng Siong: 4% extra off housebrand.
  • Watch the limits: Eggs 2 packs, Fair items 3 per product, vouchers max 3/receipt.
  • Don't forget MILO Van: Free MILO at Marina Barrage / Bukit Batok on 9 Aug.

The National Day weekend is the best time of year to stock up on groceries. Every chain is competing for your dollar, and the overlap means a strategic shopper can save 30-50% on their monthly grocery bill.
