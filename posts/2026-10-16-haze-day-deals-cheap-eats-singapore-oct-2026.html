Haze Day Deals & Cheap Eats in Singapore (Oct 16–26, 2026): PSI Discounts, $5.80 Meals, 1-for-1 Coffee

When the 24-hour PSI crosses 100, a handful of Singapore cafes quietly switch on discounts — here's the full haze-day playbook, plus every cheap eat worth your money for the next ten days.

Haze season has an unusual silver lining in Singapore: a small group of F&B outlets runs PSI-triggered promotions that only activate when the 24-hour PSI hits 100 or above. Combined with a stacked week of fast-food launches — a new McDonald's burger line, KFC's Buldak comeback, and a 1-for-1 coffee opening promo — here is everything worth eating between now and the end of October, with dates, prices and catches checked.

1. The PSI-Triggered Deals: Discounts That Only Exist on Hazy Days

These are the strangest deals in Singapore right now: the discount activates only when the 24-hour PSI reading reaches 100 or above, so on a clear day you pay full price. Check the NEA PSI reading before you head out.

Outlet Deal Window
La Pizzaiola 20% off all à la carte takeaway (3 outlets) From 9 Oct 2026 (end date not announced)

⚠️ How to Actually Claim These

All three require the 24-hour PSI to be at 100 or above at the time of purchase — the 1-hour reading does not count. More Yogurt restricts the discount to 1pm–5pm, and La Pizzaiola's deal is takeaway-only across its three outlets (Pizza 3 Funghi, Hawaiana, Quattro Formaggi & Honey and pastas are all included). Matchaya's discount covers eligible dine-in items like the Matcha Cloud 9 and rice burgers. If the sky clears, none of these apply — no PSI 100, no discount.

2. Fast Food This Week: Two McDonald's Lines and a Buldak Comeback

McDonald's is running two promotions at once right now, which is unusual — so pick your line carefully.

  • Chilli Lime McSaver™ Meals — from $5.80 (from 15 Oct 2026, end date not announced). Three new burgers pairing zesty lime with mild chilli heat. The cheapest entry point for trying the new flavour line.
  • Bulgogi Feast — $9.95 (12–31 Oct 2026). Beef or Chicken Bulgogi Burger with fries, a drink and a sundae. App-only: you must order through the McDonald's app.
  • KFC Samyang Buldak Carbonara (from 21 Oct 2026, end date not announced). Loaded Fries from $3.95 and a cheesy macaroni bowl set at $5.50 — the spicy-Carbonara mashup returns next Wednesday.
  • Sushiro Fugu & Shirako specials — $4.90++ each (from 13 Oct 2026, limited availability, while stocks last). The Fugu taste-and-compare with ponzu jelly and grated radish, plus a creamy Fugu Shirako wrap. Adventurous plates at kaiten prices.
💡 McDonald's Math

The $9.95 Bulgogi Feast includes fries, drink AND a sundae; the $5.80 McSaver is the bare meal. If you were buying a full combo anyway, Bulgogi Feast is the better per-item value — but only through the app, and only until 31 Oct. The Chilli Lime line has no announced end date, so there's less urgency there.

3. Drinks Under $4: Half-Price Lemon Tea and 1-for-1 Coffee

  • Keming Bing Sat — Jumbo Iced Lemon Tea at $2.45 (from 12 Oct 2026, end date not announced). A flat 50% off the usual $4.90 at Plaza Singapura, Funan and Tampines 1. The best pure drink deal running right now.
  • Luckin Coffee — 1-for-1 drinks (13–26 Oct 2026, SUTD and Limbang Shopping Centre only). New-outlet opening promo; the Luckin app also lists an introductory first cup at $0.99 for new users. Location-gated, so plan around the two malls.
  • Rokeby Protein Milk Smoothies — $3.95 (15–18 Oct 2026 only, four days). Down from the usual $6.30, saving $2.35 per cup. Ends this Sunday — check the original announcement for participating outlets before going.
  • Beutea — 20% off Coffee & Fresh Fruit Tea series on GrabFood (13–31 Oct 2026, selected outlets). Delivery-app discount on two drink series; check GrabFood for eligible beverages.

4. Sweet Treats: Halloween Donuts and a 21st Birthday

  • Mister Donut Halloween — 3 donuts for $8.50 (12 Oct–1 Nov 2026). Mummy, Spidey and Monsta designs; works out to about $2.83 per themed donut.
  • Toast Box 21st Anniversary — 40-cup Kopi/Teh package (from 15 Oct 2026 onwards, Toast Box Funan B1-27, end date not announced). Includes savings and freebies worth more than $20. Package price is only listed in store — check at the counter.
  • Kenangan Coffee — free Moncha plushie with 3 handcrafted drinks (from 12 Oct 2026, while stocks last). Split the order with two colleagues and the plushie is essentially free with your usual coffee run.

5. Stay-Home Feasts: Late-Night Pizza and Free-Delivery Claypots

  • Pizza Hut Late Night Takeaway — $15 (9 Oct–1 Nov 2026, Fridays to Sundays from 9pm). Large Favourite Pizza plus a 1.5L Coca-Cola; selected $10 Regular Pan Pizza add-ons and delivery deals also run during the window. Weekend-only — this is a post-9pm Friday deal, not a weekday lunch.
  • Lo Hey HK Restaurant — free delivery with $100 spend (from 9 Oct 2026, end date not announced). More than 20 claypot dishes from $12.80, including Claypot Black Pepper Angus Beef Slices and Claypot Lemongrass Prawn Balls, plus signature hairy crab roe noodles.
  • HEY KEE HK Seafood — free delivery with $100 spend (from 9 Oct 2026, end date not announced). Hong Kong dai pai dong favourites: Typhoon Shelter stir-fried crab, Temple Street chicken with spicy chips, crab roe rice.
💡 Family Order Strategy

Both HK seafood outlets waive delivery only above $100 — that is a family-size order, not a solo dinner. If you're two people, the Pizza Hut $15 late-night combo (Fri–Sun after 9pm) delivers a full meal for less than a single lobster at the HK places. Save the $100 minimums for a weekend family spread and split the claypots across four people.

6. Quiet Value Picks: $3.80 Yakiniku and an $8 Sushi Kickback

  • Yakiniku Like Sliced Spicy Ika — sets from $3.80 (from 15 Oct 2026, end date not announced, 13 participating outlets). Combination meals with beef, pork and chicken start at $12.80. A rare sub-$4 grilled-seafood entry.
  • Genki Sushi — $8 return voucher with Trust Card (from 12 Oct 2026, end date not announced). Pay with a Trust Card on any purchase, get an $8 voucher for your next visit. If you already bank with Trust, this is free money on sushi you were buying anyway.

7. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Fri, Oct 16 (today): Rokeby $3.95 smoothies and 7-Eleven 4-day specials (up to 64% off, 15–18 Oct) both end Sunday — grab them on the way home. If PSI is above 100, More Yogurt between 1pm–5pm.
  • Sat–Sun, Oct 17–18: Last weekend for Rokeby, 7-Eleven and Giant's yuu member 3-day special (up to 30% off, 16–18 Oct, members only). After 9pm, the Pizza Hut $15 late-night combo kicks in.
  • Mon, Oct 19: Low-urgency day — Keming Bing Sat half-price lemon tea ($2.45) if you're near Plaza Singapura, Funan or Tampines 1.
  • Tue, Oct 20: Last day of More Yogurt's PSI promo window (9–20 Oct) — only if the haze cooperates.
  • Wed, Oct 21: KFC Buldak Carbonara arrives (Loaded Fries from $3.95, $5.50 bowl set). Giant's Super Savings promo (up to 51% off) also ends today.
  • Sun, Oct 26: Last day of Luckin Coffee 1-for-1 at SUTD and Limbang.
  • Sat, Oct 31: Hard deadline day — McDonald's Bulgogi Feast, Beutea GrabFood 20% and DON DON DONKI's Autumn Fair all end.

8. What to Skip

Not every deal earns a trip. If you are not near SUTD or Limbang Shopping Centre, the Luckin 1-for-1 costs more in travel time than it saves — the $0.99 first-cup app offer only applies to new users on top of that. The Toast Box 40-cup package is only worthwhile if you genuinely drink kopi daily and can collect from Funan specifically; casual buyers should just order normally. And the PSI deals are a bonus, never a plan — do not leave the house into bad air for 10% off a rice burger.

Bottom line for the week: the $2.45 jumbo lemon tea and the $5.80 McSaver are the lowest-effort wins, the Bulgogi Feast is the best full-meal value until 31 Oct, and the PSI discounts are a quirky upside if the haze rolls in.
