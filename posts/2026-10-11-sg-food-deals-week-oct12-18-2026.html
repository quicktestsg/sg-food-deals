Singapore Food Deals This Week (Oct 12–18, 2026): $3.90 Ribeye, Free Drinks & Last-Chance 10.10 Steals

The 10.10 sales are winding down, but the week ahead more than makes up for it: a beef fair with ribeye from $3.90, a new truffle chicken menu at Popeyes, 1-for-1 afternoon crepes and a pasta buffet with a cult-favourite guest chef. Here is the day-by-day plan.

With the 10.10 shopping festival mostly wrapped up by Monday night, this week belongs to a different kind of deal: a four-day beef fair in Tai Seng, a truffle chicken launch at Popeyes, free drinks for tea run squads, and a long tail of month-long promotions you can cash in on whenever it suits you. This guide is organised by deadline — what dies tonight, what dies this week, and what runs all month — because the only thing worse than missing a deal is missing one you actually wanted.

1. Today Only (Sunday, Oct 11): Four Deals Expire Tonight

If you are reading this on Sunday, four promotions end today. Plan your afternoon accordingly.

  • Molly Tea Buy 1 Get 1 Free (10am–10pm): The Hillion Mall outlet's opening promo covers six signature drinks — Premium Jasmine Milk Tea, Premium Jasmine Grape Milk Tea, Pistachio Jasmine Coconut and Tongmu Lapsang Milk Tea among them. Two drinks, one payment, today only.
  • MIXUE Tampines lucky draw (min $6 spend): The new Tampines outlet's grand-opening draw runs today only — spend $6 to enter, with Snow King tumblers, blind boxes, Q-Blocks and King Cones as prizes. Singapore 520828 if you are navigating there.
  • Bee Cheng Hiang 10.10 online sale (until 11.59pm): The online-exclusive pricing on bak kwa and snacks ends tonight at midnight. If you have a CNY-top-up mentality in October, this is your window.
  • Dunkin' Sunday BOGO: Every Sunday from Oct 5 onwards, buy one Sunny Egg item and get another free at selected outlets including AMK Hub, Suntec City Mall and Jurong Point. Today counts.

2. Ending This Week: Grab Before Monday–Wednesday Deadlines

Sheng Siong has been running its 10.10 Price Drop Deals from Oct 6 to Oct 12 (the seafood specials ad runs a day later, to Oct 13): Buy 1 Free 1 specials across groceries, drinks and household essentials, selected products from $1, a tier of $10.10 specials, and a free $5 voucher for qualifying shoppers. Running alongside it is the Housebrand Frozen Weekly Special — up to 50% off frozen food including Buy 1 Get 1 Free on selected Tasty Bites items — plus Purchase-with-Purchase deals, same Oct 6–12 window. Monday evening is your last realistic slot.

Coffee Bean 63rd Anniversary: 63% Off Black Forest Mocha Ice Blended — dates vary by source

Here is one where the sources disagree: one listing puts the 63% member discount on the Black Forest Mocha Ice Blended® at Oct 8–12, another says it runs to Oct 21. Either way, it is live now, members-only, and the discounted drink is also paired with a first look at new anniversary merchandise at selected outlets. Giant hotpot stock-up — ends Wednesday, Oct 14

At Giant Tampines Hypermarket, 10% off when you buy any 2 HAIDILAO frozen assorted products, until Oct 14. Modest on paper, but if a home hotpot night is on your calendar, it stacks with Haidilao's own restaurant promos (below) for a full hotpot week.

Also closing this week: Cold Storage's race-week campaign (Oct 14) and the 7-Eleven $4-off-$20 EasyCollect code 4OFF20 (already ended Oct 7 — don't chase it).

3. New This Week: What Launches Oct 12–18

Popeyes Mad Black Truffle Sour Crème Collection — Monday, Oct 12, 10am

Popeyes Singapore launches its limited-time truffle menu Monday morning: truffle-sauce fried chicken, a creamy truffle chicken sandwich, loaded fries and an Apple Fizzy Pop drink. Price not confirmed in the announcement — check the app on launch morning. Recent Popeyes limited-edition launches have drawn queues at lunch, so if you want first-day bragging rights, go before noon.

Singapore Beef Fair at Tai Seng — Tuesday to Saturday, Oct 13–17

The headline number of the week: ribeye steak from $3.90 each at the SAKAE Building, 11am–9pm, while stocks last. The fair also features selected halal-certified beef, premium Japanese and Australian cuts, and seafood at 3 for $10. At that ribeye price, expect end-of-day sellouts — Tuesday and Wednesday are your best odds. Bring a cooler bag.

J65 Pasta Fiesta buffet — from Saturday, Oct 11, runs to Nov 1

J65 at JEN Singapore Tanglin by Shangri-La is running a Pasta Fiesta with guest chef Muhammad Imran, better known as The Pasta Nerd, from Oct 11 to Nov 1. Pricing was not stated in the announcement — check J65's reservation channels directly before committing. Buffets with a named guest chef tend to book out weekends fast; weekday dinners are the value play if the price holds.

VivoCity 20th Anniversary — Monday, Oct 12 to Sunday, Nov 1

VivoCity turns 20 with a birthday bash of activities, exclusive shopping promotions, giveaways and rewards, including $2,000 in prizes. Not strictly a food deal, but the mall's anniversary promos historically include F&B tenants — worth a detour if you are HarbourFront-side this week.

5. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Sunday, Oct 11 (today): Molly Tea BOGO at Hillion Mall before 10pm; MIXUE lucky draw if you are east-side; Dunkin' Sunday BOGO; Bee Cheng Hiang online order before 11.59pm.

  • Tuesday, Oct 13: Beef Fair opens at Tai Seng (SAKAE Building, 11am). Best day for $3.90 ribeye before sellouts. itea Buy 2 Get 1 continues at Ubi — pair it with the beef fair if you are in the east.
  • Wednesday, Oct 14: Last day for Giant's Haidilao frozen 10% and Cold Storage's race-week promos. Weekday Haidilao Meat Feast before 6pm at Jurong Point.

  • Saturday, Oct 17: Beef Fair's final day — expect picked-over stock by afternoon; go early. MUJI's special-price fortnight (incl. Cacao Truffles) continues to Oct 18.
💡 East-Side Stacking Saturday

For one Saturday only (Oct 17), east-siders can chain three deals in a single loop: Beef Fair at Tai Seng when doors open at 11am, itea's last-day-but-one Buy 2 Get 1 at Ubi 302 a few MRT stops away, and an afternoon $3 Old Chang Kee Pop A Corn Tots cone anywhere on the way home. One trip, three promos, under $15 spent if you skip the wagyu.

6. What to Skip

Honesty segment: not everything labelled a deal earns a trip. The SimplyGo Sanrio EZ-Link set is $20 for two cards with no stored value — that is a collectible purchase, not savings. Challenger's OTO massage chair bundle and the various TV/appliance 10.10 tail-ends are big-ticket electronics, not food. And unless you are already a member, signing up mid-promotion just for Neal's Yard or Leonian points rarely pays off within the promo window. Spend your attention where the discount per dollar of effort is highest: the beef fair, the crepe window, and the $3 snack aisle.

That is the week. Prices and dates above are as published by the brands as of Oct 11, 2026 — while-stocks-last applies to nearly everything fair- and festival-shaped, so treat physical-event pricing as directional and confirm in store. See you at the ribeye.
