Cheap Eats & Value Feasts Singapore (Sep 26 – Oct 5, 2026): Kimly $61 Feast, Wingstop 16pc $14.50, G-DRAGON at McDonald's

The payday-weekend overlap keeps paying out: a five-dish zi char feast for $61, 16 boneless wings for $14.50 (today is the last day), extra salmon at Sushi Express for nothing, $9.90 nuggets at McDonald's — and the biggest collab of the year landing October 1. Every price, date and catch below.

Payday weekend, Saturday, and the deals calendar is unusually loaded. This guide covers the cheapest way to feed a group (zi char, $12.20 a head), the delivery-only 1-for-1 that dies tonight, the quiet salmon upsell at Sushi Express, McDonald's staple $9.90 box, a riverside Japanese beer 1-for-1 — plus what is opening next week, from Tokyo's Yamamoto's Hamburg to the G-DRAGON x McDonald's collab on October 1.

1. Ending Tonight — Wingstop 1-for-1 Boneless Wings, 16pc for $14.50 on GrabFood

Wingstop Singapore's 1-for-1 on 8pc Boneless Wings runs only until September 26, 2026 — today. Ordered through GrabFood, the deal works out to 16 boneless wings for $14.50, and the two portions can be different flavours. The catches: it is delivery-only (GrabFood availability), subject to participating outlets, and once the clock passes midnight the promo is gone. At roughly 91¢ a wing, this is the strongest per-piece price on this list, but only for today.

2. Best Group Deal — Kimly $61 Value Feast: 5 Dishes for 5 People

From September 24, 2026 onwards (end date not announced), Kimly Zi Char outlets islandwide are running a Value Feast: five different zi char dishes for $61, total — available for both dine-in and takeaway. That is $12.20 per person for a proper seated dinner, which is hawker-tier pricing without the tray-return logistics. The flexible format is the underrated part: takeaway means a family of five eats at home for $61, and dine-in means nobody cooks on a Saturday night. Zi char feasts scale better than almost any other promo type — this is the anchor deal of the weekend for groups.

3. Quiet Value — Sushi Express Extra Salmon at 争鲜gogo (Till Sep 30)

Until September 30, 2026, Sushi Express's Extra Salmon Treat at 争鲜gogo gets more salmon for the same money: 9 pieces of salmon sashimi for $8.50, or 14 pieces for $13, while stocks last. The per-piece math: the 14pc option works out to about 93¢ a piece versus 94¢ on the 9pc — nearly identical, so pick by appetite, not value. There is no app, membership or promo code mentioned in the source; the simpler plays sometimes have the fewest catches. Confirm the 争鲜gogo counter format (grab-and-go) at your nearest outlet before making a special trip.

4. The Staple — McDonald's 20pc McNuggets for $9.90 (Sep 24–30)

McDonald's is selling 20pc Chicken McNuggets for $9.90 from September 24–30, 2026, after breakfast hours, at restaurants islandwide. Terms worth knowing: delivery orders are excluded (dine-in and takeaway only), it is while stocks last, and standard sauce condiment policies apply — meaning extra sauces may cost more. At just under 50¢ a nugget, this remains the benchmark cheap-protein order of the week, and the timing matters: it ends September 30, the same day the G-DRAGON collab menu is about to dominate attention.

5. Date-Night Split — Watami 1-for-1 Suntory Draft & Jim Beam Highballs (From Sep 24)

From September 24, 2026 onwards, Watami at Mandai Wildlife EAST is offering 1-for-1 on Suntory THE PREMIUM MALT'S Draft Beer, Jim Beam Soda or Jim Beam Cola — with a minimum $40 spend on food and non-alcoholic drinks. It is a limited-time promotion, while stocks last, and the location is specific: Mandai Wildlife EAST, next to the Mandai wildlife parks, not the downtown Watami branches. The play here is a weekend combo: pair it with a River Wonders or Night Safari visit and the $40 food minimum covers itself with a shared meal. Weekday izakaya-goers get the same deal; the source does not restrict it to weekends.

6. Opening Next Week — G-DRAGON x McDonald's (Oct 1) and Yamamoto's Hamburg at Lau Pa Sat (Official Opening Oct 5)

Two launches define next week. First: McDonald's Singapore's collaboration with G-DRAGON and PEACEMINUSONE runs October 1–31, 2026, bringing Korean-inspired burgers — the Spicy Bulgogi Chicken Burger and Bulgogi Beef Burger — plus returning Spicy Chicken McNuggets, Hershey's soft serve, mystery photocards with meals and limited-edition merchandise from $5.90. Launch day is October 1 from 11am. Photocard collectors should expect queues at flagship outlets on day one; the menu runs all month at participating outlets, so there is no need to fight the crowd unless the merch matters.

Second: Yamamoto's Hamburg — the Tokyo Ebisu hamburger steak chain by Shohei Yamamoto (the operator behind Hikiniku To Come) — has quietly started soft-launch operations at Lau Pa Sat since September 22, with the official opening on October 5, 2026. Most mains are priced under $20, which is rare for a hyped Japanese import in the CBD. Soft-launch weeks are historically the best time to go: crowds have not fully arrived and kitchens are warming up. For something sweeter and more central, Mama Shelter Singapore in Orchard has launched a new high tea set at $30++ featuring caviar and chocolate fondue — from late September, ongoing, reservations via the hotel directly.
