Family Food Deals Singapore 5–9 Nov 2026: Free Kids' Ramen, 1-for-1 Korean Fried Chicken & $1 Drinks

The post-Deepavali week is quietly one of the best family eating windows of the season: kids eat free at five Keisuke ramen restaurants, Jinjja Chicken is doing 12 drumettes for $8.90, Mr Bean's Crazy Thursday lands today, and 7-Eleven is practically giving drinks away at $1.

Between the Deepavali long weekend and Singles' Day next week, this stretch of November is usually a dead zone for deals. Not this year. The five days from 5 to 9 November 2026 are stacked with family-friendly promotions — a genuinely free kids' meal, a cross-verified 1-for-1 fried chicken deal, a one-day soy drink happy hour and a convenience-store $1 rack that runs nearly two weeks. Here is the plan, organised by household type.

1. Families with Young Kids: Keisuke's Free Kids' Ramen

The standout family deal of the week. From 4 November 2026 onwards, Keisuke Group Singapore is giving one complimentary Kids' Ramen for every child aged 3–10 with each paying adult, at five participating restaurants — Capitol Singapore, Bugis and three other outlets. For a household with two adults and two children in the age band, that is two full bowls of ramen off the bill, every visit. No end date has been announced yet, so treat it as an open-ended promotion but confirm with the outlet before you go, and note the age limit is strict: 3 to 10 years old.

Strategy note: Keisuke's kids' portions are designed for small appetites, so the deal works best when the adults order their usual ramen and the children fill up on the free bowls rather than ordering separately. Aim for weekday dinners — the group's outlets in town get busy on weekends and the promotion applies all day at participating stores.

2. The Chicken Play: Jinjja Chicken 1-for-1 and Popeyes' $7.95 Combo

Jinjja Chicken — 12 Drumettes for $8.90 (7–9 Nov 2026)

This deal was announced by two independent deal sources with identical numbers, which is always a good sign. From 7 to 9 November 2026, Jinjja Chicken's 6-piece drumettes go 1-for-1: pay $8.90, receive 12 pieces of Korean-style fried chicken drumettes. That works out to roughly 74 cents per piece. The catches, and they matter: limit of three sets per person, available while stocks last and subject to daily quotas at participating outlets — this is the kind of promotion that dies by mid-evening on Saturday. Go Friday (7 Nov) or early on the weekend if you want it.

Popeyes — 2PC Chicken Combo at $7.95 (2-day flash deal)

Popeyes Singapore is running a flash deal on its 2-piece chicken combo meal at $7.95, saving $3.15 off the usual price, for two days only with counter redemption — meaning you have to order at the counter, not through the app or a delivery platform. The deal was announced on 4 November 2026; the announcement did not spell out which two dates apply, so check Popeyes Singapore's official channels today before making a trip. If the timing works, a $7.95 two-piece combo is one of the cheaper proper fast-food meals this week.

3. Drinks Day-Plan: Mr Bean Today, CHAGEE Tomorrow, Luckin's Cat

  • Mr Bean Crazy Thursday — today, 5 Nov 2026: 50% off the second soy drink at participating outlets, one day only. The discount applies to the lower-priced drink of the pair, and exclusions apply, so check which items qualify before ordering. The move: pair a pricier signature with a cheaper classic and let the discount bite on the bigger number.
  • CHAGEE 9th Anniversary — from 6 Nov 2026, 9AM: Four exclusive bundles pairing selected large milk teas with anniversary merchandise — a printed glass goblet, sticky screen cleaner, everyday carryall or plush hand towel — starting from $10.90. Anniversary merch bundles at CHAGEE have a record of selling through quickly; if you want a specific bundle, the 9AM launch window on Friday is the safest bet. End date not announced.
  • Luckin Coffee x GARFIELD Travel Buddies — from 5 Nov 2026: $15.80 gets you two selected drinks plus one limited-edition GARFIELD plush pouch, ordered through the Luckin app. Available while stocks last — collectible collabs at Luckin are usually app-only and gone within days, so if the orange cat calls to you, do not sit on it.

4. The 7-Eleven $1 Run (4–17 Nov 2026)

The longest-running deal of the week and the easiest to execute. Until 17 November 2026, participating 7-Eleven stores have a $1 rack featuring Milo, Lexus, Mentos and more, $1.20 selected beverages, and buy-one-get-one-free Cadbury chocolates. Stacked on top: POKKA Passion Fruit Tea Zero Sugar bottles from just $1 from 4 to 17 November 2026 at participating outlets. Terms apply and participation varies by store.

Separately, 7-Eleven is running a storewide 20% off promotion from 5 to 7 November 2026, in-store and via foodpanda Shops at selected heartland stores. One caution from cross-checking sources: the deal announcement describes it as storewide, but at least one source notes a minimum $20 spend at selected stores — so confirm the threshold at the counter before loading up the basket.

5. Grocery Corner: The 5–11 Nov Supermarket Window

All three major supermarket chains reset their weekly specials on 5 November 2026, which makes this the best single week to comparison-shop groceries in Singapore:

  • FairPrice (5–11 Nov): Weekly storewide specials up to 65% off, spanning fresh produce, groceries and household items.
  • Giant (5–11 Nov): Supermarket and hypermarket promotions up to 71% off on fresh food, groceries and household goods.
  • Cold Storage (from 5 Nov): Weekly and two-week specials up to 60% off starting 5 November 2026.

The headline percentages are ceilings on selected items, not storewide discounts — but with all three flyers resetting on the same day, the practical play for a family grocery run is to check each chain's weekly list online, buy your proteins at whichever chain discounts them this week, and let the $1 7-Eleven rack handle the snack category entirely.

6. The Five-Day Plan

  • Thursday 5 Nov (today): Mr Bean Crazy Thursday for the second-drink discount. First day of 7-Eleven's 20% off window and the Luckin GARFIELD set. FairPrice, Giant and Cold Storage weekly specials reset today — do the grocery list tonight.
  • Friday 6 Nov: CHAGEE anniversary bundles launch 9AM. Keisuke free kids' ramen for a family ramen night. Last full day of 7-Eleven 20% off.
  • Saturday 7 Nov: Jinjja Chicken 1-for-1 opens — go early, daily quotas apply. Popeyes counter deal if its two-day window is live (verify first). Last day of 7-Eleven 20% off.
  • Sunday 8 Nov: Jinjja day two, plus a $1 7-Eleven snack run. Supermarket specials continue through 11 Nov.
  • Monday 9 Nov: Final day of Jinjja's 1-for-1 — evening quota risk is highest today. The $1 7-Eleven and POKKA $1 tea keep running to 17 Nov, so no panic needed there.

The verdict: if you do only two things this week, make them Keisuke's free kids' ramen (best family value, open-ended) and Jinjja's 12-for-$8.90 drumettes (best hard-deadline value, 7–9 Nov). Everything else is a bonus layered on top — and unlike next week's Singles' Day chaos, none of these require camping a website at midnight.
