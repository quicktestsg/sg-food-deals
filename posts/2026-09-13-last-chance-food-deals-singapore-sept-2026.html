Last-Chance Food Deals Singapore (Sep 13–14, 2026): Freebies Ending This Weekend + Mid-Autumn Deals Ahead

The September school-holiday promo wave peaks and ends this weekend. Here's exactly what expires today, what dies on Monday, and the Mid-Autumn deals worth planning your week around.

Every promo cycle has a cliff edge, and this one falls on Sunday, 13 September 2026. The 9.9 sale aftershocks, the school-holiday freebies and the first wave of weekend-only supermarket specials all wrap up today or tomorrow. Miss the window and the next big cluster lands with Mid-Autumn Festival (25 Sep) and the October collaborations. Here is the triage, organised by how urgent each deal is.

  • Starbucks 25% off drinks, food & selected merchandise — running 7–13 September 2026. The catch: you must buy at least two eligible items in a single transaction and present the promotional image in-store. Pair a drink with a pastry and the discount applies to both.
  • KFC Curry Twister Value Meal at $5.50 (usual price $6.80 per the source promo) — breakfast-window deal running 7–13 September 2026 at KFC Singapore.
  • Giant yuu Member Deals, up to 50% off — 3-day exclusive at participating Giant stores, 11–13 September 2026. Requires a yuu membership (free to register via the app).
  • Giant Hypermarket Exclusives, up to 46% off — a separate 2-day weekend promo at Tampines Hypermarket and IMM only (12–13 September 2026), covering fresh food, groceries and household items.
  • Zespri free kiwifruit at Lau Pa Sat — scan the QR code at the pop-up to redeem a free 2-piece pack of Zespri kiwifruit, plus weekday free samples and a sure-win spin for KiwiBrother merchandise. Runs 7–13 September 2026, while stocks last.

  • 7-Eleven 4-day limited-time specials — in-store and via the 7-Eleven App, until 13 September 2026, while stocks last.

6. What to Skip

Not every expiring deal deserves a detour. The Jollibee lucky draw requires buying a full value meal for a chance (not a certainty) at prizes — fine if you were eating fried chicken anyway, skippable otherwise. The 7-Eleven app specials demand app-clipping for modest savings on unspecified items. And the KFC $5.50 Curry Twister only makes sense if you're already near an outlet during the breakfast window — a special trip erases the savings. Free-of-conditions deals (McDonald's cone, Zespri kiwifruit) always outrank lottery-style ones.

Bottom line: today is the day to move. Free cone, discounted latte, half-price groceries — then pivot to the long Mid-Autumn runway through 25 September, and leave the Sentosa 1-for-1 for a less crowded weekend before 30 November. Prices and dates above are as published by the sources on 11–12 September 2026; always confirm in-store or in-app before making a special trip.
