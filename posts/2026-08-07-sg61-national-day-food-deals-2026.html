  • 12:00PM: McDonald's for the free curry sauce — grab lunch there
  • 2:00PM: Chateraise to stock up on ice cream (8 for $12, or $9 with cooler bag)
  • 3:00PM: No.17 Tea for 2 fruit teas at $6.10 to cool down

Total spend for the entire day, assuming you do everything above: roughly $40-50 for two people, including ice cream, drinks, snacks, and a full grocery run. That's the power of National Day deals when you plan it right.

  • KOI Merlion Plush Bundle ($26.90): You're paying for a plush toy plus vouchers. Fun for collectors, not a food deal. Skip unless you specifically want the plush.
  • AltitudeX Free Indoor Skydiving: Only for seniors aged 61+ on selected weekends. Great if you qualify, but not relevant for most readers.
  • BEST Denki Samsung TV deal: It's $261 off a TV that costs thousands. The percentage discount is tiny. Skip unless you were already buying a TV.

The best National Day deals are the ones with a symbolic $0.61 or $6.10 price tag, the freebies, and the grocery bundles. Focus your energy there.
