Budget Steak & Japanese Food Deals Singapore September 2026: $1.88 Wagyu, 1-for-1 Teppan & $5 Beer

A hotel teppan opening with 1-for-1 steak, Wagyu at $1.88++ a plate, and a $22.90++ all-day student buffet — this week's meat-heavy deals, with every price, date and catch laid out.

September has quietly turned into one of the best months of the year for meat lovers in Singapore. A Hong Kong teppan steak brand just opened inside The Capitol Kempinski with an opening-week 1-for-1, a Thai hot pot chain is slinging Wagyu at $1.88++ a plate, and the school-holiday student promos are stacking across Japanese buffet chains. This guide breaks down every deal worth your money this week — sorted by how fast you need to move.

1. Ending This Weekend — Move Fast

Bari Bari Grand at The Capitol Kempinski — 1-for-1 teppan & steak (ends 5 Sep)

Bari Bari Grand, the elevated concept from Hong Kong teppan steak brand Bari Bari Steak, opened at The Capitol Kempinski on 1 September 2026 with a grand opening promotion running only until 5 September. For these five days you get 1-for-1 steak and teppan deals, 30% off Miyazaki Wagyu Zabuton, and complimentary free-flow access to the Gourmet Bar. Hotel-district teppanyaki at 1-for-1 is rare enough; at a Kempinski address it is almost unheard of. If you only act on one deal from this guide, make it this one — tomorrow or Saturday are your last realistic slots.

⏰ Urgency Check

Bari Bari Grand's opening promo ends Saturday 5 September 2026. The restaurant continues after that — the 1-for-1 and the 30% Wagyu discount do not (no end-date extension has been announced).

2. The Japanese Line-Up: Wagyu, Hot Pot & Oysters

Suki-Suki Thai Hot Pot — $1.88++ Wagyu AU Beef per plate (1–30 Sep)

All September, Suki-Suki Thai Hot Pot is selling Wagyu AU Beef at $1.88++ per plate. The catch: it is only available to Regular and Premium buffet diners, capped at three plates per guest, at selected outlets. So this is a buffet sweetener rather than a standalone cheap Wagyu fix — you pay the buffet price first, then top up your Wagyu quota at pocket-change pricing. Still, three plates of Wagyu for under $6.50++ on top of an already-all-you-can-eat hot pot is the single most aggressive protein add-on this month.

Suki-Ya — all-day student buffet at $22.90++ (3–30 Sep)

Suki-Ya's September Holiday Student Special runs 3–30 September at every outlet islandwide, including weekends: an all-day all-you-can-eat hot pot and sukiyaki buffet at $22.90++ per person. The condition that trips people up: a minimum of four students is required, so this is a gang deal, not a solo one. Round up three coursemates and the per-head maths beats almost every à la carte hot pot in town.

Tenkaichi Japanese BBQ — freshly shucked oysters from $2.50 each (from 31 Aug)

From 31 August 2026 onwards, Tenkaichi Japanese BBQ Restaurant is offering freshly shucked oysters at 6 for $15++ or 12 for $30++ — working out to $2.50++ per oyster — as an add-on to your yakiniku meal. The source lists it as a 1-for-1-style seafood promotion; either way, $30++ for a dozen shucked-to-order oysters alongside Japanese BBQ is fair value. No end date has been announced yet.

Pepper Lunch — Cheesy Omelette from $16.90 + a 16,000-prize lucky draw (1 Sep–2 Nov)

Pepper Lunch has brought back its popular Cheesy Omelette line from $16.90, available 1 September to 2 November 2026. The interesting part starts 4 September: a Sizzle. Tear. WIN! mechanic with 16,000 prizes up for grabs, including free meals, 1-for-1 and 2-for-1 Cheesy Omelettes, and sides. A 2-for-1 win would cut the effective price of two omelette plates to roughly a third — worth playing if you were going anyway.

Ichiban Boshi — $10 return voucher with DBS/POSB on the Autumn Menu (from 3 Sep)

From 3 September 2026, order any item from Ichiban Boshi's Autumn Menu and pay with an eligible DBS or POSB card, and you receive a $10 return voucher for a future visit. Read the mechanics carefully: the voucher comes back to you, not off the current bill — this is a come-back-again deal, ideal if you already lunch near one of their malls. No end date announced yet.

3. The Steak & Western Corner

Morganfield's @ Suntec City — four promos running at once (from 1 Sep)

Morganfield's at Suntec City is quietly running a full stack of promos right now, each with its own start date:

  • <strong>Weekday Chicken Lunch Deal (from 1 Sep):</strong> $9.90++ for a chicken lunch with fries and slaw; add soup of the day plus a drink for $3++ more.
  • <strong>Weekend AYCE Ribs Buffet (from 1 Sep):</strong> every Saturday and Sunday, unlimited Spare Ribs and Baby Back Ribs for $49 per adult and $25 per child under 12, including soup, sides and an ice cream sundae.
  • <strong>Kids Eat Free (from 3 Sep):</strong> on weekends, public holidays and school holidays, when an adult orders a main, a child aged 7 and below gets a qualifying meal free.
  • <strong>$5++ San Miguel pints (from 31 Aug):</strong> San Miguel Pale Pilsen at $5++ per pint — the cheapest beer anchor in the Suntec area right now.

The play here is obvious: weekend + kids + ribs. One adult main unlocks a free kid's meal, the $49 buffet needs no stacking, and dad's pints stay at $5++. Note the stacking logic is per-promo — don't assume the lunch deal price applies on weekends.

Lucine by LUNA — 1-for-1 all mains, midweek only (2 Sep–23 Oct)

Lucine by LUNA at TripleOne Somerset has brought back its 1-for-1 on all mains, running 2 September to 23 October 2026 — but only Wednesday to Friday, 5pm to 10pm. A seven-week window makes this the most relaxed booking on the list; treat it as your default Wednesday dinner plan through October rather than a rush job.

4. Weekend Splurges That Actually Save

Peach Garden — Weekend High Tea Buffet 1-for-1 (from 3 Sep)

From 3 September 2026, Peach Garden's Weekend High Tea Buffet goes 1-for-1 in the most generous configuration possible: pay for two adults, four people dine. Adults are $46++ and children $18++, with a minimum of four diners per table — which conveniently is exactly what the promo requires. Available at OCBC Centre and Thomson Plaza; no end date announced yet, but weekend high tea slots at these two outlets historically book out, so reserve early.

Kai Garden — free whole Peking Duck with $288++ dinner (from 1 Sep)

Kai Garden's current promotion, from 1 September 2026 onwards, gives a complimentary Signature Whole Peking Duck (worth $128) when your dinner table hits $288++ in spend. There is also a $29.90++ seven-course Dim Sum & Abalone lunch set on weekdays for a completely different budget tier. The duck is a threshold reward — only worth it if your group was going to spend close to $288++ anyway.

When a source did not state an end date, this guide says so explicitly — confirm in-store before travelling.

And Pepper Lunch's lucky draw should be treated as a bonus, never a reason: the base price is $16.90, and 16,000 prizes across an islandwide chain is a lottery, not a discount.
