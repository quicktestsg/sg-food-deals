Trust is turning four, and it is celebrating with a week of $3 food offers across Burger King, Kopitiam, Starbucks and more — no minimum spend. Here is the full $3 menu, the catches to know, and the 1-for-1 deals worth stacking on top this week.

This guide puts the whole week in one place — what is worth it, what needs the Trust app, and exactly when each deal dies.

Two banks, one Saturday, three layers of savings.

  • Trust deals require the Trust ecosystem: the Burger King $3 Spicy Chicken King must be redeemed via the Trust App and paid with a Trust card. The Kopitiam and Springleaf redemptions also run through the Trust app. No Trust account, no $3.

  • Cheese tart 1-for-1 is outlet-specific: only Jurong Point, Parkway Parade (Sep 2) and Bugis (Sep 3), from 12pm, selected outlets only — do not expect it islandwide.
  • Udders limits redemptions: max two 1-for-1 redemptions per customer, free scoop must be equal or lower value, while stocks last.
  • DBS Saturday cashback is a separate promotion: for DBS/POSB customers paying via PayLah! at participating heartland merchants (29 Aug–19 Dec 2026), subject to eligibility and the campaign's cashback pool.

5. The Verdict

A flat $3 with no minimum spend is about as clean as bank-sponsored food deals get — the main cost is the app download and the discipline to check which item is live each day. The real winners this week are people near Jurong Point, Parkway Parade or Bugis on Wednesday and Thursday (cheese tarts), and anyone in the South Buona Vista area over the weekend (Udders). Everyone else can happily ride the $3 menu through Monday and let the 1-for-1s come to them. Next week the birthday confetti settles, but September still has the month-long DON DON DONKI meat deals and Sheng Siong Japan & Korea Fair running through 30 September — so the budget eating does not stop when the candles go out.
