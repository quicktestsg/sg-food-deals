Children's Day 2026 Singapore: Free Ice Cream, Kids Eat Free & the Best Family Deals

Children's Day falls on Friday, October 2, 2026 — and brands across Singapore are giving things away. Free ice cream at IKEA, free pretzels every October weekend, free zoo entry for kids and half-price KidZania tickets.

Children's Day in Singapore is quietly one of the best deal-hunting days of the year. Restaurants and attractions roll out kids-eat-free offers, and because the holiday lands on a Friday this year, the promos stretch across a long family weekend.

1. The Free Stuff

  • IKEA — free Oat Vanilla ice cream for kids, October 2 only. A one-day Children's Day giveaway. Treat it as walk-in while stocks last.
  • Auntie Anne's — free 3pc Pretzel Stix set for kids, every Saturday and Sunday in October (3–31 Oct), 12pm–5pm. One free set per child with each paying adult's pretzel purchase, all outlets islandwide.
  • Singapore Zoo & Rainforest Wild — kids enter free, book by October 4. One free child admission with every paying adult — but the booking deadline is October 4.
  • Takashimaya SG-Japan Friendship 60th anniversary, September 29 – October 11. Complimentary ice cream plus a Digital Stamp Rally earning a S$10 voucher with no minimum spend.
  • Beard Papa's — Buy 1 Free 1 Custard Pie Cream Puffs, until September 30 (today). Grand opening promo at the new Bugis Junction outlet (#B1-K05A).
⚠️ Read the Fine Print

Kids-eat-free promos almost always require an adult purchase, and 'child' usually means an age cap — commonly 12 and under, though each brand sets its own limit.

2. Cheap Family Mains (S$8.80 and Under)

  • Saizeriya — new menu from S$6.90 (start date not announced as a fixed window; ongoing while the menu lasts). Gnocchi at S$6.90, seafood fritters and more, all nett prices.
  • Penang Culture — four new dishes at S$8.80++ each, October 1–31. Sesame Chicken Drumlet, Blackcurrant Fish Fillet, Chicken Chop with Green Belado and Satay Chicken Stew.
  • GoroGoro Steamboat & Korean Buffet — 2+2 buffet, October 1–31, The Centrepoint. Four diners pay for two, at selected timeslots with reservations required.
  • Mister Donut — Halloween treats at S$3.20, September 28 – November 1. Spidey, Mummy and Monsta designs, with Buy 6 Get 1 Free through the same window.

3. Discounted Days Out

  • KidZania Singapore — 50% off all tickets, October 3 only, code KIDZANIA50. Tickets stay valid for visits until December 31, 2026.
  • Museum of Ice Cream — up to 35% off for F1 ticketholders, October 2–11. Requires a valid Singapore GP ticket; admission includes unlimited ice cream.
  • SUPERPARK — 2 off-peak day passes for S$58.80 (S$9 off), until October 4. Weekday play only — which covers the Children's Day holiday itself.

4. Worth Planning Ahead

  • Molly Tea at Hillion Mall — opening October 9. Third outlet, first in the west, launching with 1-for-1 drinks and free tumblers. No end date announced.
  • Genki Sushi — 7th Members' Anniversary e-stamp card, September 28 – November 22. One stamp per S$15 nett spent unlocks 15% off, free salmon sushi and vouchers.
  • Pizza Hut 10.10 online deals — October. S$10 pizza specials, online-only; lineup may rotate, so check the app.

The Game Plan

The optimal sequence: IKEA free ice cream on October 2, the S$10 Takashimasa voucher while the anniversary fair is on, a zoo booking anchored before the October 4 deadline, Auntie Anne's for every remaining October weekend, and KidZania tickets bought on October 3.

One deal ends today: Beard Papa's buy-1-free-1 at Bugis Junction. Everything else gives you at least through the weekend.
