Black Friday & Payday Food Deals Singapore (25 Nov – 1 Dec 2026): $3 Coffee, $10 Combos, 1-for-1 Matcha

Payday landed, Black Friday is coming, and Singapore's cafes and restaurants are running their deepest promos of the season. Here is the fact-checked week-ahead playbook: what to grab today, what to save for the weekend, and what to skip.

This is the rare week when two savings events stack: payday promos launched 23–24 November, and the actual Black Friday weekend runs 27–30 November. That means the window 25 November – 1 December is dense with overlapping deals — including a one-day-only egg tart special that ends tonight and a hotpot buffet launch with a free upgrade that expires on 1 December. Every price and date below is taken from the official promotion announcements; where a price was not published, that is stated explicitly.

1. Ending in the Next 48 Hours — Act Today

  • Tai Cheong Bakery — 4 egg tarts for $8 (25 November only): The Hong Kong-style bakery's one-day mid-week special runs today only. Four buttery egg tarts for a flat $8 — no app, no membership, just walk in. If you are near an outlet this afternoon, this is the single most time-sensitive deal of the week.
  • Kenangan Coffee — Buy 1 Get 1 Free (last day today, 23–25 November): The payday promotion ends today. Buy one selected regular coffee drink and get the second regular cup free — but only when ordered through the Kenangan Coffee app, at 11 participating locations.
  • 4Fingers — 3 meal combos at $10 each (23–26 November): The 'Good Deals 4 U' promotion lets you pick from three selected meal combos at $10 each. One more day after today — Thursday 26 November is the final day.
  • KFC Chicken Wafflewich breakfast return (from 25 November): The sweet-savoury waffle-chicken breakfast item is back on the menu from today. Price not confirmed in the announcement — check the KFC app or in store before ordering.
  • The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf — new weekday breakfast set (from 25 November): The new Cranberry Walnut Sourdough set comes with mini chicken sausages, scrambled eggs, salad and a hot or iced Americano. Price not confirmed — check in store. Weekdays only.
💡 Today's Highest-Value Move

Combine the Tai Cheong $8 egg tart box and the Coffee Bean weekday breakfast set into one morning walk — both start today, and the egg tarts end tonight. Kenangan's 1-for-1 is the afternoon pick-me-up: order through the app, not at the counter, or the free cup does not apply.

2. Running Through the Black Friday Weekend (26–30 November)

The real Black Friday weekend starts Thursday. Two standouts are timed almost perfectly for it.

  • BBQ Box Bugis — grand reopening, up to 40% off (26–29 November): The Bugis outlet celebrates its reopening with a sliding discount: 40% off grilled skewers, hotpot and grilled fish on 26 November, stepping down to 30%, 20% and 10% on the following days. There is a lion dance performance at 1PM on reopening day. The math is simple — go Thursday, not the weekend.
  • TOMORO COFFEE — payday 1-for-1 coffee & matcha (24–30 November): Two drinks for the price of one across 10 listed outlets, including NUS, NUH, Novena Square 2, PLQ 3, Sengkang Grand Mall and Woodlands. This one comfortably covers the Black Friday weekend — ideal for a matcha run on Saturday.
  • Beauty in The Pot — new $23.90 hotpot buffet at The Star Vista (opens 25 November, free one-tier upgrade until 1 December): The famous hotpot brand's first-ever buffet concept opens today exclusively at The Star Vista, with prices starting from $23.90, over 100 hotpot ingredients, eight signature broths, international dishes, dim sum and more than 80 pastries and desserts. During 25 November – 1 December, diners get a free one-tier buffet upgrade. Early reviews of the buffet have already landed — worth reading before booking.
  • KFC — 5 pieces of fried chicken for $10.80 (16 November – 23 December, Monday to Wednesday only): With the eligible 20% GrabUnlimited self-pickup voucher, five pieces cost $10.80; without it, $13.50. Participating outlets only. Note the day restriction: this is a Mon–Wed deal, so this week's last window is Wednesday 25 November — Black Friday weekend itself does not qualify.
  • Poke Theory — 20% off all acai bowls, 3PM–5PM (23–29 November): For Poke Club members, every acai bowl is 20% off during the 3–5PM window daily. Membership sign-up is the gate — non-members pay full price.
  • Nespresso — Black Friday machine sale (27–30 November): For the home-coffee crowd: selected coffee machines up to 40% off from $199, accessories up to 50% off, plus a limited-edition Swarovski crystal ornament for early buyers. Online and at boutiques.

3. Running Into December — The Long-Date Plays

Not everything needs to happen this week. These deals run into December, so schedule them around the festive crowds instead of fighting them.

  • Baskin-Robbins — $3 coffee, +$1 float upgrade (20 November – 31 December): Coffee for $3 at participating outlets, or add $1 for a Coffee Float. One catch: Jurong Point is excluded. This is the cheapest confirmed caffeine price of the entire period and it runs all the way to New Year's Eve.
  • Daily Chicken — $36 Christmas feast (24 November – 31 December): Half a chicken in your choice of four flavours, Nachos Cheese Fries, Hangang Ramyeon and two soft drinks for $36 (usual price $46). Available at Bugis Junction and Waterway Point only.
  • A-One Claypot House — $8 off first online order (from 24 November): New customers get $8 off their first eligible online order with promo code WELCOME8 — the cheapest route to claypot rice and porridge delivery this season.
  • GoroGoro Steamboat & Korean Buffet — 2+1 early bird (1–3 December & 15–17 December): The Christmas 2+1 early-bird runs in two three-day windows at Tampines 1 and The Centrepoint, with lunch from 11.30AM and dinner from 5.30PM. If the Beauty in The Pot opening-week crowds are too much, this is the December hotpot alternative — just note the buffet price tier is set by the outlet's regular rates.
  • Barcook Bakery — 20% off Christmas log cakes (pre-order 23 November – 18 December): All Christmas log cakes, including the Strawberry Star Log and Classic Chocolate Yule Log, are 20% off, with Panettone at 10% off. Collection by 27 December. The early-bird window closes 18 December — after that, full price.
  • Chao K Party — December karaoke from $5++/pp/hour: From 24 November onwards, the Bugis karaoke spot's December special offers rooms from $5++ per person per hour with free-flow drinks, complimentary snacks when you sing for three hours, and free birthday room decorations. Weekdays 12PM–6PM only.

5. What to Skip or Downgrade

A few angles to keep the week efficient. First, BBQ Box's reopening discount decays every day — Sunday's 10% off is barely a promo, so if you are going at all, go Thursday. Second, the KFC Wafflewich and Coffee Bean breakfast set both launched without published prices, so treat them as 'try if you are passing by' rather than planned trips. Third, if you only care about coffee, the confirmed floor price this week is Baskin-Robbins at $3 — with the 1-for-1s stacked on top (TOMORO to 30 November), you can realistically keep caffeine spend under $2 per cup all week. Finally, the Peperoni Pizzeria festive mussels bundle ($96++ for 500g mussels, tortellini, fries and a bottle of Pinot Grigio, 1–31 December) is a share-with-the-table play, not a solo deal — schedule it for a group dinner instead.
