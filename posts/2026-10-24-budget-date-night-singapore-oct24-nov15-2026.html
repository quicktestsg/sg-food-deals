Budget Date Night Singapore 2026: The Best Cheap Dining Deals for Couples (Oct 24 – Nov 15)

Half-price soufflé pancakes, a $5.50 macaroni bowl, 4th-diner-free buffets for double dates, and a S$12 wagyu steak next month — the full cheap date playbook.

Date night in Singapore looks expensive, but this fortnight is unusually generous. This guide covers 24 Oct – 15 Nov, sorted by the kind of date you're planning.

☕ The Café Date: Hoshino Coffee, Half-Price Soufflé Pancakes

Hoshino Coffee Plaza Singapura — 50% off Signature Soufflé Pancakes with any main or drink (21 Oct – 1 Nov). Reopening promo; any Grand Menu order unlocks it. This branch only.

Oriental Kopi — new taro seasonal menu (until 30 Nov). Taro Egg Tart, Taro Polo Bun, new mains and drinks. Prices not stated — check in store.

💸 The Ultra-Budget Date: $11 for Two at KFC

KFC — Samyang Buldak Carbonara Macaroni Bowl $5.50 (U.P. $10.75, from 21 Oct, end date not announced). Free Medium Sprite upsize included. Two bowls = $11 before GST.

Nan Yang Dao — 50% off the 2nd Kaya Toast Set, weekdays 3–6pm (from 21 Oct, end date not announced). Selected outlets. The cheapest proper sit-down meal for two here.

Boost Juice Bugis Junction — Buy 1, Get 1 for $1 (next window: Mon 26 Oct only). Berries & Tropical range. One drink, two straws, one extra dollar.

🍣 The Japanese Night: Donki, Sushi Express, Genki Sushi

Don Don Donki Edomae Jo Fair — deluxe sushi from $16.90 (19–31 Oct, all stores). Nigiri Selection $16.90, Classic Selection $22.90 (S$4 off stated).

Sushi Express & Sushi Plus — Salmon Favourites (23–31 Oct, dine-in only). Salmon in the spotlight; no flat discount stated.

Genki Sushi — Shinchan promo, free Gashapon spin with $50 nett spend (until 8 Nov). Capsule collectibles include phone straps, cup holders and cable accessories.

👯 The Double Date: A Buffet Where the 4th Diner Is Free

Ban Heng at Orchid Country Club — S$39.80++ per adult, 4th diner free (from 22 Oct, end date not announced). Perfect for two couples: about S$29.85++ per head across four. Lunch 11.30am–2.30pm, dinner 6pm–9.30pm.

Kai Duck Ngee Ann City — 60% off any 2 of 6 dim sum dishes, weekday lunch, min $60 spend (anniversary special, end date not announced). Discount applies to two dishes only; the $60 minimum is on the whole bill.

📅 Book Ahead: Two Calendar-Worthy Splurges

Yakiniku-GO — Meat It Yourself Set $7.90++ instead of $17.90++ (30 Oct only). Two sets = $15.80++ before service/GST. Members get an extra perk. Go early.

Barossa Steak & Grill VivoCity — F1 Wagyu Oyster Blade Steak S$12++ instead of S$68++ (2–15 Nov). Walk-in, no booking stated. Aim for week two to dodge the queue.

💰 Three Date Budgets, Mapped

Cheapest circuit: $11 for two at KFC. Café classic: two drinks + one half-price pancake at Hoshino. Occasion play: ~S$29.85++ per head at Ban Heng, or the $12++ wagyu at Barossa.

⚠️ Condition Check

Hoshino: this branch + menu order. Nan Yang Dao: weekday 3–6pm, 2nd set. Boost: 22 & 26 Oct only. Genki: $50 nett. Kai Duck: weekday lunch, $60 min, 2 dishes. Ban Heng: 3 adults pay. Yakiniku-GO: 30 Oct, members' perk. Barossa: walk-in.
