Weekday Fast Food Deals Singapore (16–22 Nov 2026): $1 Sugar Roll, $10 Popeyes Bucket, $6.50 KFC Zinger

Monday is payday for deal hunters this week: Polar's 100th anniversary $1 Sugar Roll runs 3–5pm TODAY, KFC's $6.50 Zinger meal is today only, and Popeyes just kicked off a $10 chicken bucket that runs every weekday until 22 December.

Weekday-only promotions are the fast food industry's way of filling the Monday-to-Friday lull, and this week the chains have gone unusually hard. Two of the biggest deals expire TODAY (Monday, 16 November), one runs all week, and one stretches all the way to 22 December. Here is the full breakdown, with every catch spelled out.

1. Today Only (Mon 16 Nov): Move Fast on These Two

Polar Puffs & Cakes — $1 Sugar Roll (100th Anniversary)

To mark its 100th anniversary, Polar is selling its iconic Sugar Roll for just $1 on 16 November 2026, from 3pm to 5pm, at eight selected outlets including One Raffles Place. The limits are strict: 100 pieces per outlet and one redemption per customer. This is a queue-early situation, not a drop-by-after-work situation — office-tower outlets near Raffles Place tend to clear their allocation well before 5pm.

KFC — $6.50 Zinger Meal (App Deal)

KFC's app-exclusive for 16 November 2026 is the Zinger meal at $6.50 — the crispy chicken burger plus fries and a Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, which the source lists as a 22% saving. It's one day only, so if spicy crispy chicken is your thing, order through the KFC app today; walk-in counter prices won't match it.

⏰ Today's Timing Plan

Lunch: grab the KFC $6.50 Zinger via the app. Mid-afternoon: swing by a Polar outlet between 3–5pm for the $1 Sugar Roll. Do both and you've had a main meal plus a century-old bakery treat for under $8.

2. All Week (Mon 16 – Fri 20 Nov): The Weekday Line-Up

McDonald's — App Exclusive Weekday Deals, Up to 30% Off

From 16 to 20 November 2026, McDonald's is running its App Exclusive Weekday Deals: value meals from $6.90, the McSpicy BLT meal at $8.90, and free delivery on qualifying app orders — the promotion is advertised at up to 30% off. As always with McDonald's, the deal lives inside the app: check the deals tab before ordering, because the specific meals rotate and the free delivery component has conditions set in-app.

Auntie Anne's — Buy 3 Get 1 Free Pretzels

Auntie Anne's buy-3-get-1-free runs 16 to 20 November 2026 at all outlets, while stocks last. The fine print: the free item is the lowest-priced one in the order, and it applies to the Signature Pretzel category — so three Original Pretzels gets you a fourth Original, but mixing in dips or nuggets won't lift the value of the freebie. Office groups and families get the most out of this one; solo buyers barely benefit.

3. The Long Player: Popeyes $10 Chicken Bucket to 22 Dec

The best-value deal of the batch is also the most durable: Popeyes is selling its 4-piece Signature Chicken bucket for $10 (usual price $19.60) on weekdays from 16 November to 22 December 2026. That is $9.60 off — roughly 49% — and you can choose Classic or Spicy. The only catches: weekdays only (skip weekends), and the window is over a month long, so there's no need to rush — but note that this type of promo is the one most likely to have per-order limits at the counter, so check with staff if you're buying for a group.

4. Bonus Sweet Deal: The Coffee Bean Tiramisu Log Cake

Also starting today (16 November) and running to 29 November 2026: The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf's flash deal on the 600g Tiramisu Log Cake at $29.90, down from $45. Not fast food, but if you were going to buy a festive log cake anyway, this is one of the sharper discounts we've seen this season on a named brand — worth pre-ordering early since flash-deal stock at this price historically moves fast.

6. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Monday 16 Nov (today): KFC $6.50 Zinger via app at lunch; Polar $1 Sugar Roll between 3–5pm (queue before 4pm to be safe); pre-order the Coffee Bean log cake if you want one.
  • Tuesday 17 Nov: Popeyes $10 bucket day one without the opening-weekend crowd. Pair with the McDonald's $6.90 value meal for a colleague if you're doing a group order.
  • Wednesday 18 Nov: Auntie Anne's buy-3-get-1 — best executed as an office order of four Signature Pretzels.
  • Thursday 19 Nov: McDonald's $8.90 McSpicy BLT meal via the app — check the deals tab in the morning, as weekday deals rotate.
  • Friday 20 Nov: Last day for McDonald's weekday deals and Auntie Anne's — final call for both. After today, the Popeyes $10 bucket is your only weekday fast-food anchor until 22 December.

7. What to Skip

Honest filter: if you're buying alone, the Auntie Anne's promo adds nothing (you'd need to buy three to get one free). The McDonald's deals only make sense through the app — at counter price you're paying full freight. And the Popeyes bucket at $10 is excellent, but at weekends it reverts to $19.60, so don't let a Saturday craving wipe out the savings; wait for Monday.
