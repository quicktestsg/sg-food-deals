Weekend Food Deals Singapore (Oct 3–6, 2026): $3 GYM Burrito, Free Prata, KFC 5pc $10.95 & 1-for-1 Delivery

A one-day $3 brekkie burrito, a free-prata Telegram join, the return of KFC Chicken Tuesday and a whole month of Popeyes delivery deals — this weekend's eating plan, sorted by deadline, with every catch spelled out.

The first weekend of October 2026 is stacked: a one-day burrito flash deal on Saturday, a payment-app cashback that quietly refunds your hawker lunch the same day, a free prata giveaway on Tuesday, and the big one — KFC's Chicken Tuesday returning for a five-week run. Then there's a month of fried-chicken delivery deals for anyone who'd rather not leave the house. Here's the full plan, ordered by how fast each deal expires.

1. Today Only: Guzman y Gomez $3 Brekkie Burritos (Sat, Oct 3)

The tightest deadline of the lot: GYG Singapore is slinging its Brekkie Burritos for S$3 on Saturday, 3 October 2026 — one day only. That's a full breakfast wrap (eggs, cheese, your pick of fillings) for less than a kopi-c and a roti set. It's breakfast-menu territory, so this is a morning mission: check your nearest outlet's breakfast hours before heading out. The source did not state an end time within the day or a per-order cap, so don't be the person who arrives at 2pm expecting burritos.

2. Also Saturday: DBS PayLah! $3 Cashback at Hawkers & Heartland Shops (Oct 3)

Stack this on top of the burrito run. DBS PayLah! is bringing back its Saturday cashback on 3 October 2026: scan and pay with PayLah! at participating heartland shops, wet markets, coffeoshops and hawker centres islandwide and enjoy up to S$3 cashback. The cap: first 160,000 redemptions only. That sounds like a lot, but on a Saturday across all of Singapore it moves fast — do your marketing and lunch payment early. The source does not state a minimum spend, but "up to $3" means the cashback scales with the transaction, so bigger baskets earn more back (capped at S$3).

💡 The Saturday double-stack

Pay for your $3 GYM burrito with PayLah! — assuming the outlet accepts it — and your hawker groceries on the same morning. One app, one day, two layers of savings. Even at a hawker stall alone, the cashback effectively makes a S$6 lunch a S$3 one.

3. Tuesday's Freebie: Springleaf Prata Place 2 Free Pratas (Tue, Oct 6)

The condition here is refreshingly simple: join Springleaf Prata Place's Telegram channel and get two free plain pratas on 6 October 2026. No minimum spend is stated in the source — though free-prata giveaways at prata chains historically come with fine print (sometimes dine-in only, sometimes with a drink order), so confirm the redemption steps on their Telegram before queueing. The prata itself normally sells for around a dollar-plus a piece at most chains — but since the source doesn't quote Springleaf's exact regular price, skip the percentage math and just enjoy the free carbs.

4. The Headliner: KFC Chicken Tuesday Returns — 5pc for $10.95 (Oct 6 – Nov 10)

Singapore's most reliable cheap-chicken deal is back. From 6 October to 10 November 2026, every Tuesday gets you five pieces of Original Recipe or Hot & Crispy chicken for S$10.95 at participating outlets — that works out to S$2.19 a piece against the stated usual price of S$22.50, a 51% saving per the promo. The useful detail most people miss: you can buy up to two sets per transaction, meaning 10 pieces for S$21.90 — enough for a family of four with sides left in the budget. Five Tuesdays in the window (Oct 6, 13, 20, 27 and Nov 3, plus Nov 10 as the finale) — pick your favourite.

⚠️ Condition check

Participating outlets only — not every KFC in Singapore runs Chicken Tuesday, so check the app or store locator. Tuesday-only, obviously. And if you'd rather stay in: KFC also has up to 40% off selected breakfast items every Monday to Wednesday on GrabFood and foodpanda until 31 December 2026, with Buddy Value Meals from S$9.

5. The Delivery Month: Popeyes 50% Off & 1-for-1 (Till Oct 27)

Popeyes is running a month-long delivery promotion from 28 September, 10am to 27 October 2026, 10am — but the two halves live on different apps. Per the published terms: 50% off via Grab runs 28 September to 17 October, while the foodpanda 1-for-1 runs later in the window (with the overall promo ending 27 October). The strategic read: use Grab's half-price first (it expires first), then switch to foodpanda's 1-for-1 for the final stretch. Check each app for minimum spend and menu exclusions before ordering.

6. Delivery Sweetener: $6 Off Foodpanda with FUELUP (Till Oct 18)

Race season means delivery discounts. foodpanda is offering S$6 off food delivery from 28 September to 18 October 2026 with promo code FUELUP, plus a new Group Order feature for watch parties. The code carries a minimum spend and standard exclusions — but S$6 is one of the bigger flat discounts around right now, and it stacks on the ordering decision you were already making. Not just for chicken: Genki Sushi is running the same code (S$6 off, delivery from S$2.99) from 28 September to 18 October, with a minimum spend applying.

7. Quick Mentions: Worth a Detour This Weekend

  • <strong>Genki Sushi Children's Day freebie (Oct 2–4):</strong> a FREE mini table tennis set with dine-in visits, while stocks last, at participating outlets. The kids' meal isn't included — the toy comes with the visit, so check the redemption terms in store.
  • <strong>Giant Tampines free chicken (Oct 3–4):</strong> spend S$20 on Sakura Chicken at the newly opened Tampines Hypermarket (9am–5pm) and take home a free whole leg worth S$5.40, while stocks last. The store's opening specials (Buy 1 Free 1 deals, up to 57% off) run till 7 October for yuu members.
  • <strong>Kiztopia Buy 5 Play 6 (Oct 1–18):</strong> one free extra visit with any 5× pass, from S$23 per visit at Marina Square — useful if the kids need burning off after all the above.

Strategy: The Four-Day Plan

  • <strong>Saturday, Oct 3 (today):</strong> GYG $3 brekkie burrito in the morning, PayLah! cashback on every hawker/heartland payment you make (first 160,000 redemptions — go early). Giant Tampines free whole leg if you're in the east, 9am–5pm.
  • <strong>Sunday, Oct 4:</strong> Nothing expires today — recovery day, or use the Grab-side Popeyes 50% off delivery (runs to Oct 17) with FUELUP stacking checks.
  • <strong>Monday, Oct 5:</strong> KFC breakfast up to 40% off on GrabFood/foodpanda (every Mon–Wed till 31 Dec). Join Springleaf's Telegram channel ahead of Tuesday's free prata.
  • <strong>Tuesday, Oct 6:</strong> The double-header — two free plain pratas at Springleaf in the morning, 5pc KFC for S$10.95 (or 10pc for S$21.90) from participating outlets. This is the best value day of the week; plan appetite accordingly.

The Bottom Line

Two deadlines matter this weekend: the $3 GYG burrito and PayLah! cashback both live and die on Saturday, 3 October. After that, the calendar relaxes — free prata on Tuesday, Chicken Tuesday every week till 10 November, and Popeyes delivery deals carrying you to 27 October. If you play all four days as scripted, the damage is roughly S$18 per person for a burrito, a hawker lunch (post-cashback), two pratas and five pieces of chicken — with the FUELUP code trimming any delivery orders on top.
