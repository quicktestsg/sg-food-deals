Sweet Tooth Deals Singapore (Oct 2026): 1-for-1 Bubble Tea, Half-Price Ice Cream & $1 Coffee

Bubble tea buy-one-get-one this weekend only, Hershey's pints at half price, a free Cornetto for every Cornetto — October is treating Singapore's sugar addicts well. Here's every deal worth your calories, with exact dates, prices and catches.

Some weeks the deals calendar leans savoury; this one is unapologetically for dessert people. Between a two-day bubble tea reopening giveaway, a supermarket ice cream price war and two separate $1 coffee plays running all month, you can cover every afternoon sugar craving in Singapore this October without breaking a S$10 note. Below, the full breakdown — sorted by how soon each deal dies, because the bubble tea one dies Sunday.

1. This Weekend Only: PlayMade 1-for-1 (Oct 2–3)

The tightest deadline of the week: PlayMade is celebrating the reopening of its Westgate and Waterway Point outlets with a 1-for-1 promotion on 2 and 3 October 2026. Buy one drink, get a second free, from five featured favourites including Taiwan Milk Tea, Chrysanthemum Milk Tea and Osmanthus Oolong Tea. Orders go through the PlayMade app or walk-in, while stocks last.

🎯 The catch

Only two outlets — Westgate and Waterway Point. If you're not in the west or north-east, the MRT fare may cost more than the free drink. Bring a friend and split the bill; solo drinkers just get a bigger sugar rush.

2. FairPrice Ice Cream Stock-Up: Haagen-Dazs 3-for-$25, Hershey's Pints 50% Off (Till Oct 7)

FairPrice's current promo sheet (running until 7 October 2026) is stacked with freezer-aisle value: Haagen-Dazs pints at 3 for S$25, Magnolia Hershey's pints at 50% off, plus discounts on chocolates, snacks and fresh buys alongside. This is the classic group-buy move — three housemates, three pints, S$8.33 each for proper Haagen-Dazs.

3. 7-Eleven: Cornetto Royale Buy 1 Get 1 FREE (Till Oct 4)

The 7-Eleven 4-day special runs until 4 October 2026: buy one Cornetto Royale, get the second free, alongside other short-window bargains. The convenience-store play here is speed — no app, no membership, walk in and the deal applies. One for now, one back into the office freezer for the 3pm slump.

4. Baskin-Robbins: Japan Flavours from $8.50 + $1.90 Coffee Top-Up (From Oct 1)

Two overlapping Baskin-Robbins promos started 1 October 2026. The headline act is the Japan Summer Flavours lineup — Matcha, Musk Melon and Popping Shower — with the Japan Triple Fun Scoop at S$8.50 and the Japan Trio Waffle at S$13.90. The quieter one is better value: add S$1.90 for a Brewed Coffee, or S$2.90 for a Coffee Float, with any ice cream order. That turns a dessert run into an affordable dessert-plus-kopi session — assuming you go on a weekday (more on that below).

⚠️ Condition check

The coffee top-up is valid on weekdays only, at participating outlets, and excludes Jurong Point. The Japan flavour pricing — no end date was announced in the source, so treat it as "while it lasts" and confirm in store if you're making a special trip.

Two fast-food giants, two $1 coffee deals, two different clocks. How they stack up:

The play: use KFC's version first (it expires 8 October), then switch to McDonald's — which conveniently has no stated end time within its 31 October window, meaning daily $1 McCafé for the rest of the month. Both require slightly different behaviour: McDonald's needs the app ordered ahead; KFC's is a breakfast-window walk-in. Neither requires a minimum purchase per the published terms — but do check the app on the day, as fast-food terms can change mid-promotion.

6. Worth the Calendar: KFC Gen B Boneless Buffet (Oct 16)

Not strictly dessert, but if the sugar crash hits hard: KFC's Gen B Boneless Buffet lands at *SCAPE on 16 October 2026 — 90 minutes of unlimited O.R. Tenders, Hot & Crispy Chicken Bites, whipped potato, fountain drinks and five free-flow sauces for S$25.95 per person. There's an extra 10% off for groups (conditions per the official booking page). Two weeks out, so book early if the inner circle wants in.

Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • <strong>Friday, Oct 2 (today):</strong> PlayMade 1-for-1 opens — grab it today or Saturday, not Sunday (stocks run out). Swing by 7-Eleven for the Cornetto BOGO before it ends Oct 4.
  • <strong>Weekend, Oct 3–4:</strong> Last call for Cornetto BOGO (ends Oct 4). Baskin-Robbins Japan Trio Waffle weekend treat — S$13.90 to share. FairPrice run: Haagen-Dazs 3-for-$25 and Hershey's pints 50% off both end Oct 7.
  • <strong>Weekdays, Oct 5–7:</strong> Baskin-Robbins coffee top-up works on weekdays only — S$1.90 brew with your scoop. Final FairPrice stock-up by Oct 7. KFC $1 breakfast coffee ends Oct 8.
  • <strong>Rest of October:</strong> McDonald's $1 coffee daily via the app till Oct 31. KFC Gen B buffet on Oct 16 if you're quick on the booking.

The Bottom Line

The weekend is the pressure point: PlayMade's 1-for-1 and 7-Eleven's Cornetto BOGO both die within 48 hours, and FairPrice's ice cream pricing follows on October 7. After that, October settles into a comfortable rhythm of $1 McDonald's coffee and weekday Baskin-Robbins top-ups. Total damage if you did everything in this guide: roughly S$50 for two people — and a freezer full of Haagen-Dazs to show for it.
