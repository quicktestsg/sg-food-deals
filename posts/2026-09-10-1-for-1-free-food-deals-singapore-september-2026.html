1-for-1 & Free Food Deals Singapore September 2026: Shihlin Rice Bowls, Free Bakkwa & $7 Student Meals

A 48-hour Shihlin 1-for-1, free bakkwa for CHAS cardholders this weekend, and student meals that run all the way to October — this week's best free and buy-one-get-one food offers, ranked by urgency.

September's second week is quietly one of the better weeks of the month for free and 1-for-1 food. Shihlin is celebrating its revamped IMM outlet with a two-day-only rice bowl promotion, Bee Cheng Hiang is handing out free bakkwa to CHAS cardholders over the weekend, and a few longer-running offers — 7-Eleven's 2 for $4.90 and A&W's $7 student meal — give you until late September or October. Here is the full breakdown, with every date and condition spelled out.

1. Ending in 48 Hours — Shihlin IMM 1-for-1 Rice Bowls (Sep 10–11)

Shihlin Taiwan Street Food has revamped its IMM outlet, and it is marking the occasion with a 1-for-1 rice bowl promotion on 10 and 11 September 2026 only. Two rice bowls cost $7.50 in total — the usual price is $7.50 each, so this is effectively half price when you go with a friend. The promotion runs at the IMM outlet; the fine print on which rice bowls are included is on Shihlin's official channels, so check before you order if you have a specific favourite.

⏱️ Why this one is first

This is a two-day opening special, not a monthly promo. Once 11 September closes, there is no indication it returns. If you work or live near Jurong East, this is today or tomorrow.

2. This Weekend Only — Free Bee Cheng Hiang Bakkwa Bites for CHAS Cardholders (Sep 12–13)

On 12 and 13 September 2026, Bee Cheng Hiang is giving away free 100g packs of Bakkwa Bites at its Toa Payoh Central outlet only. The giveaway is exclusive to Blue and Orange CHAS cardholders — Green cardholders and non-cardholders are not included. Redemption happens in two time windows each day, starting from 12pm, and packs are available while stocks last, so the early window is the safer bet. One redemption per cardholder is the norm for this type of event, but confirm the exact limit with the store.

3. New Store, 7 Days of Specials — Sheng Siong Woodlands Blk 905 (Sep 10–16)

Sheng Siong opens a new supermarket at Blk 905 Woodlands Avenue 5 #02-01 on 10 September 2026, with a 7-day opening special running 10 to 16 September that includes 1-for-1 deals among the offers. The store operates daily from 7am to 11pm, and opening day features a lion dance. Opening-week promos at Sheng Siong are typically genuinely aggressive — new outlets are stocked to impress — so Woodlands residents have a proper excuse to do this week's grocery run there. Exact item pricing varies by day, so check the in-store flyer.

4. Running Till Late September — 7-Eleven 2 for $4.90 (Till Sep 23)

7-Eleven Singapore's 2 for $4.90 promotion runs until 23 September 2026. You mix and match selected food items — from hearty bowls to potato sides — with no app or membership required; just pick two participating items and the register applies the bundle price. That works out to $2.45 per item, which is a solid lunch-building block if you pair a bowl with a side. Selection differs by store, so the larger 7-Eleven branches have more to mix.

5. The Long Runner — A&W $7 Student Meal (Till Oct 30)

A&W Singapore's student meal is priced at $7 and runs until 30 October 2026. It includes a Coney Dog or Cheeseburger with Straight Fries and a Root Beer. Curly Fries and Root Beer Float upgrades are optional add-ons at extra cost (prices for the upgrades are not stated in the source — check in store). Condition: you need to be a student, so bring your student ID. With the September school holidays in full swing, this is a straightforward budget option for students in town.

6. Sizzling Till End October — The Soup Expert 1-for-1 Claypot Dishes

The Soup Expert has added 10 new Cantonese claypot dishes (啫啫煲) to its menu this September, and to launch them it is running a buy-1-get-1-free claypot promotion until 31 October 2026. The catch: a minimum spend is required — the source announcement does not state the exact threshold, so confirm with the outlet before ordering. If the minimum spend fits your table size, two claypots for the price of one is one of the better dine-in values running right now.

7. Mark the Calendar — McDonald's x G-Dragon PEACEMINUSONE (From Sep 17)

Not live yet, but worth planning for: McDonald's Singapore's collaboration with G-Dragon's PEACEMINUSONE label launches on 17 September 2026, bringing a limited-edition menu and merchandise. Pricing has not been announced. If past celebrity collabs are anything to go by, the merchandise sells out fast and the sauce-burger combos draw queues at peak hours — going in the first morning or after the opening weekend is the usual play.
