Student & NS Food Discounts Singapore November 2026: $9.80 Ramen, $10 Burger Pair & More With Valid ID

Exams are wrapping up and the year-end break is close. That student card in your wallet — or the 11B for those in green — is quietly worth real money at ramen shops, burger counters, pizza joints and even KTV rooms this month. Here is every verified student and NS dining discount running in Singapore right now.

November is one of the best months of the year to be a student or full-time national serviceman in Singapore. Restaurants know the school holidays are coming, exam-weary crowds are looking for comfort food, and a wave of student-exclusive promotions has just landed. The catch: almost every deal below requires proof of status, and several are weekday-only. This guide sorts them by what starts today, what runs all month, and what NS men can claim through SAFRA partnerships — with every price, date and condition verified against the original promotions.

1. Starting Today (9 Nov): Tonkotsu Kazan Student Ramen Meal — S$9.80++

The freshest student deal of the week launches today. From 9 November 2026 onwards, Japanese ramen chain Tonkotsu Kazan is running a weekday student-exclusive promotion at its Northpoint City and Century Square outlets: a bowl of ramen with iced green tea for S$9.80++. It is dine-in only, and you need to bring a valid student ID. No end date has been announced, so treat it as an ongoing perk and verify with the outlet before making a special trip.

🎒 Why Yishun and Tampines students win here

Both participating outlets are in heartland malls (Northpoint City in Yishun, Century Square in Tampines) — a short walk from two of the biggest student populations in the north and east. A weekday lunch between classes at S$9.80++ for ramen plus a drink is one of the better sit-down Japanese meal prices around right now.

2. All Month: Lotteria Student Pair — Any 2 Burgers for $10 on Weekdays

Korean burger chain Lotteria is running its student promotion from 31 October to 30 November 2026 at outlets islandwide. The mechanics are simple: any 2 eligible burgers for $10 on weekdays, with a valid student ID. There is also an optional meal add-on — 2 regular soft drinks plus 1 large fries for another $3.50, which brings the full pairing to $13.50 for two people. Note the wording: only eligible burgers qualify, so check which items are included before ordering. The deal ends 30 November 2026.

3. Ongoing: 4Fingers $2 Student Discount on Selected Items

Crispy chicken fans, this one is for you. From 26 October 2026 onwards, 4Fingers Crispy Chicken is taking $2 off selected items for eligible students, including chicken combos such as the 6-piece Wingettes and Drumettes combo, the Boneless Chicken combo, plus selected rice boxes and burgers. The discount started as an exams-finished celebration and no end date has been announced — it is still listed as an ongoing promotion. Bring your student ID and confirm at the counter which menu items carry the discount.

4. Ongoing: La Pizzaiola Weekday 1-for-1 Pizza & Pasta Lunch — Students AND NS Men

The most inclusive deal on this list. From 28 October 2026 onwards, Italian casual diner La Pizzaiola is offering a weekday 1-for-1 pizza or pasta lunch deal open to eligible students and NS men — a rare promotion that explicitly includes those serving national service. Separately, the restaurant is also running 50% off the lower-priced second dish from its New Creations Menu for all diners. No end date has been announced for either offer, so call ahead to confirm the 1-for-1 lunch is still on before heading down.

5. After-Exams Fun: Chao K Party Night KTV From $9++ Per Student Per Hour

Not strictly food, but there is free-flow drinking involved. From 5 November 2026 onwards, Chao K Party is running a Night KTV special at its Bugis and Dhoby Ghaut outlets from $9++ per student per hour ($10++ per adult), with free-flow orange juice, grape juice and sour plum juice — provided someone at the table completes a qualifying Google Review or Xiaohongshu check-in. That condition is the catch: no review, no free-flow. No end date has been announced. Perfect for a post-exams group session that will not wreck the wallet.

6. For the Men in Green: SAFRA Dining & Lifestyle Perks

NS men with a SAFRA membership have a separate stack of perks this season. At Yum Cha (Chinatown and Changi outlets), SAFRA members and servicemen can enjoy 50% off a whole Peking Duck with a minimum $80 spend, 20% off the Changi weekday dinner buffet, 30% off Chinatown à la carte dining and free delivery for Classic Mini Buffet orders above qualifying amounts — all running until 31 December 2026. Beyond food, SAFRA members get 1-for-1 Affordable Art Fair tickets from 26–29 November 2026 at the F1 Pit Building, 20% off Singapore Ballet The Nutcracker tickets (9–13 December 2026, from $44, promo code N20mgBWR), and an additional 15% off StarCruises Star Voyager cabins with up to S$250 onboard credits per cabin for sailings until 13 March 2027.

  • Tonkotsu Kazan: weekdays only, dine-in only, valid student ID required, Northpoint City & Century Square outlets only, end date not announced.
  • Lotteria: weekdays only, valid student ID required, only eligible burgers count towards the 2-for-$10, ends 30 November 2026.
  • 4Fingers: $2 off applies to selected combos, rice boxes and burgers only, end date not announced.
  • La Pizzaiola: 1-for-1 lunch is weekday-only and limited to eligible students and NS men, end date not announced.
  • Chao K Party: free-flow juices require a qualifying Google Review or Xiaohongshu check-in, end date not announced.
  • Yum Cha SAFRA deals: Peking duck 50% off requires minimum $80 spend; SAFRA membership needed for all Yum Cha perks.

  • Wednesday: Mid-week pizza day — La Pizzaiola 1-for-1 lunch works for students and NS men alike. Split the bill and both eat for the price of one.

💡 The ID habit that pays all year

Most students only remember their ID during back-to-school promos, but restaurants run student pricing year-round — Tonkotsu Kazan joins a growing list of Japanese chains (Sushiro, Sushi Express and Genki Sushi have all run member or time-based discounts this month alone) that reward status or off-peak timing. Make it a reflex: before paying anywhere, ask if there is a student rate. The worst answer is no.

Final Verdict

The standout this month is Tonkotsu Kazan — a proper sit-down ramen with a drink at S$9.80++ is strong value, and it starts today. Pair it with the Lotteria 2-for-$10 for group hangs and the La Pizzaiola 1-for-1 (bonus points for including NS men), and a student can eat out every weekday in November without breaking the S$10-per-meal line. NS men with SAFRA membership should bookmark the Yum Cha dining stack before it ends 31 December 2026. Prices and dates above are taken from the original promotions as published; always confirm at the outlet, since restaurants can change terms mid-promotion.
