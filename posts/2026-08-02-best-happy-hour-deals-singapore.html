Best Happy Hour Deals in Singapore 2026

A cocktail in Singapore can easily set you back S$25-30. It doesn't have to. From 1-for-1 drinks at Clarke Quay to free-flow wine on Duxton Hill and ladies night freebies, this is the complete 2026 playbook for drinking well without the guilt.

Let's get one thing straight: drinking in Singapore is not cheap. A single craft beer at a riverside bar can hit S$18, and a fancy cocktail at a Marina Bay lounge will happily relieve you of S$30. But here's what the regulars know — almost every decent bar in the city runs a happy hour, and if you time it right, you can drink at half the price (or less).

The Golden Window: 5–7 PM

Happy hour in Singapore almost always falls between 5 PM and 7 PM, with some bars stretching it to 8 PM on slow weeknights. This is the sweet spot — the after-work crowd hasn't fully arrived, bars want to fill seats, and prices drop dramatically.

⏰ Timing Hack

Most happy hours end sharply at 7 PM. Order your last round at 6:55 — you lock in the happy-hour price and the bar staff won't blink.

Clarke Quay & Boat Quay — The Riverside Deal Belt

If you want maximum happy hour density, head to the river. Clarke Quay and Boat Quay are packed with bars competing for the after-work crowd, which means aggressive promos all week long.

  • The Pump Room: A Clarke Quay staple — happy hour deals on beers and house pours, plus live music most nights.
  • Level 33: Nearby at Marina Boulevard — in-house craft beer with a skyline view. Happy hour pints are a steal.
  • Boat Quay stretch (Harry's, Molly Malones, indochine): S$10-12 pints and 1-for-1 house pours are standard. Pick whichever has the vibe you like.

Orchard & The City — Hotel Bar Bargains

Hotel bars sound expensive, but they're secretly some of the best happy hour deals in Singapore. They need to fill seats between lunch and dinner, so they run generous early-evening promos — often free-flow packages that are impossible to beat per glass.

  • Archibar at Archer House: Reliable 1-for-1 happy hour on selected drinks. Solid bar snacks, central location.
  • ANTI:DOTE at Fairmont Singapore: Upscale, but happy hour cocktails are genuinely good value for the quality.
  • Braci: Aperitivo-style happy hour pairs Italian drinks with a view. Book ahead.

Duxton Hill & Tanjong Pagar — Cocktail Territory

This is Singapore's cocktail heartland — hidden speakeasies, polished bars, and a cluster of places that take drinks seriously. Happy hours here tend to be shorter but the quality per dollar is exceptional.

  • The Spiffy Dapper: A Duxton Road favourite — industrial-chic, excellent cocktails, and a happy hour that makes their drinks feel reasonable.
  • Native: Amoy Street's locally-sourced cocktail bar. Happy hour slots are limited but the drinks are worth rearranging your evening for.
  • Operation Dagger: A basement bar with a cult following. Happy hour on selected pours.

Free Flow & 1-for-1 — The Big Savers

If you're drinking more than two rounds, free-flow and 1-for-1 deals are where the real savings live. The math is brutal in your favour.

  • Wine free-flow: Many hotel bars and wine bars run 90-minute or 2-hour packages at S$45-68. Check what's included.
  • 1-for-1 cocktails: Clarke Quay and Boat Quay are the heartlands. These stack beautifully with Burpple Beyond or The Entertainer.
  • Beer buckets & towers: Bucket deals and beer towers at sports bars offer the cheapest per-millilitre beer in Singapore.

Ladies Night — Wednesdays Are Sacred

Wednesday is ladies night across Singapore, and the deals can be absurdly good — free-flow drinks for women, free first drink, discounted entry, the works.

  • Clarke Quay clubs & bars: Many run free-flow or free-first-drink promos for ladies on Wednesday. Check social media before you go.
  • Hotel lounges: Several extend ladies night to include free-flow wine or bubbles for a fixed window.

Bar Bites on a Budget

Drinking cheap is only half the win — the other half is not blowing your savings on a S$28 bowl of truffle fries.

  • Happy-hour bar bites: Look for discounted wings, sliders, or nachos alongside drink promos.
  • Bar snacks (free): Some old-school pubs still put out free nuts, keropok, or popcorn during happy hour.
  • Eat first, drink cheap: Grab a S$5 meal nearby, then walk into the bar for happy hour drinks only.

Deal Multipliers — Stack Your Savings

🎯 Stack These On Top

Happy hour is your base layer. Stack these on top to save even more on every round:

  • Credit Card Drink Promos: DBS, UOB, OCBC, and Citibank all run rotating bar and dining promos — often 15% off or 1-for-1 at partnered venues.
  • The Entertainer App: Buy-one-get-one offers at hundreds of bars and restaurants. Pays for itself in two or three outings.
  • Burpple Beyond: Better known for food, but covers bars too — 1-for-1 deals at curated venues.
  • Fave / Chope / Klook: Discounted vouchers for popular bars and restaurant-bars.
  • Bar loyalty programmes: Harry's, Brewerkz, and chains run membership apps with birthday drinks, points, and member-only happy hours.

Group vs Solo — Pick Your Play

Different deals suit different group sizes. Match the deal to your crowd:

  • Solo or a couple: Go for happy hour cocktails and wine at hotel bars and speakeasies.
  • Group of 4–6: 1-for-1 deals shine — everyone gets a drink, the bill splits evenly, and The Entertainer pays for itself in one round.
  • Big group (8+): Beer towers and buckets at sports bars or Clarke Quay. Call ahead to reserve a table.

The Smart Drinker's Playbook

Here's the short version. Hit the river for 1-for-1 deals and the biggest concentration of cheap-drink bars. Head to Duxton Hill and Amoy Street for quality cocktails at happy-hour prices. Use hotel bars for free-flow wine when you want more than two glasses. Stack credit card promos, The Entertainer, and Burpple Beyond. And whatever you do — order your last round at 6:55 PM.

Happy hour lineups change constantly — bars swap promos monthly, new spots open, old favourites close. Bookmark this page and check back; we update this guide as the scene evolves. Now go forth and drink cheaply.
