Café & Dessert Deals Singapore: Starbucks 1-for-1, Häagen-Dazs 3 for $25 (27 Nov – 2 Dec 2026)

A 1-for-1 Starbucks coffee, tub ice cream at warehouse prices and a free noodle gift set — this week's sweetest deals, ranked by how fast they expire.

Black Friday is mostly about laptops and mattresses, but the actual best value this weekend is in the fridge aisle. A 1-for-1 Starbucks promo ends Sunday, FairPrice is selling Häagen-Dazs tubs in 3-for-$25 bundles, and a local ice cream brand is at exactly half price for Link members. Here is the full cheat sheet, organised by deadline — because half of these are gone by Monday.

1. Ending This Weekend — Grab Before Monday (by 29 Nov)

Starbucks 1-for-1 Tall Drinks (Trust Bank cards)

Until Sunday 29 November 2026, paying with a Trust Bank card gets you a second Tall handcrafted drink for free — and yes, you can mix and match, so one latte plus one cold brew counts. The deal works through Apple Pay and Google Pay too, so you don't even need the physical card in your wallet. This is one of the cleaner 1-for-1 mechanics around: no minimum spend, no specific drink list beyond handcrafted favourites. If you've been meaning to apply for a Trust card, this weekend is a decent excuse.

FairPrice 3-Day Specials: Häagen-Dazs 3 for $25, Ferrero 48% Off

FairPrice's 3-day specials run through 29 November 2026, and two items stand out for anyone with a freezer: Häagen-Dazs tubs at 3 for $25, and Ferrero Collection chocolates at 48% off. The same promo window also cuts 58% off New Moon bird's nest (a saving of $69.40 on the featured pack) — more of a gifting play than a dessert run, but worth knowing with December dinner invitations coming. FairPrice caps 3-day special purchases at 4 units or sets per product, so plan accordingly if you're stocking the freezer for the school holidays.

💡 The Weekend Math

One FairPrice run this weekend can lock in dessert for the whole school-holiday fortnight: 3 Häagen-Dazs tubs at $25 plus a half-price tub of The Ice Cream & Cookie Co. at $6.98 (see below) — four tubs of premium ice cream for under $32, within the 4-unit purchase cap.

2. Running Into Early December (till 2 Dec)

The Ice Cream & Cookie Co. at Half Price — $6.98 for Link Members

Until 2 December 2026, Link members get 50% off The Ice Cream & Cookie Co. tubs at FairPrice, bringing them to $6.98 each. Eight flavours were available online at the time of checking. You don't need to be a paid member of anything — Link is free to join through the FairPrice Group app, so if you've never bothered, this is the deal that makes the two-minute sign-up worth it. The same window has half-price blueberries and other frozen favourites if you want to pad the basket.

DON DON DONKI Golden King Crab — $30.90, Save $18

Strictly this is a 'cook at home' deal rather than a café one, but it deserves a slot for the December-dinner-at-home file: from 27 November to 6 December 2026, DON DON DONKI Singapore is selling Premium Golden King Crab (450g) at $30.90, down from $48.90 — an $18 saving over the 10-day window. It's the kind of centrepiece that makes a home seafood feast feel like an occasion without restaurant prices.

3. Longer Runway — Worth a Calendar Entry

TamJai SamGor Chinatown Point: Spend $40, Get a Free Mixian Gift Set

The TamJai SamGor outlet at Chinatown Point (#02-47/48) has reopened, and from 26 November 2026 onwards, spending $40 or more gets you a free TamJai Mixian gift set while stocks last. That's a four-person table hitting the threshold with one round of noodles and drinks — very achievable for a weekend family lunch near Chinatown MRT. The catch is stock availability: no announced end date, so treat it as 'go sooner rather than later' rather than a guaranteed late-December freebie.

Haidilao Novena: Six Visits, Up to S$46 in Free Dish Vouchers

Through 30 December 2026, Haidilao's Novena outlet is running a membership rewards promo: your first five visits earn half-portion dish vouchers (think Tender Fish Slices, Duroc Pork Belly, Iberico Pork, fresh oysters and US SRF Black Beef Topblade), and completing six qualifying visits unlocks vouchers worth up to S$46. This is a loyalty play, not a one-shot deal — it only makes sense if Novena is already on your hotpot route. If it is, registering early means every visit from now on compounds toward the full payout.

4. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Friday, 27 Nov (today): FairPrice after work — Häagen-Dazs 3 for $25 and Ferrero 48% off end Sunday, and weekend crowds will clear the good flavours first.
  • Saturday, 28 Nov: Starbucks 1-for-1 on a Trust card (via Apple Pay/Google Pay) — last full weekend day for it. Pair it with a TamJai SamGor lunch at Chinatown Point if you can rally a $40 table for the free gift set.
  • Sunday, 29 Nov: Final day for both the Starbucks 1-for-1 and the FairPrice 3-day specials. If you skipped Friday, this is the drop-dead day.
  • Monday, 30 Nov: Calm day. Buy The Ice Cream & Cookie Co. at half price ($6.98, Link members) — runs till 2 Dec, no weekend rush needed.
  • Tuesday, 1 Dec: DON DON DONKI king crab ($30.90, save $18) — still five days left, but mid-week shopping means full stock.

6. What to Skip

Not every sweet-sounding promo earns a trip. The same feeds pushing these deals are also full of bank deposit promos and electronics sales dressed up as lifestyle content — a 2% p.a. time deposit is a fine financial product, but it is not a dessert deal, and this blog won't pretend otherwise. Likewise, if you don't live or work near Novena, a six-visit Haidilao loyalty grind is a poor reason to travel; free vouchers that require six hotpot sessions to unlock only pay off when the sessions were happening anyway. The rule for Black Friday weekend: buy the freezer stuff that keeps, the coffee you'd buy regardless, and nothing that requires a special journey.
