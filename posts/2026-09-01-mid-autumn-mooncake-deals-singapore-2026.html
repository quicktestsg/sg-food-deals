Mid-Autumn Mooncake Deals Singapore 2026: Hotel Mooncakes Up to 40% Off, $3.80 Old Chang Kee & More

Mid-Autumn Festival falls on 25 September 2026, and the discount window is already open. Every deal below is sorted by deadline — early-bird cut-offs first, then member-only stacks, then no-strings budget picks.

Mooncake prices in Singapore follow a predictable arc every year: steep early-bird discounts in August, shrinking savings through mid-September, then full price (or sell-outs) in the final week before the festival. With Mid-Autumn falling on Friday, 25 September 2026, the sweet spot to buy is right now. This guide rounds up every verified mooncake deal currently running, organised the way shoppers actually need it — by deadline.

5. Strategy: The Buying Order That Maximises Savings

The rational play is to split purchases into three waves. Wave one (this week): lock in the Tokachi matcha lava boxes at $30.80 before the 9 September early-bird cut-off, and use the Thye Moh Chan EARLYBIRD code before 14 September if Teochew-style pastry is preferred. Wave two (by 18–22 September): hotel boxes for gifting — SAFRA members head straight to Wan Hao for the 40% stack, everyone else uses GET20OFF plus self-collection at Marriott. Wave three (final week): only snack-level treats and supermarket stocks remain reliably available, and even those are while-stocks-last.

💡 Gifting Budget Math

Four gift boxes at Peach Garden's $60 nett works out to $240 with zero surprises — GST is already in. Do the same with '++' hotel pricing carefully: a box quoted at $50++ becomes roughly $59+ after service charge and GST, nearly matching the nett-price box. Always compare nett vs ++ before assuming the hotel discount wins.

One final note on stock: every deal listed here that mentions 'while stocks last' means exactly that in 2026's compressed season — popular snowskin flavours historically sell out days before 25 September. Buy early, collect late (where the promotion allows deferred collection), and keep receipts for pickup.
