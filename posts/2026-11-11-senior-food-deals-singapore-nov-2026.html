Senior Food Deals Singapore November 2026: $1 BreadTalk Buns, $1 Kopi & Cheap Eats for 60+

The POSB Seniors Promotion is back for its final stretch of 2026 — $1 buns, $1 kopi and $1 snacks for cardholders aged 60 and above, all the way to 31 December. Here is the full breakdown, plus senior-friendly weekday treats from dim sum to Peking duck.

Most deal roundups chase the young crowd — 1-for-1 bubble tea, student ramen specials, NSF discounts. But some of the best-value food promos in Singapore right now are reserved for the 60-and-above crowd, and they are hiding in plain sight. The headline act: the POSB Seniors Promotion 2026, running from 1 November to 31 December 2026, which turns an eligible POSB Mastercard into a $1-menu pass at BreadTalk, Dough Culture, Nan Yang Dao and Toast Box.

1. POSB Seniors Promotion 2026: The $1 Menu, Explained

From 1 November to 31 December 2026, seniors aged 60 and above holding an eligible POSB Mastercard can claim a stack of $1 food and drink deals across four familiar brands. The promo is confirmed by both SINGPromos and EverydayOnSales with consistent details, so the facts below are cross-checked:

Outlet Deal Price

A $1 kopi with a $1 bun makes a genuinely old-school breakfast for S$2 — about as close to 1980s prices as 2026 Singapore gets. ⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal in this promo requires: (1) age 60 or above, (2) an eligible POSB Mastercard, (3) participating outlets only. The promo runs 1 Nov – 31 Dec 2026. Selected items only at each brand — the $1 bun is not the whole BreadTalk counter.

2. The Family Day Out: Mandai Wildlife Reserve × POSB

If the grandparents are watching the kids these school holidays, one card perk stretches well beyond food. From 1 January 2026 to 3 January 2027, POSB cardmembers get up to 50% off selected Mandai Wildlife Reserve attraction tickets, an extra 10% off Zoolympix bundles, 15% off eligible annual memberships, and a special Friends of Mandai senior membership at S$93. A morning at the zoo followed by a $1 kopi on the way home is a full grandparent-grandchild day out at a fraction of the usual cost.

Note the discount applies to selected tickets and eligible memberships only — check which parks and ticket types are covered on the official Mandai booking page before committing to a date.

3. Weekday Dim Sum: Peach Garden's Holiday Special

For something more sit-down, Peach Garden launched its new Holiday Special dim sum menu from 5 November 2026 (end date not announced), with up to 50% off during weekday lunch and all-day dining, and 30% off on weekends and public holidays, across three outlets. The new menu leans festive-traditional — think Mao Shan Wang avocado bo lo bun and black truffle mushroom dumpling — which suits a multi-generation table perfectly. Weekday timing is also when seniors prefer to eat out anyway: quieter rooms, unhurried trolley service.

4. The Special-Occasion Treat: Kai Duck's Anniversary Peking Duck

For a proper treat with the elders, Kai Duck's 6th Anniversary Special serves its Signature Peking Duck for S$75++ (usual price S$123++) during weekday dinners, Monday to Friday (end date not announced). Prepared by award-winning Chef Fung Chi Keung using premium corn-fed Irish duck, this is the kind of deal worth building a family dinner around — and at S$48++ off the usual price, one duck roughly pays for the table's drinks and desserts.

5. Bonus: Yakiniku LIKE Tea Time from S$5.90

One more weekday-only pick that works for early dinners: Yakiniku LIKE's Tea Time sets start from S$5.90, available weekdays 3PM–5PM at 12 selected outlets, from 3 November 2026 onwards (end date not announced), with savings of up to 40% on chicken, karubi, pork belly, tiger prawn and sausage sets. The 3-to-5 window happens to be the classic grandma-grandpa kopi hour — a grilled set before the crowd hits is a solid alternative to a pastry.

💡 Strategy: The S$3 Grandparents' Morning

Stack the POSB Seniors deals into one route: a S$1 BreadTalk bun or two Dough Culture snacks for breakfast, a S$1 kopi at Toast Box or Nan Yang Dao, and — on weekdays — dim sum at Peach Garden at up to 50% off, or a S$5.90 yakiniku set at 3PM. A full eating day for two seniors lands under S$20 at the $1 layer alone. Just remember: eligible POSB Mastercard, age 60+, participating outlets, and the $1 menu ends 31 December 2026.

The Bottom Line

The POSB Seniors Promotion 2026 is the rare deal where the fine print is small and the value is large — but it expires 31 December 2026, and the weekday-only treats (Peach Garden dim sum, Kai Duck duck, Yakiniku LIKE tea time) reward those who plan ahead. If there is a 60+ POSB cardholder in the family, this is the month to start sending them out for $1 kopi.
