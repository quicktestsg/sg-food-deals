Christmas Food Deals & New Launches Singapore (18 Nov – 30 Dec 2026): KFC, Din Tai Fung, TungLok 30% Off

The festive floodgates opened this week: KFC's purple potato mochi lands today, BreadTalk teams up with Tony's Chocolonely, Din Tai Fung's plush ornaments return, and TungLok is about to run 30% off for over a month. Plus a LiHO 1-for-1 that ends tonight.

If it somehow slipped past you: Christmas season in Singapore food-land starts in mid-November, not December. This week alone brought a purple mochi at KFC, a chocolate-brand bakery collab at BreadTalk, a collectible plush set at Din Tai Fung, and news that an established Cantonese restaurant group will run 30% off for five-plus weeks. Here is the full rundown, sorted by what needs your attention first.

1. Act Today: LiHO 1-for-1 Ends Tonight (17–18 Nov)

LiHO is running a straight 1-for-1 at its LiHO GO! outlet in Compass One: buy any eligible drink, get the second free. It runs 17–18 November only, which means today is the last day. Two catches: walk-in and counter orders only — no app or delivery — and seasonal drinks are excluded. If you work or live near Sengkang, this is your lunch-break move.

2. New This Week: KFC Sweet Purple Potato Cheese Mochi (From 18 Nov)

KFC launches its Sweet Purple Potato Cheese Mochi today — a chewy purple sweet potato mochi with a cream cheese filling, available for a limited time. The launch announcement does not state a price, so treat this as a “check the app before you order” situation rather than a confirmed dollar figure. Limited-time KFC sides in Singapore typically last a few weeks, but no end date has been announced — go sooner if you want the Instagram photo before the crowd moves on.

3. Christmas Bakes: BreadTalk x Tony’s Chocolonely (From 18 Nov)

BreadTalk’s Christmas collection this year is a collaboration with Dutch fair-trade chocolate brand Tony’s Chocolonely, landing in stores from 18 November. Expect festive breads, gingerbread treats, chocolate creations and Christmas log cakes. The headline number: Christmas donuts at 3 pieces for S$6.80. No end date has been announced for the collection, but Christmas bakes historically sell through to late December and the popular designs sell out on weekends.

4. The Collectible: Din Tai Fung Bao Zai & Shao Mai Plush Set (From 16 Nov)

Din Tai Fung’s cult-favourite Christmas plush ornament set — Bao Zai the bun and Shao Mai the dumpling — returns from 16 November at 15 selected outlets. Two ways to get it: buy the set outright for S$28.80++, or spend a minimum of S$100++ per table and receive it free. The set includes Steamed Chocolate Lava Buns (3 pieces), so the free route effectively converts a big group dinner into dessert plus collectibles. No end date has been announced; last year’s sets ran while stocks lasted.

5. The Big Discount: TungLok 30% Off ‘90-Minute Madness’ (23 Nov – 30 Dec)

From 23 November to 30 December, TungLok Group runs “The 90-Minute Madness”: 30% off à la carte food items and regular set menus at selected outlets. The catch is in the name — you get the table for 90 minutes, which is plenty for a normal dinner but rules out the four-hour family banquet. This is the window for a year-end team dinner or Christmas gathering at a proper Chinese restaurant without the Christmas-surcharge pricing that hits in late December. Reservations are strongly advised once the promotion opens.

6. More Chinese Feast Options in November

  • Famous Kitchen (Sembawang Road), 1–30 Nov: 25% off the à la carte menu every Monday to Thursday, Peking Duck at S$49 per whole duck, plus free corkage (two bottles of hard liquor or three bottles of wine). North-side dinner plans, sorted.
  • Bank of China cardholders, now till 31 Dec: 15% off eligible purchases at Cat & the Fiddle, in-store and online. Cheesecake for the potluck just got cheaper — payment must be by BOC card (promo code applies online).

7. Quick Meals Under $17 This Month

  • Pepper Lunch Velocity@Novena Square, 10–30 Nov: opening celebration with up to 24% off meal sets — Striploin Steak with drink at S$16.90, Salmon & Chicken with drink at S$15.90. Dine-in only.
  • BAO Fast Foods (BFF) at New Bahru, 11 Nov – 28 Dec: a pop-up serving Taiwanese fried chicken from S$6.50, chicken combo sets from S$9.50, creative bao, Dan Dan noodles and Taiwanese drinks — including an unusual pickle slushie. Worth a detour if you are already heading to the New Bahru strip.

8. Freebies & Free Festive Outings

  • Smöoy $1 second cup, 18–20 Nov: at Ngee Ann Polytechnic’s Block 22 Food Club only — second cup of frozen yoghurt from S$1, three days only. Strictly a campus deal; do not trek across the island for this unless you study there.
  • CHAGEE 9th Anniversary, 18 & 25 Nov: members redeem exclusive merchandise from just 9 Tea Leaves, with an 11.17am flash redemption on both days; eligible CHA Explorer, Pioneer and Master members get 20% off drinks via the CHAGEE app.
  • Sanrio Chinatown free shaved ice, 18 & 24 Nov: with any purchase at the Chinatown Sanrio store, get free Sanrio-themed shaved ice in a collectible bowl — limited to the first 25 guests per session at 11am, 1pm, 3pm and 5pm. Queue strategy matters more than appetite here.
  • Monster Planet kids meal, from 17 Nov: every S$9.90 Kids Meal Set comes with a free Christmas colouring page for children aged 7 and below, plus a festive Toasty Winterland dessert on the menu. End date not announced.
  • Free Christmas light show, 12 Nov – 30 Dec: The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands hosts the Dior Sweet Atelier of Dreams — a gingerbread-inspired winterland of festive trees and golden decorations, free to walk through. Pair it with dinner nearby for a zero-cost date night.
  • Mark the calendar: World Christmas Market at Fort Canning, 10 Dec – 3 Jan: free admission, a 38-foot real Christmas tree, festive food stalls and live performances — the big free food-adjacent event of the season.
⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal above has fine print: LiHO 1-for-1 is counter-order only and excludes seasonal drinks; TungLok’s 30% off caps your table at 90 minutes; Din Tai Fung’s free plush set needs S$100++ spend; Famous Kitchen’s 25% off runs Monday to Thursday only; Pepper Lunch’s sets are dine-in only; Cat & the Fiddle’s 15% off requires a Bank of China card; CHAGEE’s 20% off requires CHA Explorer/Pioneer/Master member status via the app; the Sanrio giveaway is capped at 25 guests per session.

Strategy: What to Do Each Day

  • Wed 18 Nov (today): LiHO 1-for-1 at Compass One (last day), first taste of KFC purple potato mochi, BreadTalk x Tony’s Chocolonely donuts hit shelves, CHAGEE 11.17am flash redemption, Sanrio shaved ice session one.
  • Thu 19 – Fri 20 Nov: Smöoy $1 second cup at Ngee Ann Poly (ends Fri); grab Famous Kitchen 25% off while it is still a weekday.
  • Mon 23 Nov: TungLok 90-Minute Madness opens — book your year-end dinner slots early, weekends go first.
  • Tue 24 – Wed 25 Nov: Sanrio shaved ice final day (24th) and CHAGEE anniversary finale (25th). End of the November freebie cluster.
  • Through 30 Nov: Pepper Lunch Velocity sets, Din Tai Fung plush sets (while stocks last), Monster Planet kids meals — all ongoing.
  • 10 Dec – 3 Jan: World Christmas Market at Fort Canning — free entry, festive food, the season’s best free outing.

The pattern to notice: the freebies cluster in late November (queue-heavy, social-media-driven), while the serious dining discounts — TungLok’s 30%, Famous Kitchen’s 25% — stretch deep into December. Smart play: knock out the one-day freebies this week, then save your restaurant budget for the 23 Nov onwards discount window, before Christmas Eve pricing takes over.
