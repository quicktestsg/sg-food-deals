Halloween Food Deals Singapore 2026: Hot Pot Sets, Cheap Beer & Free Coffee

Halloween season has quietly become one of the best times of year to eat cheap in Singapore. From Haidilao's Ghosts & Hot Pot set with free festive cakes to a genuinely free cup of coffee at IKEA, here are this week's best deals — with every date, price and catch spelled out.

Halloween used to be a non-event in Singapore. Now every F&B outlet wants a slice of the spooky season, and that means real discounts — not just pumpkin-shaped decor markups. This week's crop of deals is unusually strong, and every one has been checked against the original announcements.

1. Haidilao Ghosts & Hot Pot Set — $79.90++ With Free Halloween Cakes

Running 20 October to 2 November 2026, Haidilao's Halloween set is priced at $79.90++ and includes two free random Halloween cakes. The catch: it's at Haidilao MBS Premium only, daily from 10pm until closing — a late-night supper deal, not a dinner one.

2. DON DON DONKI Halloween Beer — 570ml for $16.50 Nett

From 16 to 31 October 2026, all DONKI Bars are pouring 570ml glasses of Suntory Sunset Beer at $16.50 nett, down from $17.80 — a saving of $1.30 per glass, with no ++ add-ons. No membership needed.

3. FREE Coffee or Tea at IKEA — No Minimum Spend

The rarest kind of deal: a true freebie. From 19 to 23 October 2026, IKEA Singapore's Swedish Restaurants are giving away free hot coffee or tea, with no minimum spend required. Walk in, order your drink, pay nothing.

☕ Freebie Strategy

IKEA's free drink runs Monday to Friday only (19–23 Oct). Don't weekend-it and leave disappointed — the offer applies at the Swedish Restaurant dine-in area.

4. Peach Garden Peking Duck — From $25++ on Weekdays

Peach Garden @ The Metropolis is serving whole roasted Peking duck from $25++ on weekdays and $35++ on weekends and public holidays, starting 16 October 2026 (end date not announced). Only 10 ducks a day, advance reservations required.

5. Eat Pizza — 50% Off Sides With Any Pizza, Till 1 Nov

Until 1 November 2026, Eat Pizza is knocking 50% off Mac & Cheese or Corn Cheese sides when you buy any pizza. You can't order the discounted side alone — the pizza is the ticket in. Ordering online in advance is recommended.

6. Grocery Corner: Wine Fair, Halloween Treats & Yoghurt Cartons

  • Cold Storage International Wine Fair (15–28 Oct, Suntec City): wine, premium sake and cheese deals with Buy-More-Save-More tiers and a $30 online welcome coupon. Ends 28 October 2026.
  • Cold Storage Halloween treats (till 31 Oct 2026): seasonal sweets and party snacks — availability varies by outlet, so check yours before a special trip.
  • Scarlett 6th Anniversary (16–25 Oct, selected outlets): Yili Ambrosial oat yoghurt at $8 for one carton or $14.90 for two — two-carton math works out to $7.45 each. Ends 25 October 2026.

The Week's Best Play

If you only do one thing: weekday Peking duck at Peach Garden (call ahead — 10 ducks a day), then IKEA's free coffee before Friday. Festive type? Haidilao MBS late-night set from 20 October. Stocking up? The Scarlett yoghurt deal and the tail end of the Wine Fair both expire within days.
