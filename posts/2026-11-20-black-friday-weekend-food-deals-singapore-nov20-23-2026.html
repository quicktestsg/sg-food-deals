Black Friday Weekend Food Deals Singapore (20-23 Nov 2026): $1 Juices, Half-Price Groceries & a Hokkaido Fair

Black Friday is not just for gadgets. This long weekend (20-23 Nov), supermarkets slash prices by up to half, a juice chain sells the second cup for $1, Japan flies in fresh Hokkaido produce, and a sushi chain gives the third plate free. Here is the full eating-and-stocking-up plan.

Everyone hunts TVs and laptops on Black Friday, but the quietest bargains this weekend (20-23 November 2026) are edible. Three supermarket chains run overlapping 3-to-4-day specials, a reopening juice bar sells the second Original-size drink for $1 — today only — and DON DON DONKI wheels out its Hokkaido Fair with produce flown in from Japan. This guide organises everything by urgency: what must happen today, what can wait until Sunday, and what runs all month.

1. Today Only (Friday, 20 Nov): Boost Juice Second Cup for $1

Boost Juice Bars is celebrating the reopening of its The Star Vista outlet with a Buy 1, Get 1 for $1 promotion on Original-sized Berries and Tropical range drinks — valid on 20 November 2026 only, at that outlet. Two fruity drinks for barely more than the price of one is about as good as beverage math gets. Usual terms and conditions apply; head down before the evening crowd.

⏰ Don't Miss It

The Boost Juice deal is a ONE-DAY offer at The Star Vista only (20 Nov 2026). If you're in the Buona Vista / one-north area today, this is the single highest-value quick stop of the weekend.

2. Also Starting Today: Three Supermarkets, Three Overlapping Sales

The 20-22 November window is the busiest supermarket weekend of the month so far. FairPrice, Giant and Prime all switch on their specials at the same time, so a single well-planned trip can stack savings across chains — or you can price-compare before choosing one.

  • FairPrice 3-Day Specials (20-22 Nov): Up to 48% off selected groceries, chocolates, snacks, beverages and household essentials, with Ferrero Rocher T30 among the featured items. Separately, Much Moore 1L ice cream is at $8.45 (half price, seven flavours) plus Ben & Jerry's deals, running until 25 Nov.
  • Giant yuu Member Deals (20-22 Nov): Up to 34% off selected fruits and meats at participating stores — but only for yuu members, so have the app ready at the checkout. Also at Giant until 25 Nov: Häagen-Dazs any 2 for $19.80 (about $9.70 saved) and Dynamo 1-for-1.
  • Prime Supermarket WOW Deals (20-23 Nov): The longest window of the three — four days, up to 51% off fresh vegetables, fruits, meat and seafood, with multi-buy offers, while stocks last.
  • Sheng Siong (if that's your nearest): Seafood specials 18-24 Nov (crab, squid and more) and a Housebrand Festive Fiesta from 19-25 Nov with up to 57% off selected housebrand groceries from $2.50.
💡 One-Trip Stacking Strategy

The three sales overlap on 20-22 Nov. If you live near more than one chain: do fresh produce and seafood at Prime or Sheng Siong (biggest fresh discounts), then hit FairPrice or Giant for chocolate, snacks and household restock (48% off / yuu member tiers). That's one weekend, two receipts, three layers of savings.

3. Japanese Weekend: DONKI Hokkaido Fair + Genki Sushi 2+1 Free

DON DON DONKI's Hokkaido Fair runs 20-30 November 2026 at participating stores, featuring premium produce flown directly from Hokkaido. Insider detail: Orchard Central gets exclusive special-price offers on 21 and 28 November only, from 2pm to 3pm — a one-hour window worth planning around if you want the sharpest prices.

Sushi fans in Punggol get their own treat: Genki Sushi at Waterway Point is running a 2+1 FREE sushi promotion from 20 November to 10 December 2026, with Salmon, Unagi and Ebi sushi rotating weekly. Spend a minimum of $50 nett and you also take home a free pair of Genki Sushi socks worth $3.90. The free sushi has no stated minimum spend, but the socks do — plan your order if you want both.

4. Cheap Eats Running Through the Month

Not everything expires on Sunday. These deals run deep into November or December, so there's no need to queue this weekend:

  • Peach Garden $1 Siew Mai Platter (from 18 Nov, end date not announced): If your NRIC contains the digit '1', show your IC at six participating outlets and get a Six Flavours Siew Mai Platter for $1. Minimum two diners, no minimum spending required. A genuinely unusual NRIC gimmick — check your IC before you go.
  • Mr Bean 31st Anniversary (18 Nov-16 Dec): The new Soy Coconut Crispy Rolls drop from $12.50 to $6.50 with the anniversary coupon — that's $6 off. Grab the coupon in-app or from the promotion page before ordering.
  • Lotteria Student Deal (19-30 Nov): Any 2 burgers for $10 with a valid student ID, or add $3.50 to upgrade with 2 regular soft drinks and 1 large fries. Available at all outlets while stocks last — a solid after-school combo for students.
  • 7-Eleven $1 Snacks (18 Nov-1 Dec): Coco Life Coconut Water and Meiji Hello Panda at $1 each, Snickers/Mars/Twix bars at $1.50, plus a separate 4-day specials push (19-22 Nov) with up to 63% off selected drinks, chocolates and ready-to-eat meals.
  • Burger King $1.50 Coffee (from 18 Nov onwards, end date not announced): Claim it in the Offers section of the BK App — the cheapest mainstream coffee fix around right now.
  • Old Chang Kee Free Puff (till 30 Nov): Buy any 5 snacks and choose a free puff from three signature varieties — an easy add-on to a regular order.

5. Hotpot Season: Haidilao 14th Anniversary (All November)

Haidilao's 14th anniversary promotion runs 1-30 November 2026: S$14++ Buy-1-Get-1-Free on selected meats, S$14++ seafood specials, and a S$29.90 recharge set at the Sun Plaza outlet. Big spenders get a lucky-draw entry with every S$140 nett spent, with prizes including an Apple iMac, iPhones, AirPods Max 2 and Dyson items. Note the ++ — meat deals are S$14++ each, not S$14 flat. With barely ten days left in November, this is the weekend to cash it in.

⚠️ Condition Check — Read Before You Go
  • Giant member prices require the yuu app/membership (20-22 Nov).
  • Lotteria $10 burger deal requires a valid student ID (19-30 Nov).
  • Mr Bean $6.50 price requires the anniversary coupon (18 Nov-16 Dec).
  • Peach Garden $1 platter requires an NRIC containing '1' and at least 2 diners (from 18 Nov).
  • Genki Sushi free socks require a $50 nett minimum spend (20 Nov-10 Dec, Waterway Point only).
  • Burger King $1.50 coffee must be claimed from the BK App (from 18 Nov).
  • Prime and Sheng Siong specials are while-stocks-last; FairPrice/Giant tiers end 22-23 Nov.

6. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Weekend

  • Friday, 20 Nov (today): Boost Juice Buy 1 Get 1 for $1 at The Star Vista (one day only). Supermarket specials all switch on — beat the weekend crowd tonight if you can.
  • Saturday, 21 Nov: DONKI Hokkaido Fair opens its first Orchard Central exclusive hour (2-3pm). Pair it with the FairPrice/Giant/Prime specials and do the week's grocery run in one trip.
  • Sunday, 22 Nov: Last day for FairPrice 3-Day Specials, Giant yuu member deals and 7-Eleven 4-day specials. Dinner option: Haidilao anniversary meat BOGO before the month runs out. Prime's WOW deals continue one extra day (till 23 Nov).
  • Next week: No rush on Mr Bean, Lotteria, Peach Garden, 7-Eleven $1 snacks or BK coffee — all run into December or until month-end. Mark 26 Nov: McDonald's launches its Maple BBQ burgers (from $10.50) and Biscoff McPops (from $2.95).

The Bottom Line

Black Friday weekend in Singapore is really a grocery-and-comfort-food weekend. The two genuinely time-critical items are today's Boost Juice $1 second cup and the 20-22 Nov supermarket window; everything else gives you runway into December. Deal facts (prices, dates, conditions) are sourced from the promotions currently running — always double-check the fine print at the store or in the app before committing, as participating outlets and stock vary by location.
