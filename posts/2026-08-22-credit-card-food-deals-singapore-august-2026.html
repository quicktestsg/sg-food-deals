No guessed regular prices, no vague 'ongoing' — every date below comes from the merchants' own announcements.

2. #1 Pick: Amex Shop Small — 30% Back, But the Clock Stops August 31

American Express's Shop Small campaign runs from August 4 to August 31, 2026, and the mechanics are unusually generous for a bank promo: S$3 cashback for every S$10 spent at a participating local business, with around 3,600 merchants signed up islandwide, and the cashback repeats up to five times — a ceiling of S$15 back per cardmember. Because the trigger is a clean S$10 threshold rather than a monthly minimum, the smart play is to keep individual transactions at exactly S$10–13: a kopi and toast set, a bowl of laksa, a couple of kaya buns. Each of those stops returns 30% if the shop is on the list.

🧾 The Math, Plainly

Five separate S$10 transactions at participating merchants = S$50 spent, S$15 returned. Effective return: 30%. One S$50 transaction at the same merchant = still only S$3 back (6%), because the S$10 threshold pays per transaction, not cumulatively, and the five redemptions are the hard cap. Split the same coffee money into five visits and the cashback rate is five times higher.

Two conditions from the announcement to flag. First, 'eligible Card Members' — Amex excludes some card types from these campaigns, so check your specific card on the Shop Small page before planning the fortnight around it. Second, the ~3,600 participating merchants are the universe; plenty of coffee shops and hawkers are not on it. The merchant map on Amex's campaign page is the deciding factor, not this article.

3. Travel Cards: Wednesday Is the New Payday

If a year-end trip is on the calendar, two cardholder deals are live and they cover different halves of the booking. Trip.com's OCBC Wander Wednesday runs every Wednesday from July 15 to December 30, 2026: eligible OCBC Visa cardholders get up to S$120 off selected flights and hotels, working in S$60 and S$120 discount tiers. The next windows are August 26, September 2, and September 9 — worth checking before booking anything on a Tuesday night out of impatience.

The hotel half: Agoda is giving DBS/POSB cardholders an extra 20% off hotel stays until August 31, 2026, on top of sale rates the platform advertises at up to 60% off. The two promos don't combine — they sit on different platforms and different cards — but they do mean both halves of a trip (flight + room) each have a card angle this month. The Agoda offer expires the same day as Shop Small, so Monday, August 31 is the shared deadline for both.

4. The One-Day Specials Pattern: Missed the Eggs? Here's How to Catch the Next One

On Saturday, August 15, DBS/POSB cardholders got S$3 off eggs (30-count) and S$3 off rice (5kg) at participating Sheng Siong and Giant stores, with no minimum spend, paid with any DBS/POSB credit or debit card. That deal has passed — we flag it precisely because it is a recurring format, not a one-off. DBS runs these single-day cardholder grocery specials at roughly monthly intervals, always on a Saturday, always no-minimum, always at the two supermarkets. The play: if you hold a DBS or POSB card, check the bank's own promotions page on the first Friday of each month. Two of these specials per quarter easily cover a month of weekend grocery runs.

5. Beyond Bank Cards: The Membership Discount Ladder

The non-bank cards are pulling weight this cycle too. The three supermarket weekday discounts are live in the current leaflet cycle (which runs to Wednesday, August 26): Giant gives seniors an extra 4% off every Tuesday, Cold Storage gives shoppers aged 60 and above an extra 3% off every Wednesday, and FairPrice gives Blue CHAS cardholders an extra 3% off every Thursday. These stack on top of the weekly promotional prices — FairPrice's current leaflet is up to 61% off, Giant's is up to 63% — and they recur every week, which no bank cashback campaign does.

Two shorter-lived membership perks close this weekend. Esso Smiles cardholders get an instant 23% off all Esso Synergy fuels from August 21 to August 24, ending at 12pm on Monday — if the tank is running low, this is the weekend to fill. And at Takashimaya, the August 21–23 Brabantia event (up to 42% off home essentials, plus a gift-with-purchase) also carries Double Bonus Points for Takashimaya cardholders, which effectively pays twice on the same receipt for anyone who already shops there.

7. What to Skip, and the Fine Print That Actually Bites

Skip counting the headline percentages as savings. 'Up to 63% off' at Giant describes the single deepest item discount, and availability differs store to store; 'up to 60% off' on Agoda is the platform's sale sticker before the cardholder 20% is applied to eligible rates. The only numbers in this guide you can bank are the fixed ones: S$3 per S$10 (Amex, 5×cap), S$60/S$120 tiers (OCBC, selected items), 20% (Agoda, cardholder rates), 23% (Esso, all Synergy fuels), and 3–4% (supermarket weekday tiers, on eligible baskets).

⚠️ Condition Check

Amex Shop Small: eligible cards only, ~3,600 participating merchants, ends Aug 31. OCBC Wander Wednesday: selected flights/hotels, Wednesdays only, ends Dec 30. Agoda: DBS/POSB cardholder rates, ends Aug 31. Esso: Smiles card required, ends 12pm Aug 24. Supermarket weekday tiers: proof required (CHAS card or age verification); exclusion lists were not published in the announcements — confirm eligible items at checkout before loading the trolley. Credit card promotions also sit outside any card's own reward caps — confirm with the bank how the spend is categorised.

Bottom line: the best card deal of the fortnight is also the smallest one. Five deliberate S$10 meals at Amex-participating shops return S$15 — a 30% effective discount on food you were buying anyway. The travel deals are bigger in absolute terms but need a trip to justify them; the membership days need nothing but the right card and the right weekday. Stack the ones that match your actual spending, ignore the headline percentages, and get it all done before the August 31 double deadline.
