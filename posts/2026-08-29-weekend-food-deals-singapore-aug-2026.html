Weekend Food Deals Singapore (Aug 29–31, 2026): 1-for-1 Bubble Tea, $0.35 Sushi & Free Crab Bee Hoon

The last weekend of August is stacked: HEYTEA's reopening 1-for-1 ends Sunday, DBS/POSB cardholders get $3 off eggs and rice on Saturday only, and September's free-food deals start in three days. Here's the full battle plan.

August bows out with one of the densest deal weekends of the month. Payday promotions are still running, the supermarkets are fighting over your grocery basket with overlapping 4-day sales, and a wave of September buffet freebies — free crab bee hoon, kids dine free, 5-dine-1-free Korean BBQ — starts on Tuesday. This guide organizes everything by urgency: what dies this weekend, what's running all month, and what to book ahead for.

1. Dying This Weekend — Grab Before Monday

HEYTEA ION Orchard: 1-for-1 Drinks (ends Sun, Aug 30)

HEYTEA's newly renovated ION Orchard outlet is celebrating its reopening with a 1-for-1 drinks deal from August 28 to 30, 2026 — Sunday is the last day. Expect Orchard-weekend queues; go off-peak if you can. Bonus for patient fans: from September 14–20, the same outlet gives a free oil-wax bag with any in-store drink purchase, limited to one per customer while stocks last.

Kenangan Coffee: Buy 1 Get 1 Free (ends TODAY, Aug 29)

The payday BOGO at Kenangan Coffee runs August 27–29 only: buy one Regular coffee, get a second Regular coffee of the same flavour free. The catch — it's exclusive to the Kenangan Coffee app, so claim the voucher before you order. Today is the final day.

Kei Kaisendon: 1-for-1 Kaisendon at AMK Hub (ends Sun, Aug 30)

Northeast folks, this one's yours: Kei Kaisendon at AMK Hub is running a 1-for-1 kaisendon promotion from August 28 to 30, 2026. Bring a friend, order two dons, pay for one. The source doesn't specify which dons are included, so check the in-store terms before committing to the premium toppings.

Menya Kokoro: Cosplay for Up to 20% Off (Sat–Sun only, Aug 29–30)

The most fun deal of the weekend: Menya Kokoro's Suntec City outlet gives 10% off your meal if you show up in cosplay AND follow their Instagram this Saturday and Sunday (Aug 29–30, 2026). Post a Reel or TikTok of your visit tagging the brand and you stack another 10% — that's up to 20% off your mazemen for the price of a little courage.

Baskin-Robbins: 31% Off Handpacked Tubs (Aug 29–31)

The classic 31%-off handpacked promotion returns for the last three days of the month: August 29–31, 2026, mix-and-match flavours in your tub. Stock the freezer before the long September stretch.

Also closing this weekend

  • TOMORO COFFEE 1-for-1 (till Aug 31, app-only): buy one drink, get one free exclusively via the TOMORO COFFEE SG App.
  • umisushi 19th anniversary (Aug 26–30): spend $19 in a single receipt, get a $5 voucher for a future visit.
  • YIMANFEN at 313@SOMERSET (Aug 28–30): opening promo with 50% off the second drink plus a guaranteed-win scratch card.
  • Museum of Ice Cream anniversary bash (Sat, Aug 29): free face painting, glitter tattoos and balloon sculpting as part of the 5th-anniversary all-day celebration. Separately, eligible past visitors can claim a free return General Admission ticket until Sep 30.
💡 Saturday One-Two Punch

If you're a DBS/POSB cardholder, Saturday Aug 29 is your day: hit Sheng Siong or Giant for the $3-off eggs-and-rice card deal (details below), pay your hawker lunch with PayLah! for the returning $3 Saturday cashback, and finish with Kenangan's last BOGO coffee. Three separate promotions, one afternoon.

That's up to $6 off pantry staples in one trip, but it's strictly a one-day offer — miss Saturday and it's gone.

3. Ongoing Cheap Eats Under $5

Megumi Japanese Restaurant: Sushi at $0.35 a Piece (Joo Chiat)

From August 25, 2026 onwards (end date not announced), Megumi in Joo Chiat offers sushi at $0.35 per piece — up to 8 pieces — with a $35 minimum spend. It's a dine-in sweetener rather than a standalone cheap lunch: go with a friend, order two mains past the $35 mark, and pad the table with near-free sushi. The source notes this is well below the usual per-piece price.

Hello Arigato: $2.99 Coffee & Soda, $3.99 Matcha (Cross Street)

Until September 5, 2026, Hello Arigato's Cross Street outlet pours selected coffee and soda for $2.99 and matcha for $3.99 — but it's capped at 50 cups a day, so this is a morning-call deal, not a late-afternoon one.

Yakiniku Like: Smoked Duck From $2.90

The popular Smoked Duck with Original BBQ Sauce is back from August 26 to October 7, 2026: $2.90 as a set add-on or $3.90 à la carte. A cheap way to upgrade a rice bowl.

  • Pizza Hut $10 Large Pan Pizza (Aug 25 – Sep 9): takeaway deal on selected Tuesdays and Wednesdays only — check which dates qualify before ordering. The new Cheesy 7 Korean Soy Garlic pizza runs from $13.95 in a separate promo till Sep 16.

4. September Preview: The Free-Food Buffet Wave Starts Tuesday

September 1 flips on a cluster of buffet-based freebies across the island. None of these require a promo code at the table, but each has a structural condition — group size, day of the week, or who's paying — so read the catches before you book.

  • Penang Culture (Compass One) — FREE Crab Bee Hoon with buffet (from Sep 1, ongoing): the buffet upgrades with a new crab bee hoon dish included free. Buffet price not confirmed in the source — check with the restaurant before you go.
  • I'm Kim Korean BBQ (Dhoby Ghaut) — 4PLUS1 (Sep 1–30): dine in a group of five with four paying, and the lowest-paying diner eats free. Sessions are capped at 90 minutes. Students get their own separate 1-for-1 BBQ promo Sep 1–13 — booking required, lunch and dinner windows apply, check eligibility terms.
  • Typhoon Taiwanese Cafe (Orchard Road) — Kids Dine Free (Sep 1–30): children eat free with two paying buffet adults — built for the September school holidays.
  • Seoul Garden — Two Gentlemen Dine Free (every Thursday in Sept): with two paying adults at Thursday dinner, two gentlemen buffet places are complimentary.
  • TSUJIRI — Buy 2 Get 1 Free Tea (Sep 1–30, selected outlets): Mid-Autumn promo alongside two new Japanese tea additions.
  • Starbucks Voucher Pack (Aug 31 – Sep 6): $12.90 for six vouchers covering drinks, a Venti BOGO, merchandise savings and a free size-up.
⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal in this guide has fine print: Kenangan and TOMORO BOGOs are app-exclusive; Giant/Cold Storage yuu specials need a yuu membership; Megumi's $0.35 sushi requires $35 minimum spend; I'm Kim's 4PLUS1 caps your table at 90 minutes and frees only the cheapest diner; Pizza Hut's $10 pizza works only on selected Tuesdays and Wednesdays; the DBS egg-and-rice $3 discounts are Saturday-only and need a DBS/POSB card as payment. Where a source didn't publish an end date (Mr Bean, TANYU skewers, Megumi, Penang Culture's buffet price), we say so — confirm in-store before making a special trip.
