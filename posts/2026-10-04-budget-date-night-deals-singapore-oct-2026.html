Budget Date Night Singapore (October 2026): $7.50 Highballs, $16.99++ Hotpot Buffet & 2-for-$20 Pizza

Date night doesn't need a $100-a-head price tag. October 2026's promo calendar has off-peak hotpot buffets, a pay-4-get-more Korean BBQ, two-for-$15 highballs and a caviar high tea that somehow stays under $35 — here's the full couples' playbook, with dates, prices and every catch.

The October 2026 promo calendar is quietly one of the best months for cheap dates in a while. Two different buffet chains are running group promos all month, a Japanese megastore is pouring two highballs for $15 nett, and there's even a caviar-and-chocolate-fondue high tea in Orchard for $30++. Here's how to build a date night (or date afternoon) around them — ordered roughly from cheapest to most indulgent, with the fine print attached.

1. The Couch Date: Canadian Pizza 2-for-$20 (From Oct 3, Ongoing)

The cheapest proper dinner for two on this list. From 3 October 2026 onwards, Canadian Pizza Singapore is doing two pizzas for $20 — pick-up only, from the Supervalue flavour range. That's $10 a pizza for a couch-and-Netflix night, which is hard to beat for a zero-effort date. Two catches: it's pick-up (no delivery at this price) and the promo is listed as ongoing with no announced end date — treat it as available until the chain says otherwise, and confirm at your outlet when ordering.

2. The Drinks Legs: DON DON DONKI Highballs, 2 for $15 Nett (Oct 1–31)

All October (1–31 October 2026, all DONKI Bars islandwide), the Daimyo-No Shinobu Highball is $8.80 a glass — or 2 for $15 nett, which the promo itself pegs as a $2.60 saving. Two glasses at $7.50 each, nett (no ++ surprises), at a bar inside a Japanese megastore. This is the rare promo that works as a pre-dinner drink stop, a post-dinner nightcap, or the entire date if you're both tired. The vibe is decidedly not a cocktail bar — it's donki neon and grocery baskets — but for $7.50 a pour with race-season energy, it does the job.

3. The Late-Afternoon Move: Shabu Sai Off-Peak Buffet $16.99++ (Ongoing)

From 2 October 2026 onwards, Shabu Sai's Off-Peak Special puts its shabu-shabu buffet at $16.99++ per person — but only Monday to Thursday, 2.46pm to 4.45pm, with 70 minutes of dining time. The unusual timing is actually a feature for couples with flexible schedules: a 3pm hotpot date costs $16.99++ where dinner would run much more (the source doesn't quote the dinner price, so no fake comparison here — check their site). Valid at all Shabu Sai outlets until further notice, excluding eves of public holidays and public holidays themselves. Remember ++ means service charge and GST on top, so budget a little over $20 all-in per person.

4. The Group Play: I'm Kim Korean BBQ 4PLUS1 (Oct 1–31)

Grab another couple. At I'm Kim Korean BBQ (School of the Arts, Dhoby Ghaut), the 4PLUS1 promotion runs all October 2026: gather four and one eligible diner eats free. The buffet price isn't stated in the source material, so check the current rate when booking — what's confirmed is the mechanics (four diners, one free eligible diner) and the catches: advance booking is required and you'll need a promo code. A KBBQ date where the bill splits five ways but only four pay is one of the better group-value plays this month.

5. The Double-Date Alternative: GoroGoro 2+2 Steamboat (Oct 1–31)

If Korean BBQ isn't your group's thing, GoroGoro Steamboat & Korean Buffet at The Centrepoint has its own October four-person play: the 2+2 Steamboat Buffet Promotion, 1–31 October 2026, at selected dining times with reservations required. Like I'm Kim, the source doesn't publish the buffet rate in the excerpt — treat that as a check-before-booking item, and note the "selected dining times" means you can't just walk in at 8pm on a Saturday and expect the promo. The Centrepoint location does make it easy to pair with a Somerset stroll after.

6. The Fancy One: $30++ Caviar & Chocolate Fondue High Tea in Orchard

For a date that feels ten times its price: Mama Shelter Singapore in Orchard has launched a new high tea set — Chocolate, Caviar & more — at $30++ per person. Caviar and chocolate fondue at thirty dollars is an anomaly in this neighbourhood, where hotel afternoon teas routinely run well past that before the ++. The set is described as newly launched with no end date announced in the source, so treat it as available until stated otherwise and book ahead — high-tea slots in Orchard go fast on weekends. At $30++ per person, the all-in figure lands north of $35 after service charge and GST, which is still the most "expensive-feeling" item on this list.

7. The Cheap Shared Feast: Penang Culture $8.80++ New Dishes (Oct 1–31)

Penang Culture is celebrating its Big Boss's birthday all October 2026 with four new dishes at $8.80++ each: Sesame Chicken Drumlet, Blackcurrant Fish Fillet, Chicken Chop with Green Belado, and Satay Chicken Stew. The play here is simple — order three or four between two people and you've built a shared Nyonya-style feast at hawker-adjacent prices. The source doesn't state which outlets are participating, so check before heading to your nearest branch.

💡 The Sub-$30 Date Night Blueprint

Here's the cheapest full evening for two this month: pick up two Canadian Pizza Supervalue pies ($20 total), skip drinks out, and save the fancy outing for a weekday — Shabu Sai at 3pm ($16.99++ a head) followed by two DONKI highballs ($15 nett for two) lands the whole afternoon-plus-evening at roughly $24.50 a person before service charge and GST on the buffet, comfortably under $30 all-in. Or invert it: keep weeknights cheap and save the $30++ caviar high tea for the one Saturday you want to feel fancy — still under $40 a head, all-in.

The Bottom Line

October 2026 is the month of the group promo: 4PLUS1 KBBQ, 2+2 steamboat, 2-for-$15 highballs, 2-for-$20 pizza — the arithmetic of dining out has quietly shifted in favour of pairs and groups. For couples, the calendar offers a clear ladder: $10/head on the couch, ~$20/head at a 3pm hotpot, mid-$20s with drinks, or a sub-$40 caviar afternoon in Orchard. The only real deadlines are the month-enders (most promos run 1–31 October) and the KFC one-dayer on the 16th — everything else rewards planning over speed. Book the ones needing bookings, set an alarm for Shabu Sai's 2.46pm window, and enjoy a month where dating doesn't cost extra.
