Coffee & Bubble Tea Deals Singapore September 2026: Starbucks 25% Off, Luckin 1-for-1 & Free Pancakes

Starbucks' 25% off ends tomorrow, Dunkin' Dozen Day is today only, and Luckin's grand-opening 1-for-1 runs through 16 Sep. Here's the full caffeine-savings playbook for the week of Sep 12–16, with every condition spelled out.

Singapore runs on kopi and bubble tea, and this short week is unusually generous: three of the biggest chains are running overlapping promotions, two of them expire within days. This guide organises everything by deadline — what to grab today, what can wait until Monday, and what runs all month — with the fine print (minimum items, outlet restrictions, membership requirements) for each deal.

1. Ending in 1–4 Days — Grab These First

Starbucks: 25% Off Drinks, Food & Merch (ends Sun, 13 Sep)

From 7 to 13 September 2026, Starbucks Singapore is taking 25% off eligible drinks, food and selected merchandise when you buy at least two eligible items in a single transaction. The catch is twofold: you must purchase a minimum of two qualifying items, and you need to present the promotional image in-store to redeem. Grab a friend, order two drinks, and screenshot the promo before you queue — tomorrow is the last day.

Dunkin' Dozen Day: Buy 9 Donuts, Get 3 Free (today only, 12 Sep)

Saturday 12 September 2026 is Dunkin' SG's Dozen Day: buy 9 donuts and get 3 free — a full dozen while paying for nine. It's a one-day-only offer, so if the office or family has been hinting at snacks, today is the day. The source did not state a per-customer limit, so check in store if you're planning a big order.

Luckin Coffee: 1-for-1 Drinks at AMK Hub (ends Wed, 16 Sep)

To celebrate its new outlet at AMK Hub Cheers, Luckin Coffee is running a grand-opening 1-for-1 drinks promotion from 3 to 16 September 2026, with daily operating hours of 7:00am to 11:00pm. Bonus for first-timers: the Luckin Coffee app also has a $0.99 first-cup offer. Two deals, one trip — bring a colleague and both walk out paying cents on the dollar.

iTea: Buy 2 Get 1 Free at Aljunied (ends Tue, 15 Sep)

iTea's new Aljunied outlet is celebrating its grand opening from 9 to 15 September 2026 with a buy-2-get-1-free drinks promotion. East-siders, this is your week — the promo is tied to the new store, so don't expect it at other branches.

TenRen's Tea: 50% Off Second Cup, 913 King's Oolong Day (13–15 Sep)

From 13 to 15 September 2026, TenRen's Tea celebrates 913 King's Oolong Sharing Day: buy any two selected 913 Series tea drinks and get 50% off the second cup, plus 10% off selected 913 Series retail items. Important condition: this runs at The Star Vista outlet — plan accordingly if you're not in the west.

⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal above has a catch: Starbucks needs 2+ eligible items AND the promo image shown in-store (ends 13 Sep); Dunkin's Dozen Day is 12 Sep only; Luckin's 1-for-1 is at the AMK Hub Cheers outlet only (app needed for the $0.99 first cup); iTea's B2G1 is Aljunied only; TenRen's second-cup deal is The Star Vista only, on selected 913 Series drinks.

2. Weekday Recurring Deals — Worth Planning Around

ChaPanda: 20% Off Everything, Mondays & Tuesdays (from 5 Sep, ongoing)

ChaPanda's Century Square outlet in Tampines takes 20% off all drinks, all sizes, every Monday and Tuesday from 5 September 2026 onwards. The source lists no end date — treat it as ongoing until announced otherwise. Tampines residents just found their fixed Mon/Tue tea appointment.

The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf: 2 Breakfast Sets for $12.50 (weekdays before 12pm)

From 7 September 2026 onwards, The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf offers its new Mini Croissants & Scrambled Eggs Breakfast Set at $8.50 — or two sets for $12.50. Conditions: Monday to Friday only, until 12pm. No end date has been announced. Pair it with a colleague and each set effectively costs $6.25.

3. Ongoing Snackable Deals (No Rush, But Don't Sleep on Them)

Mr Bean: Buy 2 Orh Nee Mochi Pancakes, Get Any Pancake Free

From 11 September 2026 onwards, buy two Orh Nee Mochi Pancakes at Mr Bean and get any pancake of your choice free — while stocks last. No end date announced yet, but 'while stocks last' deals tend to die with supply, not dates. Orh Nee season is here; act accordingly.

Shake Shake In A Tub: Matcha Latte From $3.80

Shake Shake In A Tub's Matcha Series features a Ceremonial Matcha Latte at $3.80 and a Matcha Strawberry Latte at $4.80 (promoted as of 11 September 2026; end date not announced). For matcha fans on a budget, this is one of the lower posted prices this week — no gimmicks, just a straight menu price.

Mister Donut: Flossy Pon De Lion 2 for $5.20 (till 1 Nov, members only)

Mister Donut's first-ever savoury donut in Singapore — the Flossy Pon De Lion, topped with Japanese mayonnaise and chicken floss — runs 1 September to 1 November 2026 at 2 for $5.20, with a choice of Fluffy Floss Lion or Spicy Floss Lion. Condition: the price is for &Rewards members only, so sign up before you order.

Krispy Kreme: Half Dozen $17.99 / Dozen $33.88 (from 8 Sep, while stocks last)

From 8 September 2026 onwards, Krispy Kreme Singapore offers box promotions at Half Dozen for $17.99 and Dozen for $33.88, available at all outlets while stocks last. End date not announced. Useful for office treats or seasonal-flavour sampling without commitment to a full-priced dozen.

Bonus: VITAGEN Free Sanrio Glass Container (7 Sep – 4 Oct, home delivery only)

Not coffee, but the drinks fridge deserves this one: from 7 September to 4 October 2026, every four eligible VITAGEN packs ordered through VITAGEN Home Delivery comes with a free 640ml Sanrio characters Glass Container — four Singapore-inspired designs to collect, with free doorstep delivery. Condition: home delivery orders only; supermarket purchases don't count.

The three outlet-locked deals sit in different corners of the island: Luckin's 1-for-1 is at AMK Hub (north-central), iTea's B2G1 at Aljunied (east), TenRen's at The Star Vista (west), and ChaPanda's Mon/Tue 20% at Tampines (far east). Don't try to chain them in one day — pick the one nearest you and bank the rest for another week if their dates allow. Only TenRen's (ends 15 Sep) and iTea (ends 15 Sep) are truly urgent.
