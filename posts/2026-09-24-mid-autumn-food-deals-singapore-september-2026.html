Mid-Autumn Food Deals Singapore (Sep 24–30, 2026): Pizza Hut $19.90 Feast, 1-for-1 McSpicy & TamJai

The Mid-Autumn long weekend lands Sep 25–26, and it collides head-on with payday deal season. Here is the full game plan: last-chance 1-for-1s dying this week, festival feasts under $20, and the deals that stretch into October.

Mid-Autumn Festival falls on Friday, September 25 this year, and Singapore's F&B scene has clearly decided to celebrate with a deal avalanche. A 61%-off Pizza Hut bundle, a 1-for-1 McSpicy window that ends this Friday, TamJai SamGor quietly launching a takeaway 1-for-1, and a panda-themed lantern night at River Wonders for $14 — all within the same seven days. This guide sorts everything by urgency: what dies this week, what carries the festival weekend, and what can wait.

1. Ending This Week — Grab Before They're Gone

McDonald's 1-for-1 McSpicy — ends Fri, Sep 25

The app-exclusive 1-for-1 McSpicy runs daily from 2pm to 5pm until September 25. Order through the McDonald's app during the afternoon window and two McSpicy burgers cost the price of one. If you have been putting this off, Thursday and Friday afternoons are your last shots. Bonus for early risers: from September 24, McDonald's is also giving away free Breakfast Wraps through its #WrapUpYourWorkout giveaway — clock 6,500 steps in a day and upload a screenshot from any fitness app to redeem (terms apply, while the giveaway lasts).

Kenangan Coffee buy-1-get-1 — ends Thu, Sep 24

Kenangan Coffee's payday BOGO wraps up today (September 22–24), exclusive to the Kenangan Coffee app. Two drinks for the price of one — the cheapest way to survive the pre-festival workday. Also ending today: Dunkin's payday deals (buy 5 donuts, get the 2 lowest-priced free, plus 1-for-1 Cold Brew and Teh Tarik all day at participating outlets).

Shihlin 1-for-1 Ricebowls at The Seletar Mall — Wed–Thu only

A blink-and-miss deal: Shihlin Taiwan Street Snacks at The Seletar Mall is running 1-for-1 on its Braised Chicken or Chiayi Scallion Chicken Ricebowls (usually $7.50 each) on September 23–24 only, while stocks last. Northeast-side readers, this is today.

More 1-for-1s dying by Friday, Sep 25

  • Shake Shack Summer Greek (Sep 21–25): 1-for-1 burgers, chicken bites and fries on a daily rotating schedule — redeem the 'Double the Greek flavour' voucher via the Happy Point app at participating outlets.
  • Bari Bari Steak @ JEM (Sep 22–25): 1-for-1 teppan mains for the new west-side outlet's grand opening, plus a $10 return voucher with every transaction (voucher for a subsequent visit with minimum spend, terms apply).
  • CHICHA San Chen $3.50 Pure Tea (Sep 21–25): members get Pure Tea in one of six tea bases for $3.50 with a member voucher — walk-in only at participating stores, excludes the Jewel outlet.
⚠️ Condition Check

McSpicy 1-for-1: app orders only, 2–5pm window. Kenangan BOGO: app only, ends Sep 24. Shake Shack: Happy Point app voucher required. CHICHA: member voucher, walk-in, excludes Jewel. Bari Bari: JEM outlet only. Shihlin: The Seletar Mall only, Sep 23–24. Almost every deal here is channel-locked — download the app before you queue.

2. Mid-Autumn Weekend (Sep 25–26) — Festival Feasts & the Lantern Night

Pizza Hut Mid-Autumn Feast Bundle — $19.90 (61% off)

Running September 24–30: one Large Favourite Pizza, a 10-piece Crispy Chicken Fries and Garlic Bread for $19.90 takeaway, down from the usual $50.90 bundle price — a stated 61% saving. For a family gathering where nobody wants to cook on festival night, this is the strongest sub-$20 play of the week. Check the Pizza Hut app or site for ordering channels and any outlet exclusions.

River Wonders Mid-Autumn Night — tickets from $14, free lantern

On September 25 and 26 only, Mandai River Wonders runs evening festivities from 6.30pm to 9pm: admission from $14 and a complimentary lantern for guests, in a panda-themed Mid-Autumn setting. A rare cheap-and-magical family option — book online ahead, because two nights only means finite capacity.

Festival sets at sit-down spots

  • Nong Geng Ji Reunion Set (Sep 21 – Oct 4): a 4-person reunion set at $99++ with signature dishes and drinks, plus a limited-edition keychain while stocks last, at Collyer Quay Centre and VivoCity. Budget roughly S$116+ after the prevailing service charge and GST for the table.
  • PlayMade Mid-Autumn Duo Bundle (from Sep 23, while tote stocks last): Pomelo Blossom Milk Tea + Everyday Tote for $10.90, or the Lemon Yuzu Pomelo Blossom Tea version for $11.90, via app, walk-in or delivery. The drink is fine — you are really here for the tote.
  • The Westin mooncake clearance (final week): the Assorted Mooncake Set is $48.60 nett instead of $108 — four traditional and tea-inspired flavours in a gift-ready carrier. 'Final week' means buy now or accept it is gone.

3. Newly Launched — No End Date Announced Yet

TamJai SamGor 1-for-1 Mixian (takeaway only)

The biggest sleeper deal of the week: from September 23 onwards, TamJai SamGor is offering 1-for-1 Guo Qiao Mixian at Chinatown Point, The Clementi Mall and Junction 8 — takeaway only, with your choice of soup base and up to 16 fan-favourite toppings. No end date has been announced, so treat it as 'could vanish any day'. Pro move: order two different soup bases, since both bowls are priced independently at the lower tier.

  • CHAGEE $5 Members' Day voucher (from Sep 23): CHA Explorer, Pioneer and Master members get a $5 drink voucher valid on selected Fresh Milk Tea, Brewed Tea and Fresh Fruit Tea — effectively a premium cup for loose change.
  • BlackTree @ 313@somerset (Sep 22 – Oct 5): 50% off the second drink and 20% off new launches; the Celebrity Recommended Set (Milky Cat Cheese Thai Milk Tea plus pairing) is $11.50, down from $16.80.
  • Takagi Ramen 1-for-1 Donburi on GrabFood (from Sep 23): the new Egg Mayo Salad Don and Tori Karaage Don, two for one while the promotion runs.
  • Honeybee Wednesday BOGO (every Wednesday, 11.30am–3pm): buy one get one free ice cream at Yishun Junction 9 and Downtown East — a standing midweek treat with no announced end date.

Mid-Autumn dinner at home just got cheaper.
