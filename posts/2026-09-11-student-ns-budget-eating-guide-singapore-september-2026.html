Student & NS Budget Eating Guide Singapore September 2026: $19.90 Yakiniku Buffet, Free Häagen-Dazs & $10.90 Lunch Sets

Between the September school holidays and SAFRA Open House, this is peak season for ID-card dining. A $19.90 all-you-can-grill buffet, a free 473ml Häagen-Dazs pint for SAFRA members, and group buffets where the third diner eats free — every deal verified with dates and conditions.

Students and NSmen are the two most underrated discount classes in Singapore. This month proves it: Yakiniku Like has launched a $19.90 student-only afternoon buffet, SAFRA Open House is giving away full pints of Häagen-Dazs vanilla, and two Korean buffet chains are running group promos that slash the per-head cost if you show up with the right number of friends. Add $10.90 café lunch sets and 50% off sashimi, and September covers every meal slot from breakfast to dinner. Here is the full guide, sorted by how much time you have left.

1. Ending This Weekend — Starbucks 25% Off (Till Sep 13)

Starbucks Singapore is running 25% off from 7 to 13 September 2026, and the mechanics matter: you must buy a minimum of two eligible items in-store and present a digital voucher at checkout. Mobile Order & Pay is excluded — this is a walk-in-only deal. Grab a study partner, order two drinks, and the discount applies to both. With the school holidays still on, it is a cheap way to anchor a three-hour revision session.

2. Two Weeks Left — The Korean Buffet Double (Both Till Sep 21)

Two group-friendly buffet promos are running in parallel until 21 September 2026, and they suit different squad sizes. Captain Kim Korean BBQ & Hotpot is running a 2-dine-1-free buffet at selected timeslots across its Tampines, Woodlands and Clementi outlets — three eat, two pay. GoroGoro Steamboat & Korean Buffet at The Centrepoint on Orchard Road is running a 2-for-2 hotpot buffet promo, limited to selected weekday and weekend lunch and dinner timeslots, and reservations are recommended because slots are capped. Neither requires a student ID, which makes them ideal for a mixed group of NSmen, students and working friends.

3. The Headliner — Yakiniku Like $19.90 Student Buffet (Sep 7–Oct 2)

Yakiniku Like's new Student Buffet runs 7 September to 2 October 2026, priced at $19.90 per person, and the timing is clever: it is only available on weekdays from 2pm to 5pm — the dead zone between lunch and dinner when the grills sit empty. Locations are AMK Hub, Bukit Panjang Plaza and Pasir Ris Mall. The spread includes unlimited Karubi (beef shortplate), pork belly and chicken, plus free-flow sides and one bottled drink per person (per the source announcement). The buffet is explicitly a student promotion, so expect to show a valid student ID — the source does not spell out the verification method, so bring the physical card rather than assuming a photo will do. Skip the lunch crowd, walk in at 2pm sharp, and grill for three hours.

4. All Month — Free Häagen-Dazs for SAFRA Members (Sep 1–30)

SAFRA Open House 2026 runs through September, and the food freebie is a good one: a free 473ml pint of Häagen-Dazs vanilla ice cream for SAFRA members, available 1 to 30 September 2026. The catch is volume: redemption is capped at the first 500 per club, and you need to register before heading down. NSmen get SAFRA membership as part of serving, so if you have not activated yours, this plus the rest of the Open House lineup is the excuse to do it. Check which club near you still has stock before travelling — by mid-month the popular clubs run dry.

5. Also All Month — Tomi Sushi 50% Off Sashimi (Sep 7–30)

From 7 to 30 September 2026, Tomi Sushi is taking 50% off selected Buri (yellowtail) and Kanpachi sashimi, sushi and platters at all outlets. No student ID needed — this one is open to everyone, which makes it the upscale counterweight to all the hawker-level deals this month. Half-price sashimi is the rare promo where the discount is deep and the product barely discounted in quality terms; just note it applies to selected items, so check which cuts are on the list before committing to a platter.

6. The Weekday Lunch Anchor — Cedele $10.90 Sandwich Set (Sep 7–Oct 11)

Cedele's Weekday Sandwich Set costs $10.90 and runs 7 September to 11 October 2026, weekdays only. You pick a sandwich — Signature Rosemary Chicken, Roasted Chicken or Grilled Beetroot Patty — and pair it with a drink, soup or cookie. It works at any Cedele outlet, no membership required. For a school-week lunch that is not food court fare, this is the most reliable sub-$11 sit-down option running right now.

7. Ongoing, No End Date Announced — Breakfast & Snack Fillers

Three more deals plug the remaining meal slots, all with start dates confirmed but end dates not announced. The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf (from 7 September 2026): the new Mini Croissants & Scrambled Eggs Breakfast Set is $8.50, or two sets for $12.50, Monday to Friday until 12pm — split with a friend and it is $6.25 each. Ayam Penyet President (from 5 September 2026): every main course set ordered between 7:00am and 11:30am comes with a free coffee or tea, at Hillion Mall and White Sands only. Mister Donut (1 September to 1 November 2026): the new Flossy Pon De Lion savoury donut is 2 for $5.20 for &Rewards members — free to join, and the membership pays for itself on the first box.

Strategy: The Student Week, Mapped

  • By Sunday, Sep 13: Starbucks 25% off ends — last study-session coffee run. CB&TL breakfast (2 for $12.50) is the weekday alternative.
  • Weekday mornings: Ayam Penyet President free kopi with breakfast set (Hillion/White Sands), or CB&TL two-set split at $6.25 each.
  • Weekday lunch: Cedele $10.90 sandwich set — runs to Oct 11, no rush.
  • Weekday 2pm: Yakiniku Like $19.90 student buffet — the best food-per-dollar slot of the month, but only till Oct 2.
  • Weekend squads (till Sep 21): three people hit Captain Kim (3 eat, 2 pay); pairs hit GoroGoro at The Centrepoint — book the timeslot first.
  • Somewhere this month: register and redeem the SAFRA Häagen-Dazs pint before your club's 500 run out, and treat the family to Tomi Sushi's half-price sashimi (till Sep 30).

Bottom line: the student card and the SAFRA card are worth more in September than any credit card promo for this crowd. Every price and date above comes from merchant announcements this month; where an end date was not published (CB&TL, Ayam Penyet President), it is labelled as ongoing rather than assumed. Confirm timeslot availability before travelling, and eat like a king on an allowance budget.
