Payday Food Deals Singapore (30 Oct – 9 Nov 2026): 1-for-1 Starbucks, $7.90 Yakiniku & Cheap Eats

Salary just landed on 29 October — and the deals landed with it. Here is the smartest way to treat yourself this payday week: one-day yakiniku at $7.90++, a build-your-own Haidilao feast at S$38.80++, $8.90 sushi by the box, and a 1-for-1 coffee run that ends Sunday.

Payday week is the best week to be a foodie in Singapore — brands know your account just got topped up, and they roll out their biggest hooks right on cue. This guide rounds up every worthwhile food deal running between 30 October and 9 November 2026, sorted by urgency, with the exact dates, prices and catches spelled out so you never get to the counter and find out the fine print got there first.

1. This Weekend Only — Gone by Sunday

These deals expire between 31 October and 1 November. If any of them is on your list, today or tomorrow is the window.

  • Yakiniku-GO 5th Anniversary — $7.90++ sets, TODAY ONLY (30 Oct): One-day anniversary special across 6 Singapore outlets. The "Meat It Yourself" sets go for $7.90++ per person. One day only, so expect queues at dinner — go early or go late.
  • Starbucks 1-for-1 Tall drinks — until 1 Nov: Pay with a Trust Bank card and the second Tall handcrafted drink is free. Runs until 1 November 2026. The catch is purely the card — if you don't have a Trust card, this one is not for you (sign-up takes minutes in the app, but approval is not instant).
  • Guzman y Gomez Cheesy Kimchi Beef — 2 regular mains for $22, until 31 Oct: Order through the GYG app and two regular mains with the new Cheesy Kimchi Beef cost $22 altogether. App-order only; deal ends 31 October 2026.
  • Lucine Halloween — 50% off all food, 30–31 Oct, 5pm–10pm: Half off all food for the whole group at TripleOne Somerset during dinner hours. Two evenings only, so book ahead.
  • Burnt Cones Gelato pop-up — free scoop with purchase, 30 Oct–1 Nov: At the United Square pop-up, buying a scoop gets you an extra scoop free. Three days only at this location.
  • TANYU Halloween — $5 jumbo lemon & lime drink, until 31 Oct: Show up dressed in orange at any of 8 TANYU locations and the jumbo lemon & lime drink is $5. The catch is literally the dress code — a $2 Daiso orange tee counts, we checked the spirit of the rule.
  • Ghost Kakigori — $2 dorayaki, 31 Oct only: On Halloween itself, any dorayaki is $2 with any purchase, at all outlets. Perfect dessert stop after your $7.90 yakiniku if you plan the weekend right.

  • Lickers Cherry Cola pint — more than 30% off, until 3 Nov: The pint of the month is discounted at all 8 Lickers locations while the month's batch lasts. Deal runs until 3 November 2026.

3. Payday Sets & Ongoing Feasts — From 29 Oct, No Announced End Date

These started with payday on 29 October and are officially "onwards" deals — no end date announced yet. That usually means weeks, not months, but verify in-store before making a special trip.

  • Haidilao Build-Your-Own Hotpot Feast — S$38.80++, Jurong Point: The payday showpiece: pick 3 meat dishes, 2 soup bases, 4 hotpot dishes, 1 snack and 2 staples for S$38.80++ per person.   • Le Shrimp Ramen Saver Sets — from $10.30, Sengkang Grand Mall: Paradise Group's prawn ramen shop runs Saver Sets from $10.30 against stated usual prices of up to $23.50. Hours are the catch: 11am–5pm on weekdays, 3pm–5pm on weekends. A mid-afternoon early-bird deal, not a dinner deal.
  • Nong Geng Ji Mix & Match Value Set — S$38.80++ for 2–3 pax: The Chinese-cuisine twin of the Haidilao deal: a mix-and-match set feeding two to three people at S$38.80++, from 11am, at all 5 Singapore outlets. No stated end date.
  • Dunkin' Payday Treats — 2 free donuts with 5 bought, plus 1-for-1 Cold Brew and Teh Tarik: Buy 5 donuts and 2 are free; the coffee and teh tarik 1-for-1 stacks on top. Running from 29 October; the source flags these as ending soon with no firm end date, so this weekend is the safe bet. Participating outlets only — check the store locator before travelling.
  • CHAGEE Geelato — tea-inspired gelato from $6.90 a scoop: New from 30 October at Orchardgateway, One Raffles Place and Pagoda House (Chinatown). Flavours include BO·YA Jasmine, Signature Matcha and Yunnan Pu'er Caramel. No end date announced.
  • The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf — 2 Organic Matcha Strawberry Lattes for $10: Listed at i12 Katong from 29 October onwards. Two lattes for $10 works out to $5 each — cheaper than most specialty-coffee matcha by a wide margin. Confirm the outlet before heading down, as the source listing carries some uncertainty on location.
  • La Pizzaiola — 50% off second dish, plus 1-for-1 pizza/pasta lunch: From 28 October at 3 outlets: half price on the second main dish all day, and 1-for-1 on pizza or pasta at lunch. No end date announced.

💡 The Payday Rule of Thumb

The deals that reward planning are the time-boxed ones (yakiniku, sushi day, $3 burrito); the deals that punish procrastination are the ongoing ones with no end date — they vanish without warning. Priority order this week: one-day offers first, dated offers second, "onwards" offers whenever. One payday splurge from this list costs less than a movie ticket for two — that is a well-spent salary, not an impulse.

7. The Fine Print — What to Skip

Not everything payday-themed is worth the trip. Skip the CBTL matcha deal if you are nowhere near Katong — $5 a latte is only a deal when you are already in the neighbourhood. Skip TANYU if dressing up feels like work (it is). And if you were hoping to bring the whole family to Haidilao on Saturday — you cannot; the set excludes Saturdays and public holidays, so plan it for a weekday dinner or a late-night weekday session instead. Deals only save money when they match plans you already had.

That is the payday week in full. Bookmark this page, work the playbook top to bottom, and by 9 November you will have eaten like a king on a hawker's budget. The next big wave lands in mid-November: Mr. CHEESECAKE's pop-up on 11 Nov and Yee3's free opening gift — both at ION Orchard, so keep that calendar slot open.
