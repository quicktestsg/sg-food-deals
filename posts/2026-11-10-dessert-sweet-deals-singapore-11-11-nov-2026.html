11.11 Dessert & Sweet Treat Deals Singapore (10–15 Nov 2026): 1-for-1 Ice Cream, Free Cake & More

Singles' Day isn't just for gadgets and Shopee carts — Singapore's dessert counters are running some of the best sweet deals of the year this week. Here's every verified deal, with dates, prices and the fine print.

11.11 has quietly become one of the best weeks of the year for sweet tooths in Singapore. Ice cream parlours, cake shops and even prata joints are all running Singles' Day promotions between now and 15 November — and several of them end within 48 hours. This guide is organised by deadline, so you know exactly what to grab today versus what can wait until the weekend.

1. Ending Tomorrow — Buy 3 Free 1 Cheese Tarts at Hokkaido Baked Cheese Tart (7–11 Nov)

The most urgent deal on this list: Hokkaido Baked Cheese Tart's 11.11 promotion runs 7 to 11 November 2026 at all outlets. Buy any three tarts — classic cheese, chocolate or seasonal flavours — and the fourth is free. If you've been meaning to try this chain, tomorrow (11 Nov) is the last day.

2. The 11.11 Day Itself — Udders 1-for-1 Ice Cream Pints (11–13 Nov)

Udders' "11.11 Udderly Sale" starts on Singles' Day itself: from 11 to 13 November 2026, buy one takeaway pint and get a second pint of equal or lower value free. Local word is that durian flavours are included in the promotion — a big deal for fans, since durian pints typically sit at the top of Udders' price range. The deal runs three days only, while stocks last, so don't expect the popular flavours to survive day three.

Also on 11 November: Cedele is marking the day with 11% off, and its new Christmas menu has just landed with a Spiced Apple Log Cake and Tiramisu Tart (single-day promo — confirm participation at your outlet). And if coffee is your dessert, Starbucks' Shopee 11.11 sale is live from 1 to 13 November: 1-for-1 Venti drink vouchers from $7.40 for two, covering Cold Brew, Iced Latte, Refreshers and Frappuccinos, redeemable within 30 days at eligible outlets.

3. Sweet Week — Free Castella Cake at Ah Mah & 20% Off Whole Cakes at Polar (until 13–15 Nov)

Ah Mah Homemade Cake is running two 11.11 promos from 9 to 13 November 2026. Online: buy two flavoured cakes and get an Original Castella Cake free. In-store at Paya Lebar Square and VivoCity: buy any flavoured cake and add the Original Castella for just $6 — a saving of over 39% — while stocks last. Both versions end on 13 November.

For whole cakes, Polar Puffs & Cakes is taking 20% off online orders from 8 to 15 November 2026 — strawberry, chocolate, mango and more are covered. Handy if a birthday falls this month. Need something savoury to balance the sugar? Eat Pizza's 11.11 combo runs 10–12 November: a pizza paired with OG, Gochujang or White Truffle Mac & Cheese for $11 instead of the usual $15.80 (save $4.80), at all outlets except Temasek Polytechnic.

4. Runs Longer — $11 Ramen at YAYOI, $6.90 Prata Tacos at Springleaf, S$1 Kopi for Seniors

Not everything ends this week. YAYOI's 11.11 ramen deal runs 11 to 25 November 2026: Pork Shabu Tonkotsu Ramen for $11 instead of $16.90 (save $5.90), in classic non-spicy or Mala tonkotsu. Available on weekdays at five participating outlets; on weekends it's exclusive to the 100AM Mall branch. Sweet-adjacent and festive: Springleaf Prata Place's Deepavali Trio (6 November – 5 December 2026) serves three prata tacos — Butter Chicken, Chilli Fish and the dessert-like Gulab Jamun — with a hot Masala Tea for $6.90 at participating outlets. And for seniors 60+ with an eligible POSB Mastercard, S$1 buns at BreadTalk, snacks at Dough Culture and coffee or tea at Nan Yang Dao and Toast Box run all the way to 31 December 2026.

5. Bonus: Burger King 1-for-1 All Week (9–15 Nov)

One more worth flagging while it overlaps the sweet deals: Burger King Singapore's 11.11 celebration runs 9 to 15 November with 1-for-1 on selected items — two of the same item for the price of one, including the WHOPPER, Long Chicken, Truffle Mayo Deluxe Angus Beef, Spicy Chicken King, the Chicken Sausage Croissan'wich with Egg and iced MILO. Catch: orders must be placed through BK ordering kiosks or the BK app; counter orders don't qualify.

Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Tuesday, 10 Nov (today): Ah Mah in-store $6 castella deal and Eat Pizza's $11 combo both start today. Order a Polar whole cake online (20% off) if a birthday is coming.
  • Wednesday, 11 Nov (11.11): The big day. Hokkaido Baked Cheese Tart Buy 3 Free 1 ends today. Udders 1-for-1 pints begin. Cedele 11% off (single day). Grab Shopee vouchers — Starbucks 1-for-1 from $7.40, KFC vouchers from S$1.10.
  • Thursday, 12 Nov: Last day for Eat Pizza's $11 pizza + Mac & Cheese. Udders pints continue (day 2 — stocks still decent).
  • Friday, 13 Nov: Final day for Udders 1-for-1 pints, Ah Mah's promos and the Starbucks Shopee sale. Go early — popular flavours sell out.
  • Weekend, 14–15 Nov: Burger King 1-for-1 and Polar's online cake sale wrap up on 15 Nov. YAYOI's $11 ramen runs till 25 Nov — on weekends, remember it's 100AM Mall only.
💡 Freezer-Stocking Tip

The Udders 1-for-1 is a takeaway-pint deal, which makes it the one 11.11 promotion you can 'bank' — two pints in the freezer turns a three-day sale into dessert for the next two weeks. Pair it with the Ah Mah online deal (free castella keeps well for days) and you've stockpiled sweets for under the cost of one restaurant dessert.

Prices and dates above are as published by the merchants as of 10 November 2026; promos are while stocks last and outlets may vary — check the official channels before travelling. Happy 11.11, and may your freezer be full.
