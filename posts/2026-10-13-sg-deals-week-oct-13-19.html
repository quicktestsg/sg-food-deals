Cheap Eats & Best Deals This Week: 13–19 October 2026

Student yakiniku from $8.90, a free large CHAGEE milk tea, teen-only bubble tea cashback, Sheng Siong seafood specials and an SIA flight promo — every deal checked for dates, prices and fine print.

Mid-October is quietly one of the better deal weeks of the season: students get fed for under $9, bubble tea brands are fighting for the after-school crowd, and the supermarkets are rotating fresh seafood into the promo aisle.

1. Yakiniku Like: Student Sets from $8.90 (Ends 14 Oct)

The tightest deadline of the week. From 12 to 14 October 2026, students who flash a valid student card get selected 200g Karubi or Pork Belly sets from $8.90, at up to 40% off — available all day. Six outlets are participating, including Clementi Mall, Compass One and Tampines 1. Tomorrow is the last day.

2. Sheng Siong: Fresh Seafood Specials (Ends 13 Oct, While Stocks Last)

Sheng Siong's seafood promotion runs 7 to 13 October 2026 islandwide, with crabs and squid featured in this week's circular. Two caveats: no specific prices are listed, so treat this as a "go compare in store" tip, and it's while stocks last — the good stuff goes early on the final day.

3. KOI Thé: S$3 Cashback for Teens via DBS PayLah! (Till 18 Oct)

KOI Thé is giving eligible teens a S$3 cashback coupon through DBS PayLah!, with a S$10 minimum spend, running now until 18 October 2026. Conditions: limited to eligible teen PayLah! accounts, and the S$10 minimum means ordering large or pairing with a friend. Note — the source contains conflicting end-date details, so verify in the app.

4. Free Large CHAGEE Milk Tea via Reward Land (From 12 Oct, Ongoing)

From 12 October 2026 onwards, redeem Reward Land coins plus promo code AB5KRL for a $6 GrabFood voucher — enough for a large CHAGEE milk tea, with membership points and pickup savings possibly stacking. Check the Reward Land app for coin cost and stock. No end date announced — assume it lasts only while voucher stock does.

5. Takashimaya: Up to 60% Off Cookware (14–20 Oct)

Starting tomorrow (14 October) and running to 20 October 2026, Takashimaya's Household Department on Basement 1 takes up to 60% off selected cookware from Zwilling, Staub, Corelle DuraNano and Visions. It pairs with the Sheng Siong seafood promo above. Note "up to 60%" means the deepest cuts are on specific lines.

6. Singapore Airlines: S$50 Off Return Flights with DBS/POSB (13–19 Oct)

For the travel planners: from 13 to 19 October 2026, booking eligible Singapore Airlines return flights with a DBS or POBS card takes S$50 off, with total savings of up to S$100 on qualifying bookings. The discount applies only within the one-week window, so commit this week if you've been deciding on a year-end trip.

The Play for This Week

If you only do three things: grab the Yakiniku Like student deal before 14 October, do a seafood-and-cookware double run (Sheng Siong's last day flows into Takashimaya's first day on 14 October), and lock in SIA travel with a DBS/POSB card before the 19th.
