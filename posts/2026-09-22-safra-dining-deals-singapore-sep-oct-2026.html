SAFRA Dining Deals Singapore 2026: 20% Off Haidilao, Hotel Buffets & More (Sep–Oct Guide)

Everyone chases app coupons and bank card promos, but the quietest dining discount in Singapore sits on a SAFRA card: flat 20% off at more than ten restaurants, from Haidilao hotpot to hotel buffets and Teochew institutions. Here is every current deal, its dates, and its fine print — plus the grocery and lifestyle offers that stack with it.

If you serve or have served in uniform, or you simply hold a SAFRA membership, September and October 2026 are unusually good months to eat out. Promotions scraped from current deal feeds show a coordinated wave of SAFRA partner offers running right now — most of them flat 20% off the total bill, several running until the end of 2026 or beyond. This guide organizes all of them by expiry, flags the conditions, and suggests how to plan a month of eating around them.

How the SAFRA dining discount works

Almost every deal in this guide follows the same mechanic: flash a valid SAFRA card (physical card, e-Card in the app, or an 11B for servicemen) when paying, and 20% comes off the bill. A few deals instead use a promo code for online or delivery orders. The important nuances:

  • Who qualifies: SAFRA members, and for several deals (notably Haidilao) also serving NSmen with an 11B — no SAFRA membership needed for that one.
  • What's excluded: most offers apply to dine-in only unless stated otherwise; some specify 'eligible food items' (alcohol and set promotional menus are commonly carved out). Always check the outlet's terms before ordering the drinks bucket.
  • One deal per bill: SAFRA discounts generally cannot stack with other in-house promotions or credit card dining offers on the same bill. Pick whichever is bigger.

Expiring 31 October 2026 — the two headliners

Two of the strongest offers in the current batch have a hard stop at the end of October, so they go first.

1. Haidilao Tampines Century Square — 20% off dishes

Running from 1 September to 31 October 2026, this is 20% off dishes (dine-in only) at the Haidilao outlet in Century Square, Tampines. Crucially, the deal covers SAFRA members and servicemen — a valid SAFRA card, e-Card or 11B works. For a table of four at a typical hotpot spend, one-fifth off the food bill is a serious chunk; Haidilao's free snacks and service extras make the effective saving feel even larger. The catch: it's dishes only, so drinks and any seasonal set menus likely fall outside the discount — verify at the counter before you order the lot.

2. OATSIDE — 10% off first order (code COMEOATSIDE)

From 15 September to 31 October 2026, SAFRA members and first-time OATSIDE customers get 10% off their first qualifying online order with promo code COMEOATSIDE, minimum spend S$40. Smaller percentage than the restaurant deals, but it applies to pantry staples — if your household goes through oat milk, S$40 arrives fast, and this stacks with whatever your supermarket isn't discounting this week.

Running to 31 December 2026 — the hotel buffet circuit

This is where the SAFRA card genuinely shines. A wave of hotel restaurants — from Orchard Road institutions to waterfront buffets — are running member-only 20% off offers through the end of 2026. Six standouts, all with the same window (1 January – 31 December 2026, terms permitting):

  • Food Capital, Grand Copthorne Waterfront: 20% off the total bill at the international buffet — sashimi, a signature laksa station and rotating themed buffets (Peranakan, Japanese, Malaysian, Thai among upcoming themes). Buffet + 20% off is the single best value shape in this whole list.
  • Hua Ting, Orchard Hotel: 20% off at this established Cantonese restaurant — the deal for family dinners and wedding-adjacent occasions.
  • The Orchard Café, Orchard Hotel: 20% off total bill; Singapore-inspired dishes, Asian noodle favourites and a live noodlet bar.
  • Tien Court, Copthorne King's Hotel: 20% off contemporary Teochew cuisine and handcrafted dim sum — quiet luxury at a discount.
  • Princess Terrace, Copthorne King's Hotel: 20% off authentic Penang and Peranakan-style cooking — the buffet line here has a decades-long reputation.
  • Beast & Butterflies, Robertson Quay: 20% off eligible dine-in food items — sharing plates and hearty signatures by the riverside.

Two more in the same all-year bucket: MEMO Cafè at Studio M Hotel (20% off total bill; diner-style burgers, hotdog buns, steaks and milkshakes) and Bar Intermezzo at Orchard Hotel (20% off total bill, botanical-inspired cocktails, weekday live music from 7:00pm to 10:15pm — the rare SAFRA deal that covers a proper night cap).

Running into 2027 — Furama RiverFront's two restaurants

  • Apollo, Furama RiverFront (Level 3, 405 Havelock Road): SAFRA members get 20% off the total bill from 1 June 2026 to 28 February 2027. The buffet here runs to hearty favourites, seasonal creations and honey-infused highlights; dine-in and eligible delivery orders both qualify.
  • Kintamani, Furama RiverFront: 20% off the Indonesian restaurant's total bill until 28 February 2027. For delivery, promo code SAFRA20 applies — one of the few SAFRA dining discounts that explicitly covers food delivery.

Beyond the table: groceries and lifestyle adds

The same September wave brought non-dining SAFRA offers worth knowing about, because they fill the gaps between restaurant trips:

  • Quan Shui Wet Market (1–30 September 2026): $5 off with S$60 spend, $10 off with S$110 spend, or 15% off with no minimum for first-time customers — online delivery (next-day islandwide) and the Upper Thomson store. This is the rare deal where being a NEW customer beats being a member.
  • Wild Wild Wet (all of 2026): 20% off off-peak and peak day passes with promo code WWWSAFRA, minimum two passes bought online — Downtown East's water park for the September school holidays.
  • NoelGifts.com (until 31 December 2026): 10% off flowers and gifts capped at $20 with code SAFRA10, plus free standard delivery (waiver up to $9.50 before GST, location exclusions apply).

⚠️ Condition Check — read before booking

Every deal above requires proof of SAFRA membership (card, e-Card, or 11B for the Haidilao and golf-type offers). Most are dine-in only; alcohol, promotional set menus and festive/seasonal pricing are commonly excluded even when the base discount applies. Hotel restaurant offers are typically not valid on public holidays and special occasion days (Christmas, CNY) — call ahead before booking those dates. Discounts generally cannot combine with other promotions on one bill.

Strategy: how to sequence the next six weeks

  • This week (by 30 Sep): Place a Quan Shui Wet Market order — the tiered discounts end 30 September. First-timers should take the 15%-off-no-minimum option; members should consolidate one S$110+ order for the full $10 off.
  • Before 31 October: The Haidilao Tampines run and the OATSIDE first-order code both die on 31 October. Hotpot with the 11B crowd is the priority booking; slot the OATSIDE S$40 order around your next supermarket gap.
  • November onwards: the hotel buffet circuit keeps running to 31 December — pace one hotel per fortnight rather than cramming, and watch for the themed buffet rotations at Food Capital (Peranakan, Japanese, Malaysian, Thai) to pick your favourite cuisine week.
  • Into 2027: Apollo and Kintamani at Furama RiverFront run till 28 February 2027 — save them for the January blues when every other promo has expired.
💡 One card, eleven kitchens

The pattern across this wave: 20% off with almost no legwork — no app queueing, no flash-sale timing, no minimum spend at most restaurants. That's rare in Singapore's deal landscape, where discounts usually demand a coupon ritual. If you hold a SAFRA card and have been paying full price at any of these eleven outlets, this is the quarter to correct that.

What to skip

Not everything in the September SAFRA wave is a food win. The VAIO laptop sale (12% off, plus 2% with PayNow, until 31 October 2026) and the golf lesson packages are lifestyle items most readers won't need on a dining budget. And a note on the non-member front: the same feeds flagged a Bank of China time deposit promo (up to 1.8% p.a. from 21 September) — relevant to savings, not suppers, so it stays off the plate here.
