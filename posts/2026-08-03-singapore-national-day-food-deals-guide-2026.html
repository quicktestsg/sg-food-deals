Singapore National Day Weekend Food Deals 2026

National Day is August 9th — and the long weekend is prime time for food deals. Here's how to celebrate Singapore's birthday without going broke.

Singapore turns 61 this National Day, and the food scene is pulling out all the stops. Every deal listed here is active right now.

1. $1 Coffee & $2 Breakfast at Kopitiam

Start NDP morning the Singaporean way — with kopi that costs less than a dollar. Through the FairPrice Group app, Kopitiam offers hot kopi/teh for S$1 and a French Toast set for just S$2, running until September 30th.

💰 The Math

At S$1 vs the usual S$1.20-1.70, you save 40-70 cents per cup. One kopi every workday for a month puts S$8-14 back in your pocket.

2. Sushi Tei: 50% Off Lunch (Mon-Thu)

Sushi Tei Rewards members get 50% off selected dishes Monday to Thursday, 11:30am-4:30pm, from August 3rd through October 29th. Sign up for free in the app before you walk in.

  • Deal: 50% off selected dishes (Mon-Thu, lunch hours)
  • Requirement: Free Sushi Tei Rewards membership
  • Dine-in only — not valid for takeaway or delivery

3. Pepper Lunch: 1-for-1 Opening Special (Aug 3)

Pepper Lunch opens at Hougang Mall on August 3rd with a one-day 1-for-1 Beef Pepper Rice. From August 4-31st, the deal shifts to discounted Salmon & Chicken combos.

4. TSUJIRI: 1-for-1 Floats Until Aug 9

TSUJIRI's 1-for-1 float promotion runs right through National Day (August 9th). Signature matcha soft serve floats — buy one, get one free. Perfect for an afternoon NDP cooldown.

5. Grocery Stock-Up: Ice Cream & Snacks for Your NDP BBQ

Hosting a National Day barbecue? The supermarkets are running overlapping deals that make stocking up genuinely cheap. Some end August 5th — shop early:

  • FairPrice: Up to 50% off selected ice cream and snacks until August 5th. Stock the freezer before the weekend.
  • Cold Storage: Häagen-Dazs tubs at 3-for-S$25 until August 5th — about S$8.33 per tub vs S$12+ retail.
  • 7-Eleven: S$1 snacks and drinks until August 11th — Knoppers, Milo Nuggets, Pepsi, and more.
🛒 Pro Strategy

Hit Cold Storage first (Häagen-Dazs ends Aug 5), then FairPrice for snacks, then 7-Eleven for drinks. Three stores, one trip, full BBQ coverage.

6. Coffee Bean: New Egg Mayo Breakfast Set

Coffee Bean's new Egg Mayo Wrap Breakfast Set launched August 3rd, available Monday-Friday until noon. A fresh sit-down option for the NDP week.

7. Sweet Treats: Paris Baguette & Baskin-Robbins

For the sweet tooths celebrating NDP, two ongoing promotions are worth a detour:

  • Paris Baguette: 2-for-S$20 Roll Cakes and 5-for-S$10 Madeleines until August 31st. Madeleines work out to S$2 each.
  • Baskin-Robbins: 31% off handpacked ice cream on the 31st of select months.

8. Budget Mains Under S$10

Need actual lunch or dinner through the NDP window? These rotating mains deals are active now:

  • Texas Chicken: S$9.90 for 4 pieces every Monday & Tuesday — S$2.48 per piece.
  • Wingstop: 8 boneless wings for S$7.20 on flash promo days.
  • KFC: 5 pieces for S$9.95 every Tuesday.

The NDP Weekend Game Plan

Here's how to string these deals into a full National Day weekend of eating well on a budget:

📅 3-Day NDP Plan
  • Friday Aug 8: Sushi Tei 50% off lunch (rewards member), then Cold Storage + FairPrice grocery run.
  • Saturday Aug 9: S$1 kopi morning, TSUJIRI 1-for-1 float afternoon (last day!), BBQ with stocked groceries.
  • Sunday Aug 10: Coffee Bean breakfast, 7-Eleven S$1 snacks, Texas Chicken or KFC budget dinner.

Stack These for Maximum Savings

Every deal above can be amplified with deal multipliers that cost nothing extra:

  • Credit card dining promos: DBS, UOB, OCBC run NDP-season discounts — check your card app for stackable 15-25% off.
  • FairPrice Group app: Unlocks the S$1 kopi AND earns points on every FairPrice/Kopitiam spend.
  • NTUC member perks: 50-cent hot kopi/teh islandwide for union members.

What to Skip This NDP

Not every 'NDP promo' is actually a deal. A few traps to avoid:

  • Hotel NDP set menus: Often carry a 20-30% holiday premium for a flag on the dessert.
  • 'Limited edition' red and white snacks: Usually regular products at a markup. Buy the normal version.
  • Delivery app surcharges: GrabFood and Foodpanda add public holiday surcharges. Walk in or self-collect.
