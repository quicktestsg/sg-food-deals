Christmas Food Deals Singapore 2026: Early Bird Log Cakes, Festive Sets & Free Events

The festive season promotions have landed a full six weeks before Christmas — and the best prices all have early-bird deadlines. Here is every food-worthy Christmas deal running right now, organised by how soon you need to act.

Christmas in Singapore does not wait for December. By mid-November the supermarkets have rolled out their festive catalogues, restaurants have launched holiday set menus, and the malls are already putting up their Christmas villages. The pattern repeats every year: the deepest discounts are front-loaded into early-bird windows that close in late November, while everything from mid-December onward runs at full festive pricing. This guide walks through the deals actually live as of November 15, 2026 — with the deadlines that matter called out first.

1. Act This Week: Early-Bird Deadlines Closing Soon

These are the deals where waiting costs money. Three deadlines land within the next two weeks.

  • ASTONS Christmas Feast Takeaway — order by 24 November 2026. Festive takeaway sets for 6–8 pax start from $172, and every $250 spent on qualifying orders before the deadline earns a $40 ASTONS gift voucher. If a steakhouse Christmas dinner at home is the plan, the voucher effectively claws back a chunk of the bill. Sets and ordering details are on ASTONS' official channels.
  • Cold Storage Christmas Treats — ends 18 November 2026. The first supermarket festive wave wraps this Wednesday: festive goodies and early Christmas shopping deals across the store. This is the window for stocking non-perishable festive items before the December price reset.
  • Prime Supermarket free wine — until 30 November 2026, first 100 customers. Spend $88 or more on Christmas gourmet items and/or festive log cakes and receive a free bottle of wine worth $25 (choose between Le Nouveau Parisien Syrah or Gossips Cabernet Sauvignon, 750ml). The first-100 cap makes this a shop-early deal, not a shop-late one.
  • Sofitel Sentosa festive afternoon tea giveaway — ends 20 November 2026. Three winners get a Festive Afternoon Tea for two at LeBar. Entry is the usual Instagram routine: follow the participating accounts, like and repost, comment. Free to enter, long odds, but someone wins.
💡 Early-Bird Math

Compare like-for-like: the ASTONS voucher route returns $40 per $250 spent (16% back) if you clear the threshold, while Prime's wine deal returns $25 per $88 (about 28% back in wine value) — but only on gourmet/festive items and only for the first 100 customers per the promotion terms. Neither is a reason to overspend; both are reasons to consolidate purchases you were already making into one qualifying receipt.

Prime is the only chain publishing firm festive food prices so far: Christmas feast sets from $37.50 and log cakes from $29.90, alongside whole roast turkey, honey baked ham and premium meats. Sheng Siong's Christmas catalogue runs the longest (till 3 January 2027) which makes it the safest bet for post-Christmas restocking; its housebrand Festive Fiesta — up to 65% off selected items — runs 12–18 November. 💡 Prime Stacking Strategy

One trip, two layers: build an $88+ basket from the Christmas gourmet range (which includes the $29.90 log cakes — two plus sides clears the threshold), claim the free $25 wine before the first-100 cap bites, and you have dessert plus a host gift sorted in a single receipt. Valid until 30 November 2026.

3. Festive Dining: Buffets, Dim Sum and Star-Shaped Pizza

For eating out rather than cooking, the live festive menus stack up like this.

  • Peach Garden Holiday Dim Sum — from 6 November 2026 onwards, no announced end date. Four exclusive holiday dim sum creations at 50% off on weekdays, 30% off on weekends and public holidays, at OCBC Centre, The Heeren and Thomson Plaza. Weekday lunch is the value play here.
  • Pizza Hut Festive Star Pizzas — 12 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. Three festive star-shaped pizzas — including the new Chicken Starizo with folded crust tips and cheese dip — for $18.95 (usual price $44.95 per the promotion). That is the single biggest percentage discount in this round of festive dining deals, and it feeds a table.
  • 21 on Rajah SEA-travaganza Feastmas buffet — 14 November 2026 to 1 January 2027. A Southeast Asian spin on festive favourites with more than 30 curated items. Buffet pricing is not stated in the deal summary — check the restaurant's official channels before booking.
  • Swensen's Christmas 2026 collection — seasonal, dates per outlet. Ice cream cakes, seasonal sundaes, festive dining specials, takeaway feasts and Christmas buffet offerings. A log cake alternative for households that prefer ice cream cake; prices vary by item, so confirm before ordering.
  • Jack's Place Festive Cheers Tumbler — from 7 November 2026 onwards, while stocks last. The limited-edition Christmas tumbler at $13.80, a Blackcurrant Cooler with Lychee at $4.80, or both bundled for $15.80 at Jack's Place and Eatzi Gourmet. The bundle is the only sensible option if you want the tumbler.

4. Sweet Christmas: Bakery and Dessert Deals

  • Tiong Bahru Bakery — until 15 December 2026. Buy any three Crombolonis (their new croissant-cromboloni hybrid) and get a Signature Kouign Amann free, at all outlets. A rare free-item deal from a bakery that almost never discounts.
  • Starbucks Bearista Holiday Experience — 11 November to 6 December 2026, Raffles City and Bugis+. Buy-1-get-1-free Venti vouchers, 10% off for Starbucks Rewards members, and a free collectible Bearista pin set with a minimum $100 spend. The Pompompurin 30th Anniversary merchandise drops here too. The BOGO vouchers are the part worth queuing for.
  • McDonald's x BT21 Happy Meal — 12 November to 16 December 2026. Eight BT21 characters (KOYA, RJ, SHOOKY, MANG, CHIMMY, TATA, COOKY, VAN) released across five weekly drops at participating outlets. Not a discount, but the cheapest festive collectible chase for kids — and adults who know which character they are.

5. Free Christmas Events Worth the MRT Trip

The festive calendar's free tier is unusually strong this year. None of these require a ticket.

  • Butterbear Christmas Wonderland — 11 November to 30 December 2026, orchardgateway. Free entry to the 6.5-metre Sweet Candy House, festive photo spots, and meet-and-greets with Butterbear (and Biancabear) at 6.30pm and 8.30pm daily.
  • Christmas Together 2026 — 27–29 November 2026, Marina Bay Sands. A free indoor Christmas village with 150+ brands, 50 live performances, 40 workshops and 18 festive experiences, including a KIDSforKIDS market and carnival activities.
  • VivoCity Super Mario Holiday Gift Tags — 13 November to 27 December 2026. Collect all four exclusive Super Mario gift tag designs (Mario, Luigi, Toad, Yoshi) by visiting four stamping stations around the mall — no purchase required. Free gift wrap accessories, essentially.

One paid event deserves early attention: Gardens by the Bay's Christmas Wonderland returns 27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 for its 13th edition — flying Santa, the 20-metre Italian Spalliera light display, tropical snow blizzards, and 40+ photo installations across 30,000 square metres. Ticket prices were not published in the deal summary; early-bird pricing is advertised, so check the official site this week before standard pricing kicks in.

6. Strategy: Your Week-by-Week Festive Playbook

  • By Wednesday 18 Nov: Cold Storage Christmas Treats ends — do the first festive grocery run. Sheng Siong's 65%-off Festive Fiesta also ends 18 Nov.
  • By Friday 20 Nov: Enter the Sofitel Sentosa afternoon tea giveaway (free) and close out the Starbucks BOGO voucher run if you are near Raffles City or Bugis+.
  • By Tuesday 24 Nov: ASTONS early-bird deadline — place the Christmas Feast takeaway order if that is the plan, and make sure the receipt crosses $250 for the voucher.
  • By Monday 30 Nov: Prime Supermarket $88-for-wine deal closes (first 100 customers — go early in the window, not late). Giant's Year-End Savings Sale also wraps 25 Nov.
  • 27–29 Nov weekend: Christmas Wonderland opens at Gardens by the Bay (ticketed, early-bird pricing) and Christmas Together runs free at MBS — one weekend covers both.
  • Ongoing / low urgency: Peach Garden weekday 50% dim sum, Pizza Hut $18.95 star pizzas, Tiong Bahru Bakery free Kouign Amann (till 15 Dec), Butterbear wonderland (till 30 Dec), VivoCity Mario tags (till 27 Dec), Sheng Siong catalogue (till 3 Jan).
⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal above has a catch worth knowing: ASTONS' voucher needs a $250 qualifying spend by 24 Nov; Prime's wine needs an $88 gourmet-only basket and is capped at the first 100 customers; Peach Garden's 50% is weekdays only (30% weekends/PH); Starbucks' pin set needs $100 spent, though the BOGO voucher and member discount do not; the BT21 chase requires five separate weekly Happy Meal purchases to complete; and McDonald's, Pizza Hut and Jack's Place promos all run at participating outlets only — check the store list before travelling.

7. What to Skip

Not everything with a snowflake on it is a deal. Skip the first-week December log cake pre-orders at full price when Prime has them from $29.90 now; skip paying for any Christmas village experience before checking the free ones (MBS, orchardgateway, VivoCity); and skip the $13.80 tumbler-only purchase at Jack's Place — the $15.80 bundle with the cooler makes the standalone price pointless. The festive season rewards the early and the organised; it quietly surcharges everyone else.
