Cheap Eats Under $10 in Singapore 2026 — Best Budget Food by Neighbourhood

Singapore isn't cheap — but eating well doesn't have to drain your wallet. From $1 coffee to $1.80 jumbo prawn noodles, 61¢ pretzels, and $2 burgers — the neighbourhood-by-neighbourhood guide to a full meal under ten dollars.

The myth that you need S$15-20 per meal in Singapore is exactly that — a myth. August 2026 might be the cheapest month to eat out in years, thanks to SG61 specials, fast-food price wars, and hawker gems that never went up.

💡 The Golden Number: $6.10

Why S$6.10? It celebrates Singapore's 61st birthday. Don Don Donki, Kopitiam, Sushiro, Penang Culture, and Hokkaido-ya all have SG61 specials at this price. That's your benchmark this month.

What to Skip

To stay under S$10, know what to avoid:

  • Food court 'value meals' at S$8-9: Smaller portions than S$4-5 hawker equivalents. You're paying for aircon.
  • Cafe mains over S$12: Check if Coffee Bean's S$6-8 set or a S$6.10 SG61 special satisfies the same craving.
  • Delivery app markups: The S$6.10 Donki deal costs S$9-10 on GrabFood after fees. Walk in — 10 minutes saves you S$3-4.
  • Bottled drinks at restaurants: A S$1.50 Sinopec tea bottle replaces a S$3-4 restaurant drink. Carry one.
