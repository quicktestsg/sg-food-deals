Free Things To Do This Weekend in Singapore (Nov 21-22, 2026): Free Cable Car, $6.90 Prata and Cheap Eats

Free Sentosa cable car rides start today, a free jelly pop-up takes over Orchard Central, and one prata chain is selling three tacos and tea for $6.90. Here is the full weekend playbook — with dates, prices and every catch spelled out.

Weekends in Singapore do not have to be expensive. This particular weekend (November 21-22, 2026) is unusually generous: a cable car line goes free for a month starting Saturday morning, a nostalgic local drinks brand is giving away jelly in Orchard, and if you pay for your hawker lunch the right way, DBS will hand you up to $3 back before dinner.

1. The Big One: Free Sentosa Cable Car Rides (Nov 21 - Dec 20, 2026)

Starting today, November 21, 2026, and running daily until December 20, 2026, the Singapore Cable Car Sentosa Line is free every morning from 8.45am to 11am. Board at Sensoryscape, Imbiah Lookout or Siloso Point — no advance booking, no voucher redemption, just walk up and ride.

🚡 Strategy: Beat the Queue

Free cable car promos get crowded fast on weekends. Aim for the 8.45am first cabin — you also get the coolest weather and the softest morning light for photos.

Not a Sentosa person? Singapore's coastal parks remain the city's most underrated free weekend outing, and they pair well with a $1.50 coffee (see below).

2. Free Jelly, Free Keyrings: Yeo's Jelly O'Clock Pop-Up at Orchard Central (Nov 20-22)

For one final weekend — November 20 to 22, 2026, 11am to 8pm — Yeo's Jelly O'Clock pop-up at Orchard Central is giving away free Chia Seed Jelly with prebiotic, a personalised photo keyring, interactive games and sure-win gachapon prizes. Sunday evening is your last chance.

3. Pay for Lunch Correctly: DBS PayLah! $3 Saturday Cashback (Nov 21 Only)

Today only: scan and pay with DBS PayLah! at participating heartland shops, wet markets, coffeeshops and hawker centres islandwide and get up to $3 cashback. The offer runs from 6am and is capped at the first 160,000 redemptions.

💡 Stack It

The cleanest stack today: hawker breakfast paid via PayLah! (up to $3 back), then the free Sentosa cable car before 11am, then a $1.50 Burger King coffee on the way home.

4. Eat Serious for Under $7 This Weekend

  • Springleaf Prata Place Deepavali Trio — $6.90 (Nov 20 - Dec 5, 2026): Three crispy prata tacos filled with butter chicken, chilli fish and gulab jamun, plus one hot masala tea, at participating outlets.
  • Burger King brewed coffee — $1.50 (from Nov 18, 2026, ongoing): Claim it in the Offers section of the BK app.
  • Peach Garden jelly desserts — from $4.40 for five pieces (Nov 20 - Dec 31, 2026): The $4.40 price is weekday-only; the cute jellies run up to 50% off throughout.

5. Family Table: Senior Buffet and Festive Sets

Bringing the grandparents along? Big Fish Small Fish at Hillion Mall runs an all-day senior buffet at $24.90++ for diners aged 60 and above, from November 17, 2026 onwards — end date not announced.

6. Weekend-Only: Bazaars and Fresh Market Deals (End Sunday)

Three promos die at the end of this Sunday, November 23. Prime Supermarket's 4-Day WOW! deals take up to 51% off selected fresh items with cherries from $10.20, plus two weekend bazaars.

7. Next Week's Openers: Plan Ahead

  • TungLok 90-Minute Madness (Nov 23 - Dec 30, 2026): 30% off a la carte and set menus during Early Bird or Late Dinner sessions, no blackout dates.
  • Genki Sushi platters (Nov 20, 2026 - Jan 3, 2027): 20% off takeaway when you buy any two eligible platters.
  • Nespresso Black Friday (Nov 27-30, 2026): Up to 40% off selected machines, selected models from $199.

Strategy: What To Do Each Day

  • Saturday, Nov 21 (today): PayLah! hawker breakfast, free cable car before 11am, Yeo's pop-up 11am-8pm.
  • Sunday, Nov 22: Last day for the Yeo's pop-up, both bazaars, and Prime fresh deals.
  • Monday, Nov 23: TungLok 30% off opens — book Early Bird lunch.
  • Friday, Nov 27: Nespresso Black Friday goes live — machines from $199.
⚠️ Condition Check

Free cable car: mornings only, Nov 21 - Dec 20. PayLah! cashback: Saturday only, first 160,000 redemptions, participating stalls. Yeo's pop-up: ends Sunday. Peach Garden $4.40: weekdays only. BK coffee: app required. Senior buffet: 60+, Hillion Mall only. Bazaars: while stocks last.

The pattern to notice: Singapore's best November value is time-sensitive and morning-biased. Set one alarm this weekend and everything on this list costs less than a movie ticket.
