1-for-1 & Free Food Deals Singapore This Week (8–15 Nov 2026)

Mr Bean is giving away soy drinks today, Famous Amos is handing out up to 200g of free cookies, and cheese tarts are buy 3 get 1. Every BOGO and freebie running in Singapore this week — sorted by how soon it expires.

The 11.11 sales season has spilled over into food, and this week is unusually generous with give-away promotions. Mr Bean runs a one-day buy-one-get-one on almost everything, a new bagel shop is throwing in a free bagel with no minimum spend, and one cookie chain is stacking up to 200g of free cookies on top of your purchase. Here is the full breakdown, organised by deadline — because the Mr Bean deal dies at closing time tonight.

1. Today or Ending This Weekend — Go Now

Mr Bean: Buy 1 Get 1 FREE on Almost All Soy Drinks — Sunday 8 Nov only

The annual Soymilk Sunday treat lands today, 8 November 2026, and it covers almost the entire soy drink range — including the refreshing fruity flavours and limited-time selections, not just the original. Walk in, buy one, get one free. No app or membership mentioned in the source; the only catch is the calendar — this is strictly a one-day promotion, so it ends when outlets close tonight.

Famous Amos: Up to 200g FREE Cookies — 7 to 9 Nov

From 7 to 9 November 2026, Famous Amos is giving away up to 200g of free cookies with selected purchases at participating outlets. The published deals: 400g for $19.20 or 600g for $25.40, with total savings of up to $13. The catch is real, though — it is limited to the first 100 customers per store per day, so an evening visit may walk away empty-handed. Go before lunch if you want the full freebie.

Bagel Chef: Buy 3 Get 1 FREE, No Minimum Spend — 7 to 9 Nov

New opening alert: Bagel Chef has just opened at Sengkang Grand Mall (unit #01-73) and is running a Buy 3 Get 1 Free opening promotion from 7 to 9 November 2026, with no minimum spend required. If you are in the northeast, this is the cheapest way to try a new bakery this weekend — three bagels plus one on the house.

Sheng Siong: 3-Day Fresh Seafood Specials — 7 to 9 Nov

Not a BOGO, but a freebie-adjacent win for home cooks: Sheng Siong's three-day fresh seafood specials run 7 to 9 November 2026 at participating outlets, with the catch of the day including crabs and squid. Prices vary by outlet and catch, so check the wet-market corner at your nearest branch rather than assuming a national price. A separate Purchase-With-Purchase promo (every $20 spent unlocks special-price items, up to 62% off, limited to 1 unit per item) also runs 3 to 9 November — one grocery run can stack both.

2. Running Through 11.11 — Till 11 Nov

Hokkaido Baked Cheese Tart: Buy 3 Get 1 FREE — 7 to 11 Nov, all outlets

The cheese tart chain's 11.11 promotion runs 7 to 11 November 2026 at ALL outlets: buy three tarts, get a fourth free, with choices spanning classic cheese, chocolate and seasonal flavours. No membership or app mentioned — the free tart applies at the counter. Grab three colleagues and this becomes afternoon tea for the price of a coffee each.

PlayMade: Protein Fresh Milk Tea with Grape Protein Boba for $5.90 — 7 to 11 Nov

PlayMade's Deepavali promotion runs 7 to 11 November 2026 at participating outlets: the Protein Fresh Milk Tea with Grape Protein Boba drops from $7.30 to $5.90, saving $1.40 per cup. Not a BOGO, but the cheapest branded protein bubble tea this week if that is your thing.

3. 11.11 Week Proper — 11 to 15 Nov

Udders Ice Cream: Buy 1 Get 1 FREE Takeaway Pints — 11 to 13 Nov

The 11.11 Udderly Sale runs 11 to 13 November 2026: buy one takeaway pint, get a second of equal or lower value FREE. Three days only, while stocks last — popular flavours tend to clear out by day two, so do not leave this to the final evening. This is takeaway only; a cone at the counter does not qualify.

Starbucks: 1-for-1 Venti Drinks from $7.40 for 2 via Shopee — 1 to 13 Nov

Starbucks' Shopee 11.11 promotion gives 1-for-1 Venti drinks from just $7.40 for TWO cups — covering Cold Brew, Iced Latte, Refreshers and Frappuccinos — valid 1 to 13 November 2026 at selected outlets. The mechanism matters: you buy vouchers through Shopee Singapore, then redeem in store. That means planning ahead — purchase the voucher before you queue, not at the counter.

Pizza Hut: 1-for-1 Melts at $9.95 — until 15 Nov

Still running from last week: two Melts for $9.95 instead of the usual $19.90, including the new Cheesy Otah and Korean Garlic Cream Cheese flavours, until 15 November 2026. That is effectively $4.98 per Melts — one of the better flat 1-for-1s this fortnight.

5. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Sunday 8 Nov (today): Mr Bean BOGO before closing — the single most time-critical deal on this list. Famous Amos free cookies and Bagel Chef B3G1 also end tomorrow.
  • Monday 9 Nov (last call): Final day for Famous Amos, Bagel Chef and Sheng Siong seafood specials. Shin Katsu's Monday-only 1-for-1 premium tonkatsu (Hokkaido pork from Hirata Farm) also lands today if you want a proper dinner.
  • Tuesday 10 Nov: Quiet day. Use it to pre-buy Shopee vouchers for Starbucks and KFC before the 11.11 rush.
  • Wednesday 11 Nov (11.11): Cheese tart B3G1 and PlayMade $5.90 both end today; Udders pint BOGO starts. The peak freebie day of the week.
  • Thursday–Friday 12–13 Nov: Udders BOGO continues (go early for flavours); Starbucks Shopee 1-for-1 ends 13 Nov.
  • Weekend 14–15 Nov: Pizza Hut Melts 1-for-1 wraps up on the 15th; the all-month deals (Kopitiam $1.80 tea break, Haidilao member discounts, McCrispy $7.95) remain your steady fallbacks.

6. What to Skip

Honesty section. The Kimly $38-for-3 set works out to about $12.70 per head — decent, but zi char prices at neighbourhood coffee shops are already close to that, so do not cross the island for it. The Gao Ji sponge freebie requires locking $20 into store credit for a dish sponge; only worth it if you already eat there weekly. And if a 'deal' requires you to buy vouchers you were not going to use (KFC's $1.10 vouchers are cheap, but only buy what you will actually redeem this month — they expire with the promo on 30 November).

💡 The One-Trip Plan

If you only leave the house once this week: today (Sunday). Mr Bean BOGO in the morning, Famous Amos before the 100-customer cap clears, and Sheng Siong seafood specials for dinner ingredients — three deals, one day, all ending within 48 hours.
