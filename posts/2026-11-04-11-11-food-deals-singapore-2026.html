11.11 Food Deals Singapore 2026: Haidilao $11.11 Hotpot Set, $1 KOI Milk Tea & Singles' Day Bargains

Singles' Day has quietly become one of the best eating weeks of the year in Singapore. Here is the full 11.11 battle plan — a $11.11 hotpot set, a $1 milk tea, and a string of one-day flash deals landing between now and 11 November.

Once a purely online shopping festival, 11.11 has gone fully offline in Singapore. Here is everything worth your money, organised by when you need to act.

5. What to Skip

Not everything with a big number deserves a trip. Season-launch merchandise collabs (Sanrio included) are worth it only if you personally collect — as pure food economics, a $4 latte beats a $11.90 blind bag. Buffets with ++ pricing deserve a quick mental math check: S$48++ for two works out to roughly S$57 nett after service charge and GST, still fair for the spread, but budget accordingly. And the Domino's code is great value only if pickup is genuinely convenient — a cross-island MRT ride for a $5.99 pizza is paying with your time.

Bottom line: between today's $4 latte, Friday's 1-for-1 trail, the $1 milk tea and the $11.11 hotpot set, Singles' Day 2026 is arguably the cheapest full week of eating Singapore has seen this year. Pick two or three, skip the rest, and eat well.
