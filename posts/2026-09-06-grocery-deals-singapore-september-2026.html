Grocery Deals Singapore September 2026: Sheng Siong vs Giant vs Prime vs FairPrice (Sep 6–12)

This is the single best grocery week of the month: Sheng Siong is cutting up to 72% off at its brand-new Rivervale Mall store, Giant's hypermarkets are running a two-day 50% blitz, Prime has fresh deals on salmon and eggs, and FairPrice just opened its biggest fresh-food store yet in Pasir Ris. Here is which chain to hit on which day — with every condition spelled out.

Supermarket promotions in Singapore usually trickle out one flyer at a time. This week is different: a grand opening, a hypermarket two-day special, a fresh-food promotion and a brand-new FairPrice all land inside the same seven days. The catch — and there is always a catch — is that each deal is chained to a specific chain, store, or window. This guide sorts the week by urgency, tells you exactly where each deal lives, and closes with a stacking plan that turns one trip into three layers of savings. Item-level prices that were not published in the announcements are marked as unconfirmed rather than guessed.

1. Ending Today or Tomorrow — Go Now

Giant Tampines & IMM: 2-day special, up to 50% off (Sep 5–6)

Giant is running exclusive 2-day deals at its two hypermarkets — Tampines and IMM — on September 5 and 6, 2026, with discounts of up to 50% off across fresh food, groceries, household essentials and kitchenware. Some offers are exclusive to these two hypermarket locations, so don't expect the same tags at neighbourhood Giant stores. Sunday (Sep 6) is the last day. Specific item prices were not published in the announcement, so treat 'up to 50%' as the ceiling, not the average — check the in-store flyer for what actually carries the discount. If you are heading to IMM anyway, note that Sheng Siong's king-crab opening deal (below) is at Rivervale, not IMM — different trip.

Little Farms: buy 2 get 1 free WA strawberries (Sep 5–6)

For the Teacher's Day weekend only — September 5 and 6, 2026 — Little Farms is running Buy 2, Get 1 FREE on its sweet Washington strawberries. A premium-grocer B2G1 on fruit is rare; the usual play at Little Farms is bundle pricing, not units. Bonus if you are near Tanglin: the Eat Spain, Drink Spain farmers' market runs 10am to 3pm the same weekend, so a strawberry run can double as a market browse. Per-pack price was not stated in the announcement — check the shelf before committing to three packs.

Sheng Siong Housebrand weekly special: up to 50% off, B1G1 frozen (ends Sep 7)

Separate from the Rivervale opening, Sheng Siong's islandwide Housebrand Sale runs September 1–7, 2026: up to 50% off selected items, with the Housebrand Frozen Weekly Special featuring Buy-1-Get-1-Free deals on selected Tasty Bites frozen foods, plus Purchase-with-Purchase offers. This one works at regular Sheng Siong branches, so it's the version for people who are not near Rivervale. Monday, September 7 is the final day. Which Tasty Bites items qualify varies by store — the freezer aisle tags are the source of truth.

2. The Main Event: Sheng Siong Rivervale Mall Grand Opening (Sep 4–10)

Sheng Siong's new Rivervale Mall outlet is celebrating with seven days of opening specials, September 4–10, 2026, and the headline number is big: up to 72% off. The stack at this opening runs four layers: (1) Buy-1-Free-1 offers on groceries; (2) general opening bargains across the store; (3) free gifts with qualifying spend; and (4) a king crab Purchase-with-Purchase at $18.80 — buy what you came for, then pay $18.80 to add the crab. Opening-week discounts are typically the steepest a new supermarket ever goes, because the goal is traffic, not margin. Thursday, September 10 is the last day. Item prices beyond the $18.80 crab PWP were not published — the 72% is a 'selected items' ceiling, so go in with a list, not a fantasy.

💡 Sheng Siong Stacking Strategy: One Receipt, Four Layers

The Rivervale play, in order: fill the trolley with Buy-1-Free-1 staples first (layer 1: instant 50% on those items), add opening-discount fresh items (layer 2), check your receipt total against the free-gift spend threshold before checkout (layer 3 — the qualifying spend is stated in-store), then do the $18.80 king crab PWP at the same counter (layer 4). One trip, four promos on one receipt. If the queue at Rivervale is brutal on the weekend, go Monday–Thursday morning: the deals run to Sep 10, and weekday openings are calmer.

Also at Giant: Sakura Chicken launch (till Sep 16)

Running slightly longer, September 3–16, 2026, Sakura Chicken has launched at selected Giant outlets with special chicken deals and sampling sessions. The one-day flash within it: on September 3, spending $30 on Sakura Chicken products got you a free $28 thermal bag — that date has passed, so don't chase it. What remains useful is the chicken pricing itself (specific prices not published — check in store) and the sampling, which lets you taste before bulk-buying. Selected Giant outlets only.

3. All-Week Fresh Deals: Prime Supermarket (Sep 4–10)

Prime Supermarket's Fresh Buy & Wow promotion runs September 4–10, 2026 across its stores, covering Norwegian salmon, fresh fruits, chicken, grass-fed beef, eggs, coffee and pantry essentials. Prime is the quiet value player of the week: less hype than a grand opening, but its fresh-item promos are consistently among the sharpest in the heartland. Prices are store-level (not published centrally) — check the weekly aisle flyer. Note that Prime is also running its Mid-Autumn promotion (below), so a single Prime trip can cover both fresh stock and festive shopping through September 25.

4. New Store: FairPrice Elias Mall, Pasir Ris (open now)

FairPrice has opened at Elias Mall in Pasir Ris, with a heavy fresh-food focus: the store features FairPrice's largest 24-foot two-tier seafood ice bed, live seafood, and an emphasis on freshness, convenience and value. Opening promotions are running now (end date not announced). For East-siders this becomes the fresh-seafood destination of the week — an ice bed that size usually means promotional seafood prices during the opening window. Pasir Ris residents effectively woke up to a new competitor three minutes from the Sheng Siong Rivervale opening, which is the best thing that can happen to a neighbourhood's grocery prices.

6. Chain-by-Chain: Who Wins What This Week

If you only have time for one supermarket this week, the decision tree is simple. Live near Sengkang/Rivervale: Sheng Siong Rivervale, no contest — a grand opening with four stackable layers beats a routine weekly special every time. Live near Tampines or Jurong East: Giant's 2-day special is your play, but it ends Sunday. Need fresh proteins without drama: Prime's Fresh Buy & Wow (salmon, beef, eggs) runs all week to Sep 10. East coast/Pasir Ris: the new FairPrice Elias Mall and its giant seafood ice bed. Everywhere else: the islandwide Sheng Siong Housebrand sale (to Sep 7) plus the month-long Japan & Korea Fair covers a normal grocery run. And whatever you buy on a Saturday, pay attention to payment: DBS PayLah!'s $3 Saturday cashback at wet markets, hawker stalls and heartland shops runs every Saturday until December 19, 2026 — it applies to heartland merchants rather than supermarket counters, so pair it with a wet-market run, not the hypermarket.

7. What to Skip

The king crab PWP at $18.80 is only a deal if you were already doing a big shop — a dedicated trip to Rivervale just for the crab means paying full price on everything else in the trolley to qualify. Skip it if your basket is small. The My Melody & Kuromi organiser bundle (2 multipacks, $16.80, free $19.90 set) is a Sanrio tax: the 'free' set is worth more than the gap only if the household genuinely eats through two multipacks of ice treats before October 31 — families without kids (or Sanrio fans) should pass. And don't cross the island for the Giant 2-day sale: 'up to 50% off' with unpublished item prices means the trip could net less than the petrol or Grab fare. Distance discipline is a grocery skill.

Bottom line: this week's grocery crown goes to Sheng Siong — twice. The Rivervale opening (to Sep 10) for anyone who can reach it, and the islandwide Housebrand sale (to Sep 7) for everyone else. Giant's hypermarket blitz ends Sunday, Prime keeps fresh value going all week, and Pasir Ris gets a brand-new fresh-food FairPrice. Next week the flyers reset; the freebies and opening gifts will be gone. Stock the freezer now.
