September 2026 Food Deals Singapore: Free Korean BBQ, $3 McDonald's Bagel, 1-for-1 Mr Bean

August closes tonight and a fresh batch of September promotions starts rolling in — free KBBQ for groups of five, 50% off A5 wagyu, a $3 bagel game and 17 Saturdays of hawker cashback. Every date checked, every catch listed.

This is the best kind of week in the Singapore deals calendar: the short-lived August promos hit their final days while the big September campaigns launch at the same time. That means two passes at the savings buffet — grab the expiring stuff today and tomorrow, then queue up the month-long September deals before the school-holiday crowds discover them. Here is the full playbook, organised by what needs action first.

1. Ending In The Next 48 Hours — Grab Before Monday

Mr Bean 1-for-1 Soy Drinks — Sunday 30 August only

A one-day treat from Mr Bean: 1-for-1 on soy drinks on 30 August 2026. That is today, so this is the single most time-sensitive item on the list. Grab a friend (or claim both cups yourself — no judgement) and pair up before outlets close. Price of the drink varies by item and outlet; the 1-for-1 applies to the qualifying soy drink range — check the counter for excluded flavours.

McDonald's Jalapeño Cheese Bagel for $3 — game ends 31 August

McDonald's Singapore has launched the new Jalapeño Cheese Bagel — jalapeño and cheese baked into the bagel itself, stacked with egg, mushrooms, melted cheese and your pick of chicken sausage or chicken ham. From 27 to 31 August 2026, you can unlock it for $3 by scoring at least 800 points in the Bagel Town game in the McDonald's app. If mini-games are not your thing, the sandwich can simply be bought at the regular menu price — which the source does not state, so check the app before ordering.

Giant Tampines & IMM two-day hypermarket deals — 29 & 30 August

Giant is running exclusive two-day deals at its Tampines and IMM hypermarkets only, on 29 and 30 August 2026, with discounts up to 57% off across fresh food, groceries, household essentials and kitchenware. Some offers are exclusive to these two locations, so do not expect the same prices at neighbourhood Giant supermarkets. If a stock-up trip was on the plan anyway, today is the day — the promo ends when the store closes on Sunday.

Menya Kokoro cosplay discount — 29 & 30 August, Suntec City

The quirkiest promo of the week: mazesoba chain Menya Kokoro at Suntec City is giving 10% off if you dine in cosplay and follow them on Instagram, plus another 10% off (so up to 20% total) if you post a Reel or TikTok of your visit and tag the outlet. Dates are 29-30 August 2026 only. Cosplay is a hard requirement for the discount — arriving in a regular T-shirt gets you noodles at full price, which the source does not state, so confirm pricing in store.

⚠️ Condition Check — Weekend Deals

Mr Bean: one day only (30 Aug). McDonald's $3 price: requires the Bagel Town game at 800+ points, via the app, until 31 Aug. Giant: Tampines and IMM hypermarkets only. Menya Kokoro: cosplay outfit + Instagram follow required for the first 10%, social post for the second 10%.

2. New September Deals — Starting This Week

I'm Kim Korean BBQ: dine in a group of 5, 1 eats free — 1-30 September

The headline deal of the new month. I'm Kim Korean BBQ at Dhoby Ghaut is running its 4PLUS1 promotion from 1 to 30 September 2026: gather four paying diners and a fifth, and the lowest-paying diner of the group eats free. Sessions run 90 minutes. The source notes the free meal applies "when the promotion terms are met" — meaning specific conditions (such as everyone ordering the buffet tier) apply, so read the fine print at the counter or on their official channels before booking. Buffet price is not stated in the source, so confirm current pricing directly with the restaurant.

Penang Culture: FREE crab bee hoon with buffet — from 1 September

Penang Culture at Compass One is upgrading its buffet from 1 September 2026 with a new Crab Bee Hoon — sweet crab with savoury bee hoon — and the dish comes FREE with the buffet. No end date has been announced for this one, so treat it as "from 1 September, ongoing until further notice." Buffet pricing is not stated in the source; check with the restaurant before heading down. Pro tip: seafood-plus-buffet combos at suburban malls tend to draw September school-holiday queues, so an early dinner slot beats a 7pm walk-in.

Yakiniku Like: 50% off A5 wagyu set — 29 August to 28 September

Solo-yakiniku chain Yakiniku Like is taking 50% off its A5 Wagyu Set (150g) from 29 August to 28 September 2026. The set features A5 Miyazaki Wagyu Brisket served with rice, soup, and salad or kimchi. Two catches: a minimum spend of $40 nett per transaction is required, and you need to be a Foodie San member (free to join if you are not already). Since the discount applies to the set while the $40 threshold applies to the bill, this works best as a pair — two diners ordering sets together should clear the minimum comfortably, but do the maths on the final bill before ordering.

DBS PayLah!: $3 Saturday cashback at hawkers — 29 August to 19 December

The longest-running deal on this list and arguably the easiest money. DBS has brought back its $3 PayLah! cashback promotion from 29 August to 19 December 2026: eligible DBS PayLah! users get up to $3 cashback every Saturday when paying at participating hawker stalls, coffee shops, wet markets and heartland businesses. That is 17 Saturdays in total. You were probably buying lunch anyway — paying through PayLah! instead of cash turns a regular meal into a quietly discounted one. Availability is at participating merchants only, and eligibility criteria (account type, activation) apply per DBS's terms.

💡 September Stacking Strategy

The September deals stack neatly in one week: Saturday 5 September, pay for hawker lunch with DBS PayLah! ($3 cashback). Round up four friends for I'm Kim that evening (one diner free). On a separate weekday, hit Yakiniku Like with one companion to clear the $40 nett minimum and halve the A5 wagyu set. Three promos, one calendar week, minimal extra spend.

3. Free School Holiday Fun — September 2026

StarHub WatchParty: free live Premier League — 6 September, SAFRA Punggol

On 6 September 2026, StarHub is hosting a free WatchParty at SAFRA Punggol screening two Premier League matches live: Everton vs Manchester United and Arsenal vs Chelsea. Registered attendees also get games, prizes, photo opportunities, free snacks and goodie bags — while stocks last. Registration is required, so do not just show up; watch for StarHub's official registration channel. Entry is free.

Gardens by the Bay Mid-Autumn Festival — from 12 September, free entry

Gardens by the Bay's Mid-Autumn celebrations open on 12 September 2026, and as always the outdoor displays are free to walk. This year's edition features dreamy lantern installations, a giant sky whale, LED lion dance performances and a food fair. It is an evening outing that costs nothing to enter — budget only for whatever you end up eating at the food market. Running through the Mid-Autumn period; exact closing date is announced by the gardens, so check their site before planning the last-week visit.

Aperia Mall school holiday family programme — September 2026

Aperia Mall is positioning itself as a school-holiday destination this September with free play areas, interactive activities and what it calls "rewarding culinary discoveries" — a mall-sized answer to holiday crowds. Specific activity dates and any spend requirements are not detailed in the source, so treat this as a free-to-roam option rather than a fixed event, and check the mall's channels for the activity schedule. Entry to the mall is, of course, free.

4. For Senior Shoppers: FairPrice Xtra Senior Week ends 2 September

Worth flagging for parents and grandparents: FairPrice Xtra's Senior Week runs 27 August to 2 September 2026 with Buy 1 Get 1 Free deals on selected health supplements and FairPrice Adult Pants Plus, plus savings on Ensure Life, Polident, Eu Yan Sang and selected Dr. P products. Selected MOBOT mobility-aid purchases also come with free extras. If something on that list is a regular buy, this is the week to double up — BOGOF on supplements effectively halves the unit price. Conditions: deals are on selected items only, at FairPrice Xtra stores.

5. Strategy: What To Do Each Day This Week

  • Sunday 30 Aug (today): Mr Bean 1-for-1 before closing; Giant Tampines/IMM stock-up run (both end today). Menya Kokoro cosplay 20% at Suntec if you have a costume.
  • Monday 31 Aug: Last day to hit 800 points in McDonald's Bagel Town for the $3 Jalapeño Cheese Bagel. FairPrice Xtra Senior Week continues.
  • Tuesday 1 Sep: Penang Culture's free crab bee hoon starts with the buffet at Compass One. I'm Kim 4PLUS1 begins (runs all month, but book early for weekends).
  • Wednesday 2 Sep: Last day of FairPrice Xtra Senior Week — final call for the BOGOF supplements.
  • Saturday 5 Sep: First full weekend of the new promos — pay hawker lunch with DBS PayLah! for the $3 cashback, then gather four friends for I'm Kim.
  • Sunday 6 Sep: StarHub WatchParty at SAFRA Punggol — register in advance for the free EPL screenings and goodie bags.
  • Saturday 12 Sep: Gardens by the Bay Mid-Autumn Festival opens — free evening lantern walk.

6. What To Skip Or Read Carefully

Not every promo deserves a trip. The Menya Kokoro cosplay deal is genuinely fun but only pays off if you already own a costume — buying one for a 20% noodle discount is upside-down maths. The Yakiniku Like wagyu half-price looks flashy, but a solo diner will struggle to reach the $40 nett minimum after the discount, so treat it as a two-person deal. And the DBS PayLah! $3 is capped at $3 per Saturday at participating merchants — great as a habit, pointless as a destination. The pattern to notice across all of these: September promos reward groups (I'm Kim, Yakiniku Like) and routines (PayLah! Saturdays), while the flash stuff (Mr Bean, Giant) rewards speed. Know which type of deal-scarer you are, and September pays you back accordingly.
