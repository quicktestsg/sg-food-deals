November 2026 Food Deals Singapore: $12 Wagyu Steak, Korean BBQ 4+1 & 1-for-1 Sushi — Full Calendar

November is stacking up to be one of the strongest deal months of the year: a reopened steakhouse slinging wagyu at $12++, a Korean BBQ 4+1 all month, two grand openings with 1-for-1 fanfare, and a hot pot anniversary lucky draw. Here is every date, price and catch, organised by what you need to do first.

October is closing out with a whimper of Halloween candy, but the deals calendar flips to November with real firepower. Between October 28 and December 1, at least a dozen food promotions start, peak or expire — and the best of them (a $12++ wagyu steak that normally goes for $68++) have hard end dates. This guide organises everything by urgency: what opens this week, what starts November 1–2, what runs all month, and what dies in the next few days.

1. Opening This Week: Two Grand Openings and a Wagyu Fair

Sushiro Bukit Panjang Plaza — 50% off sushi (Oct 30 – Nov 12)

Japan's biggest conveyor-belt sushi chain opens at Bukit Panjang Plaza on October 30, and grand opening promos run through November 12. The headline: 50% off selected sushi plates, and the selection is not filler — bluefin fatty tuna and salmon belly with ikura are both listed in the promo. Alcoholic drinks go for $6.00++ during the same window. Expect queues in the first weekend; weekday late afternoons are your friend.

Luckin Coffee Yew Tee Point — $3.99 drinks & 1-for-1 (Oct 28 – Nov 10)

Luckin's newest outlet lands at Yew Tee Point (right by Yew Tee MRT) on October 28, with welcome coupons valid until November 10. Through the Luckin app you can grab drinks at $3.99 and a 1-for-1 drink deal at the new store. If you work or live in the west, this is the cheapest caffeine week you'll get all quarter — coupons are store-specific, so check the terms in the app before ordering.

GYUPOP Wagyu Beef Fair — A5 from $30, $5 skewers (Oct 30 – Nov 8)

A pop-up wagyu market at Perennial Business City in Jurong, running 11am–9pm daily from October 30 to November 8. Japanese A5 wagyu starts from $30 per 200g, wagyu skewers go for $5, and shabu beef at $9.90. It's a buy-and-cook-at-home format rather than a restaurant, so think of it as a chance to stock the freezer at pop-up prices. Prices are while-stocks-last at a fair like this — go early in the run.

2. Starting November 1–2: The Heavy Hitters

Barossa Steak & Grill VivoCity — $12++ wagyu oyster blade (Nov 2 – 15)

The single best steak deal of the month. Barossa has reopened at VivoCity (#01-161/162) with a refreshed waterfront space, and is marking the occasion with Australian F1 Wagyu Oyster Blade Steak at $12++ — down from its usual $68++, a saving of $56 per steak. The promo runs November 2–15. Two independent sources confirm the same price and window. At 82% off, assume the catch is simply volume: go at off-peak hours or book ahead.

I'm Kim Korean BBQ 4+1 — one diner eats free (Nov 1 – 30)

All November at the School of the Arts outlet near Dhoby Ghaut: every four paying diners get one diner free. The mechanics matter — you must book online and write "4+1" in the Reservation Notes, and the restaurant's standard conditions apply. For a group of five, that works out to 20% off the whole table before you've even opened the banchan. It's the rare group deal that scales cleanly: eight friends = two free diners.

Haidilao 14th Anniversary Lucky Draw — 85 prizes (Nov 1 – 29)

Haidilao Singapore turns 14 and is giving away 85 prizes across four lucky draws from November 1–29, including iPhones, AirPods Max 2, an Apple iMac and Dyson air purifiers. Entry requires all three of: being a member, spending S$140 nett on a single dine-in bill, and logging into your membership account on the in-store iPad. That's a high bar for a lucky draw — treat the prizes as a bonus on a hot pot meal you were already having, not a reason to inflate your bill.

💡 Haidilao double-play

The Novena outlet is running a separate Meat Feast Set till November 30: four meat selections from six options for S$19.90++, plus one free plush toy per table before 6pm (while stocks last). A couple can eat well under $40++ and walk out with a plushie — much better value-per-dollar than chasing the $140 lucky draw entry.

5. Strategy: The Next 10 Days, Day by Day

  • Wed, Oct 28 (today): Tai Cheong 4 tarts for $8 — one day only. Download the Luckin app for Yew Tee coupons.
  • Thu–Fri, Oct 29–30: Sushiro Bukit Panjang and GYUPOP Wagyu Fair both open Oct 30 — hit Sushiro on weekday afternoon of Oct 30 or 31 to beat the weekend crowd.
  • Sat–Sun, Oct 31–Nov 1: Last weekend for Hoshino soufflé pancakes and Eat Pizza sides (both end Nov 1). Stock 7-Eleven ice cream before Nov 3.
  • Mon, Nov 2: Barossa $12++ wagyu opens at VivoCity. Book now for the first week — an 82% discount on steak does not stay quiet.
  • Week of Nov 3–7: McDonald's McCrispy relaunch lands Nov 5 — check prices in app at launch. Book I'm Kim with "4+1" notes for a November weekend gathering.

6. What to Skip

Not everything on the November board earns a trip. The Haidilao lucky draw is the obvious one — S$140 nett is a lot of hot pot for a lottery ticket, and the Meat Feast Set at S$19.90++ is the better play at the same restaurant. The Texas Chicken IVE promo only makes sense if you were already eating two combo meals; buying two combos for photocards is paying full price for merch. And if you're not in the northwest, the Sushiro grand opening discounts are real but not worth a cross-island trip during opening week chaos — conveyor sushi rewards patience.

Bottom line: if you only do three things this month, make them the Barossa wagyu before November 15, a five-person I'm Kim BBQ night, and one off-peak Sushiro run before November 12. Everything else is gravy — possibly literally, at $12++.
