Cheap Eats Under $10 in Singapore This Week (6–15 Nov 2026)

From $8 Korean fried chicken delivered free on Grab to $7.95 Burger King sets and a one-day 1-for-1 mixian fiesta on 11.11 — every price, date and catch verified.

Double-digit restaurant bills are optional this week. A tight cluster of promotions launched on 5–6 November means you can eat fried chicken, cream puffs, noodles and even karaoke drinks for under $10 a head — if you know the windows and the catches.

1. Jinjja Chicken: $8 Value Meals on Grab (3–15 Nov)

Korean-inspired fried chicken chain Jinjja Chicken is running $8 value meals exclusively on the Grab app from 3 to 15 November 2026, with free delivery when you hit the minimum order value. The deal lives inside Grab's app — ordering at the counter won't get you this price.

2. Burger King: $7.95 Quarter Chicken Leg + Drink (Till 30 Nov)

The longest-running deal on this list: a crispy, spicy quarter chicken leg with a medium drink for $7.95, valid until 30 November 2026. No promo code gymnastics or membership tiers — one of the simplest set-meal prices around.

3. Beard Papa's: $9.90 Pie Cream Puffs, 5 Outlets (6–10 Nov)

To celebrate its new Funan store, Beard Papa's is selling any eligible pie cream puff for $9.90 from 6 to 10 November 2026 — members only. Participating outlets: Bugis Junction, Plaza Singapura, Great World City, Northpoint and Funan.

4. Chao K Party: Night KTV From $9++/Hour With Free-Flow Drinks (From 5 Nov)

Technically entertainment, but the math is food-adjacent: Chao K Party's Night KTV Special starts from $9++ per student and $10++ per adult per hour at Bugis and Dhoby Ghaut, from 5 November 2026 onwards (end date not announced). The free-flow drinks require a Google Review or Xiaohongshu check-in first.

The 1-for-1 Watchlist: Two Dates to Circle

  • 11 Nov — TamJai SamGor 1-for-1 Mixian: One day only, at The Clementi Mall and Junction 8 (J8 takeaway only). Minimum two toppings to qualify.
  • 7–13 Nov — Secret Recipe Double Treats: Toa Payoh branch only. Every main earns 1-for-1 on selected cakes or starters. Dine-in only, one redemption per main.
🎯 11.11 Strategy

TamJai's 1-for-1 lands on Singles' Day itself, while Secret Recipe's window (7–13 Nov) wraps around it. Sequence: Secret Recipe early, mixian on 11 Nov, then Subway's BOGO breakfast wraps (13–14 Nov, before 11am) as the finale.

Worth Stretching Past $10

  • DON DON DONKI Snow Crab (500g) — $26 (U.P. $48), 6–15 Nov: A $22 saving at all stores while stocks last — the biggest fresh-food discount this week.
  • Boon Heng Atas Duck — $18.80 + free half chicken (worth $11.90), 6–12 Nov: Food Junction NEX only. Two roasted meats, effectively under $10 each.
  • Popeyes Festive Buddy Bucket — $22.90, 6 Nov–7 Jan 2027: 6-piece chicken, loaded fries and drinks, running straight through the festive season.

This Week's Best Value Play

Stack the calendar, not the spend: Burger King's $7.95 set covers lazy weekdays all month, Jinjja's $8 Grab meals handle delivery nights until 15 Nov, and the 11.11 cluster rewards those who plan ahead. A full week of deal meals for two: comfortably under $60 — including snow crab.

All prices and dates above are taken from promotions announced by the brands on 5–6 November 2026 — always double-check in-app or in-store, as outlets can sell out or end promos early.
