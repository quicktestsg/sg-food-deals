1-for-1 Food Deals Singapore (17–24 Nov 2026): Starbucks, Geláre, Yee3 & More

School holidays kick off this week and the 1-for-1 offers came out to play. Here is every two-for-one deal running right now, sorted by how fast you need to move.

The third week of November is quietly one of the best deal windows of the year in Singapore: the school holidays have just started, brands are fighting for family footfall, and the first Black Friday warm-up promos are landing. This week alone there are at least four genuine buy-one-get-one offers running — coffee, waffles, coconut drinks and chocolate beverages. Below, everything is organised by deadline, because with 1-for-1 deals the only thing worse than missing one is showing up the day after it ends.

1. Ending This Week — Move Fast (by 20–24 Nov)

☕ Starbucks — 50% OFF selected drinks on GrabFood (17–24 Nov)

The headline deal of the week. From 17 to 24 November 2026, Starbucks Singapore is running a 1-for-1 drinks promotion on GrabFood — effectively 50% off selected beverages, including Caramel Macchiato, Mocha Frappuccino® and Iced Matcha Latte. It works all day, with both pick-up and delivery options, at selected participating outlets. Order through the Grab app; the discount is applied on the platform, no promo code gymnastics needed. If you are feeding a study group or an office, this is the cheapest way to get everyone caffeinated this week.

🧇 Geláre — 1-for-1 Classic Waffles at Cineleisure (until 22 Nov)

Geláre is running 1-for-1 on its Classic Waffles at its Cineleisure outlet only, until 22 November 2026 — framed as a thank-you to customers as the outlet's run at Cineleisure winds down. Two classic waffles for the price of one. Note the single-location catch: this is not islandwide. If you are catching a movie at Somerset this week, dessert is effectively sorted.

🥥 Yee3 (三号椰) — Grand opening: 20% off + 1-for-1 + mystery gift bags (16–22 Nov)

China's viral coconut drink brand Yee3 opens its first Singapore outlet at ION Orchard this week, and the opening promotion runs 16–22 November 2026: 20% off all drinks, 1-for-1 deals, guaranteed prizes with purchase and free mystery gift bags. The menu spans more than 20 coconut-based beverages starting from $4.90. A new-opening week is the cheapest this brand will ever be in Singapore — if curiosity has been nagging you, this is the window.

🥨 Auntie Anne's — Buy 3 Get 1 Free pretzels (16–20 Nov)

Positioned squarely as a school-holiday family treat: from 16 to 20 November 2026, Auntie Anne's Singapore is running Buy 3 Get 1 Free on pretzels at participating outlets. Ends Friday — the shortest runway of the bunch. Three pretzels plus a free fourth is a genuinely cheap afternoon snack for a group of kids (or coworkers who eat like kids).

2. Running Through Late November — Plan, Don't Panic (till 29–30 Nov)

🍗 Wingstop Rivervale Mall — 50% off a 6PC Boneless Wings Meal (until 29 Nov)

To celebrate its new outlet at Rivervale Mall (#01-17/18), Wingstop is offering 50% off a 6PC Boneless Wings Meal when you purchase a 6PC Wings Meal — a stacking deal, not just a straight discount. Available until 29 November 2026, exclusively at the Rivervale Mall outlet. Northeast-side residents, this is your week to inaugurate the new store.

🍱 DON DON DONKI — App member coupons on sashimi, A5 wagyu & more (16–30 Nov)

From 16 to 30 November 2026, DONKI App members get exclusive dWallet coupons on selected items including Sashimi 3 Kind, A5 Wagyu Chuck Roll Shabu-Shabu, Hane Gyoza Chicken, Tokyo Banana Cake 4's and Orihiro Konnyaku Jelly. Not a 1-for-1, but the cheapest legitimate route to A5 wagyu at home this month. Requires the DONKI app and membership — clip the coupons in-app before you shop.

3. Marathon Deals — Running Into January

🍔 Burger King — App deals from $2.95 + 1-for-1 KitKat drinks (15 Nov – 3 Jan)

Burger King's long-running app-exclusive promotion covers 15 November 2026 to 3 January 2027: a Single Mushroom Swiss Burger for $2.95 for new BK App sign-ups, BBQ Cheesy Gouda Burgers at $5.95, and 1-for-1 KitKat Chocolate Drinks from $4.20 for two — at participating outlets. Nearly seven weeks of runway makes this the lowest-urgency deal on the list, but the $2.95 burger requires a fresh app sign-up, so plan accordingly if you already have an account.

🍟 KFC — NEW Sweet Purple Potato Cheese Mochi from $3.20 (18 Nov – 12 Jan)

KFC's new Sweet Purple Potato Cheese Mochi launches tomorrow, 18 November 2026, and runs until 12 January 2027: chewy purple mochi with a creamy cheese filling and sweet purple potato centre, from $3.20 for 2 pieces when added to a meal, at participating outlets and on KFC Delivery. Not 1-for-1, but a low-commitment way to try the year's most curiosity-inducing fast-food launch.

🍕 Pizza Hut — $10 large pizzas every Tue & Wed (17 Nov – 9 Dec)

From 17 November to 9 December 2026, Pizza Hut sells selected large pizzas for $10 every Tuesday and Wednesday — four flavours including the new Chicken Corn Ranch. Takeaway only: online self-collection order or walk-in. That is four discounted Tuesdays and Wednesdays in a row; tonight (Tuesday) is day one.

4. Bonus: The $3.80 Western Lunch in Kallang Bahru

Not a 1-for-1, but the standout cheap eat of the week: from 17 November 2026, Hearty Western Food at Kallang Bahru is doing Chicken Wing with Rice for S$3.80, with chicken chop, fish & chips, burgers and pasta at S$5.50 each — available daily from 11am to 5pm. If you work in the Kallang area, this is a hawker-priced Western lunch that undercuts almost everything else on this list on a per-person basis.

  • Burger King: BK App required; $2.95 burger is for NEW app sign-ups only.
  • Pizza Hut: takeaway only (self-collection or walk-in), Tuesdays and Wednesdays only.
  • Wingstop: 50% off requires purchase of a 6PC Wings Meal first; Rivervale Mall outlet only.
  • DONKI: app membership + in-app coupon clipping required.

5. Strategy: Your Action Plan This Week

  • Tuesday 17 Nov (today): Pizza Hut $10 large pizza day one — order self-collection tonight. Starbucks 1-for-1 on GrabFood starts today too. Hearty Western's S$3.80 lunch deal begins.
  • Wednesday 18 Nov: Second Pizza Hut $10 day; KFC's purple potato mochi launches — add 2 pieces ($3.20) to a meal to try it.
  • Thursday 19 Nov: Orchard run: Yee3 at ION for 20%-off coconut drinks and the mystery gift bag, then check Geláre Cineleisure's 1-for-1 waffles nearby — both end Saturday.
  • Friday 20 Nov: Last day for Auntie Anne's Buy 3 Get 1 Free — grab pretzels after school pickup.
  • Saturday 22 Nov: Final call for Geláre and Yee3.
  • Tuesday 24 Nov: Last day of Starbucks 1-for-1 on GrabFood — max it out before it goes.
  • Weekend of 28–29 Nov: Wingstop Rivervale half-price wings before the 29 Nov deadline.

6. Verdict: What's Worth It

If you only act on one thing this week, make it the Starbucks GrabFood 1-for-1 — all-day availability, pick-up or delivery, and drinks people actually order anyway. Second priority is the Orchard double-header: Yee3's opening week is the cheapest entry point to a hyped new brand, and Geláre's farewell waffles pair naturally with it. Pizza Hut's $10 Tuesdays-and-Wednesdays is the best recurring play for families. Burger King and KFC, meanwhile, run into January — no urgency, so bank them for a lean week later in December when the deal calendar goes quiet.
