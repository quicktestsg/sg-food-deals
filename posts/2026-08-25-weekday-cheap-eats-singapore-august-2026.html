Weekday Cheap Eats Singapore: $3.90 Desserts, $5 Michelin Noodles, $10 Pizza (Aug 25–31, 2026)

The best food deals in Singapore this week are hiding behind day-of-week gates: $10 large pizzas on Tuesday and Wednesday only, a $5 Michelin-recognised fishball noodle run that ends Friday, payday-week 1-for-1 coffee, and weekday-only $3.90 avocado ice cream. Here is the day-by-day plan to catch all of them.

Restaurants price their weekday promos aggressively for one simple reason: office workers go out on weekdays, families come out on weekends. That is backwards for bargain hunters — the deals are stacked Monday to Friday precisely when most people cook at home or eat at their desk. This week is a textbook example: a Tuesday/Wednesday-only pizza deal, a five-day Michelin celebration price, and payday-week 1-for-1 coffees all land before Saturday. Miss the window and you pay full price on the weekend for the exact same meal.

1. Today & Tomorrow Only: Pizza Hut $10 Large Pizza (Tue & Wed)

Pizza Hut has brought back its $10 large takeaway pizza deal for six selected days: 25 and 26 August, then 1–2 and 8–9 September. Today (Tuesday 25 Aug) and tomorrow are the first two windows — and at $10 for a large, this is one of the lowest per-slice prices you will find from a major pizza chain this month. The deal covers selected flavours including BBQ Chunky Chic, Very Beefy and Chic Ham ‘N’ Shroom. Note the catch: it is takeaway only, so no dine-in, and the cheap dates skip Thursday through Sunday entirely.

The other expiring-quick item this week: LiHO’s Shine Muscat promotion, which runs only 24–26 August. Buy one drink and the second is 50% off across the entire Shine Muscat range — fruit teas, yoghurt drinks, smoothies, tea lattes and punch all qualify. Wednesday is the last day. Pair it with the pizza deal tomorrow and you have a full $15-ish takeaway dinner for two sorted.

💡 Two-Stop Tuesday Combo

Order the $10 Pizza Hut large for takeaway, swing by LiHO for the second-at-half-price Shine Muscat drink before Wednesday ends, and you have fed two people dinner plus drinks for roughly what one large pizza normally costs alone.

2. Free Chicken Pie on Wednesday: Ya Kun Far East Square, 3PM

Ya Kun Kaya Toast is celebrating the grand reopening of its Far East Square outlet with a free Classic Comfy Chicken Pie giveaway on 26 August 2026. From 3pm onwards, the first 200 customers get one pie each, no purchase required. If you work in the CBD, this is a zero-dollar afternoon snack — but the 200-pie cap means arriving closer to 3pm than 4pm is the difference between a free pie and a sad walk back to the office. One day only, one outlet only.

3. Michelin Recognition for $5: LiXin Teochew Fishball Noodles, Till Friday

LiXin Teochew Fishball Noodles just earned its fifth consecutive Michelin Bib Gourmand nod, and it is marking the occasion with a five-day promotion: Traditional Teochew Fishball Noodles at S$5, from 24 to 28 August 2026, across 11 participating outlets — including the new Jewel Changi Airport location. Handmade-fishball noodle dishes at hawker-adjacent prices are exactly what the Bib Gourmand list is supposed to surface, and a $5 celebration price makes this the best value sit-down meal of the week. Friday is the hard cutoff; there is no indication the price extends into September.

4. Payday-Week Fuel: Luckin 1-for-1 & Dunkin’ Free Donuts

It is the last week of August, which means payday promotions. Luckin Coffee is running a 1-for-1 coffee deal from 24 to 30 August under its Payday Best Day banner — selected coffees qualify, including the Pandan Coconut Latte and Coconut Latte. Buy one, get the second free, which effectively halves your caffeine budget for the entire week. Sunday 30 August is the end date.

Dunkin’ has its own Payday Deals running: buy 5 donuts and 2 are free (the two lowest-priced ones), plus a 1-for-1 on Cold Brew and hot or iced Teh Tarik. The source promotion does not state an end date, so treat it as valid while the campaign lasts and confirm in the Dunkin’ app before making a special trip.

5. Weekday-Only Treats: $3.90 Avocado Ice Cream & YAYOI Afternoon Sets

Two new weekday-only promotions started on 24 August. At Lucky Plaza in Orchard, Avocadoria is selling its Light Avocado Ice Cream for $3.90, Monday to Friday — made with real avocados and no preservatives, it is one of the cheapest “real fruit” desserts in the Orchard area right now. The promotion is ongoing with no announced end date, but weekday-only, so skip the weekend crowd pricing and go during your lunch break.

YAYOI is also running a weekday afternoon dessert-and-drink treat from 24 August onwards at five selected outlets: 100AM, Pasir Ris Mall, United Square, The Star Vista and Northpoint City. The source does not publish the price or the exact menu items, so check in store or on YAYOI’s official channels before ordering — do not assume a specific price. What is confirmed: weekday afternoons only, those five locations only.

6. Stock the Pantry: DON DON DONKI Premium Kaya From $4.90

DON DON DONKI has launched a Singapore-made Premium Kaya range from $4.90, in four flavours: Coconut, Sea Salt Caramel, Matcha and Durian. Available from 24 August onwards at DONKI stores, while stocks last, with no announced end date. If your breakfast routine is kaya toast, buying the jar at promo price and toasting at home works out considerably cheaper per serving than café kaya toast — and the Durian flavour is not something the traditional kopitiam jars offer.

7. Friday Splurge: Morganfield’s 1-for-1 Angus Ribeye at Suntec

If one proper restaurant meal is on this week’s budget, Morganfield’s is running a 1-for-1 Angus Ribeye Steak promotion from 21 to 30 August at its Suntec City outlet only: two ribeye steaks for $42.90++, served with French fries and garden salad, and multiple sets can be ordered. That works out to about $21.45++ per steak with sides — steakhouse territory for roughly food-court-plus pricing. Remember the ++ means service charge and GST on top, and it is Suntec only, ending Sunday 30 August.

Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Tuesday, Aug 25 (today): Grab the Pizza Hut $10 large for takeaway (day 1 of the Tue/Wed window). Order a second LiHO Shine Muscat at 50% off before the promo dies Wednesday.
  • Wednesday, Aug 26: Last day for the $10 pizza. Be at Ya Kun Far East Square by 3pm for the free chicken pie (first 200 only). Last day of LiHO Shine Muscat 1-for-1-at-half-price.
  • Thursday, Aug 27: LiXin $5 Michelin Bib Gourmand fishball noodles (day 4 of 5). Luckin 1-for-1 coffee to power through. Weekday afternoon: YAYOI dessert set or Avocadoria $3.90 ice cream.
  • Friday, Aug 28: Final day of LiXin $5 noodles — after today it is gone. Evening option: Morganfield’s 1-for-1 Angus ribeye at Suntec for a end-of-week steak dinner.
  • Saturday–Sunday, Aug 29–30: No weekday-gated deals left, but Luckin 1-for-1 (till Sun) and Morganfield’s 1-for-1 (till Sun) still run. Pick up a DONKI kaya jar for next week’s breakfasts while stocks last.

The Verdict

This is a strong weekday week. The two moves that matter: pizza today or tomorrow ($10 large, takeaway), and the LiXin $5 Michelin-recognised noodles any day before Friday. Everything else — Luckin’s 1-for-1, the free Ya Kun pie on Wednesday, $3.90 avocado ice cream — slots in around those anchors without extra effort. If you only act once this week, make it the fishball noodles: a fifth consecutive Bib Gourmand at a fifth-ish of typical restaurant pricing is exactly the kind of deal that justifies breaking your lunch routine. And if you miss the pizza window entirely, it returns 1–2 and 8–9 September — same rules, same $10.
