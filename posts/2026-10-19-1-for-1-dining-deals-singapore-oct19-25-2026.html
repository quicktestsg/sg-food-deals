1-for-1 Dining Deals in Singapore This Week (19–25 Oct 2026): PastaMania, KFC, Luckin Coffee & More

World Pasta Day turns PastaMania into a two-for-one canteen all week, KFC's Nanban bites are back at $6.95 for 12 pieces, and a SAFRA-only hotel buffet rounds out the strongest 1-for-1 line-up this October.

Payday is still over a week away, but this week's 1-for-1 line-up does most of the heavy lifting for your food budget. The headliner starts today: PastaMania is running 1-for-1 pasta for World Pasta Day at every outlet in Singapore. Stack that against KFC's weekday Nanban bites, Luckin Coffee's new-store promotion, and two very different buffet plays, and you can eat half-price from Monday to Sunday. Here is the full breakdown, with dates, prices and every catch spelled out.

1. The Headliner: PastaMania 1-for-1 Pasta (19–23 Oct)

From today, Monday 19 October, through Friday 23 October 2026, PastaMania is offering 1-for-1 on any pasta at all Singapore outlets for World Pasta Day. Two caveats matter: the deal runs from opening until 5pm only, with last orders at 4.45pm — this is a lunch and early-dinner play, not a 9pm supper run. And per the promotion details, you'll want the TriplePlus app: register with referral code T53f for additional member offers on top of the 1-for-1.

💡 Pasta Pairing Strategy

Order the pricier pasta you actually want as the first item — the 1-for-1 applies to the pair, so two premium pastas split down the middle beats pairing a cheap and an expensive one. Go before 12pm or after 2pm; weekday lunch crowds at outlet malls (where PastaMania often sits) are thinnest off-peak.

2. KFC 1-for-1 Japanese Nanban Chicken Bites — 12pc for $6.95 (Wed–Fri)

KFC's 1-for-1 Japanese Nanban Chicken Bites promotion (running since 14 October 2026, ongoing with no announced end date) gives you 12 pieces for $6.95 every Wednesday to Friday. This week that means Wednesday 21 to Friday 23 October. The deal is valid for dine-in and takeaway at participating outlets, while stocks last — but it excludes Sentosa, Singapore Polytechnic and Zoo locations, and public holidays are blacked out. At roughly 58¢ a piece, splitting a box between two people is one of the cheapest hot lunches on the island this week.

3. Luckin Coffee 1-for-1 Drinks + $0.99 First Cup (till 26 Oct)

Luckin Coffee's grand opening promotion at its two newest outlets — SUTD and Limbang Shopping Centre — runs 13 to 26 October 2026 with 1-for-1 drinks, plus a first-cup offer at $0.99 through the Luckin app (subject to terms and conditions). This is location-locked: the deal celebrates these two specific new stores, so don't expect it at your CBD branch. If you work or study near SUTD or live in the Limbang area, this is the coffee play of the week — and the $0.99 first-cup offer is app-only.

4. Two Buffet 1-for-1s: Hotel Seafood vs Local Classics

Two very different buffet deals overlap this week. First, Food Capital at Grand Copthorne Waterfront is giving SAFRA members a 1-for-1 Seafood Mania Buffet with Vietnamese specialties on weekdays, from 5 October to 1 November 2026, plus a separate 20% off total bill promotion. Second, Opus Bar & Grill has a 1-for-1 deal for adults on its Sunday "Curry On!" Local Lunch Buffet in collaboration with Old Chang Kee — think curry puffs and laksa on a hotel buffet line (end date not announced; this Sunday 25 October is your window). One requires a SAFRA membership, the other doesn't — pick accordingly.

5. New Opening: Spicy Wife's 1-for-1 Nasi Lemak at The Centrepoint

Amoy Street Food Centre cult favourite Spicy Wife has opened a new outlet — Spicy Hubby — at The Centrepoint in Orchard, with a 1-for-1 nasi lemak promotion to celebrate the opening. The original stall built its following on aromatic rice and generous portions. Opening-promo end date not announced — these typically run one to two weeks, so treat this week as the safe window and check the store's social pages before making the trip to Orchard.

6. The Free-and-Cheap Supporting Cast (19–25 Oct)

  • IKEA free coffee or tea (19–23 Oct): No purchase required at the Swedish Restaurant at all IKEA stores; IKEA Family members get two free cups per visit. Zero conditions beyond showing up.
  • Auntie Anne's Buy 3 Get 1 Free (19–23 Oct): Buy any three items and the free item must come from the Signature Pretzel category — so make sure one of your three picks is what you want anyway, and take the free pretzel as the bonus.
  • Sushi Tei Jewel reopening (19–25 Oct): Kirin Fuji Sanroku 50-degree whisky at $6 a glass, limited to the first 20 orders daily. The free 3-piece salmon sashimi with minimum spend follows from 26 Oct to 1 Nov.
  • McDonald's pre-payday $6.90 app deals (19–23 Oct): MyM app exclusive weekday deals — Monday to Wednesday the Scrambled Egg Burger with Sausage Meal from $6.90 (usual price from $8.00) during breakfast hours, with McCrispy offers in the later part of the week; app orders of $35 and above also get free McDelivery. Full terms in the app.
  • Bonus — Uncle Leong whole crab at $38 (all October): Not a 1-for-1, but a whole crab in your choice of 14 flavours for a flat $38 at the Joo Koon and Kampong Ampat outlets until 31 October 2026 — the strongest seafood value running right now.

McDonald's: MyM app membership required, breakfast hours for the Mon–Wed deal.

7. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Monday, 19 Oct: PastaMania 1-for-1 starts — beat the crowd, lunch before 12pm. IKEA free coffee if you're near Alexandra or Tampines.
  • Tuesday, 20 Oct: PastaMania again, or Spicy Hubby's 1-for-1 nasi lemak at The Centrepoint if you're in Orchard.
  • Wednesday, 21 Oct: KFC Nanban bites 1-for-1 window opens (Wed–Fri). Sushi Tei Jewel's $6 whisky if you can grab one of the first 20 orders.
  • Thursday, 22 Oct: PastaMania last full weekday — pair with Auntie Anne's Buy 3 Get 1 Free for an afternoon snack. McDonald's McCrispy app deals for dinner.
  • Friday, 23 Oct: Final day for PastaMania, IKEA coffee, Auntie Anne's and McDonald's pre-payday deals — everything closes today except KFC and Luckin.
  • Saturday, 24 Oct: Luckin Coffee 1-for-1 continues at SUTD and Limbang (till 26 Oct). Uncle Leong's $38 whole crab for a weekend family meal.
  • Sunday, 25 Oct: Opus Bar & Grill's 1-for-1 Old Chang Kee local buffet for Sunday lunch — book ahead, and Sushi Tei's $6 whisky order is live till 25 Oct.

One fine-print habit worth keeping all week: prices marked with ++ (like hotel buffet set lunches) attract service charge and GST on top, so the "1-for-1" headline at hotels always lands slightly above half of what two adults would have paid. The fast-food and café deals above are all nett or close to it. Deals move fast in pre-payday week — if a promotion page is down or an outlet says "while stocks last" and looks picked over, the deal is gone, not paused.
