Teachers' Day Free Food Singapore 2026: Free Doughnuts, Froyo Upgrades + Weekend 1-for-1 Deals (Sep 3–9)

Teachers' Day falls on Friday, September 4, 2026 — and Singapore's cafes are loading up freebies that day: free Krispy Kreme doughnuts, llaollao tub upgrades and free matcha soft serve, all with a teacher's ID. Pair that with a 1-for-1 seafood bowl weekend, a ribs buffet, a $12 Popeyes bucket and the fresh supermarket sale week, and the next seven days are unusually loaded.

Three storylines converge this week. First, Teachers' Day on Friday September 4 brings a stack of one-day freebies that all require a valid teacher's ID. Second, the Sep 4–6 weekend carries the heavy hitters: a Kei Kaisendon 1-for-1 at Great World City and Morganfield's weekend ribs buffet at Suntec. Third, the new supermarket cycle starts today (Sep 3) — FairPrice, Giant and Cold Storage all launched their weekly storewide sales within hours of each other, and FairPrice has doubled its CHAS discount to 6% for the rest of the year. This guide walks through all three, with dates, prices and the catches spelled out.

1. Teachers' Day Freebies: Friday, Sep 4 Only (Teacher's ID Required)

Four one-day promotions are running on Friday, and three of them are genuinely free-with-purchase rather than vague 'appreciation' marketing. Every one of them requires a valid teacher's ID at the counter — this is the single condition that disqualifies most people, so check before queuing.

Deal What You Get Where / Catch
Krispy Kreme Buy a dozen doughnuts, get 6 Original Glazed FREE All outlets · limited redemptions per store, while stocks last
llaollao Buy 2 Medium Tubes, FREE upgrade to 2 Large Tubs All outlets · one day only · teacher's ID

The Krispy Kreme offer is the headline: six free Original Glazed on top of a dozen is effectively half-price doughnuts if you were buying a box for the staff room anyway. The llaollao upgrade is the easiest redemption of the day — same counter, same order, just bring the ID. Note that Dunkin's $4 Latte Day is NOT teacher-exclusive: anyone can walk in and get a regular latte, hot or iced, for $4 at participating outlets on Sep 4 only. And running alongside all of this, TOUS les JOURS has a Teachers' Day and Mid-Autumn appreciation promo from Sep 2 onwards — Buy 1 Get 1 Free on Granola Crunch plus 30% off the new French Cookie Gift Set at selected outlets (Tang Plaza, Bukit Pasoh among them; end date not announced).

2. The Weekend Playbook: 1-for-1 Sashimi Bowls + A Ribs Buffet (Sep 4–6)

  • Kei Kaisendon — 1-for-1 Seafood Bowls (Sep 4–6): the Great World City outlet is running a three-day 1-for-1 on its Japanese-style seafood bowls. Buy one, get the second free — the cleanest way to share sashimi donburi with a friend this weekend. The promotion is outlet-specific: it applies at Great World City, not islandwide.
  • Morganfield's — Weekend All-You-Can-Eat Ribs Buffet (from Sep 1 onwards): at Suntec City, unlimited Spare Ribs and Baby Back Ribs for $49 per adult and $25 per child under 12, with soup, sides and an ice cream sundae included. It runs on weekends from 1 September 2026 onwards (end date not announced) — treat it as the standing weekend option rather than a flash deal.
  • Morganfield's — Kids Eat Free (from Sep 2 onwards): on Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays and school holidays, a child aged 7 or below eats free with every adult main meal purchased. Stack this mentally with the buffet: for a family with a young kid, the math depends on the child's appetite — the $25 buffet child price vs a free kids' meal.
  • Morganfield's — $5++ San Miguel Pints (from Aug 31 onwards): for the adults not driving, San Miguel Pale Pilsen at $5++ per pint is running at participating outlets from 31 August 2026 onwards (end date not announced).
  • Guzman y Gomez — $6 Minis Day (Sunday, Sep 6 only): one day only, selected favourite Minis for $6. Worth flagging that GYG also restarted its $3 Brekkie Burrito Wednesdays this week (started Sep 3, breakfast hours till 10:30am, U.P. $7, across 19 participating restaurants including Northpoint) — so Wednesday mornings and Sunday lunch at GYG are both officially cheap now.

3. The $12 Popeyes Bucket: Cheapest Big-Mouth Deal of the Month (Sep 3–20)

Popeyes Singapore's Ultimate Chicken Bucket runs from 3 to 20 September 2026: four pieces of Signature Chicken for $12, down from the usual $19.60 — that is 38% off, confirmed pricing from the source. Classic or Spicy, your choice, while stocks last. Two independent deal blogs carried the same numbers this week, so the price is consistent across sources. At $3 per piece of proper fried chicken, this is the most reliable sub-$15 family add-on running right now, and it lasts over two weeks — no need to rush.

4. Supermarket Week: FairPrice vs Giant vs Cold Storage (All Start Sep 3)

The three big chains flipped to their new weekly cycles on the same day, which makes comparison unusually clean:

  • FairPrice (Sep 3–9): up to 59% off across fresh produce, frozen and chilled food, pantry staples, beverages and household products. The bigger story: FairPrice has doubled its CHAS discount to 6%, effective 3 September 2026 all the way to 1 January 2027. If you hold a CHAS card, this is a four-month structural discount, not a weekly special.
  • Giant (from Sep 3): 7-day and 14-day sale with discounts up to 63% off — the highest headline percentage of the three. Availability differs store to store, while stocks last. Giant has also extended its own 6% CHAS discount until 25 September 2026.
  • Cold Storage & CS Fresh (from Sep 3): weekly storewide offers while stocks last, and shoppers aged 60 and above get an additional 3% off. The standout line item: Häagen-Dazs tubs at 2-for-$19.80, running till 9 September 2026 — stock the freezer now if premium ice cream is your weakness.
💡 CHAS Double-Discount Season

CHAS cardholders are the quiet winners of September: FairPrice at 6% (Sep 3 – Jan 1, 2027) and Giant at 6% (till Sep 25) both stack a loyalty-tier discount on top of whatever weekly specials are running. The two programs run at different chains, so the move is simple — bring the CHAS card to whichever supermarket you are already in.

5. Weekday Warriors: The Ongoing Cheap Eats (All September)

These are the slower-burn promotions that run through the month — less urgent, but they fill the weekdays between the weekend splurges:

  • Ramen King (Sep 1–30): add $3 to any soup ramen for unlimited free-flow noodles and green tea. Monday to Friday, dine-in only, selected outlets.
  • Cai-Ca (Sep 2 – Oct 11): 30% off the second and every subsequent drink in the same transaction, Monday to Friday 2pm–5pm, at Bugis Junction and Northpoint City. The classic afternoon tea-top-up deal.
  • Chen's Mapo Tofu (Sep 1 – Oct 31): order any non-Mapo dish and get a complimentary Mini Mapo Tofu at selected outlets — limited to the first 100 bowls daily, so dinner beats late supper.
  • Tanglin Cookhouse (Sep 1–30): buy two mains, get one selected item free, Monday to Friday from 5:30pm to 10pm at Tanglin Mall and Paya Lebar Quarter. A weekday-dinner play, not a weekend one.
  • PAWA Bakery (now till Sep 30): 20% off the Lava Cheese Prawn Bagel, from $6.50 down to $5.20, at selected outlets including PLQ Mall, Suntec City, The Star Vista and Cecil Street.
  • Mr Onigiri at Lot One (Sep 2–4 — ends tomorrow): grand-opening special, 2pm–5pm only, second Yakitori Onigiri for $1.99 while stocks last. This one is nearly over — Thursday is the last day.
⚠️ Condition Check — Read Before You Queue
  • Teachers' Day freebies need the ID: Krispy Kreme, llaollao and 108 Matcha Saro all verify a valid teacher's ID on Sep 4. Krispy Kreme caps redemptions per store; 108 Matcha Saro is selected outlets only while stocks last.
  • Kei Kaisendon 1-for-1 is one outlet only: Great World City, Sep 4–6. Do not expect it at other Kei Kaisendon branches.
  • Morganfield's kids promos have age gates: the buffet child price is for under-12s; Kids Eat Free is for children aged 7 and below, and only on Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays and school holidays, with an adult main purchase.
  • Supermarket deals are while-stocks-last and store-dependent: Giant explicitly notes availability differs store to store; Cold Storage's offers are limited-time while stocks last. FairPrice's CHAS 6% runs 3 Sep 2026 – 1 Jan 2027; Giant's CHAS 6% ends 25 Sep 2026.
  • Weekday promos mean weekday: Ramen King's $3 add-on is Monday to Friday dine-in; Cai-Ca's 30% is Monday to Friday 2–5pm; Tanglin Cookhouse's buy-2-get-1 is Monday to Friday dinner. None of them apply on the weekend.

6. The Verdict

If you are a teacher, Friday is simply your day: start with the 108 Matcha Saro mochi-and-soft-serve pairing, collect a Krispy Kreme box for the department, and let llaollao handle dessert. If you are not, the weekend decision tree is short — sashimi lovers go to Great World City for the Kei Kaisendon 1-for-1 (Sep 4–6), rib carnivores go to Suntec for Morganfield's $49 buffet, and everyone else grabs the $12 Popeyes bucket, which conveniently runs until Sep 20 and forgives procrastination. The one deadline that actually bites this week is Mr Onigiri's $1.99 second onigiri at Lot One, which dies after Thursday's 2–5pm window. And with FairPrice's CHAS discount now doubled to 6% until New Year's Day, the grocery runs quietly got cheaper for the rest of 2026.
