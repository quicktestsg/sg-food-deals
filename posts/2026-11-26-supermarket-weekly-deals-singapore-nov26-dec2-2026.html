Supermarket Weekly Deals Singapore: FairPrice vs Cold Storage vs Giant (26 Nov – 2 Dec 2026)

All three major chains reset their promotions on 26 November — the same day Black Friday food deals kick off. Here's which store to hit for what, how to stack member discounts, and the cheap eats under $4 to grab along the way.

Every Thursday-ish, Singapore's supermarket chains roll over their weekly promotions. This week is unusually well-timed: FairPrice, Cold Storage and Giant all launched new promo cycles on 26 November 2026, right as Black Friday food promos start. That means one shopping trip can catch three overlapping layers of discounts — but only if you know which store does what best.

No single store wins everything. The pattern that repeats week after week: FairPrice for the deepest weekly cut on fresh and staples, Cold Storage for BOGO quality items if you hold a yuu card, and Giant for housebrand volume pricing when you're stocking the pantry for December.

1. FairPrice: Two Sales Running at Once

FairPrice is running its regular weekly promotions from 26 November to 2 December 2026, with savings of up to 55% across fresh produce, groceries, frozen food, beverages, personal care and household essentials. On top of that, NTUC and Link members get exclusive member discounts, and there are Buy 1 Get 1 Free offers mixed into the cycle.

The bigger story this week is at the Xtra hypermarket format. The FairPrice Xtra Year-End Sale 2026 runs alongside the weekly promos and adds discounts of up to 49% on selected groceries, household essentials and kitchen appliances, plus limited-time seafood promotions and festive wine deals. The Xtra-specific angle: a salmon fair and weekend bulk-buy savings that the regular FairPrice supermarkets don't carry. If a salmon fillet is on your December hosting list, the Xtra salmon fair is the single best window this month — salmon is normally one of the least-discounted proteins in Singapore, so any dedicated fair is worth a detour. Exact salmon pricing varies by cut and store, so check the in-store fair board or the FairPrice app before making the trip (prices not confirmed in the source listings).

💡 FairPrice Stacking Strategy

One trip, three layers: shop the 55%-off weekly items, hit the Xtra salmon fair and bulk-buy savings in the same visit, and pay with a card linked to Link/Plus! points so the member discount and points land on one receipt. Timing matters — bulk-buy savings are weekend-weighted, so Saturday gives the deepest combined cut.

2. Cold Storage: The yuu Window Is Only Four Days

Cold Storage's new promo cycle also runs 26 November to 2 December 2026, with savings on fresh produce, premium meats, seafood, groceries, household essentials and Christmas treats. But the sharp edge here is the yuu Member Specials — up to 50% off, and only from 26 to 29 November 2026. After Sunday, the yuu layer drops off and you're back to the standard promo prices.

One concrete yuu deal already confirmed this week: 1-for-1 on Magnolia Mag-A-Cone ice cream, running till 29 November 2026. The source also flags additional savings of up to 50% on other deals. Buy 1 Get 1 Free offers run across the wider cycle — that's the format where Cold Storage consistently beats the other chains, because the free item is full-size, not a smaller 'promo size'.

⏰ The 29 Nov Cliff

The yuu Member Specials (up to 50% off) and the Magnolia Mag-A-Cone 1-for-1 both end on 29 November 2026. The general 26 Nov – 2 Dec promos continue after that, but at weaker pricing. If you only make one Cold Storage trip this week, make it before Sunday.

3. Giant: The Bulk-Buy Store This Week

Giant's new round of promotions started 26 November 2026 with savings on fresh produce, groceries, seafood and household essentials. Three things make Giant worth the trip in a Black Friday week: four-day specials (short, deep cuts that rotate fast), year-end bulk purchase deals (timed for festive stock-up season), and Value Heroes — the budget item lineup. Housebrand savings are the quiet winner: Giant's own-label staples are already the cheapest shelf in the market, and the housebrand promo slices another layer off.

Strategy: reserve Giant for the boring-but-heavy list — rice, oil, cleaning supplies, canned goods, drinks for December gatherings. Buy those in bulk now during the year-end deals, then do a light FairPrice or Cold Storage run midweek for fresh items. Splitting the trip is the difference between a $200 receipt with everything at medium discounts and two $90 receipts where each category is bought at its cheapest store.

5. Black Friday Add-Ons Worth Pairing With the Grocery Run

A&W Singapore's Black Friday deals run 26–29 November 2026: a free waffle of your choice (worth up to $9.20) with any Buddy Meal or Family Feast, 15% off delivery combos, and limited-edition tumblers at $26.90. The waffle-with-meal structure makes it the natural reward for a weekend grocery haul — one Family Feast covers the family and the free waffle covers dessert.

Sa Sa's weekend beauty deals (26–30 Nov) overlap the same window: any 2 items at 50% off across selected skincare, suncare, makeup and fragrances, box masks at buy-2-get-3-free, and an extra 10% off fragrance sets. If the household list includes skincare, the Sa Sa math beats the department-store Black Friday counters for entry-level brands — though the selection is smaller.

Two promos end today, 26 November: the UNIQLO Thank You Festival (free Pocketable Travel Bag with a $120 minimum spend for UNIQLO APP members, or a Mini Fluffy Fleece Charm when paying in-store with a DBS/POSB card) and Mr Bean's durian pancake discount. If either is on your list, today is the last day.

  • Friday, 27 Nov: Black Friday proper — A&W free waffle deals and delivery discount live. Giant four-day specials worth checking.

7. What to Skip This Week

Jack's Place's 60th anniversary ribeye deal — 180g New Zealand ribeye at $15++ (usual price $29++) for members — ended on 25 November. It was the best steak deal of the month, and it's gone. Skip any social media post still circulating it. Also skip the impulse 7-Eleven collectibles unless you're already a collector: the Gundam Minikit blind boxes ($4.90 each, five designs, from 25 Nov, selected stores) and Sanrio mascot packs with gummies (12 designs, from 25 Nov) are fun, but at $4.90 a blind box, 'collecting them all' costs more than a decent dinner.

⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal in this post has a catch: FairPrice member discounts need NTUC/Link membership; Cold Storage's best layer needs a yuu account (free to register); MIXUE's Blueberry Series is limited to three locations and while stocks last; Mr Bean's pancake discount requires a soy drink purchase and expired after 26 Nov; the A&W free waffle requires a Buddy Meal or Family Feast; UNIQLO's free travel bag needed the app plus a $120 spend (DBS/POSB card for the in-store charm); I'm Kim Korean BBQ's December 4+1 buffet and 3+1 student promos (1–31 Dec, SOTA Dhoby Ghaut) require advance online reservations and, for the student deal, selected timeslots only. Read the fine print before travelling to any outlet.

Looking Ahead to December

The 26 Nov – 2 Dec cycle is the bridge between Black Friday and the Christmas stretch. Once it ends, the next promotional rhythm to watch is the December festive set: I'm Kim Korean BBQ has already announced its Christmas 4+1 (one free buffet per four paying diners, all December, advance online reservation compulsory) and a 3+1 student version at selected timeslots. Book early once December opens — Korean BBQ group deals fill weekend slots fast in the two weeks before Christmas.

Bottom line this week: Saturday for the FairPrice Xtra bulk and salmon run, Sunday before 29 November for the Cold Storage yuu layer, and today for the Mr Bean pancake if you're anywhere near an outlet.
