10.10 Ice Cream Wars & $1 Snacks: Best Food Deals This Week

Every October, Singapore's supermarkets and convenience stores go to war over your snack budget — and this week is the peak. Häagen-Dazs vs Ben & Jerry's vs half-price NZ Natural, $1 deals at 7-Eleven, the Spicy McNuggets comeback and a three-day 1-for-1 matcha window.

The 10.10 shopping festival isn't just for gadgets and skincare. This week the action is in the chiller aisle: three separate premium ice cream promos are running at the same time, convenience stores are practically giving snacks away, and a certain spicy nugget has returned to golden arches across the island.

The Ice Cream Three-Way Battle

This is the rare week where you can comparison-shop premium ice cream like it's a hawker centre:

  • Häagen-Dazs at Sheng Siong — 3 tubs for $25 (8–11 Oct 2026). Part of Sheng Siong's 4 Days Special, up to 55% off other groceries too. About $8.33 a tub.
  • Ben & Jerry's at Cold Storage & CS Fresh — 3 for $24.50 (till 14 Oct 2026). Slightly cheaper per tub at about $8.17, and three extra days.
  • New Zealand Natural at FairPrice — 50% off for members (till 14 Oct 2026). Condition: FairPrice membership (free via the FairPrice Group app).
🍦 The Verdict

Buying a single tub? FairPrice's half-price NZ Natural wins on flexibility. Feeding a household? Ben & Jerry's 3-for-$24.50 edges out Häagen-Dazs by 50 cents. Note the different end dates: Sheng Siong closes 11 Oct, the others run to 14 Oct.

7-Eleven Is Basically a Discount Bin Right Now
