Supermarket Weekly Deals Singapore (12–18 Nov 2026): FairPrice vs Cold Storage vs Sheng Siong vs Giant

All four major supermarkets switched on new promotions on the same day — FairPrice up to 66% off, Sheng Siong up to 55% off plus Häagen-Dazs at 2 for $19.80, Giant up to 62% off, and Cold Storage storewide. Here is who actually wins this week, and how to stack one trip into three layers of savings.

Thursday, 12 November 2026 is one of those rare alignment days in the Singapore grocery calendar: FairPrice, Cold Storage, Sheng Siong and Giant all rolled fresh promotions within 24 hours of each other. When that happens, the smart move is not to pick a favourite store — it is to read the four leaflets side by side, split your list by category, and hit at most two stores. This guide breaks down what each chain is actually offering this week, which conditions apply, and the exact day-by-day play for the 12–18 November window.

The Scoreboard: Four Chains, One Week

Store Headline Deal Window

Cold Storage Weekly storewide promotion, while stocks last; seniors +3% off on Wednesdays 12–18 Nov

A caveat before the fanfare: these are all "up to" numbers on selected items, not storewide flat discounts. The 66% at FairPrice and 62% at Giant will be one or two hero SKUs each; the realistic basket-level saving across a full trolley is far more modest. Treat the headline number as the bait, and the actual item list as the meal.

1. FairPrice (12–18 Nov): Biggest Headline, Best Membership Stack

FairPrice's weekly storewide specials run 12–18 November with discounts of up to 66% across fresh produce, frozen and chilled food, pantry staples, beverages and household products. Two things make FairPrice the anchor shop this week. First, the parallel Weekday Super Picks promotion (9–12 November) — a 4-day fresh window with China Shine Muscat grapes at 43% off and deals on Holland potatoes, which ends TODAY, so if grapes are on your list, go before tonight. Second, the chain has increased CHAS discounts to 6% from now until 1 January, which stacks on top of weekly special prices for CHAS cardholders — the only straightforward discount stack among the four chains this week.

Mark the calendar for Saturday: on 14 November, FairPrice Xtra Parkway Parade hosts the grand opening celebration of LOTTE Mart Express — a Korean grocery corner. The first 100 shoppers get a shot at the GOOD TODAY Scoop Bag challenge, there is Korean food sampling, a live Korean cooking showcase by Eric Youn, and an appearance by Tasha Low. Free samples plus an opening-day challenge is a genuinely fun free family outing — just go early if you want the first-100 slot.

2. Sheng Siong (10–16 Nov): The Stack King

Sheng Siong is running THREE overlapping promotions at once, which is where the real value hides. The 11.11 Price Drop (10–16 November) brings Buy-1-Free-1 deals and special prices across groceries, snacks, beverages and household essentials. On top of that, the Weekly Specials (10–16 November) take up to 52% off housebrand frozen food and up to 63% off purchase-with-purchase (PWP) deals. And from 12 to 15 November, the 4-Day Special adds up to 55% off selected groceries — including Häagen-Dazs ice cream at 2 for $19.80, TOP NANOX at 55% off and Glade at half price. If you shop Sheng Siong on Friday or Saturday, all three layers are live simultaneously.

The seafood counter is also worth a look this week — Sheng Siong has been highlighting fresh catches like crab and squid as daily specials, so prices vary by outlet and by the day's haul. Unlike the printed promotions, these are walk-in-and-see deals: worth checking if you are cooking seafood at home this weekend, but do not expect a fixed islandwide price.

3. Giant (from 12 Nov): The Bulk-Buy Play

Giant's November programme started 12 November with up to 62% off through its Super Savings weekly deals, hypermarket-exclusive specials and a year-end savings push, covering fresh groceries, everyday essentials and snacks (end date not announced — these roll weekly, so the item list refreshes). Giant's edge has never been the headline percentage; it is the hypermarket format. Big-pack staples, cleaning supplies and pantry refills are where Giant typically undercuts the smaller-format chains, and this week's promotion explicitly includes hypermarket-only specials. If your list is bulk toilet paper and rice rather than artisanal greens, Giant is the better single stop.

4. Cold Storage (12–18 Nov): Premium Picks, Senior Wednesday

Cold Storage's weekly storewide promotion also runs 12–18 November, limited-time offers while stocks last. Cold Storage rarely wins the discount-percentage arms race — its playbook is imported and premium items (Western cheeses, premium produce, housebrand jams) getting weekly special treatment. Two conditions worth knowing: seniors aged 60 and above get an EXTRA 3% off every Wednesday (that is 18 November — the last day of the promo, so Wednesday shoppers effectively get a double reason), and yuu membership perks apply in addition to weekly prices. For households that buy imported goods anyway, this is the week those baskets get cheaper.

⚠️ Condition Check

(1) All "up to X% off" figures apply to selected items only, while stocks last — none of the four chains is discounting the whole store. (2) FairPrice CHAS 6% requires a CHAS card and runs to 1 January. (3) Cold Storage's senior 3% requires age 60+, Wednesdays only. (4) Sheng Siong's three promos have different end dates: B1F1 and Weekly Specials end 16 Nov, the 4-Day Special ends 15 Nov. (5) Giant's programme is rolling weekly with no announced end date — item lists change.

💡 The One-Trip Stack: Friday or Saturday at Sheng Siong

Here is the highest-density shopping trip this week: walk into Sheng Siong on 13 or 14 November and three promotion layers are live at once — grab the Häagen-Dazs at 2 for $19.80 (4-Day Special, ends 15 Nov), pair a frozen staple from the housebrand up-to-52% list, and check the Buy-1-Free-1 racks for snacks and drinks before they end 16 Nov. One receipt, three overlapping promos. Then do a single FairPrice run before 18 November for fresh produce with the CHAS 6% on top if you hold the card. Two trips, everything covered.

The Bottom Line

This week's verdict: Sheng Siong wins on stackability (three overlapping promos through the weekend), FairPrice wins on membership maths (CHAS 6% on top of weekly prices, plus today's last-chance fresh picks), Giant wins for bulk staples, and Cold Storage wins for imported goods — especially for seniors on Wednesday 18 November, when the extra 3% lands on the promo's final day. The classic mistake is treating "up to 66%" as a storewide sale and doing one giant trolley at a random store. The winning play is two short trips: Sheng Siong on Friday or Saturday for the stack, FairPrice before the 18th for fresh — with a $1 sando or a dozen donuts slotted in between.
