Free Food Monday: The Week's Best Free & 1-for-1 Deals (Oct 12–18, 2026)

A free egg tart with zero purchase required, buy-9-get-3 donuts, a free McDonald's cone and a S$1 second Starbucks Venti — Oct 12–18 is one of the best freebie weeks of the year.
