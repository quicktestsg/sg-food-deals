And if a promo requires a detour of more than two MRT stops for under $2 of savings, the transport fare has already eaten the margin. The plays worth travelling for are the ones above $5 of savings: the $8 McSpicy meal, the nuggets box, and GoroGoro-style group promos — this month, the Curry Times $8.90 bento only if Woodlands is already on your route.
