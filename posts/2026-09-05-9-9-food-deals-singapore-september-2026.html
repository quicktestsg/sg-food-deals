9.9 Food Deals Singapore September 2026: $0.99 Coffee, $0.10 Matcha & Free Häagen-Dazs

The 9.9 sale season is not just for e-commerce. Luckin Coffee's new AMK Hub outlet is pouring first cups at $0.99, Shake Shake In A Tub is adding matcha lattes for ten cents, and SAFRA members can walk away with a full pint of Häagen-Dazs for free. Here is the complete playbook, sorted by what expires first.

Every September, the 9.9 shopping festival dominates the internet with vouchers and flash sales — but the quiet winners are the F&B promotions riding the same wave. This week alone there is a coffee at under a dollar, a matcha latte priced at ten cents, and a free tub of premium ice cream with no purchase required beyond a registration. This guide walks through every food deal worth your time between September 5 and 13, 2026, in order of urgency, with all the conditions spelled out.

1. This Weekend First (Sep 5–6): Free Ice Cream & $3 Cashback

Gelato Pique: free ice cream at Raffles City

Japanese loungewear brand Gelato Pique is giving away free ice cream at its Raffles City pop-up on September 5 and 6, 2026, from 1pm to 3pm. There is no purchase requirement mentioned in the announcement — the only catch is volume: 250 cups per day, so arriving before 1pm is the safe play. This one is first-come-first-served while stocks last.

DBS PayLah! $3 Saturday cashback — every Saturday till Dec 19

The promotion runs every Saturday from August 29 to December 19, 2026, including today (Sep 5) and next Saturday (Sep 12): pay with DBS PayLah! at participating hawker stalls, coffee shops, wet markets and heartland shops and get up to $3 cashback, starting from 6am. It is capped at the first 160,000 redemptions per Saturday, so morning hawker breakfasts are safer than late-night suppers. Pair it with any of the heartland deals below and the maths gets silly: a $3 cashback against a $4 breakfast is close to eating free.

💡 Stacking Strategy: Saturday Morning Triple Play

Saturday, Sep 12 plan: (1) breakfast at a hawker stall with PayLah! for up to $3 cashback; (2) swing by Giant or FairPrice for the VITAGEN Gut Health roadshow (Sep 12–13 weekend at selected stores); (3) if you are near a Giant with the Sakura Chicken promotion (runs till Sep 16), free sampling happens 11–13 Sep too. One morning, three freebies.

2. The 9.9 Window (Sep 7–9): Ten-Cent Matcha & $0.99 Coffee

Shake Shake In A Tub: $9.80 combo + $0.10 matcha latte (Sep 7–9 only)

For exactly three days — September 7 to 9, 2026 — Shake Shake In A Tub is running a 9.9 special: a $9.80 Popcorn Chicken Combo, with the option to top up just $0.10 for a Ceremonial Matcha Latte. That is a full chicken combo plus a matcha drink for $9.90 all-in, at Tampines OTH and 111 Somerset. The 9.9 branding is the whole gimmick, so expect queues at lunch — go off-peak if the schedule allows.

Subway: buy 1 breakfast wrap & coffee set, get 1 wrap free (Sep 7 only)

Subway Singapore just launched five new Breakfast Wraps from $3.90 (available daily from opening until 11am, from September 4 onwards, with chicken, egg, cheese, mushroom and avocado options). On Monday, September 7 only, buying one Breakfast Wrap & Coffee set gets a second wrap free. For a colleague pair or a shared morning, that is two wraps and a coffee at breakfast-hawker prices.

Luckin Coffee: $0.99 first cup + 1-for-1 at AMK Hub (Sep 3–16)

Luckin Coffee's grand opening at AMK Hub (inside Cheers) runs September 3 to 16, 2026, with two layers: 1-for-1 on drinks, and a first cup at $0.99 for first-time customers ordering through the Luckin Coffee app. If you have never used the app, this is about as close to free caffeine as September gets. Conditions: the $0.99 price is app-only and first-customer-only; the 1-for-1 runs the full promo window at the outlet.

Popeyes: Late Night Crunch supper sets from $8.90 (from Sep 7 onwards)

Starting Monday, September 7, 2026, Popeyes launches its Late Night Crunch promotion: three supper sets from $8.90, available after 10pm. The end date has not been announced, so treat it as an open-ended launch promo — the safe assumption is that it runs through September, but verify in-store before making a dedicated late-night trip.

Non-members are excluded — this is a membership play, pure and simple, but for existing SAFRA members there is genuinely nothing to buy. A 473ml Häagen-Dazs pint is premium supermarket-shelf stuff, so 'free with registration' is the best price ratio on this entire page.

Haidilao for two at $29.90++

5. What to Skip

Not everything labelled 9.9 deserves a trip. The SimplyGo Sanrio EZ-Link cards ($10 each, no stored value, from Sep 4) are for collectors, not commuters — as a transport card the value is zero until topped up. The F&N Magnolia My Melody & Kuromi bundle (2 multipacks at $16.80 for a free $19.90 organiser set, till Oct 31) only makes sense if the household actually drinks through two multipacks before the ice treats expire. And the BreadTalk bun bundles (15 for $32 / 30 for $59, till Sep 30) are excellent value per bun but demand freezer space and a serious household appetite — a 30-bun box is a commitment, not a snack.

Bottom line for the 9.9 week: the ten-cent matcha and dollar coffee are the headline acts, but the quietly best plays are the free Häagen-Dazs pint (if a SAFRA member in the family registers early) and the recurring Saturday $3 PayLah! cashback, which runs all the way to December 19. Deals come and go; cashback discipline compounds.
