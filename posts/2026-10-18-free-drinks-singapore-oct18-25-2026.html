Free Drinks in Singapore This Week: Mr Bean 1-for-1, Free IKEA Coffee & More (18–25 Oct 2026)

A soy drink bonanza that ends tonight, free coffee at IKEA all week, specialty coffee in the CBD for Trust cardholders, and a 65¢ soup side-quest — every drink deal worth your calendar between 18 and 25 October 2026.

Singapore's cafés will drain your wallet at S$6 a cup, but this particular week is unusually generous with free and near-free drinks. The catch: almost every deal below is date-locked to a narrow window between 18 and 25 October 2026, and two of the best ones are same-day-only. Here is the full schedule, sorted by what needs to happen first.

1. Today Only (Sun 18 Oct): Mr Bean Buy-1-Get-1-Free

Mr Bean is running buy-1-get-1-FREE on almost ALL soy drinks this Sunday, 18 October 2026 — one day only. Two sources (SINGPromos and EverydayOnSales) published the same promo within hours of each other, so the details are consistent: it covers nearly the whole soy drinks range, but some beverages are excluded. Confirm the eligible list at the counter before ordering that second cup.

Also closing tonight: KOI Thé's S$3 cashback coupon for teens via DBS PayLah!, which requires a minimum spend of S$10 and runs until 18 October 2026. The source notes the fine print contains some conflicting details, so read the coupon terms in the PayLah! app before counting on it.

⏰ Don't Miss These Windows

Mr Bean 1-for-1 and the KOI teen cashback both END tonight, 18 Oct. IKEA free coffee starts tomorrow (19–23 Oct), the $0.99 Ribena/Nissin redemption opens 19 Oct on the FairPrice Group app (collect by 25 Oct), and the free Tiong Hoe coffee pop-up runs 19–22 Oct.

2. Starting Tomorrow (Mon 19 Oct): Free IKEA Coffee or Tea, No Purchase Needed

From 19 to 23 October 2026, IKEA Singapore is giving away FREE coffee or tea at the Swedish Restaurants in all its stores — with no purchase required. Even better: IKEA Family members get two free coffees or teas per visit instead of one. Both SINGPromos and EverydayOnSales confirm the same dates and the no-minimum-spend condition.

Strategy note: a free drink plus IKEA's famously cheap food court makes this the cheapest 'cafe break' in Singapore this week. If you're not yet an IKEA Family member, sign up via the IKEA app first — membership is free and it instantly doubles your free cups.

3. CBD Bonus (19–22 Oct): Free Tiong Hoe Specialty Coffee from Trust Bank

From 19 to 22 October 2026, Trust Bank is giving away FREE Tiong Hoe specialty coffee at Ocean Financial Centre Outdoor Plaza, tied to eligible Trust Freedom Card benefits. Beyond the coffee, there's a sure-win scratch card with prizes from $50 to $8,000 and a promotion dangling up to 100,000 Max Miles. If you work in Raffles Place, this is a two-minute detour; if you don't hold a Trust card, the coffee may be out of reach — check the eligibility terms before making the trip.

4. The $0.99 Corner-Shop Pickup (19 Oct Redemption, Collect by 25 Oct)

On 19 October 2026, Cheers and FairPrice Xpress open a $0.99 app reward redemption for either a Ribena Blackcurrant Jelly Drink (160ml) or a Nissin Chicken Cup Noodles (72g) — collect your chosen item at participating outlets by 25 October 2026. The redemption happens through the FairPrice Group app, so install it and get your account sorted before Monday. Not free, but 99¢ for a drink-plus-snack run is close enough.

5. Ongoing Deals With No Rush (But Conditions Apply)

  • Goro Japanese Cuisine — free coffee with any main: from 16 October 2026 onwards, Hong Leong Finance customers get a free cup of coffee (Kopilah promotion) when ordering any main meal — just inform the restaurant team. End date not announced.
  • CHAGEE large milk tea via Reward Land: running from 12 October 2026 onwards, shoppers may redeem a $6 GrabFood voucher using Reward Land coins plus promo code AB5KRL toward a large CHAGEE milk tea (worth $6), with possible extra pickup discounts. Terms are fuzzy — treat this as 'potentially free' and verify in the app before relying on it. End date not announced.
  • Astons 65¢ mushroom soup (food, but irresistible): from 16 October to 31 December 2026, HLF Digital App users get the signature mushroom soup for $0.65 with a qualifying main course — dine-in only, every Friday and Saturday. The 65¢ price celebrates Hong Leong Finance's 65th anniversary.

7. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Sunday, 18 Oct (today): Mr Bean 1-for-1 soy drinks — last day. Teens: claim your KOI S$3 cashback before it expires tonight.
  • Monday, 19 Oct: Redeem the $0.99 Ribena or Nissin deal on the FairPrice Group app (collection open through 25 Oct). First day of free IKEA coffee. First day of the Tiong Hoe pop-up at Ocean Financial Centre.
  • Tuesday–Thursday, 20–22 Oct: Tiong Hoe free coffee continues in the CBD (ends 22 Oct). IKEA coffee runs all week — pair it with a meatball lunch.
  • Friday, 23 Oct: Last day of free IKEA coffee. It's also an Astons Friday — HLF Digital App users can pair a main with the 65¢ mushroom soup.
  • Saturday, 24 Oct & Sunday, 25 Oct: Collect your $0.99 FairPrice Group redemption if you haven't (deadline 25 Oct). Astons 65¢ soup is available again Saturday. Mr Bean fans — no promo today, that ship sailed on the 18th.

The Verdict

The headline act is Mr Bean's one-day 1-for-1 — act today or miss it entirely. The best no-strings deal of the week is IKEA's free coffee: no purchase, no membership requirement, five whole days. Everything else is a targeted promo — worth it if you already fit the condition (Trust card, HLF app, PayLah! teen, FairPrice Group user), skippable if you'd have to sign up for a financial product just to save S$3. That last math never works out.
