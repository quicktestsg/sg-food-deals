Grocery Deal Strategy Singapore (Sep 27 – Oct 7, 2026): FairPrice vs Giant vs Cold Storage vs Prime

Month-end is when every supermarket pushes its hardest promos at once. This week: Häagen-Dazs at half price at FairPrice, Ben & Jerry's 3-for-$25 at Giant, a 4-day Prime WOW sale, Welcia's 20% storewide weekend and Scarlett's fruit run — with every membership catch flagged.

Supermarket promotions in Singapore cluster around month-end — payday wallets meet expiring promo calendars — and this week is the densest window since June. Six separate grocery campaigns are running simultaneously across FairPrice, Giant, Cold Storage, Prime, Welcia and Scarlett, plus a mooncake buy-1-free-1 at Don Don Donki for the Mid-Autumn stragglers. This guide sorts them by what actually saves money: which store wins ice cream, which wins fresh produce, which wins household essentials, and which deals die tonight.

⏰ Expires This Weekend — Act Today
  • Giant yuu member special (up to 50% off): ends today, Sep 27, 2026. Needs yuu membership.
  • Giant Tampines & IMM 2-day store exclusives: Sep 26–27 only — today is the last day.
  • Welcia 20% off storewide: Sep 25–27 — also ends today, excluding Bedok Mall and Takashimaya outlets.

1. The Ice Cream War: Häagen-Dazs Half Price vs Ben & Jerry's 3-for-$25

Two premium ice cream promos are running head-to-head. FairPrice has Häagen-Dazs Mini Cups at 50% off until September 30, 2026 — the discount also covers butter, coconut, scallops and snack bites in the same campaign, so it is worth a wider sweep of the aisle. Giant counters with Ben & Jerry's at 3-for-$25 until September 30 as part of its weekly deals, alongside coffee, snacks and household bargains. Which wins? Without the regular per-pint prices published in either source, a strict per-unit comparison cannot be made honestly — but the structural difference matters: FairPrice's is a straight half-price discount (buy one, save 50%), while Giant's requires committing to three tubs. Single-person households get a better fit at FairPrice; freezer space and a serious sweet tooth favour Giant.

2. Giant: Three Stacked Promos, Two Memberships

Giant is running the most aggressive multi-layer campaign this week. Layer one: the yuu Members Special from September 25–27 at participating stores — up to 50% off selected fresh produce, fruits and meats, but only if you are a yuu member (free to sign up on the spot, but budget five minutes at the counter). Layer two: the Tampines Hypermarket and IMM 2-day exclusives on September 26–27 — fresh food, groceries and household essentials at promotional prices, no membership mentioned. Layer three: the islandwide weekly deals including the Ben & Jerry's 3-for-$25, running until September 30. The play: if you are near Tampines or IMM, go today — layers one and two are both live and stackable in a single trip, and the yuu discount on fresh produce is the deepest cut on proteins and vegetables this week.

3. FairPrice: The Two-Week Grind (Sep 24 – Oct 7)

FairPrice's 2-week promotion block runs September 24 to October 7, 2026 — the longest window of any chain this week, which means zero urgency. The campaign spans local favourites under the Singapore's Homegrown Treasures range, beverages, pet essentials and convenient meal options, plus Price Drop items. The headline Häagen-Dazs 50% offer is the time-limited slice (ends Sep 30); the rest of the block stretches into next week, so FairPrice is the store for the relaxed mid-week top-up rather than today's dash. Watch for the fresh promo leaflet when the block rolls over in early October.

4. Prime Supermarket: The 4-Day Heartland WOW Sale (Ends Sep 28)

Prime Supermarket's 4-Day Weekend Super Savings runs September 25–28, 2026, across all Singapore outlets — daily groceries, fresh produce and household essentials, while stocks last. Sources list the window slightly differently (one as Sep 25–26, one as Sep 25–28); the longer four-day window appears in the more detailed listing, so treat Monday Sep 28 as the end date but shop sooner rather than later for the fresh items. No membership is required. Prime's heartland locations make this the most convenient option for anyone not near a Giant or FairPrice extra — the value is proximity as much as price.

5. The Specialists: Welcia (20% Off) and Scarlett (Fruits)

Two specialists fill category gaps. Welcia — the Japanese drugstore-style chain — is taking 20% off storewide on participating brands from September 25–27 (today is the final day), covering beauty, personal care, health supplements and daily essentials. The catch is location: Bedok Mall and Takashimaya Shopping Centre outlets are excluded. If body wash, supplements or shampoo are on the list, today is the day. Scarlett Supermarket's Mid-Autumn fresh fruits promotion runs September 25 to October 1, with seasonal fruits — grapes, blueberries, pears among them — at promotional prices; no membership required, and the window extends past the weekend, so it can wait until the weekday crowd thins.

6. Still Mid-Autumn Somewhere: Don Don Donki Mooncake Buy-1-Free-1, $9.90

For those who missed the festival gifting window, Don Don Donki Singapore is selling its mooncakes at buy-1-free-1 for $9.90 in-store, for a limited time (end date not announced — Mid-Autumn was September 25, so expect this to vanish within days). Four flavours: Matcha Red Bean, Matcha Strawberry Milk, Sweet Potato with Yolk, and Black Sesame. At effectively $4.95 a box, it is dessert-tier pricing for mooncake-tier items — the cheapest way to keep the festival going a few extra days. In-store only, while stocks last.

7. Cold Storage: The Quiet Baseline (Till Sep 30)

Cold Storage's Grocery Selections promotion runs until September 30, 2026, with savings on everyday essentials — the least flashy campaign of the week but useful for one thing: Cold Storage carries product lines the heartland chains do not always stock, and a month-end essentials discount there can beat a wasted trip elsewhere. No membership or app requirement was mentioned in the source. Treat it as the fallback store: check its shelf first for imported items, then price-match mentally against FairPrice's two-week block.

  • Today (Sun, Sep 27), morning: Giant Tampines or IMM — the yuu 50% fresh special and the 2-day store exclusives both end tonight. Sign up for yuu at the counter if not already a member.
  • Today, if near a Welcia: 20% storewide ends tonight — personal care and supplements are the highest-value categories here.

💡 The Strategy in One Sentence

Front-load the perishables today (Giant yuu fresh at up to 50% off ends tonight), ride the ice cream promos through Wednesday, and let FairPrice's two-week block absorb everything else — the month-end rush rewards whoever shops by expiry date, not by store loyalty.

Prices and windows above come from the retailers' promo listings as aggregated this week; stock and participation vary by outlet, and month-end leaflet items sell out early — confirm on each chain's official channels before travelling for a specific item.
