1-for-1 Dining Deals Singapore September 2026: McDonald's, Burger King, Luckin & More

Six buy-one-get-one food promos are live in Singapore right now — two start tomorrow, one is a three-day weekend special, and two quietly expire this month. Here is the full checklist, sorted by how fast you need to act.

Singapore's 1-for-1 scene just got crowded. Between tomorrow's McDonald's x foodpanda tie-up, a Burger King spicy burger twin deal, and new-outlet promos from Luckin Coffee, the second half of September is stacked with buy-one-get-one options. But every single one has a catch — an app, a membership, a payment method, a daily quota — and mixing them up means paying full price at the counter. This guide sorts the deals by deadline pressure, states every condition, and skips nothing.

1. Harry's $0.99 Fish & Chips — The Only One Live Today

Harry's fan-favourite $0.99 Fish & Chips promotion is back across participating Singapore outlets, running 14–27 September 2026. The conditions are the strictest on this list: you must be a Harry's Rewards member, spend a minimum of $10++ at the table, and pay via Shopback. Do all three and the fish & chips drops to $0.99 — while stocks last. If you are not yet a Rewards member, sign up before you order, not after.

💡 Pro Tip

The $10++ minimum means $10 before service charge and GST — your real bill threshold is closer to $11–12 after taxes. Order a main plus a drink and you are usually over the line.

2. Scoot Everywhere Sale — Flights From S$79, Book by 20 September

Not strictly food, but if a short getaway counts as eating with a view: Scoot's Everywhere Sale runs 15–20 September 2026, with one-way fares from S$79. Sample fares from Singapore: Taipei from S$130, Seoul from S$169, Tokyo from S$225, and Europe from S$315, with selected travel dates available through 2027. Booking closes this Sunday — after that, fares revert without warning.

3. IKEA Swedish Meatball Ice Cream — 3-Day Weekend Special

For exactly three days — 18 to 20 September 2026 — IKEA Singapore's Swedish Bistro brings back the Swedish Meatball Ice Cream at $4.90: five meatball-shaped ice cream pieces with caramel sauce and lingonberry jam. It is a dessert built for the 'gram, priced like a regular cone. While stocks last, so go Friday rather than Sunday if you actually want one.

4. McDonald's x foodpanda 1-for-1 — Starts Tomorrow, Runs to 30 September

The headline deal of the week: from 17 to 30 September 2026, McDonald's Singapore and foodpanda are running an exclusive 1-for-1 promotion — two selected McDonald's favourites for the price of one when you order through foodpanda. The rotating lineup covers Breakfast Bagels, Hash Browns, McSpicy, Chicken McNuggets and the Samurai Chicken Burger, with different items featured each week. Check the app on Thursdays to see what the next week's rotation includes, and note the fine print: eligible DBS/POSB cardholders can stack an extra $5 off.

⚠️ Condition Check

This deal is delivery-app exclusive — you cannot get it by walking into a McDonald's or using the McD app. Budget for foodpanda's delivery and service fees when comparing against dining out; the 1-for-1 maths only wins if the fees stay under what the second item would have cost.

5. Burger King 1-for-1 Spicy Chicken King — App-Only, Till 30 September

Two spicy chicken burgers for the price of one, from 16 to 30 September 2026, exclusively through the Burger King Singapore app on dine-in and pickup orders placed after breakfast hours. The after-breakfast window matters — try it at 9am and the app simply will not apply the deal. No minimum spend is stated in the source promotion.

6. Luckin Coffee 1-for-1 + First Cup $0.99 — New Outlets, Till 28 September

Luckin Coffee has opened two new outlets — Bugis Junction and International Plaza — and is celebrating with a limited-time 1-for-1 drinks promotion from 15 to 28 September 2026. First-time app users get an even better entry point: their first cup for $0.99. Each store has different promotional dates and opening hours, so check the app for your nearest outlet before making the trip. For regulars, the wider implication: Luckin's expansion keeps pressure on Singapore's coffee price war, and new-outlet promos have been reliably recurring with each opening.

Bonus: Haidilao Pork From $0.99/Plate — Not 1-for-1, But the Cheapest Plate in Town

A different kind of multiplier: from 15 September to 15 October 2026, Haidilao's Northpoint City outlet runs a Pork Extravaganza with selected pork dishes at $0.99 per plate. The gates: Monday to Friday only, 11am to 4pm, capped at 20 promotional plates per day, and the table must spend at least $30 (the source notes a completion requirement tied to the spend). This is a weekday-lunch play for Yishun residents — 20 plates across the whole outlet means early arrival, not a 2pm stroll.

What to Skip

Not every promo on this week's calendar deserves a trip. Garrett Popcorn's Micro Bag keychain (from 16 September, S$7.70 with S$15 minimum spend, excluding Changi Airport, while stocks last) is a collectible, not a meal — you are paying S$22.70 minimum for popcorn plus a charm. And the MILO BREAKTIME plush sling bags (from 15 September, with selected MILO UHT Less Sugar packs at participating retailers, while stocks last) only make sense if you drink MILO daily anyway. Both are 'want' purchases dressed as deals; the seven items above are the actual 'save' list.
