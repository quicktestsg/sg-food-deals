Supermarket Price War: FairPrice vs Giant vs Cold Storage vs Sheng Siong (15-21 Oct 2026)

All four major chains reset their weekly promotions on the same day — FairPrice up to 66% off, Giant up to 61% off, Cold Storage buy-1-get-1, Sheng Siong 57% off plus a free $5 voucher.

Rare alignment this week: FairPrice, Giant, Cold Storage and Sheng Siong all launched overlapping promotion windows starting 15 October 2026. When every chain resets on the same Thursday, comparison shopping actually pays.

1. FairPrice — Two Overlapping Sales, Up to 66% Off

FairPrice is running two promotions at once: the Weekly Storewide Special (15-21 Oct, up to 56% off) and the 2-Week Promotions (8-21 Oct, up to 66% off — the deepest headline discount this week). Verified example: Adams Crunchy Peanut Butter 454g at $2.95 until 19 Oct, down from the usual $7.32. CHAS cardholders get an extra 6% off until 1 Jan 2027.

2. Giant — Three Sales in One, Up to 61% Off

From 15-21 October 2026, Giant runs three promotions at once: Super Savings (up to 61% off), Taste of China, and Tampines Hypermarket Opening Specials. Giant has also doubled its CHAS discount to 6%, confirmed until 30 October 2026 — unlike FairPrice's, which runs to January.

3. Cold Storage — Buy 1 Get 1 Free (15-21 Oct)

Cold Storage's Fresh & Grocery Selections runs 15-21 October: up to 50% off plus Buy 1 Get 1 Free multi-buys. Seniors 60+ get an extra 3% off every Wednesday — this cycle that's 21 October, the promotion's final day.

4. Sheng Siong — 57% Off Plus a Free $5 Voucher

Sheng Siong runs four promos at once, most closing 19 October: Price Drop up to 57% off (Dettol body wash saves $13.30), a free $5 voucher with $50 spend, Housebrand Frozen BOGO from $4.95 (13-19 Oct, all outlets), and a PWP unlock at $20 spent for up to 61% off. Seafood specials run 14-20 October.

⏰ Deadline Warning

Sheng Siong's Price Drop, frozen BOGO and PWP all end 19 October — two days before the other chains close on the 21st. Sunday the 18th is the last day to catch everything at once.

The Verdict: Which Chain for Which Basket

  • Deepest headline discount: FairPrice, up to 66% off until 21 October, stackable with the 6% CHAS discount.
  • Frozen food and stackable mechanics: Sheng Siong — BOGO from $4.95, $20 PWP unlock, $50-gets-$5-voucher — until 19 October.
  • Premium and deli fresh: Cold Storage — the 15-21 October BOGO matters most on meat, seafood and deli.
  • East-siders and CHAS holders before 31 Oct: Giant — Tampines Opening Specials plus the doubled 6% CHAS discount, until 30 October only.
  • Seniors: Cold Storage gives an extra 3% off Wednesdays; FairPrice and Giant run 6% CHAS discounts usable any day.

The Online Angle: RedMart Freebie Worth Adding

For online shoppers: POKKA gives a free foldable tote bag with $18 spent on POKKA Zero Sugar Teas at RedMart, from 14 October 2026. End date not announced; check the qualifying product list.

This Week's Grocery Playbook

The optimal split: Sheng Siong first (before the 19th) with a planned $50 basket, then FairPrice before the 21st with your CHAS card, Cold Storage only for BOGO items you'll finish (Wednesday the 21st for seniors), and a Giant Tampines trip if you're east.
