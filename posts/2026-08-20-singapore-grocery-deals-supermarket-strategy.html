Singapore Grocery Deals (Aug 20–26, 2026): FairPrice vs Giant vs Cold Storage vs Sheng Siong

All four major supermarkets refresh their weekly specials on the same day — August 20 — and each chain parks an extra cardholder discount on a different weekday. Here's the full playing field, the named-price picks worth grabbing, and a day-by-day plan that matches store to day.

Thursday, August 20 is the biggest grocery-reset day of the month: FairPrice, Cold Storage (CS Fresh) and Giant all launch new weekly specials today, while Sheng Siong is mid-way through its Double Deal Week. The real edge this week isn't picking one store over another — it's matching the right store to the right day, because three of the four chains attach an extra discount to a specific weekday. Every price and date below comes from the chains' own promo announcements, and every catch is spelled out.

1. The Playing Field: Four Sales, One Table

A word on the headline numbers: 'up to 61%' and 'up to 63%' describe the deepest single-item discounts, not the average saving across the store. Giant's number is technically the highest of the week, but its availability varies store to store — the promotional price list at one outlet may not match another. Treat the headline percentages as a reason to browse the weekly leaflet, not as a promise on any specific basket.

2. The Senior & CHAS Discount Ladder

The quietly powerful part of this week: the chains have staggered their cardholder discounts across different weekdays, so an eligible shopper can collect an extra 3–4% off three weeks running of sales — on three different days, at three different chains. Per the announcements: Giant gives seniors an additional 4% off every Tuesday; Cold Storage gives seniors aged 60 and above an additional 3% off every Wednesday; FairPrice gives Blue CHAS cardholders an additional 3% off every Thursday.

Today — Thursday, August 20 — is FairPrice's Blue CHAS day, and it's also day one of the new weekly specials. That combination matters: shopping the first day of a cycle means the promotional stock is at its fullest, and the weekday discount stacks on top of the promotional prices. If there's a FairPrice run in the plan this week, today is the day to do it.

🪪 Eligibility & Fine Print

The weekday discounts require proof: a CHAS card at FairPrice, and age verification at Giant and Cold Storage. The promo announcements note the discounts sit alongside the weekly specials, with some exclusions possible — confirm the eligible items at the customer service counter or checkout before filling the trolley, as specific exclusion lists weren't published in the announcements.

3. Sheng Siong's Double Deal Week — Ends Sunday

Sheng Siong is running two concurrent promotions from August 18 to 24, and the clock is the story here: the whole thing closes on Sunday, August 24. The Housebrand Frozen Weekly Special takes up to 52% off frozen items under its Happy Family and Tasty Bites house ranges, while a second track offers Buy 1 Get 1 Free items plus 'spend $20' specials on the same receipts. For a freezer-restock trip — frozen dumplings, prawns, processed favourites — this is the week's densest concentration of frozen-food value across the four chains.

4. Named-Price Picks: Three Items With Real Price Tags

Headline percentages need leaflet-reading; these three don't. Each has a confirmed price, a confirmed window, and — where the usual price was published — a savings figure straight from the source.

U Supermarket: Maggi 2 for $2.50 (Aug 21–23)

A three-day hot deal running Friday to Sunday only: two selected packs of Maggi Chicken or Curry flavour for $2.50 — $1.25 a pack — while stocks last. It's pantry-stocking money, not life-changing money, but if a U Supermarket is on the way home anyway, it's the cheapest named grocery price of the week. Selected flavours only, and the window is genuinely short.

VITAGEN Collagen Less Sugar: $3.95, Was $4.40 (till Aug 26)

From August 12 to 26, VITAGEN Collagen Less Sugar is $3.95, down from its published usual price of $4.40 — a 45-cent (about 10%) saving. The useful part is the footprint: the promo runs at Sheng Siong, Giant, Prime, FairPrice Online and selected FairPrice outlets, so it can ride along on almost any of this week's store trips rather than needing its own.

Udders × Ribena Blackcurrant Sorbet: $9.95 at FairPrice Finest (till Oct 31)

The longest-running pick: from August 19 to October 31, the limited-edition Udders × Ribena Blackcurrant Sorbet is $9.95 at FairPrice Finest stores, down from a published $11.87 — about $1.92 off. Every purchase comes with a free Ribena eco bag while stocks last, and sampling sessions are running at FairPrice Finest locations for anyone who wants to taste before committing freezer space. FairPrice Finest only — not the standard FairPrice format.

5. The Restock Everyone's Talking About: 7-Select Coconut Water

From August 19, the viral 7-Select Coconut Water is back at participating 7-Eleven outlets: 430ml, 100% real coconut water, no added sugar, not from concentrate — while stocks last. Given how the previous rounds sold through, the operative risk is availability, not price.

⚠️ Price Unconfirmed

The announcement didn't publish the selling price for this restock, so no price is quoted here. Check the chiller tag at a participating 7-Eleven before assuming last round's price still applies — restocks sometimes land at a different price point.

6. Strategy: What to Do Each Day This Week

  • Thursday, Aug 20 (today): The single best FairPrice day of the cycle — new weekly specials start AND it's Blue CHAS +3% day. Cold Storage and Giant also flip to new specials today. Grab the Udders sorbet at FairPrice Finest while the eco-bag stock lasts.
  • Friday, Aug 21: U Supermarket's Maggi 2-for-$2.50 opens its three-day window. If the weekend shop is planned for Sheng Siong, tonight or tomorrow is the time — the Double Deal Week has only days left.
  • Saturday–Sunday, Aug 22–23: Main weekend play: one consolidated Sheng Siong trip for the B1G1 + spend-$20 specials and the 52% housebrand frozen deal (closes Sunday), throwing in the $3.95 VITAGEN while there. Maggi's window closes Sunday too.
  • Monday, Aug 24: Quiet day — last day of Sheng Siong's Double Deal Week if the weekend slipped by.
  • Tuesday, Aug 25: Giant's senior +4% day, inside the 7-day/14-day sale window — the biggest single weekday bonus of the week for eligible shoppers.
  • Wednesday, Aug 26: Double-closing day: Cold Storage's senior +3% Wednesday lands on the final day of the FairPrice/Cold Storage weekly cycle — last chance on those specials before the Aug 27 reset.

💡 The One-Trip Play for the Weekend

If only one supermarket trip happens this week, make it Sheng Siong before Sunday, August 24: stack the Buy 1 Get 1 Free items with a $20-spend special on the same receipt, fill the freezer from the 52%-off Happy Family / Tasty Bites ranges, and add the $3.95 VITAGEN on the way out — one trip, three layers of the week's grocery value, all closing within 72 hours.
