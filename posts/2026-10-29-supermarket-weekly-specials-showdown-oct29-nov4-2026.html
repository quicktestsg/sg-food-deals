Supermarket Weekly Specials Showdown: FairPrice vs Giant vs Cold Storage (29 Oct – 4 Nov 2026)

All three big supermarket chains flipped their weekly specials books on the same day. Here's who's discounting what, which fine print matters, and how to build one grocery run that captures the best of all three.

October 29 is one of those rare alignment days in the Singapore grocery calendar: FairPrice, Giant and Cold Storage all kicked off fresh weekly specials this morning. That means the deals you planned around last week are gone, and a new set of prices is live — with headline discounts ranging up to 72% off. But headline numbers only tell part of the story. The real money is in the fine print: who gets an extra discount, when the promo ends, and whether stocks will actually last until the weekend.

2. FairPrice: The Steady Play (Up to 57% Off)

FairPrice's new weekly book (29 Oct – 4 Nov 2026) covers fresh produce, frozen and chilled food, pantry staples, beverages and household products, with savings of up to 57% across the board. It's rarely the flashiest single discount of the week, but the breadth is what makes it useful — you can do a full weekly shop inside one promotion cycle without wandering out of the deal pages.

💡 CHAS Bonus Most People Forget

FairPrice has increased its CHAS member discount to 6%, running now through 1 January 2027. If you hold a CHAS card, that's an extra 6% shaved off eligible items on top of the weekly specials — a quiet stack that compounds every single trip. Bring the physical card or link it in the FairPrice Group app before checkout.

3. Giant: The Big Number (Up to 72% Off)

Giant takes this week's headline crown with promotions of up to 72% off starting 29 October 2026, spanning groceries, fresh produce, meat, seafood, snacks, beverages and household essentials. Two caveats worth writing down: the chain has not announced an end date for this round, and every promotion is explicitly valid while stocks last. Translation — if a 72%-off item is on your list, shop early in the cycle, not on Sunday afternoon.

4. Cold Storage: The Senior Stack (Storewide + 3%)

Cold Storage and CS Fresh run their weekly storewide specials from 29 October to 4 November 2026, also while stocks last. The differentiator is generational: senior citizens aged 60 and above get an additional 3% off on top of promotional prices. Three percent sounds small, but on a $150 monthly grocery haul that's $4.50 back every month — and it stacks on items already marked down. If you're shopping with parents or grandparents, let the oldest card in the family pay.

5. Beyond the Big Three: Two Under-the-Radar Sources

  • Phoon Huat frozen salmon — $4.99 (26 Oct – 1 Nov 2026): The baking-supply chain is selling 180g frozen salmon fillets for $4.99, down from $7.45 — a saving of $2.46 per pack at participating retail stores, while stocks last. That works out to roughly $27.70/kg, a genuinely low number for salmon in Singapore. Stock the freezer before 1 November.
  • Sigma Food warehouse, Bukit Batok (ongoing, from 28 Oct 2026): A permanent-style warehouse outlet at 2 Bukit Batok Street 23, #01-15, selling frozen food, seafood, hotpot ingredients and snacks, open Monday to Saturday, 9am–6pm. No published discount structure — treat it as a price-check stop for bulk frozen and hotpot hauls rather than a guaranteed-deal destination.
💡 One-Trip Stacking Strategy

Don't visit three supermarkets in one weekend — that's how fuel and time eat the savings. Instead: (1) Thursday or Friday, hit Giant first for the deep-discount, while-stocks-last items; (2) swing by FairPrice for the staple basket and present your CHAS card for the extra 6%; (3) leave Cold Storage for the weekend only if a specific special is on your list — and bring a senior along for the 3%. If Phoon Huat is on your route, the $4.99 salmon alone justifies the detour before 1 Nov.

⚠️ Condition Check

Every deal above has fine print: FairPrice CHAS 6% requires a CHAS card (through 1 Jan 2027); Cold Storage's extra 3% requires age 60+; Giant's promos are while stocks last with no announced end date; Phoon Huat's salmon is at participating stores only; Mr Bean's 50% off excludes several drink items and is one-day only; The Soup Expert's $1 soup requires a $29.90 single-bill spend; Toast Box free treats require qualifying app orders; McCrispy pricing applies at participating outlets. None of these are hidden — but they all change the math if you show up unprepared.

7. The Verdict

This week's structure rewards a split strategy. Giant holds the biggest single number (72%) but the least certainty; FairPrice offers the most reliable full-basket value plus the CHAS stack through the New Year; Cold Storage is the play for households with a senior member. And the two outside bets — Phoon Huat's $4.99 salmon (ending 1 Nov) and the soup-counter $1 deals starting 1 Nov — are the kind of niche plays that quietly outperform the headlines. Shop the fine print, not the banner.
