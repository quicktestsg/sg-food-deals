1-for-1 Dining Deals Singapore (1–15 Nov 2026): Pizza Hut Melts $9.95, Haidilao Buy-1-Get-1 & More

November opens with a unusually strong buy-one-get-one lineup: two Pizza Hut Melts for $9.95, a free second platter of Haidilao beef or pork belly, Sunday BOGO donuts and a one-day sushi event at DON DON DONKI. Here is every 1-for-1 deal running 1–15 November, with dates, prices and catches spelled out.

A good 1-for-1 deal is the cleanest discount in food: no minimum spend gymnastics, no voucher hoarding — order two, pay for one. The first half of November 2026 is stacked with them, from a $9.95 Pizza Hut Melt pairing to a hotpot anniversary that gives away the second platter of meat outright. This guide sorts them by how fast you need to move, then adds the near-misses (fixed-price meal deals that beat some 1-for-1s on value) at the end.

1. Today Only (Sunday, 1 November): Two One-Day Windows

DON DON DONKI Sushi Day — Selected Sets from $7.90, Up to $4.10 Off

Today is DON DON DONKI's Sushi Day: a one-day-only sale on 1 November 2026, with discounts of up to $4.10 off selected sushi sets and seasonal Japanese favourites — prices start from $7.90. If salmon nigiri or a chutoro set is on your list, this is the day to grab it; the discount applies at all Singapore outlets while stocks last, and popular sets do sell out by evening at the busier stores. It ends tonight — there is no extension.

Dunkin' Sunny Egg Sunday — Buy 1 Get 1 Free, All Day

Every Sunday — including today — Dunkin' Singapore runs Sunny Egg Sunday: buy one Sunny Egg item and get a second free, all day, at 15 selected outlets. The lower-priced item is the free one, so if you're buying two different things, the cheaper one costs nothing. No app, no membership — the discount applies at the counter. The promotion is recurring weekly (end date not announced), so missing today isn't fatal — but today is the only day it overlaps with the DONKI sushi run above, which makes a rather efficient Sunday eating route.

2. The Headliner: Pizza Hut 1-for-1 Melts, $9.95 (1–15 November)

From 1 to 15 November 2026, Pizza Hut Singapore is selling two Melts for $9.95 — against the usual price of $19.90 for two, that is a genuine half-price 1-for-1, and the strongest flat discount of this window. The pairing works like this: one new-flavour Melt (Cheesy Otah or Korean Cream Cheese) plus one classic Melt. It is available for self-collection, takeaway and delivery, so you don't even need to leave the house. Two independent sources confirm the same $9.95 price and the 1–15 November window, which is a good sign the terms are stable. At under $5 per Melt, this beats most fast-food combo meals on cost per filling.

3. Haidilao 14th Anniversary: Free Second Platter of Meat (from 31 October)

Haidilao Singapore turns 14, and the anniversary promotion running from 31 October 2026 works on two levels. First: buy one get one free on Haidilao Beef and Shandong Pork Belly at S$14++ per portion — but only during selected off-peak hours, so check the timing window with your outlet before booking. Second, running all day: Female Chinese Mitten Crab and Fresh Live Baby Abalone at S$14++ each. The end date has not been announced — anniversary promos like this typically run for a few weeks, but treat it as 'verify before you go'. The math matters here: at S$14++ per portion with the second free, two platters of quality beef land around S$20 nett after service and GST — which, for Haidilao, is close to half off.

4. Through 30 November: The Student Special and the Soup Upgrade

Lotteria: Any 2 Burgers for $10 on Weekdays

Not a strict 1-for-1, but structurally identical: until 30 November 2026, students get any 2 eligible burgers for $10 on weekdays at any Lotteria outlet islandwide, with an optional add-on of 2 regular soft drinks and 1 large fries for $3.50. The condition is a valid photo student pass — this is checked at the counter. Five dollars per burger with the option of a full meal at $6.75 per head makes this one of the cheapest student lunches among burger chains right now.

Shabu Sai: Premium Soup at $3.99 with Free Platter (from 1 November)

From 1 November 2026, Shabu Sai's November Premium Soup promotion prices the Thai Green Curry Soup base at $3.99, down from the usual $4.99 — and every purchase comes with a complimentary platter at all outlets. A dollar off soup sounds small until you remember hotpot soup upgrades are charged per person; for a table of four, that's $4 off plus a free platter of ingredients. The promotion's end date has not been announced (the November naming suggests it runs through the month, but confirm in-store if you're planning a late-month visit).

5. Honourable Mentions: Fixed-Price Deals That Out-Value Some 1-for-1s

PastaMania $9.90 Weekday Lunch at The Seletar Mall

From 31 October 2026 onwards, the PastaMania at The Seletar Mall runs a $9.90 weekday lunch: a selected pasta dish plus a soft drink, Monday to Friday from opening until 5pm. It's location-specific — this pricing hasn't been announced for other branches. End date not announced. For Seletar-area workers, this is a full sit-down lunch under $10 without needing a companion to split a 1-for-1.

DON DON DONKI Black Friday Sale & Katsu Fair (1–30 November)

Miss today's Sushi Day and there's a consolation: the DONKI Black Friday Sale and Katsu Fair run the whole month of November, with specials on A4 Wagyu, Shirobuta pork, Angus beef, wagyu skewers and Menchi Katsu from $8.80. A4 wagyu under $10 to cook at home is the standout — the same grade at a restaurant would cost multiples more. Prices and cuts rotate weekly, so a mid-month re-visit is worth the trip.

SKAI Toy Story High Tea (31 October – 30 November)

For a splurge-tier treat: SKAI Restaurant on Level 70 of Swissôtel The Stamford is running a Toy Story-themed high tea from 31 October to 30 November 2026, daily 12.15pm–5.30pm — think Buzz Lightyear-inspired berry sponge, Alien Matcha Choux, Slinky Dog wagyu creations and themed drinks. A themed hotel high tea is a different budget class from everything else on this list, but for Toy Story fans or a special occasion, the skyline view does half the work.

⚠️ Price Unconfirmed — SKAI High Tea

The source announcements for the SKAI Toy Story high tea do not state the price. Hotel high teas in Singapore typically run in the S$60–100+ per adult range; treat that as a planning estimate only, not a quote, and confirm current pricing directly with SKAI or Swissôtel The Stamford before booking.

Festive Bonus: Rive Gauche Log Cake at 15% Off (till 15 November)

Early birds get the log cake: until 15 November 2026, Rive Gauche is taking 15% off its signature Chocolate Yuzu Rocher Log Cake — soft sponge with a crisp praline crunch. Year-end log cakes rarely get cheaper than their early-order price, and the 15 November cutoff lands well before the December rush, when discounts typically vanish and pickup slots fill. If a festive cake is in your December plans anyway, this is the cheapest window to lock it in.

What to Skip

Not everything marketed as 1-for-1 deserves the name. Skip deals where the 'free' item is a cheap add-on rather than a true duplicate (a free drink with a full-price main is a sides promo, not a 1-for-1). Be wary of pairing requirements you don't want: if the only eligible Pizza Hut pairing were a flavour nobody orders, the $9.95 price would be irrelevant — here the new-flavour-plus-classic structure genuinely works, but check the flavour list before assuming your favourite is included. And don't rush the SKAI high tea without a price check: an unpriced hotel afternoon tea booking is how a themed Instagram outing quietly becomes a S$100 per-head lunch.

The pattern across this window: the true half-price deals (Pizza Hut, Haidilao off-peak) reward planning, while the recurring ones (Dunkin' Sundays, DONKI weekly rotations) reward showing up early. Put the 15 November Pizza Hut and Rive Gauche deadlines in your calendar now — everything else can wait for the mood to strike.
