Snacks Under $5 in Singapore This Week (13–19 Nov 2026): $1 Sugar Rolls, $2.60 Banana Bites & Free Subway Wraps

A $5 note goes further than you think this week — fluffy sugar rolls for a dollar, cinnamon-dusted banana bites under three, and a second breakfast wrap on the house. Here is the full under-$5 snack lineup for 13–19 November 2026, with every date and catch spelled out.

Big set meals and 1-for-1 buffets get all the headlines, but the quiet wins happen at the snack counter. This week's lineup in Singapore proves it: a bakery classic drops to $1 for two afternoon hours, Old Chang Kee launches a new banana snack priced like it is still 2015, and Subway is quietly handing out free wraps before 11am. Every deal below was checked against its source announcement — dates first, catches second, prices exactly as published.

1. The One-Dollar Club: Polar Puffs Sugar Rolls

Polar Puffs & Cakes is selling its fluffy Sugar Rolls for just $1 on Monday, 16 November 2026, from 3pm to 5pm at selected Singapore outlets. It is a two-hour window, it is while stocks last, and that is precisely the point — this is a flash-style promo built for the afternoon-tea crowd. Show up at 3pm sharp, not 4:50pm.

  • Deal: Sugar Rolls at $1 each (usual price not stated in the source — but a $1 bakery item in 2026 is self-evidently cheap)
  • Dates: 16 November 2026 only, 3pm–5pm
  • Catch: Selected outlets only; limited stocks — go early

2. Old Chang Kee's New Banana Bites: 3 for $2.60

From 13 November to 13 December 2026, Old Chang Kee is introducing Big Crispy Banana Bites with Cinnamon Sugar — three pieces of golden, crispy banana bites with a soft centre for $2.60 per box, available at all outlets in Singapore while stocks last. No app, no membership, no time window. Walk up, pay $2.60, walk away happy. That kind of frictionless cheap eats promo is increasingly rare, which makes this one of the week's easiest picks.

  • Deal: Big Crispy Banana Bites with Cinnamon Sugar, 3 pieces for $2.60
  • Dates: 13 November – 13 December 2026
  • Catch: While stocks last

3. Subway: Buy a Breakfast Wrap + Coffee, Get a Second Wrap Free

On 13 and 14 November 2026, before 11am, Subway is running a two-day breakfast promo: buy any Breakfast Wrap with Coffee set and get another wrap free. The set price itself was not stated in the source announcement, so check your Subway app or in-store menu for the exact figure before committing. If you pick two wraps of different prices, the billing follows the promotion's terms — confirm at the counter rather than assuming. Even so, a free wrap on top of a breakfast set is one of the better morning deals this week.

  • Deal: Free wrap with every Breakfast Wrap + Coffee set purchase
  • Dates: 13–14 November 2026, before 11am only
  • Catch: Set price not confirmed in the source — check the app or in store; different-priced wraps billed per promo terms

Not a discount, but budget-relevant: from 12 November 2026 onwards, McDonald's Singapore has added two new crispy chicken bacon burgers — the McSpicy B.L.T. and McChicken B.L.T. — with crispy chicken, bacon, lettuce, tomato and creamy sauce at participating outlets. Pricing was not included in the announcement we saw, so treat it as price not confirmed — check the McDonald's app before ordering. Historically McChicken-tier burgers sit comfortably in under-$5 territory, but verify rather than budget on assumption.
