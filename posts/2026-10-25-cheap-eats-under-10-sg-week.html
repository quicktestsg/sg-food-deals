Cheap Eats Under $10 in Singapore This Week (26 Oct – 1 Nov 2026)

A $5.50 spicy KFC macaroni bowl, $4.90 takeaway pizzas, a one-day $7.90 yakiniku set and a 1-for-1 cheesecake weekend — plus November wagyu deals worth planning ahead for.

The last week of October is quietly one of the best eating windows of the month — anniversary promos, reopening deals and Halloween specials are stacked on top of each other, and several dip well under $10.

Pizza Hut's $4.90 Regular Favourite pizza deal (usual price $23.95, roughly 80% off) runs 21–27 October 2026 — but only at five participating outlets, takeaway only. Monday 27 October is the last day.

KFC's Samyang Buldak Carbonara Macaroni Bowl launched 21 October 2026 at $5.50 (usual price $10.75), with a free Medium Sprite upsize. Loaded Fries from $3.95. No end date announced.

Mark Friday 30 October 2026: Yakiniku-GO's 5th anniversary brings the Meat It Yourself set at $7.90++ (usual price $17.90++) for one day only, plus a members-only perk.

Cat & the Fiddle's anniversary 1-for-1 whole cheesecake deal runs 27–29 October 2026, with early access from 26 October. Then on 30 October, the GYUPOP Wagyu Beef Fair opens in Jurong — A5 from $30/200g, $5 skewers.
