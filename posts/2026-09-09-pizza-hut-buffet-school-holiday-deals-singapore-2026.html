Pizza Hut Buffet Singapore September 2026: $19.95 All-You-Can-Eat Returns + School Holiday Food Deals (Sep 9–27)

September school holidays are here, and the deal calendar is unusually stacked. Pizza Hut's 90-minute all-you-can-eat buffet — a January sell-out — returns September 10 to 27 at $19.95++ across six outlets, with a new Otah Laksa sourdough pizza in the rotation. Around it: IKEA's kids-eat-free week ends September 11, McDonald's is handing out free mini cones until September 13, and Sushi Express will hand you a free bento if you buy five. This guide puts every deal in date order, spells out every catch (membership, app, timing, ++), and finishes with a day-by-day holiday eating plan.

Everything below is checked against the source announcements — dates, prices, outlets, membership requirements and payment conditions. The Pizza Hut buffet details were cross-referenced across three separate sources (SINGPromos, LadyIronChef and SG榴连), which agree on the essentials: $19.95++ starting price, 90 minutes, six outlets, September 10–27. Where a source did not publish a detail (exact outlet addresses, kids' pricing), this guide says so instead of guessing. Prices marked ++ mean service charge and GST are added on top — always budget roughly 20% above the sticker number.

1. The Headliner: Pizza Hut All-You-Can-Eat Buffet Returns (Sep 10–27, $19.95++, 6 Outlets)

Pizza Hut Singapore's dine-in buffet is back from September 10 to 27, 2026, after selling out when it ran in January. The mechanics: 90 minutes, unlimited refills of six pizza flavours, selected pastas, sides, desserts and soft drinks — ice cream included. Entry price is $19.95++, and the ++ matters: after service charge and GST you are realistically paying in the mid-$20s per adult. The six participating outlets are split between 3 Dine-In Stores and 3 Eat-In Stores — the source announcements confirm the count but not the full address list, so check Pizza Hut's own channels or booking page before travelling. The flavour rotation includes classics (Hawaiian, beef, chicken supreme, thick-crust, ham-and-mushroom, cheese-and-spinach sourdough) plus this edition's local special: the Otah Laksa Sourdough — spicy otah paste with chicken on a laksa-sauced sourdough base. It is the first genuinely local flavour the buffet has run, and it is the reason to go early in the window rather than late: new flavours have a habit of running out during peak slots. Book ahead — January's run sold out, and September school holidays will add family traffic on top.

2. Ending This Week — Grab Before They're Gone

Three deals close within the next five days, so they come first. PlayMade 9.9 (September 9 only — today): Taiwan Milk Tea at $0.90 via the app, $1.90 walk-in, with a pudding pairing available. One day, two prices, app-order wins by a dollar. IKEA Kids Eat Free (September 7–11): IKEA Family members get one free children's meal with every qualifying adult main course at the Swedish Restaurant — four kids' meal choices including Kivik juice and ice cream. Membership is free, so the only real catch is joining before you order. This is a weekday promotion running through Friday, which makes it the cheapest full kids' meal of the holidays. McDonald's Free Mini Cone (until September 13): a free mini ice cream cone for kids at all Singapore outlets — no purchase condition was stated in the announcement, but 'for kids' is doing the work in that sentence, so expect to be buying something anyway. Free is free.

3. Running Through Mid-September

These have one to two weeks left — enough to plan around, not enough to forget. Seafood & Beyond $2 Seafood & Wagyu Fair (September 8–16, Tanjong Pagar Plaza): 1kg of sotong for $2, and lobster reportedly from $9.90 — the source flags 'subject to availability', which at these prices means go early in the day. Sushi Express Buy-5-Get-1 (September 7–20): buy five bento sets and the lowest-priced one is free — cashless payment required. DONKI Donpen birthday freebies (September 8–13): a free Donpen cooler bag with $50 single-receipt spend, all stores, while stocks last. Jollibee OK Laksa lucky draw (until September 13, extended): buy an eligible OK Laksa Value Meal with Coca-Cola, scan the QR code for a chance at prizes including a 2D1N staycation at The Laurus — a lottery, not a discount.

4. All Month / Ongoing — No Urgency, Real Value

The long-runners, where planning beats speed. Curry Times $8.90 bento (September 7 – October 7, Woodlands MRT): roasted super-drum chicken rice with mini spring rolls and two house sauces, at the station itself. Seorae JIB 10th anniversary platter (from September 8, end date not announced): a $59 Premium Jeju Pork Platter featuring four cuts the source values at $100. Koufu $0.50 off Bun Set A (until September 30): with the official e-coupon at participating outlets. HIP POT weekday lunch hotpot (from $14.90++, One Holland Village, end date not announced): lunch sets at a 4.7-star hotpot spot — weekday lunch pricing at dinner-quality vendors is the most underused lever in Singapore dining.

💡 Buffet Math: Is $19.95++ Worth It?
  • The real number: $19.95++ lands around $24–25 after service charge and GST. That is your true entry price.
  • Break-even: at à-la-carte Pizza Hut pricing, a main plus a side plus a drink typically clears the mid-$20s fast. Everything after minute 40 is profit.
  • Who it suits: hungry teenagers and big-appetite adults. Light eaters will not clear break-even and are better off with Curry Times' $8.90 bento — a quarter of the price, no clock.
  • Pacing tip: 90 minutes is generous but refills arrive slow at peak. Order the first round immediately, including the Otah Laksa Sourdough before it runs out.

7. What to Skip

Skip the Pizza Hut buffet if you are a light eater — $24–25 real cost against an appetite that clears two slices is a donation, not a deal. Skip driving to Woodlands specifically for the Curry Times bento. Skip the Jollibee lucky draw as a reason to buy. And skip the Seafood & Beyond fair after mid-afternoon. The deals worth moving for this fortnight are exactly three: the buffet (if you can eat), IKEA (if you have kids), and the sotong (if you wake up early).

Bottom line: the September 2026 school holidays have an unusually clean structure — a headline buffet with a firm window (Sep 10–27), a cluster of kids-eat-free deals closing this week, and a layer of long-running cheap sets underneath. Book the buffet now for a weekday slot, hit IKEA before Friday with the family membership sorted, grab today's $0.90 milk tea, and let everything else fit around your week rather than the other way around.
