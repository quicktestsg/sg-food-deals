Pizza Deals Showdown Singapore 2026: Pizza Hut 2 for $22, $4.90 Takeaway & Shakey's 1-for-1

Three overlapping pizza promos landed on the same week — Pizza Hut's $22 twin bundles, a $4.90 takeaway steal, a 48-hour Shakey's 1-for-1, and a Halloween feast with a mystery gift. Here's which one actually deserves your money, and when to strike.

It's a genuinely unusual week for pizza in Singapore. Pizza Hut has just launched a three-week promo window (21 October – 11 November 2026) covering everything from a $4.90 takeaway pizza to two-pizza bundles, a separate Halloween bundle is already running, and Shakey's has dropped a 1-for-1 that lasts exactly two days. This guide breaks down each deal — real prices, real dates, real catches — and tells you which order to attack them in.

1. The Headline Deal: Pizza Hut 2 for $22 / 2 for $33 (21 Oct – 11 Nov 2026)

From 21 October to 11 November 2026, Pizza Hut Singapore is offering 2 regular pizzas for $22, or 2 large pizzas for $33. That works out to $11 per regular pizza or $16.50 per large — a structure built for family dinners, movie nights and gatherings where one pizza is never enough. Pizza Hut is advertising savings of up to 80% across this promo window, though the deepest cuts apply to specific items like the $4.90 takeaway deal below, not automatically to every bundle.

One thing to check before ordering: the bundle applies to eligible pizzas only, so confirm which flavours are included in the official announcement or the ordering app before building your dream combo.

2. The $4.90 Takeaway Steal (Same Window: 21 Oct – 11 Nov 2026)

Running inside the same promo window is a $4.90 takeaway pizza deal. This is the single cheapest entry point into the whole Pizza Hut promotion — under five dollars for a pizza, provided you collect it yourself rather than paying delivery fees. If you live or work near a Pizza Hut outlet, this is the one to build a cheap lunch around.

💡 Combo Strategy

The $4.90 deal and the 2-for-$22/2-for-$33 bundles run in the same window. Solo lunch? $4.90 takeaway. Feeding three or more? Two larges at $33 beats stacking multiple $4.90s on variety and size — but check which eligible pizzas each deal covers before committing.

3. The Flash Deal: Shakey's 1-for-1 (22–23 Oct 2026 ONLY)

Shakey's Singapore is running a two-day 1-for-1 pizza deal on 22 and 23 October 2026 — buy one pizza, get a second free. The catch: you need to be a Parlour Club member, though sign-up is free, so this is more of a speed bump than a barrier. Register before you order rather than at the counter.

⚠️ Price & Conditions Check

Shakey's has not published the pizza prices or eligible flavours in the deal announcement — price not confirmed, so check in store or on their ordering channels. The 1-for-1 runs exactly two days (22–23 Oct) and requires free Parlour Club membership. Don't assume premium toppings are included.

4. The Halloween Bundle: Cheesy 7 Trick or Treat Feast (16–30 Oct 2026)

Already running since 16 October and available until 30 October 2026, Pizza Hut's Cheesy 7 Trick or Treat Feast bundles a large Cheesy 7 pizza, your choice of side dish, two pieces of garlic bread and a mystery gift — while stocks last. It's available for both takeaway and delivery, which makes it the only deal here that works without leaving your house. The mystery gift is a Halloween touch that makes this the obvious pick for a kids' party weekend.

Note: the bundle price is not stated in the promo announcement — price not confirmed, so check the app or store before ordering. The mystery gift is limited to stock availability, so late deciders may miss out.

If pizza isn't the plan, two other cheap-eat deals are live this week that cost less than a cup of kopi at a café:

  • <strong>7-Eleven Wednesday App Deals (21 Oct, from 8am):</strong> A1 melon toast for 10¢, Wall's Onigiri Ice Cream for $2, CP Buttermilk Chicken Burger for $2.50, and a Cheesy Coney chicken baked macaroni at $3 — app-exclusive, so clip the coupons in the 7-Eleven app first.
  • <strong>Jack's Place $8 Nett Student Set (from 20 Oct 2026, ongoing):</strong> One main (steak, chicken chop or pasta) plus a soft drink, weekday lunch only, dine-in at 12 islandwide outlets. Add $1 nett for the day's soup and free-flow garlic bread. End date not announced.

The Bottom Line

The best value depends entirely on party size. Solo: the $4.90 Pizza Hut takeaway is unbeatable. Couple: Shakey's 1-for-1 on 22–23 Oct — two pizzas, one bill, if prices check out. Family or crowd: Pizza Hut's 2 large for $33 runs three full weeks, so use it as the default and layer the Cheesy 7 feast on top if the mystery gifts are still in stock. Whatever you pick, every deal here has a stated window between 21 October and 11 November 2026 — after that, it's back to menu prices.
